**Scope:** source brief, source-route readiness and whole-body source selection. No ARM acceptance, canon promotion or Stage C.

## 1. End the current salvage route as the ARM solution

Retire GO-ARM-N1 and its N1_R6 derivative as candidates for advancement. Preserve them, their evidence and the U-series lineage unchanged as historical/source-artifact evidence. The U8 local axillary repair may remain a successful local repair; it does not establish suitability of that entire body as a central Gorrund reference.

This order supersedes 05V's requirement to freeze the U8 realization of "massive body organization" and its prohibition on restarting body architecture. It also supersedes any conditional closure instruction that would advance this lineage on the strength of local repair alone. Preserve historical decisions with a dated supersession note rather than rewriting their history.

Do not run another neutralization, smoothing, muscularity reduction, local field rebuild, solver or segment warp on N1/U8 to produce N2. Do not derive or promote a measurement scaffold from them.

Canon remains authoritative. "Massive load-bearing structure" remains a requirement; the particular source's bodybuilder bulk, bent knees, giant feet and garment-hidden anatomy do not become requirements.

## 2. State the actual Saurin lesson accurately

Read and cite the existing TS9 capability report, the Rodin source-adoption directive and 05M's source-body ruling. The successful route was:

**convincing external source sculpture → justified localized anatomical corrections → whole-body acceptance → measurement/scaffold work.**

The failure was not established to be Blender as an application. Code-built/SDF anatomy and broad reconstruction failed to supply the required biological surface quality. An actual skilled sculpt in Blender or another sculpting application is acceptable. A procedural reconstruction does not become a direct sculpt merely because it is saved in a `.blend` file.

## 3. Produce the canon-based source brief now

Read the current GORRUND_V1 and REFERENCE_ANATOMY_V1, relevant comparative rules and 05K–05V negative evidence. Produce a concise artist/generator-ready brief with exact repository citations and a separate negative checklist. Distinguish locked anatomy from OPEN morphology and builder choices.

Required source presentation:

- One coherent mid-adult Gorrund, fully unclothed for anatomical assessment, a neutral matte surface and an unobstructed body. No fused garments, wraps, belt, loincloth, bracers, jewellery, mane or braids concealing landmarks.
- Existing leonine beastfolk head/ear and tail requirements retained, integrated with the body; head/tail must not rescue the body-only identity. Do not invent tail dimensions or new sex-related biology. If a modesty proxy is necessary, it must not obscure measurement landmarks.
- Central current muscularity and body fat, with identity supplied by broad/deep axial architecture, structurally substantial joints and coordinated pelvis–torso–limb relationships. No bodybuilder definition, permanently bulging muscle, artificial skinny limbs or featureless clay cylinders.
- Upright neutral standing, extended but unlocked hips/knees, natural spine and head carriage, both heels/soles down, parallel feet about hip width, arms relaxed with small abduction and palms toward thighs. No crouch, hunch or aggressive pose.
- Large supportive five-toed plantigrade humanoid feet with ordinary nails, without paws, claws, prehensility or oversized monster-foot distortion. Credible adult hands and limb taper.
- Nominal Gorrund reference stature 229 cm, with the prior GOREF 230.9 cm identified as lineage bookkeeping, not a required copied realization. Construct proportions intentionally; do not uniformly enlarge another population or invent new numeric canon to fit a candidate.
- Clearly continuous biological anatomy across neck/shoulder, thorax/back, pelvis/hip, knee/ankle and extremities. No assembled muscle pieces, tubular appendages, field-blend lumps or baked lighting substituted for geometry.

Deliver clean front/profile/back and three-quarter reference requirements, a body-only review specification and explicit contrasts with matched-height Marchfolk, Skarn and Grask. Do not turn the brief into hundreds of invented numeric targets. Source quality and whole-body organization are the first gate.

Prioritize a fresh, purpose-built source from a capable sculptor or a suitable external sculpt-generation service, as Saurin actually used. A blank canvas is acceptable for an actual skilled sculptor; another capsule/ellipsoid/SDF anatomical body generator is not the fallback.

Use the known asset inventory; do not repeat an exhaustive drive search for the already-recovered but unsuitable armored source. Do not use N1/U8 or rejected 05A–05P bodies as shape targets, donors or disguised starting points. Their images may document what to avoid only, clearly labelled.

Return one recommended executable route, its inputs and actual capability/access, output formats, expected cost/credits if applicable and the next concrete action. If generation is already explicitly authorized for that service and cost, proceed within that authorization. Otherwise prepare the exact submission/commission package first and report the specific missing authorization or access; do not spend external service credits, contact an artist or submit a commission without authorization. Do not expose credentials.

If visual concept/reference generation is available in the established toolchain, prepare a neutral whole-body concept for source selection, clearly labelled concept only, not a measured 3D reference. Do not infer that a nice 2D image establishes valid hidden 3D anatomy. No claim of a capability that the session does not actually have.

## 5. Whole-body source gate before repair work

For any new candidate, submit the whole-body views and body-only views before undertaking cleanup, detailed metric validation or scaffold derivation. Show the actual candidate, its provenance and deviations from the brief. Assess:

1. Does it read as a coherent adult organism with the intended Gorrund structural identity at neutral composition?
2. Is it distinct from enlarged human/Skarn, rangy Grask, bodybuilder and monster/ogre caricature when head, tail, equipment and presentation are neutralized?
3. Are the source pose, feet, visible landmarks and continuous anatomical transitions suitable from the start?
4. Is surface quality good enough that subsequent work can remain genuinely localized?

A source failing these checks is rejected or revised at source. Do not launch a multi-stage repair campaign to justify sunk effort. Beauty, technical cleanliness and numeric protection alone are insufficient. A source passing this gate is only an accepted source for bounded corrections, not an ARM or biological canon.

## 6. Remove the validation deadlock without manufacturing acceptance

The 05V closure identifies that C1/C5-LOW requires a LOW companion while the old order forbids one before acceptance. For the new lineage, a **validation-only low-muscularity derivative** may be prepared after the source passes the initial whole-body gate and before final ARM acceptance, solely to test persistence of structural identity. It is not a scaffold, creator asset or canonical composition variant.

Pre-register its method and disclose all builder choices. Hold the same body's structure and pose; do not generate a different creature or use smoothing that erases structural anatomy as the LOW comparison. If a credible composition derivative cannot be made, report that capability dependency rather than running a logically impossible gate or treating failure to produce LOW as proof against canon.

Detailed acceptance tests follow source selection; fix the known tail-fragment contamination and show the complete clean control sheets before any fresh blind scoring. Do not rerun a known-invalid protocol as a substitute for source quality.
