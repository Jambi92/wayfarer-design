## A. Stature and proportions

**A1 Standing height**
- [ANAT] Min about 122 cm (4'0"), reference about 137 cm (4'6"), max about 152 cm (5'0") — "provisional first-pass Durrim height range" (§4–10 L15–19; restated L85).
- [OPEN] Statistical distribution "left for later" (L19).
- [ANAT] Overlap intentional: upper-range Durrim overlap lower Marchfolk and Sagekin; 152 cm Durrim vs 152 cm Marchfolk "must never look interchangeable" (L19).
- [ANAT] Height never uniform scale: coordinated leg length, torso length, spinal and neck contribution, head-body relationships, limb segments (L19).
- [VAL] Height-boundary test at about 122, 137, 152 cm (L75; L498).

**A2 Torso length/depth/width**
- [ANAT] Broad skeletal thorax relative to stature; substantial thoracic depth relative to stature; compact vertical torso; strong torso-pelvis continuity; width, depth, vertical length, circumference, skeleton, muscle, fat kept distinct (§11–17 L25).
- [ANAT] Thoracic depth: greater skeletal depth than equivalently tall Marchfolk; guard: never automatically large belly/high fat/huge chest muscle (L29).
- [ANAT] Thoracic breadth: greater skeletal ribcage breadth than equivalently tall Marchfolk; Narrow stays Durrim, Broad not caricature (L30).
- [ANAT] Greater torso contribution to height than equivalently tall Marchfolk, not an absolutely long torso; vertically compact, never long torso on short legs / compressed human torso / near-square block (Part 2 §4–14 L97).
- [DER] Ribcage breadth, depth, vertical length, lower-ribcage relationships, spine integration "vary independently"; averages trend broader/deeper relative to stature (L101).
- [VAL] Thoracic depth vs fat: lean Durrim can have substantial skeletal depth — "a major validation case" (L103).
- [ANAT] Abdomen/waist: no required belly, barrel or straight waist; narrow waist valid (L104).

**A3 Limb length & segments**
- [ANAT] Lower average limb contribution to total height than equivalently tall Marchfolk, especially legs; never stub-like; full adult articulation (§18–26 L38).
- [ANAT] Arms shorter in absolute length and contribution; upper arm, forearm, arm-to-torso ratio, joint scale and hand contribution distinguished (L38); "long enough for natural self-contact, tool use, weapon handling, climbing" (L113).
- [DER] Upper-arm and forearm lengths vary independently within relationship-aware limits; "no fixed arm ratio" (L113).
- [ANAT] Legs via coordinated pelvis, femur, knee, lower leg, ankle, foot, never one bone (L119); femur and lower leg "needn't shorten by identical percentages" (L119).
- [ANAT] Long bones: greater skeletal structural presence relative to length than Marchfolk; "skeleton, not muscle" (L40).

**A4 Shoulder/pelvic relationships**
- [ANAT] Shoulders: substantial clavicles relative to stature, strong joint presence, broad-shoulder potential; never from deltoids alone (L31; L105–106).
- [DER] Ribcage vs shoulders "not locked together" (L102).
- [OPEN] Pelvis morphology "OPEN, requires detailed anatomical design"; never a widened human pelvis (L32; L108; L518).
- [ANAT] Pelvic breadth may shift with frame; distinct from hip fat, glutes, thigh muscle (L109).

**A5 Neck**
- [ANAT] Relatively short contribution to total height; absolute length, relative length, circumference, skeleton, muscle kept separate; substantial skeletal integration without large neck muscles (L21); always a visible transition into the shoulder girdle (L107).
- [ANAT] Head-neck integration as "added major identifier", never via neck muscle (Part 3 clar. 1, L249).

**A6 Hands/feet**
- [ANAT] Hands relatively large and substantial for stature; absolute/proportional length, palm breadth/depth, finger length/thickness, wrist kept separate; not automatically muscular/calloused (L44); "never one Hand Size slider" (L115).
- [SOFT] Greater palm share of hand length is a "provisional population tendency"; relatively long fingers valid (L115).
- [ANAT] Fingers varied; never universally short and thick (L45).
- [ANAT] Feet relatively large; broad skeletal base; "never one Foot Size control"; multiple valid length/breadth combinations (L46; L121).
- [ANAT] Dexterity never reduced; crafting skill not anatomy (L115; L455).

**A7 Reach**
- [OPEN] Gameplay reach OPEN; never solved by stretching arms (L150).
- [ANAT] "Anatomical arm reach, interaction-system reach, combat reach and camera targeting reach stay separate, never one global Reach value" (L455).

**A8 Posture / resting alignment**
- [ANAT] Neutral resting alignment is "an ordinary relaxed stance, never a permanent crouch, hunch, bowed legs, wide combat stance or forward lean" (Part 5 §1–5 L428).
- [PRES] Anatomical movement constraints separate from cultural/personal body language (L428).
- [ANAT] Lower center of mass emergent; no gameplay consequence (L34; L428).

**A9 Population-specific axial structures** — SILENT (no tail or other axial appendage).

**A10 Head-to-stature (whole body)**
- [ANAT] "May have a somewhat greater head-to-total-height contribution than the Marchfolk Human Reference Population … but never an oversized fantasy-dwarf head" (L21; L170).
- [VAL] Oversized head is a permanent failure condition (L508).

## B. Skeletal Frame
- [ANAT] "Durrim skeletal structural presence must never be represented through muscle alone" (mandatory) (L50).
- [DIR] Narrow/Balanced/Broad are "frame presets within Durrim anatomy, not subtypes"; frame = continuous configuration (L52).
- [ANAT] Narrow: less breadth, keeps compact architecture and limb/joint/hand-foot identity; may reduce ribcage, shoulder, pelvic, long-bone breadth and joint scale "inside Durrim-specific minimum structural relationships" (L52; L127).
- [ANAT] Balanced: "central creator starting point, never the canonical, most correct or default NPC Durrim body" (L128).
- [ANAT] Broad: increases ribcage/shoulder/pelvic breadth and joint presence "never every dimension equally"; never automatically muscular, fat or male (L52; L129).
- [ANAT] Frame independent of height ("no fixed height-frame packages") and composition (L131).
- [ANAT] Joints substantial relative to limb length and stature, never enlarged independently (L40); elbows never "dwarf shorthand" (L113); knees not cosmetic (L119).
- [SOFT] Broad frame may correlate "softly and probabilistically" with craniofacial presence (L189).

## C. Physical Composition
- [ANAT] Full variation in muscularity, fat amount and distribution; "Durrim aren't universally stocky in composition" (L54).
- [ANAT] Regional muscle (neck … calves) varies independently; no labor-stereotype emphasis (L54; L135).
- [ANAT] Fat: broad range, "no artificially high minimum"; distribution separate from amount; sex-related patterns OPEN (L135).
- [ANAT] Mass from height, skeleton, density assumptions, frame, muscle, fat; legacy "0.65 human height, about 1.05 mass" is "only an old signal … never literal" → replaced by "unusually high body volume and structural mass relative to their stature compared with Marchfolk, with exact mass determined by anatomy and composition" (L54).
- [VAL] Low-muscle Durrim (permanent validation) — fails if removing muscle makes them look human (L135).
- [OPEN] Muscular Development Capacity — SILENT (term not used; only Current-composition variation is stated).
- No population-specific tissue systems (SILENT).
