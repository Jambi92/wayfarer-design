## A. Stature and proportions

**A1 Standing height**
- [ANAT] Min about 208 cm (6'10"), reference about 229 cm (7'6"), max about 251 cm (8'3") — "FIRST-PASS BIOLOGICAL RANGE — SUBJECT TO CROSS-RACE AND WORLD VALIDATION" (§6–10 L27–31).
- [ANAT] "height alone must never establish Gorrund identity" (L31). 251 cm = "current world-validation upper stature, never a permanent maximum" (L31; L651).
- [ANAT] Height never uniform scaling; distributes through spine, torso, pelvis, femur, lower leg, neck, head-body (L31).
- [OPEN] Final height after comparative and world validation (L159; L697). Distribution — SILENT.

**A2 Torso**
- [ANAT] Axial skeleton major identifier: broad thorax, substantial depth, vertebral/torso integration; never rectangular block (L39).
- [ANAT] "Gorrund generally possess a greater proportional torso contribution to total standing height than Grask at matched height, while retaining fully adult, functional limb lengths." (L169).
- [ANAT] Thoracic breadth trends high; greater than Grask at matched height and greater axial breadth than Skarn at equal height; never max width on everyone (L41); "the lower valid end of Gorrund thoracic breadth must still preserve Gorrund identity through depth, joints, axial integration and overall skeletal scale" (L179).
- [ANAT] Thoracic depth: "substantial thoracic skeletal depth relative to stature and breadth" (L42); "one of the strongest positive signals" (L180).
- [DER] Breadth, depth, vertical length, abdominal vertical contribution, waist, pelvic breadth and depth independent — "never one Torso Mass concept" / never one Torso Size or Body Thickness (L43; L175).
- [ANAT] Thoracic skeletal depth ≠ abdominal soft-tissue projection (L181); cross-section anatomically shaped (L182); ribcage length substantial (L183); no universal V or barrel (L184); no "ogre belly" (L186).
- [ANAT] Torso not vertically compact: "substantial ribcage vertical length and large axial vertical contribution … never a vertically scaled Durrim" (L731).

**A3 Limb length & segments**
- [ANAT] "Grask use limb elongation more strongly as a stature/reach signal; Gorrund use overall structural scale and axial architecture more strongly." (L57).
- [ANAT] Lower limbs contribute less proportionally than Grask at equal height, "never meaning short-legged" (L196); femur-to-lower-leg varies (slight femur, balanced, slight lower-leg), ratios OPEN (L196).
- [ANAT] Arms: "Grask should generally exhibit greater arm length and arm-span contribution relative to total stature than Gorrund." (L200); span emerges from shoulder/upper arm/forearm/hand, never one slider; distribution OPEN (L202).
- [ANAT] Arm clarification: large absolute arms, "increased arm length or reach relative to total standing height is NOT currently a defining Gorrund trait"; "Do NOT use 'long-armed' as shorthand" (L277–281).
- [ANAT] Forearms substantial, never Grask emphasis; upper-arm-to-forearm ratios OPEN (L67; L202).
- [ANAT] Limbs "Not shorter: limbs stay fully substantial contributors to a tall adult body" (vs Durrim, L730).

**A4 Shoulder/pelvic relationships**
- [ANAT] Skeletal shoulder breadth separate from deltoid/trapezius; low-muscle Gorrund can have very broad skeletal shoulders (L45; L187).
- [DER] Shoulder-to-pelvis "continuous variation from more shoulder-dominant through balanced to more pelvis-present, not subtypes" (L188).
- [ANAT] Pelvis: "substantial load-bearing skeletal dimensions and strong integration with both the deep axial torso and large lower limbs"; narrower valid pelvis supported; morphology OPEN (L189; L49).
- [ANAT] ALPC transitions: shoulder→torso, thorax→lower trunk (waist definition still valid), lower trunk→pelvis (pelvis "integrated with the axial body, not an independently widened hip structure"; morphology OPEN "but its relationship to the torso is now positively constrained"), pelvis→leg (never mandatory enormous thighs) (L715–718).
- [VAL] Pelvis, hip, femur, knee work as one biomechanical chain (L232).

**A5 Neck**
- [ANAT] "moderate vertical neck contribution with high structural integration into the shoulder/torso complex"; no required missing/bull/very short neck (L46); low-muscle still has skeletal neck integration (L192). Never enlarged Durrim (L47).

**A6 Hands/feet**
- [ANAT] "Five digits per hand and five digits per foot: four fingers and an opposable thumb, and five toes. This is locked" (L208; supersedes provisional L72).
- [ANAT] Hands large, substantial, fully dexterous; multidimensional, never one Hand Size (L206; L210).
- [SOFT] Palms substantial breadth and depth relative to hand length — "not a hard requirement" (L210).
- [ANAT] Feet large plantigrade, multidimensional; foot length never exaggerated to signal size (L214–216); plantigrade locked (L71).
- [DER] Hands-wrists and feet-ankles correlate inside envelopes without hard lock (L232).

**A7 Reach**
- [ANAT] "Large absolute reach is not the same as Grask-style increased reach relative to stature" (L65); substantial absolute anatomical reach, not unusual span relative to height (L623).
- [OPEN] Anatomical / interaction / combat / camera-targeting reach separate; no automatic gameplay range (L98; L623).

**A8 Posture / resting alignment**
- [ANAT] "upright plantigrade bipedal posture, never a required hunch, knee bend, forward lean, bowed legs or crouched monster stance"; resting alignment separate from cultural/personal body language (individual may slouch, loom, etc.) (L601; L48; L191).
- [ANAT] "Load-bearing skeletal architecture describes anatomy, not movement speed or animation tempo." (L94).

**A9 Population-specific axial structures**
- [ANAT] ALPC is the population-specific axial relationship (L705–721). No tail — SILENT.

**A10 Head-to-stature**
- [OPEN] Head scale suits body; avoid tiny-head giant, oversized ogre head, uniformly scaled human head; head-to-height ratio OPEN (L299).
- [VAL] Head-body integration across frames and min/ref/max height; max 251 cm fails on "tiny-head giant syndrome"; min 208 fails if head-body stops reading Gorrund (L407–409).
- [VAL] Body identity recognizable with head neutralized (L86).

## B. Skeletal Frame
- [ANAT] "Gorrund skeletal massiveness exists independently from Current Muscularity and Body-Fat Amount." (L21); "Massive" = biological structural scale first (L19).
- [DIR] Narrow/Balanced/Broad editable starting configurations, not subtypes (L80); alter skeletal breadth only, never height, muscle, fat, hand/foot size, face (L220).
- [ANAT] Narrow keeps axial depth, scale, joints, integration; "Narrow skeletal breadth does not automatically mean low skeletal depth." (L222); ALPC kept in Narrow — "a critical identity test" (L719).
- [ANAT] Broad: never auto muscle/fat/strength; "broad frame never hard-links to maximum thoracic depth" (L224).
- [ANAT] Balanced: "never canonical, most correct or average in every dimension" (L80).
- [ANAT] Bone diameter, joints, hands, feet "never scale linearly with stature" (L224).
- [ANAT] "Substantial joint structural presence appropriate to large load-bearing bones, without exaggerating the joints into monstrous anatomy." (L74); "one of the strongest body signals" (L76); knees/ankles (L63; L196); "thick-boned" not technical language (L76).
- [SOFT] "a population-level anatomical correlation does not automatically create a hard creator-control dependency" (L220).
