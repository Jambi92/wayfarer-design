# UCCA extraction — Pipkin (non-facial / whole-character)

Source: `specs/pipkin/PIPKIN_V1.md` (1675 lines, read fully; status FIRST-PASS COMPLETE, L3, L1674). Comparator: `reviews/short-race-comparative-anatomy-v1.md` (cited as SRC Lnnn). Face anatomy is out of scope (UFCA_V1); face items appear only where they touch whole-character systems.
Line refs are to PIPKIN_V1.md unless prefixed SRC. Section refs: P1–P6 = Parts 1–6; Parts 1–2 use grouped section numbers (e.g. P1 §26–49).

Primary identity (P6 §30 L1498–1506): 1 Primary = **Low-Set Compact Trunk Architecture**; 2 Supporting = comparatively light skeletal construction + proportionally sustained limbs; 3 Supporting facial = Integrated Mature Facial Architecture; 4 Secondary = overlapping ear and surface tendencies; 5 Not identifiers = hairstyle, facial hair, clothing, freckles, eye color, body hair, personality, culture, occupation, movement stereotype. "Describes diagnostic importance, not a requirement that every individual display an exaggerated version of each tendency" (L1506).

## A. Stature and proportions

### A1 Standing height
- [ANAT] Min **about 91 cm (3'0")**, reference **about 107 cm (3'6")**, max **about 122 cm (4'0")** (P1 §5–11 L25–27).
- [OPEN] "FIRST-PASS BIOLOGICAL RANGE — SUBJECT TO CROSS-RACE AND WORLD VALIDATION" (L29); final range OPEN, "current ~91–122 cm range remains provisional" (P6 §32 L1531).
- [VAL] Max meets Durrim min near 122 cm = **Cross-Population Equal-Height Boundary Test**; single-height, "no broad overlap implied" (L29, L192). SRC L28: Durrim ~122–152 cm, ref ~137.
- [VAL] Cogling/Pipkin intentional overlap **approximately 91–107 cm** (L94; SRC L26, L31). Cogling ~76–107 cm, ref ~91 (SRC L26).
- [VAL] Recognizable beside a differently scaled image of another population, "never relying on camera scale, furniture, other characters or door height" (L29).
- [DIR/OPEN] Roster envelope: **SUPERSEDED** — Pipkin no longer set lower playable boundary; Cogling extend it to about **76 cm (2'6")**; roster spans approximately **76 cm (2'6") to 251 cm (8'3")**, provisional pending world validation (L119). Short-race span ~**76–152 cm**, "approximately a twofold difference" (SRC L204).
- Distribution shape within range: SILENT (frequency/weighting OPEN, L1555).
- [DIAG] Prototype ~0.7× Marchfolk (~121 cm) places a typical prototype Pipkin near the max/Durrim boundary, not the ~107 cm reference; non-authoritative (P6 §34 L1619).

### A2 Torso
- [ANAT] **Low-Set Compact Trunk Architecture** statement: "a mature, structurally broad pelvis contributes strongly to the central body silhouette while the thorax remains comparatively moderate in breadth and depth and the limbs retain substantial proportional contribution to total stature" (P2 §1–6 L133). Core relation: **moderate thorax → compact lower trunk → mature structurally broad pelvis → proportionally sustained limbs** (L135).
- [ANAT] Term is "project anatomical terminology, **not a creator-facing slider**" (L135).
- [ANAT] "Low-set" = relative structural importance of lower trunk/pelvis; never sagging torso, low posture, short legs, crouching, low shoulders or heavy belly (L135). "Compact trunk" never Durrim vertical compression, no-waist, barrel/round body or high fat; less vertically compact than Durrim (L135).
- [SOFT] vs normalized Marchfolk: **modestly reduced vertical central-trunk contribution to total stature**, compact lumbar/waist transition, pelvis whose vertical height, depth and 3D participation remain substantial relative to thorax; persists even when pelvic breadth approaches Marchfolk values; "population-level relational tendency, not a fixed ratio" (L137).
- [ANAT] Reduced trunk share absorbed "primarily through sustained limb contribution and pelvic vertical contribution, **not through enlargement of the head**"; never requires unusually long legs or Durrim compression (L137).
- [ANAT] Torso compact but less broad/deep/massive than Durrim at matched height; vertical contribution moderate, never compressed to Durrim levels (P1 §12–25 L41–42).
- [ANAT] Thorax fully adult, moderate breadth/depth; never childishly small, Durrim-deep or paper-thin (L147; L43–44). Thoracic breadth broad individual variation, generally lighter than Durrim, Broad valid (L43).
- [SOFT] **Pelvis carries greater structural importance relative to thorax than in Marchfolk** — tendency, not fixed ratio (L148).
- [ANAT] Thorax-to-pelvis transition coherent adult (lower ribcage, waist, abdomen, pelvis); never abrupt "small chest → huge hips" (L154).
- [ANAT] Waist visible or less-defined both valid, neither defines race; abdominal roundness never identifier; lean flat-bellied Pipkin fully recognizable (L155).
- [OPEN] Torso ratios (L125, L1533).

### A3 Limbs and segments
- [ANAT] "**Limb contribution remains comparatively preserved despite short stature**" — separates Pipkin from Durrim (P1 §26–49 L54).
- [ANAT] Preserved ≠ unusually long; at matched height Pipkin greater proportional limb contribution than Durrim — "a major diagnostic" (L56).
- [ANAT] Legs contribute more than Durrim at matched height but **never automatically exceed ordinary human adult proportions** (Marchfolk relationship subject to validation); not defined by long legs (P2 §18–35 L164).
- [ANAT] Femora never uniformly shortened human femora; femoral contribution meaningful (L56, L164).
- [SOFT/VAL] Femur-to-lower-leg: slight femur emphasis, balanced, or slight lower-leg emphasis valid; extremes excluded; balance **OPEN** (L164; L56; L1536). SRC L69: "Valid slight lower-leg emphasis may occur but is not the racial anchor."
- [ANAT] Knees/ankles trend smaller than Durrim at matched height, fully adult, never fragile (L56). Knee position adult, never childlike (L164).
- [ANAT] Arms short in absolute terms, adult, functional, not reach-specialized; "short only because Pipkin are short and never stunted"; moderate upper-arm/forearm variation, **no Grask-like forearm emphasis**; unusual arm span not a trait (L56, L164).
- [OPEN] Arm proportional contribution vs Durrim "ratio OPEN" (L56); upper-arm-to-forearm and arm-span distribution (L1537).

### A4 Shoulder / pelvis
- [ANAT] Shoulders adult for stature; neither childishly narrow nor Durrim-substantial by default; Narrow/Balanced/Broad supported, **independent of muscle** (L47). Never artificially narrowed to signal smallness (L166).
- [ANAT] Shoulders may be narrower than, similar to or broader than pelvis within valid anatomy; "the race isn't one silhouette" (L166).
- [ANAT] Pelvis distinct and adult; **never a scaled-down juvenile pelvis (mandatory)**; never reused Durrim pelvis (L50; L160 "the two never share one pelvis").
- [ANAT] Pelvic breadth: meaningful adult breadth; never extremely wide hips, feminized anatomy, high fat or one hourglass silhouette; "this is skeletal anatomy" (L149). Pelvic depth adult 3D, never widened scaled human pelvis (L150). Pelvic maturity fully adult (L151).
- [VAL] Pelvic breadth not primary discriminator vs Durrim (L192); pelvic width alone isn't identifier (L196).
- [ANAT] External hip width: skeletal breadth, gluteal muscle, fat distribution and external circumference stay separate (L153).
- [OPEN] Shoulder architecture; pelvic morphology incl. depth and implementation-ready dimensions (L1534–1535; L50, L150).

### A5 Neck
- [ANAT] Adult; never tiny child neck, Durrim-like compact heavy neck or Aelari-like elongated neck by default; "relationships later" (L48). No further neck specification: SILENT.

### A6 Hands / feet
- [ANAT] Hands: "relatively moderate in size for their stature, with adult palm/finger relationships and fine dexterity, lacking the unusually substantial hand architecture approved for Durrim and Gorrund" (L58); "fully adult dexterous hands with moderate structural scale relative to stature" (L170).
- [ANAT] **Five digits per hand** (four fingers, one opposable thumb), ordinary nails, no claws (L60); five digits/toes **locked** (L172).
- [DIR-implied/ANAT] Hand length, palm length, breadth, depth, finger length/breadth, thumb, wrist dimensions are **independent** (L172). Fingers shorter/balanced/longer valid, "never long fingers as elven shorthand"; wrists never tiny to make hands look larger (L172).
- [ANAT] Feet: "adult plantigrade humanoid feet that may be somewhat substantial relative to stature … not oversized caricature feet" (L62); "modestly elevated structural contribution relative to total stature" (L174).
- [SOFT] Feet somewhat larger relative to height than Marchfolk as **population tendency, not a requirement**; "feet aren't the primary identifier" (L176; L64).
- [DIR-implied/ANAT] Foot length, forefoot breadth, midfoot, heel, structural depth, ankle relationship separate; "never one 'big feet' control" (L64, L176).
- [ANAT] **Five toes**, non-prehensile, no claws; **plantigrade (locked)** (L64). Arch distribution **OPEN**; no flat feet inferred (L176).
- [ANAT] Ankles lighter than Durrim; larger valid foot never via implausibly tiny ankle (L176).
- [OPEN] Hand proportions; foot proportions and arch distribution (L1538–1539).

### A7 Reach
- [ANAT] Less absolute reach than tall populations; never converted to interaction/combat/gameplay penalties (**OPEN**) (L56; L896–898). Reach follows approved proportions, gameplay OPEN (L164).
- [VAL] Visual reach, interaction targeting distance, combat reach and usability assistance are separate systems (L898).

### A8 Posture / Anatomical Resting Alignment
- [ANAT] Spine: **upright adult humanoid spinal alignment**, no mandatory hunch, crouch or lean (L49).
- [ANAT] "Pipkin default anatomical resting alignment is upright adult humanoid alignment" (P5 §2 L699); LSCTA does not require crouch, hunch, flexed hips/knees, low shoulders, bowed legs, exaggerated lumbar arch, belly-led, chest-first or childlike stance (L701–710).
- [ANAT] "**Anatomical Resting Alignment is separate from Cultural or Personal Body Language**" (L714).
- [SOFT] Low absolute center of mass; behavior emerges from torso/pelvis/limbs/composition; never automatic balance/knockdown/agility bonus (L68; L718). No universal "wide halfling stance" (L720); stance width differs by frame/composition (L722).

## B. Skeletal Frame
- [ANAT] **Light skeletal construction** = lower skeletal breadth and depth, smaller joints, more gracile long bones than Durrim at similar height; never weak, frail, underweight, thin, low-muscle, low-fat or delicate personality (L21).
- [SOFT] **Smaller skeletal joint dimensions** vs Durrim at matched height (tendency); Broad-frame may have larger joints than Narrow; "never one hard racial value" (L68).
- [ANAT] Long bones lower breadth/depth relative to length than Durrim — "major skeletal distinction"; never osteoporosis, weakness, fragility, injury-proneness, no gameplay consequence (L68).
- [ANAT] Light vs Durrim across long bones, joints, thorax, shoulder girdle, pelvis, while mature pelvis may still be prominent relative to own thorax — "these are compatible" (L166).
- [DIR] **Narrow, Balanced, Broad = "editable skeletal starting presets, not castes"**; never directly set height, muscle, fat amount or distribution, face, hair, personality or class (L68).
- [ANAT] Narrow: may reduce shoulder/thoracic/pelvic breadth and joint size; adult, healthy (L68); keeps mature pelvis, LSCTA, adult limbs; never scaled human/childlike/Fenn-like/fragile (L45, L166).
- [ANAT] Broad: may increase shoulder, thoracic, pelvic breadth and joints within validity; never Durrim, miniature Skarn or automatically muscular; "frame stays skeletal" (L46, L68, L166).
- [SOFT] Frame may influence pelvic breadth; Narrow never erases pelvic relationship, Broad never exaggerates to caricature (L166).
- [VAL] Marchfolk-similar frame stress: near-human shoulder/thoracic breadth stays Pipkin; "no single width carries the race" (L195).
- [OPEN] Detailed joint dimensions (L1540).

## C. Physical Composition
- [ANAT] Skeletal frame, current muscularity, body-fat amount, body-fat distribution **separate** (L72; L180).
- [ANAT] Muscularity low→high; muscle wraps skeleton without turning thorax/pelvis/joint scale/limb contribution into Durrim; muscular Pipkin may have substantial external volume over still-light skeleton (L180).
- [VAL] Low muscle reveals healthy adult skeleton and "**fails if it reads as a child**" (L180).
- [ANAT] Regional muscle varies (shoulders, arms, forearms, chest, back, abdomen, glutes, thighs, calves) (L180).
- [OPEN] **Muscular Development Capacity** — "never lowered because Pipkin are small" / never infer reduced strength from stature (L72, L180, L1541).
- [ANAT] Body fat ranges broadly, **no "round halfling" default**; never assumed chubby/round/lean/food-loving (L72, L180). Distribution independent of amount (face, torso, abdomen, hips, glutes, thighs, arms); patterns future work (L180; OPEN L1542).
- [ANAT] "**Pelvic skeletal contribution is not wide hips caused by body fat**" (L182). Higher fat may soften waist/pelvic landmarks/limb definition without creating identity (L184).
- [VAL] All Narrow/Broad × low/high muscle × higher fat combos valid; **no hidden physique packages** (never Broad → muscular → high fat or Narrow → lean → low muscle) (L184).
- [SOFT] Composition may affect inertia, soft-tissue motion, ROM; no stereotype collapse (muscular≠stiff, high-fat≠slow/waddling, Narrow≠agile, Broad≠heavy-footed) (P5 §29 L979–985).
- Population-specific tissue systems (Saurin-style): N/A, SILENT.

## D. Biological surface
- [ANAT] "**No Pipkin surface phenotype is required to prove that a character is Pipkin**" (P4 §1 L474).
- [ANAT] Skin: broad natural humanoid range, very low→very high melanin, varied undertones; no light/tan/ruddy/rosy/brown/dark default (L480). Base pigmentation independent from tanning, flushing, freckles, scars, dirt, cosmetics, magic, lighting (L482–490). Frequencies **OPEN** (L492).
- [ANAT] Tanning response minimal→substantial, not inferred from complexion; freckling propensity/density/size/distribution independent; never a hallmark; **no "freckled halfling" default** (L496–500). Moles/lentigines subject to later universal skin-detail architecture (L498).
- [ANAT] Rosy cheeks/nose not Pipkin biology (L504). Palms, soles, lips may differ naturally — modeled as biological surface systems, not painted racial masks (L506).
- [ANAT] Nails ordinary, not claws/talons/digging; length beyond ungroomed growth, polish, shaping = presentation (P4 §12 L571–575).
- [ANAT] Birthmarks/congenital pigmentation = individual variation (L597).
- [ANAT] **Four Skin Appearance Layers** (L611–617): L1 Natural (pigmentation, freckles, birthmarks, vascularity); L2 Environmental (tanning, weathering); L3 Applied (tattoos, cosmetics, paint); L4 Acquired (scars). Must remain distinguishable when rendered appearance overlaps.
- [DIAG] Whole-body surface analysis uses the Skin Appearance Layers; FD-* domains are facial-only (P4 §18 L629–631). Lighting/exposure/camera/grading (observation) and wetness/dirt (Environmental) must not change racial identity (L631).
- [SOFT] Regional exposure differences (face, hands, forearms, feet) possible; "no fixed regional-weathering map is approved yet" (L609). Detailed skin/weathering distributions OPEN (L1548).
- [VAL] Health-neutral: ordinary variation not disease/defect/penalty; real conditions need own design, not racial sliders (P4 §17 L621–623).

## F. Age
