## A. Stature and proportions

### A2 Torso length/depth/width
- [ANAT] Trunk is "not a human torso with a tail socket" (§6 L92). Thorax moderately deep rather than extremely broad; lower axial trunk has greater longitudinal contribution than Marchfolk; ribcage-to-pelvis transition integrated, not pinched at human waist; lumbar/sacral supports tail continuation; abdomen flexible, not barrel-rigid (§6 L94-99).
- [ANAT] Thorax: moderate breadth, meaningful AP depth, long lower-rib/abdominal transition, less clavicle-dominant than humans; no Gorrund axial mass / Vael thoracic depth copy (§7 L107-113). Implies no respiratory/aquatic advantage (§7 L115).
- [DIR] Creator hard bounds (§258 L4164): thoracic depth ±8 %; thoracic width ±7 %; axial trunk length ±10 %.
- [VAL] Coupled: thoracic depth/width ratio 0.80–1.00 ("deep narrow-to-moderate shell"); depth floor scales with breadth; ventral fullness (§263) counts against this guard (§258 L4166; §263 L4245).
- [SOFT] Sex: lower axial trunk female centre +7 %, male 0 %, inside ±10 % bound (§6 L101; §263 table L4225).
- [OPEN] Exact vertebral count / rib arrangement (§6 L103); exact thorax dimensions (§33 L566); numeric lower-trunk minimum relative to Marchfolk (§265 L4265).
- [VAL] Stature-accounting note: elongated lower trunk and moderate-to-long legs cannot both expand without compensation; head, neck, thoracic-height, lower-trunk and leg shares must be accounted (§33 L607); combined validity "stature × head share × thoracic share × lower-trunk share × leg share" (§158 L2571).

### A6 Hands / feet
- [ANAT] Hands: five-digit; one opposable thumb; adult grasp; moderately elongated fingers; defined knuckles; non-prehensile beyond ordinary grasp (§18 L306-312). No automatic crafting/climbing/weapon/spell/swim advantage (§18 L316).
- [ANAT] Plantigrade baseline (provisional) (§15 L267); "Plantigrade does not mean human-foot anatomy" (§15 L278). **Not digitigrade**: digitigrade stance not required (§170 L2796); SAU-BODY-17 "non-human without digitigrade shortcut" (L554).
- [ANAT] Feet: broad; longer forefoot/toe contribution than Marchfolk; strong heel-to-midfoot; five toes provisionally; non-prehensile; no automatic webbing (§16 L282-288).
- [ANAT] Claws: fingers end in keratinous claw-like nails — integrated, modestly projecting, variable curvature/thickness, compatible with grasp, not retractable, not talons (§104 L1739-1747). Toe claws same family, broader/more robust, load-bearing; plantigrade compatible; "do not stand on giant talons" (§105 L1753-1760).
- [ANAT] Palmar/plantar: fine flexible contact scales with localized pad-like thickening; not mammalian paw pads, not smooth human palms (§106A L1776-1787).
- [DIR] Creator hard bounds: hand size ±8 %; foot size ±8 % (§258 L4164). Frame changes hand/foot breadth (§258 L4168).
- [DIR] Claw controls: length, curvature, width/base width, thickness, tip, pigmentation (§106 L1764-1770; §151 L2476-2482). Hand values preserve grasp; foot values preserve plantigrade contact and footwear compatibility (§151 L2484-2485).
- [VAL] Diagnostic: foot claws lengthened along axis at reference curvature reach ground at ~+15 % length; above ~+15 % requires compensating curvature/tip change. **No numeric creator range canonized** (§261 L4203).
- [OPEN] Hand/foot claw & digit numeric ranges (§261 L4203; §265 L4269); hand/finger and foot/toe proportions, digit count revision (§33 L576-578).

### A9 Population-specific axial structure — TAIL (mandatory)
- [ANAT] All baseline Saurin possess a biological tail; mandatory, always present, **not a creator toggle**; visible; integrated sacral/caudal origin; muscular, articulated; tapers gradually; meaningful mass; not prehensile; not a weapon; not cosmetic; not aquatic-specialized (§10 L160-172). Tail is part of skeleton, never removed from biological validation (§1 L22; §3 L60).
- [ANAT] Racial envelope: **~55–80 % of standing height**, caudal base to tip along relaxed centerline; 80 % endpoint "not an entitlement for every body" (§10 L174; §228 L3606).
- [DIAG] Frozen reference tail 121.4 cm along relaxed centreline = 64.6 % of standing height (§256 L4143).
- [ANAT] Tail length must not be a visual scale detached from mass, base and articulation (§10 L178).
- [DER] **Length is the driver**; tail-base mass and mid/distal fullness are dependents. Root sufficiency ≈ (length)² / (base)^1.7; constant-sufficiency base ≈ length^1.18. Creator hard band: root-sufficiency index **0.75–1.20** of reference (§256.1 L4145).
- [VAL] Abrupt-taper guard: peak cross-sectional loss rate ≤ 1.25 × reference (§256.2 L4146).
- [VAL] Threadlike/cylindrical guard: mid-tail area 0.27–0.48 of root area; distal area (25 % from tip) ≥ 0.065 of root (§256.3 L4147).
- [DER] Frame/composition set reachable upper length: ~78 % H Balanced/reference composition; 80 % H Broad; ~72 % H Narrow high-fat (§256.5 L4149).
- [DER] Tail muscularity follows Physical Composition (proximal-mid emphasis, zero at tip); no independent tail-muscle slider (§256.6 L4150; §145 L2381).
- [DER] Caudal adipose graded through proximal ~half; fat concentrated at root invalid (§256.7 L4151).
- [DIR/DER] Resting carriage distinct from proportions; validated +8° lift … +10° droop, no ground contact; changes clearance/reach, not balance; carriage pose is later animation (§256.8 L4152; §26 L423-435).
- [DER] Tail-base breadth carries only a frame component following pelvic width (§256.10 L4154).
- [VAL] Outcomes: FAIL — heavy root + threadlike tail; cylindrical/attached-appendage tail; root-concentrated caudal fat. CONSTRAIN — root too small (base raised); root oversized for short tail (base lowered); length beyond frame/composition limit (capped). No tail-off control (§256 L4156).
- [ANAT] Tail variation (biological): total length, base breadth/depth, taper, segment proportions, muscular development, resting curvature (§11 L182-188); plus proximal vs distal mass, cross-section tendency (rounder / laterally broader / dorsoventrally deeper), segment/curvature character, neutral carriage, scale-field morphology, inherited pigment/pattern, optional restrained dorsal keratin ridges/scutes/hornlets (§11a L198-205). All coupled, never independent (§11a L207; §11 L190-192).
- [ANAT] Tail resting alignment: posterior projection, gravity-consistent curve; no constant dragging, scorpion curl, rigid horizontal, serpentine posing (§26 L425-431); not habitually dragged (§172 L2827).
- [ANAT] Tail-base integration: tail emerges from integrated sacral/caudal anatomy; posterior pelvis reads as continuation of vertebral axis (§9 L148); SAU-SILHOUETTE: sacral origin → posterior projection → caudal sweep, substantial base anchored to posterior pelvis, not tube attached, not mistakable for genital anatomy (L560).
- [ANAT] Tail participates biomechanically in balance, turning, acceleration, swimming, body language (§13 L248; §171 L2802-2809); no automatic gameplay bonus/penalty (§13 L250; §171 L2813).
- [ANAT] Tail phenotype encodes no sex, culture, class, etc.; no gameplay effect; "must not make a Saurin player easier to hit" (§11a L213-216). Stereotype mappings forbidden (crocodile = swimmer, thick = strong, etc.) (§12 L228-232).
- [SOFT] Tail receives no sex shift (§263 L4218); tail envelope/path/coupling unchanged by sex (L4253); §24 sex does not determine tail length/thickness (L394-395).
- [DIAG] World-space tail reach: "up to ~170 cm tail reach behind the heel at the stature/tail extremes" — longest tails require world-space validation (§265 L4272).
- [OPEN] Tail segment count, length distribution, mass distribution, mobility, world-space requirements (§33 L570-574; §10 L176); exact tail range after world validation (§249 L4015; §228 L3618); exact caudal-base landmark for measuring length (§265 L4263); tail extremes must be validated against SAU-BODY-20 proxies before final ranges (§166 L2736).
- [OPEN] Tail injury/loss: "Major tail loss remains an OPEN injury-system question and is never a normal creator substitute" (§11a L209); OPEN also §64 L1085, §113 L1874, §133 L2179, §225 L3557, §249 L4014; excluded from randomization (§142A L2324).

## C. Physical Composition
- [DIR] Broad independent variation: Current Muscularity, Muscular Development Capacity, body-fat amount, body-fat distribution (§22 L358-362). Lean/high-adiposity/lightly/heavily muscled/narrow/broad all valid; "Reptilian" ≠ lean/muscular/low-fat (§22 L364-373; §152 L2489-2496).

- [ANAT] Composition independent of frame (§258 L4170). Muscle regions: limbs, girdle, epaxial neck and dorsal trunk, thigh, posterior shank, proximal-mid tail; joints, hands, feet excluded. Fat regions: ventral-abdominal strongest, flank/hip, graded proximal caudal, minor gular, light general (§258 L4170).

- [DER] Tail muscularity/adiposity follow Composition (§145 L2381; §256.6-7).
- [ANAT] **Saurin E/B tissue system (§263):** E = coelomic body-wall fullness (ventrolateral-to-lateral fullness of lower rib cage/upper abdomen filling sub-costal waist); B = ventral/ventrolateral fullness (one continuous midline field over lower thorax/upper abdomen fading into flanks). Classification: Biological Anatomy / biological tissue tendency, not Presentation, not generic fat (§263 L4227-4228).

- [VAL] E/B deform existing scale fields smoothly (edge stretch ≤ 1.20 at 99th percentile, no flipped faces) (§263 L4253).
- [SOFT] Muscle and generic fat receive no sex shift (§263 L4218); muscularity does not define sex/profession/personality (§152 L2498).
