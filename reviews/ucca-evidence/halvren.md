## A. Stature and proportions

- [ANAT] Part 1 §19: "Height isn't locked in Part 1" — fixed midpoint would mislead (L54). **Superseded** by Part 2 §3–6 (L86–L101) and CR §10–14 (L481).
- [ANAT] Part 2 §3 table: Minimum about 152 cm (5'0"); Reference about 178 cm (5'10"); Maximum about 213 cm (7'0") (L86–L88). Same numbers at Part 2 §42 (L156) and v1.0 summary "Provisional 152–213 cm (5'0"–7'0"), reference about 178 cm (5'10")" (L410).
- [ANAT] **Revision (governing):** CR §10: 152–213 cm "is **no longer an absolute hard biological envelope**"; becomes **Halvren central population envelope**, "about 152–213 cm (5'0"–7'0"), with a provisional central reference around 178 cm (5'10")" (L481). In-place note at L90 ("revised by the consistency resolution into the Halvren central population envelope, not an absolute biological wall (ancestry-dependent tails are allowed)"). L156/L410 "provisional range" wording read as central envelope per L481.
- [SOFT] Tails: strong ancestry from shorter human-family distributions "may allow some Halvren below 152 cm"; strong Skarn or Aelari ancestry "may allow some above 213 cm" (L481).
- [VAL] Never set by "hard clipping, compressing all inherited heights into 152–213, uniform scaling or parental averaging" (L481).
- [DER] Conceptual model: "central Halvren distribution plus ancestry-informed probability shifts plus biologically constrained tails" (L481).
- [OPEN] "exact tail limits and frequencies are OPEN" (L481); "Exact tail heights are unresolved until the ancestry-dependent distribution is designed" (L490).
- [VAL] Guardrail: "tails don't make any source-race height automatically valid … strong Skarn ancestry may support unusually tall Halvren without authorizing the full Skarn height and structure distribution, and likewise for Aelari or Marchfolk extremes" (L481).
- [DIAG] Source comparator table (L92–L99): Marchfolk 147–203 cm / about 173 cm; Skarn 183–229 / about 208; Sagekin 152–208 / about 178; Fenn 157–211 / about 181; Aelari 168–221 / about 190; Vael 157–203 / about 178.

- [ANAT] Height "never an average of parental or reference heights"; complex trait from multiple ancestry contributions, individual variation, multigenerational inheritance, development, "sex-related population distributions where later appropriate", setting mechanisms (L101).
- [SOFT] Genealogy may shift probability: more Skarn/Aelari → taller part; Marchfolk → broad human-centered spread; Sagekin, Fenn, Vael → own tendencies (L101). Aelari "taller" normalized: trends taller than Marchfolk, Fenn and Vael reference populations, overlapping (CR L457).

### A2 Torso
- [ANAT] Torso inheritance relationship- and population-aware: ribcage width, depth, vertical length, waist-transition length, spine and torso proportions, torso-pelvis integration; "never reduced to 'human torso versus elf torso'" (Part 2 §15, L120).
- [SOFT] Examples "not packages": more human-like thoracic depth with somewhat elven vertical relationships; shallower thorax with stronger human shoulder breadth; Vael-influenced thoracic depth with otherwise mixed limbs; Aelari vertical continuity moderated by human ancestry; Fenn compact-centered anatomy moderated by human torso structure (L120).
- [SOFT] Source tendencies: Skarn deeper ribcage (L111) = "Greater average skeletal thoracic depth relative to equivalently sized Marchfolk" (CR L453); Fenn shallower ribcage (L112) = reduced vs equivalent Marchfolk and Vael, no strict Fenn-Aelari ranking (L456); Aelari greater torso and waist vertical contribution (L113); Vael greater thoracic depth than Fenn and Aelari ancestry, more compact torso-pelvis continuity than Aelari (L114); Sagekin somewhat narrower ribcage (L116) = reduced breadth vs Marchfolk, "not shallow thoracic depth unless separately established" (L460).

## B. Skeletal Frame
- [ANAT] Skeletal Frame (continuous skeletal configuration) vs Frame Preset (creator starting configuration); Narrow, Balanced, Broad as possible presets (Part 1 §20, L54).
- [ANAT] "Valid underlying ranges may depend partly on ancestry, so a Broad Halvren isn't assumed identical to a Broad Marchfolk or Broad Aelari" (L54).
- [VAL] "Narrow, Balanced and Broad remain **Frame Presets, not ancestry categories**": Broad not automatically human-leaning, Narrow not automatically elf-leaning; "ancestry is never encoded into the presets" (Part 2 §27, L137). Example: two Broad Halvren, one with gracile elven joints, one with human-family structural presence (L137).
- [ANAT] Mixed skeletal developmental space affects skeletal visual mass, long-bone proportions, joint scale, shoulders, ribcage, spine, pelvis, limbs, hands, feet; neither unchanged human nor elven skeleton is the definition (Part 2 §7, L105).
- [ANAT] Skeletal expression "spans a broad mixed distribution between human-family structural presence and elven-family gracility (strongly gracile, moderately gracile, intermediate or relatively robust), emerging from coordinated relationships, never a global 'Elf Gracility' slider" (L105). Decision: "gracility and robustness are multidimensional, not one slider" (L156).
- [VAL] "Halvren don't normally reproduce the most population-specific skeletal extremes of a pure source population" (L105).
- [SOFT] Human-family contributes "skeletal architecture and structural distributions (not universal 'robustness,' per the consistency resolution)" (L47); CR §2–3: whole human family isn't simply "robust"; Sagekin linear, Marchfolk broad, Skarn strongly structural; "human robustness" → "human-family skeletal architecture and structural distributions" (L463). Elven gracility a meaningful family tendency vs named human population (L463). Fenn greater gracility vs equivalent Marchfolk and provisionally vs Aelari and Vael (L455).
- [SOFT] Skarn greater skeletal robustness, heavier joints (L111); Vael more joint and base presence than Fenn/Aelari (L114).
