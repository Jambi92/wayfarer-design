## A. Stature and proportions

**A1 Standing height**
- [ANAT] Min about 198 cm (6'6"), reference about 218 cm (7'2"), max about 239 cm (7'10") — "FIRST-PASS BIOLOGICAL RANGE — SUBJECT TO CROSS-RACE VALIDATION" (§3–5 L15–19; L138).
- [ANAT] 239 cm Grask never a 198 cm Grask uniformly enlarged; height distributes through lower/upper limb, torso, pelvis, spine, neck, head (L19).
- [ANAT] "Grask overlap in adult stature with multiple existing populations. Height alone must never determine Grask racial readability." (L148). Overlap table: Marchfolk 198–203, Sagekin 198–208, Fenn 198–211, Vael 198–203, Aelari 198–221, Skarn 198–229, Halvren valid configurations only (L152–160).
- [ANAT] 239 cm no longer tallest — "Gorrund (about 251 cm) now sets the tallest approved playable anatomy" (L178; L675).
- Distribution — SILENT.

**A2 Torso**
- [ANAT] Torso contribution trends lower than Marchfolk and Skarn; never a tiny torso on giant limbs (L37; L198).
- [ANAT] "proportionally reduced torso contribution never means small torso" (L198); torso contribution relative to stature ≠ absolutely short torso (L174).
- [ANAT] Thorax: moderate skeletal breadth, meaningful depth, elongated but not oversized ribcage; breadth and depth independently variable (L38); ribcage breadth, depth, vertical length, lower-rib relationship, taper independent (L202).
- [ANAT] Thoracic breadth less broad relative to stature than Skarn, "surviving composition neutralization" (L203). Thoracic depth meaningful, "never approaching Durrim proportional depth" (L204; L39).
- [ANAT] Waist/abdomen broad variation; no monster waist, V-torso or belly required (L40; L207).

**A3 Limb length & segments**
- [ANAT] Legs: greater lower-limb contribution than Marchfolk HRP and Skarn at matched normalized height (L52; L213).
- [ANAT] Lower leg: "Grask lower-leg length is an important contributor to their rangy silhouette and may be proportionally emphasized relative to human reference anatomy" (approved tendency, L215); femur-dominant / balanced / lower-leg-dominant individuals valid (L217).
- [ANAT] Arms: "greater total arm length relative to standing height than Marchfolk and Skarn"; "genuine proportional elongation, not an optical effect" (L221; L164).
- [ANAT] Arm span: greater relative to standing height; ratios OPEN (L223; L166); emerges from shoulder/upper arm/forearm/hand, "never one Arm Span slider" (L225).
- [ANAT] Forearm: "meaningful contributor … trends toward increased proportional contribution" (L227); forearm-to-upper-arm variation controlled, never ape caricature (L229).
- [ANAT] Upper arm (clarification, supersedes L225 wording): greater absolute length and length relative to height; contribution to total arm length varies and is evaluated with forearm (L310–314).
- [VAL] Leg elongation fails if stilt-like, extremely high crotch, tiny-looking torso, fragile knees (L217).
- [ANAT] Hands near knees NOT mandatory (L57).

**A4 Shoulder/pelvic relationships**
- [ANAT] Shoulders race-specific; moderate-absolute-to-substantial breadth; distinct scapular/clavicular relationships; never widened human shoulders (L41); evaluated as absolute, relative-to-torso, relative-to-stature breadth and arm-origin relation, "never one Shoulder Width concept" (L205); non-ape scapulae/clavicles (L206).
- [ANAT] Pelvis locked functional requirement: "must coordinate long lower limbs, upright bipedal locomotion and the Grask torso without being created by simply scaling or narrowing a human pelvis"; morphology OPEN (L208; L45).
- [ANAT] Pelvic breadth supports frames; external hip width ≠ skeletal breadth (L209).

**A5 Neck**
- [ANAT] Moderate-to-long relative to Durrim and Skarn; no swan-neck, forward monster neck, hunch (L43).
- [ANAT] "Grask should not generally rely on extreme neck length for racial identity. Their strongest elongation signal remains limb contribution and reach." (L172).
- [VAL] Fails if neutral Grask resembles "an Aelari with heavier joints and longer arms" (L174).

**A6 Hands/feet**
- [ANAT] Hands large in absolute terms, proportionally long enough to integrate with forearms; never claws/monster hands (L233); multidimensional, never one Hand Size (L235).
- [SOFT] Approved tendency: somewhat greater finger length relative to palm than Marchfolk and Skarn (L235).
- [ANAT] "Grask possess five digits per hand and five digits per foot" (L237); no claws; ordinary nails (L239); no reduced dexterity (L239).
- [ANAT] Feet large in absolute terms, not dominating silhouette (L243); multidimensional (L245); plantigrade, not prehensile (L65; L245).
- [SOFT] Narrow→narrower feet / Broad→broader feet is a population tendency only; "Skeletal frame does not directly determine foot breadth." (L322–326).

**A7 Reach-related anatomy**
- [ANAT] Grask "genuinely have greater anatomical reach tendencies through arm length relative to height, arm span, forearm and hand contribution" (L646); reach depends mainly on skeletal arm architecture, not hands (L229).
- [OPEN] Anatomical / interaction / combat / camera-targeting reach separate; gameplay conversion OPEN (L61; L646).

**A8 Posture / resting alignment**
- [ANAT] Upright plantigrade bipeds; "Grask do not require a permanent hunch to look like trolls." (L90); not knuckle-walkers (L92); spine never source of identity (L44).
- [ANAT] Anatomical resting alignment separate from cultural/personal body language; baseline upright, no permanent spinal flexion, bent-knee crouch or ape arm hang (L622).
- [ANAT] World incompatibility never solved by slouching/hunching (L622; L673).

**A9 Population-specific axial structures**
- [ANAT] Tail not approved ("a tail … aren't approved", aquatic specialization NOT ESTABLISHED, L88). Otherwise SILENT.

**A10 Head-to-stature**
- [OPEN] "No head-to-height ratio is locked, but a comically small, oversized or uniformly scaled human head is avoided, pending cross-race prototype validation" (L433).
- [VAL] Head must be neutralizable in body tests — "A Grask must eventually remain identifiable as Grask even if the head is visually neutralized" (L78).

## B. Skeletal Frame
- [ANAT] "Rangy" never fragile/tiny-boned; absolute vs relative-to-stature structural size distinct (L31).
- [DIR] Narrow/Balanced/Broad universal concept inside Grask anatomy (L70); modify "skeletal breadth relationships only", never height, limb, hand/foot length, muscle or fat (L249).
- [ANAT] Narrow: reduced breadth, keeps limb architecture; not fragile; avoid Aelari convergence, pencil joints (L70; L249).
- [ANAT] Balanced: starting point, "not canonical, ideal or most correct" (L70).
- [ANAT] Broad: may increase breadth and joint/base dimensions; "isn't Skarn, muscular, fat or maximum everything"; avoid Skarn/Gorrund convergence, blocky torso, shortened limbs (L70; L249).
- [ANAT] Knees substantial in absolute structure, may look lean vs height; "visually narrow never means structurally weak" (L217); ankles credible (L56; L217).
- [SOFT] "A population-level anatomical correlation does not automatically create a hard creator-control dependency." (general principle, L330).
