# Stage 1 world building - shelf access and test stream

The separate v005 technical study now connects the valley approach to the provisional excavation shelf with approximately 1.28 km of graded foot access and 129 m of rise. The study remains within the existing 2,016 by 2,016 m footprint. Maximum profile grade is approximately 12.5%; vehicle access and final earthworks are not yet accepted.

A technical bridge placeholder crosses the preserved drainage channel. Its approaches were refined for the smallest traversal fixture. A native Unreal WaterBodyRiver now provides a visible test stream, surface/depth queries and southward flow. A dedicated plain water study material renders the native tiled Water Mesh using native height mapping; banks and coarse tile edges remain unfinished. No plugin change, paid asset or production replacement was required.

All eight continuous traversal legs pass: small and large height-envelope fixtures, walking and sprinting, uphill and downhill. Each fixture completes a round trip without an intermediate teleport. Actual collision clearance, falling and ground continuity are measured; fixture widths, speed and step settings remain provisional rather than race canon.

The saved map reopens successfully. Broad terrain samples, narrow road/channel samples, bridge collision and native stream queries pass. A separate real-time editor baseline was captured for CSV and Insights profiling before the final water-rendering change; final-water performance remains to be measured. It is an early technical baseline, not populated-world or minimum-hardware performance acceptance.

Original project files and all earlier study checkpoints remain unchanged, and a compressed development checkpoint is verified. Inherited preview HLOD actors are retained but excluded from the new study game; rebuilding HLOD remains pending. Approved levels and character-model work were not changed.

This remains a provisional study between southern White Reach and northern Confluence. Approved registration to Known World Map v0.2 and world-scale confirmation remain prerequisites to geographical expansion. Final bridge architecture, drainage outlets, riverbank art, swimming, carts and the excavation/refuge blockout remain later work under the canonical World and Main Story Bibles.
