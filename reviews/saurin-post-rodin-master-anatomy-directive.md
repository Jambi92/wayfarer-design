**deep mobile thorax → elongated lower axial trunk → integrated pelvis/sacral platform → substantial caudal base → mandatory counterbalancing tail**

## Target proportional envelope for the next blockout

These are reconstruction targets/guides, not new canon unless already specified:

- Standing height: use canonical ~188 cm reference.
- Tail length: canonical neutral range remains ~0.55–0.80H. Use a middle neutral value for the master, approximately 0.65–0.70H unless geometry demands a documented adjustment.
- Thoracic depth: Rodin's ~0.20H is a useful starting reference; validate visually against width and shoulder organization.
- Lower axial trunk: Rodin's ~0.16–0.17H may be a useful starting region, but remove the human waist-cylinder/pinch interpretation.

Where these guides conflict with credible anatomy or the canonical spec, stop and document the conflict rather than silently forcing the number.

## Pelvis / sacrum / tail — primary design problem

This area determines success.

Create a posteriorly substantial pelvic complex with:
- sacral/caudal platform;
- broad muscular/structural transition into the proximal tail;
- femoral sockets organized around that mass;
- no human buttock cleft;
- no two hemispherical gluteal masses;
- no abrupt cylindrical tail insertion.

The proximal tail should emerge as continuation of the axial body. Its base should occupy meaningful posterior volume and then transition progressively into the free tail.

The visual test remains:

**Does the tail look biologically inevitable on this body rather than attached to an otherwise unrelated humanoid?**

If no, stop. Do not polish.

## Thorax and abdomen

The torso must not be a human fitness model.

Build a deep thoracic shell with:
- coherent rib/costal volume;
- non-human ventral organization;
- shoulder/scapular attachment appropriate to the shell;
- structural lateral planes;
- transition into an elongated lower axial trunk.

Do not sculpt pectoral slabs. Do not sculpt a rectus six-pack.

The lower trunk can show muscular/tendinous organization, but use continuous longitudinal/oblique load paths appropriate to an upright tailed organism.

## Neck

Use the accepted head as the identity anchor.

Create a cervical system that distributes load into:
- dorsal axial structures;
- scapular/shoulder structures;
- ventrolateral throat/cervical structures;
- thoracic inlet.

Do not recreate human SCM + trapezius with reptilian surface treatment.

## Legs and feet

Legs must stop reading as columns.

Provide clear:
- femoral direction;
- knee architecture;
- crural planes;
- ankle narrowing/landmarks;
- rearfoot;
- midfoot;
- metatarsal spread;
- long articulated toes;
- claw continuation.

Remain plantigrade unless the canonical spec is deliberately revised later.

Contact surfaces are fine flexible scales with localized thickening, **not mammalian paw pads**.

## Arms and hands

Rodin's distal upper-limb studies are among the strongest references. Preserve their useful articulation while removing bodybuilder exaggeration.

Five digits and opposable thumb remain provisional accepted direction. Hands should be clearly Saurin through taper, joints, distal proportions and claws without becoming generic monster hands.

## Surface planes

Tyler's repeated criticism of excessive roundness is binding design feedback.

Use stronger planar changes and directional transitions where supported by underlying anatomy. Avoid:
- balloon muscles;
- smooth human cylinders;
- arbitrary spikes;
- armor-like plates masquerading as anatomy.

The harder read must come from structure.
