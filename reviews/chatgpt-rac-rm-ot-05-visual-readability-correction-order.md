# 1. AUTHOR REVIEW OF THE RM-OT-05 GATE

The stature register, no-uniform-scale evidence, common-scale lineups and world-envelope bookkeeping are accepted **in principle**.

However, RM-OT-05 is **NOT FINAL CLOSED YET**.

The visual evidence raises a material canon-readability issue:

> In the neutral-grey common-scale lineups, Grask and Gorrund remain too close to generic human silhouettes to confidently satisfy their existing canon requirement that their body architecture remain recognizably non-human when face, hair, clothing, culture and composition are neutralized.

This is not permission to caricature them. It is a request to verify whether the current accepted reference bodies actually satisfy the already-authored identity tests.

The user's naked-eye read is valid evidence here because these are explicit visual-readability requirements, not merely hidden measurements.

Do not dismiss the issue as "the faces / textures will make them different later."

They **will** receive their already-authored race-specific craniofacial, auricular, pigmentation and surface identities later, but Grask and Gorrund body canon already requires meaningful neutralized distinction.

# 2. CANON TO PRESERVE

## Grask

Preserve the existing requirements in GRASK_V1, including:

- distinct tall **non-human** humanoid;
- elongated / rangy architecture;
- lower torso contribution relative to limbs;
- strong lower-leg and forearm contribution;
- high limb / reach presence;
- non-human shoulder / scapular organization;
- non-human pelvis organization;
- large integrated hands / feet;
- must remain identifiable with face, hair, clothing and culture neutralized;
- must not become ape-like, hunched, stick-thin, giant-human or monster caricature.

## Gorrund

Preserve the existing requirements in GORRUND_V1, including:

- distinct massive **non-human** humanoid;
- broad / deep load-bearing architecture;
- high absolute skeletal structural scale;
- substantial joints;
- strong axial-to-limb integration;
- Axial Load-Path Continuity;
- body identity recognizable even when the head is neutralized;
- must not become enlarged human, bigger Skarn, thick Grask, ogre caricature, permanent bodybuilder or obesity shorthand.

## Skarn

Skarn remain large-scale **human** architecture. Do not make Skarn more non-human merely to create separation.

# 3. WHY THIS RECHECK IS REQUIRED

The RM-OT-05 gate states that the matched-height large-race sheet demonstrates:

- Skarn / Grask / Gorrund separation at 208 and 229 cm;
- Grask verticality / long legs;
- Gorrund transverse mass / depth;
- height is not their identity.

Those relationships may be numerically present, but the current common-scale presentation does not yet provide strong enough naked-eye evidence for the canon's explicit neutralized-readability standard.

A numeric pass is not sufficient when the canon itself contains a visual identity gate.

Therefore this order does **not** reopen the accepted proportion logic by default. It requires a stricter visual proof first, and geometry correction only if that proof fails.

# 5. REQUIRED HUMAN-BASELINE QUESTIONS

For each comparison answer explicitly:

### Grask vs Skarn / Marchfolk

With the head removed or neutralized:

- Does the Grask read as a genuinely different skeletal organization rather than a tall thin human?
- Are lower-leg contribution, femur / lower-leg relationship, arm contribution, forearm contribution, torso contribution, shoulder origin and pelvis organization visible together?
- Does the whole silhouette read as one coherent rangy anatomy rather than a few stretched segments?
- At 208 and 229 cm, can an uninformed reviewer distinguish Grask from Skarn without relying on height?

### Gorrund vs Skarn / Marchfolk

With the head removed or neutralized:

- Does the Gorrund read as non-human load-bearing architecture rather than a broad tall human?
- Are thoracic depth, thoracic breadth, lower-trunk continuity, pelvic integration, proximal-limb scale and joint presence visibly coordinated?
- Does ALPC create a visually distinct axial chain without becoming a block, barrel or ogre?
- At 208 and 229 cm, can an uninformed reviewer distinguish Gorrund from Skarn without relying on muscle or height?

### Grask vs Gorrund

- Is the contrast more than thin vs broad?
- Does Grask read reach / limb dominant and Gorrund read axial / load-bearing?
- Do both remain plausible upright tool-using humanoids rather than fantasy caricatures?
