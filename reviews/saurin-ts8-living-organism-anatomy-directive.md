# Saurin TS8 — Living-Organism Anatomy Reconstruction Directive

**Author:** ChatGPT  
**For:** Claude (diagnostic sculpt / audit workflow)  
**Status:** DIRECTIVE — diagnostic anatomy development, not final canon  
**Dependency:** TS7 clean-sheet body reconstruction  
**Do not begin UE5 implementation.**

## 1. Purpose

TS7 is the first Saurin body pass that successfully abandons the human body as its anatomical foundation. Preserve that achievement.

However, TS7 is still a structural blockout. It has replaced the rounded human mannequin with a simplified geometric construction, but it has not yet become a convincing living reptilian humanoid organism.

**TS8 must not return to human anatomy. TS8 must turn the TS7 structural system into believable living Saurin anatomy.**

Working principle:

> **TS7 establishes the non-human skeleton. TS8 establishes the living organism.**

Do not solve the remaining problems with scales, pigmentation, horns, display structures, clothing, pose, dramatic lighting, or surface noise. The body must work in bare neutral clay first.

---

## 2. Preserve the successful TS7 foundation

Do NOT discard these TS7 decisions:

- clean-sheet Saurin body rather than modified MPFB/human geometry;
- accepted TS6.1-derived Saurin cranial direction;
- Counterbalanced Pelvic-Axial Architecture;
- integrated head → cervical system → thoracic shell → elongated lower axial trunk → pelvis/sacrum → caudal base → tail chain;
- mandatory substantial tail;
- upright obligate biped;
- provisionally plantigrade stance;
- non-human pelvic/sacral construction;
- no human buttock/gluteal-cleft construction;
- no human breast/pectoral sexual-dimorphism template;
- no human waist/hip silhouette template;
- pointed claw-bearing hands and feet;
- body designed before surface decoration.

The old human body may remain only as a measurement/comparison reference. It must not become the source geometry or anatomical template again.

---

## 3. Primary TS8 problem

The current TS7 torso reads too much like a long rectangular/cylindrical column. The shoulder joints are visually appended. The neck remains columnar. Limbs are still tube-like. The pelvic-tail system has the correct conceptual direction but lacks enough secondary anatomy to explain its mass.

TS8 therefore needs **secondary anatomical architecture**.

Do not merely smooth TS7. Do not merely add muscle bulges. Construct believable anatomical systems whose shapes explain load, articulation and motion.

---

# 4. Thoracic reconstruction

The thorax must become a recognizable biological shell rather than a rectangular slab.

Develop:

- clear rib-field volume;
- meaningful front-to-back thoracic depth;
- lateral costal planes;
- restrained sternal/anterior thoracic structure;
- posterior thoracic integration into the axial column;
- superior thoracic transition into the shoulder girdle;
- inferior costal transition into the elongated lower axial trunk.

The thorax may be narrower in some dimensions than a human torso, but it must possess real volume and load-bearing logic.

Avoid:
- human barrel chest;
- human pectoral template;
- flat rectangular front;
- perfectly cylindrical ribcage;
- human V-taper;
- bodybuilder chest;
- exaggerated armor-like plates.

The transition into the lower trunk should occur through **changing anatomical planes and structural depth**, not a human waist pinch.

---

# 5. Shoulder-girdle reconstruction — HIGH PRIORITY

The current TS7 shoulders resemble spherical joints attached to the sides of the torso. Replace this completely.

Build a Saurin shoulder system in which:

**cervical base → upper thorax → scapular/shoulder-girdle field → proximal humerus**

reads as one integrated mechanism.

Requirements:

- shoulder root must visibly originate from thoracic architecture;
- establish posterior scapular or equivalent structural planes;
- create believable anterior and lateral shoulder transitions;
- deltoid-equivalent mass must follow joint mechanics rather than appear as a human deltoid pasted on;
- arm sockets must not appear as balls attached to a box;
- preserve useful humanoid arm range for gameplay without requiring human skeletal appearance.

From rear 3/4, the upper back should make the shoulder mechanism especially legible.

---

# 6. Cervical reconstruction — HIGH PRIORITY

The neck still reads too much as a tower or tube.

Construct distinct:

- dorsal cervical/epaxial mass;
- lateral cervical planes;
- ventral throat architecture;
- skull-base/occipital transition;
- cervicothoracic expansion;
- shoulder/neck integration.

The neck must support the projecting reptilian head convincingly. Its rear and lateral masses should communicate how the head is stabilized without making the creature permanently hunched.

Do not create:
- a human sternocleidomastoid neck;
- a featureless cylinder;
- an extreme snake neck;
- a giant trapezius/bodybuilder neck.

**Diagnostic requirement:** in profile, the head should look structurally supported by the neck and thorax rather than perched on a stalk.

---

# 7. Elongated lower axial trunk

Preserve the approved elongated lower axial contribution, but stop treating it as a featureless rectangular connector.

Develop:

- dorsal axial musculature;
- lateral abdominal/flank-equivalent planes;
- ventral abdominal support;
- transitions from costal field into lower trunk;
- progressive integration into the pelvic/sacral platform.

There must be **no human waist pinch**.

Instead, silhouette change should result from real structural redistribution between thorax, axial trunk and pelvis.

Use restrained angular/planar transitions where biologically credible. We specifically want to move away from the overly rounded, soft mammalian body language of the earlier attempts.

---

# 8. Pelvis, sacrum and femoral integration — CRITICAL

TS7 finally moved away from a human pelvis. Now make the system mechanically convincing.

Develop an integrated:

**lower axial trunk → pelvic platform → sacral complex → acetabular/femoral region → caudal base**

The upper legs must appear to emerge from this system, not hang underneath it.

Show:

- femoral socket region;
- lateral pelvic structural planes;
- posterior pelvic/sacral mass;
- medial/ventral pelvic closure appropriate to the Saurin design;
- proximal hindlimb muscle/tendon transitions;
- caudofemoral relationship where appropriate.

Absolutely avoid reintroducing:

- human buttocks;
- gluteal cleft;
- human iliac/hip silhouette;
- human groin sculpt;
- human sexual anatomy as a placeholder.

The neutral diagnostic body should remain anatomically non-sexualized while sex-related Saurin anatomy remains unresolved.

---

# 9. Tail-root reconstruction — CRITICAL

The tail root is substantially improved over pre-TS7 work, but its proximal mass currently behaves too much like one smooth wedge.

The tail must look **biologically inevitable**.

Construct visible structural logic through:

**sacrum → caudal base → proximal tail mass → tapering articulated tail**

and integrate it with the pelvic and proximal femoral systems.

The tail must not look:
- glued onto the rump;
- like a separate cone;
- like a genital projection from front views;
- like a mammalian tail emerging above human buttocks.

Maintain a broad, powerful root, but articulate its transitions so the mass is explained by anatomy.

Preserve the rule that tail length × base size × mass × pelvic support are coupled.

No tail variant grants automatic attacks, balance bonuses, swimming bonuses, climbing ability, or other gameplay effects.

---

# 10. Limb reconstruction

Arms and legs remain too tubular.

Build restrained long-bone and soft-tissue architecture.

For each limb, make visible through silhouette/planes:

- proximal segment;
- joint transition;
- distal segment;
- tendon paths approaching wrist/ankle;
- joint landmarks;
- direction changes caused by real articulation.

Avoid simply copying human muscle anatomy.

The goal is not extreme musculature. A lightly muscled Saurin must still possess readable non-human structural anatomy.

## Arms

Preserve functional humanoid reach and grasping.

Develop shoulder → upper arm → elbow → forearm → wrist as a coherent chain.

Do not turn the forearm into a generic human forearm with scales.

## Legs

Develop pelvic socket → proximal thigh → knee → lower leg → ankle as a coherent load path.

Preserve upright bipedal locomotion. Do not create a permanent crouch or digitigrade stance unless the approved design is explicitly reopened later.

---

# 11. Hands

The current claw direction is promising.

Preserve:

- five digits provisionally;
- opposable thumb provisionally;
- elongated non-human fingers;
- tapered distal phalanges;
- modest pointed claws continuing the digit axis.

Refine:

- knuckle/joint architecture;
- palm structural planes;
- tendon transitions;
- claw-bed integration.

The claws should be unmistakably non-human but **not giant combat talons**.

They must remain capable of ordinary grasping, weapon use, crafting and interaction.

No automatic unarmed-damage or climbing advantage.

---

# 12. Feet

Feet need a larger redesign than the hands.

Preserve provisional plantigrade locomotion, but stop treating the foot as an elongated human foot with claws.

Construct:

- non-human ankle transition;
- stable heel/load-bearing rear foot;
- structurally meaningful midfoot;
- longer forefoot/toe contribution;
- clear toe articulation;
- tapered distal phalanges;
- modest claws following toe axes;
- contact surface compatible with the previously approved palmar/plantar field.

The foot must support believable upright gait and equipment constraints.

Do not make:
- human feet with long toenails;
- raptor feet;
- digitigrade feet;
- giant talons;
- mammalian paw pads.

---

# 13. Planar / angular anatomical language

The user identified an important visual problem in earlier iterations: the body was excessively rounded while the successful head development gained identity through sharper plane changes and structural ridges.

Carry that lesson into the body.

This does **not** mean making the Saurin body a collection of hard polygons.

It means using anatomically motivated:

- ridges;
- creases;
- tendon lines;
- bony transitions;
- costal margins;
- joint planes;
- axial planes;
- pelvic planes;
- shoulder planes;
- caudal transitions.

These must arise from anatomy rather than decorative sculpting.

The bare-clay body should already have a restrained reptilian structural rhythm before scales are added.

# 19. Do not overcorrect

Avoid turning Saurin into:

- an upright crocodile;
- monitor lizard pasted onto human legs;
- dinosaur person;
- dragonborn analogue;
- snake person;
- armored monster;
- bodybuilder reptile.

Real reptile anatomy may be studied for structural principles—skeletal transitions, muscle attachment logic, tail integration, planar cranial/body construction—but the Saurin remain their own fantasy humanoid species.

Use biological references as **mechanical inspiration, not direct species copying**.

# 21. Acceptance gates

TS8 should not advance to surface treatment until all of these are reasonably true:

1. Neck no longer reads as a cylinder/tower.
2. Shoulders no longer read as balls attached to a box.
3. Thorax reads as a biological shell.
4. Lower axial trunk has anatomy without a human waist.
5. Pelvis, sacrum, femora and tail form one coherent mechanical system.
6. Tail root no longer reads as an attached wedge.
7. Arms and legs have credible segment/joint/tendon architecture.
8. Hands are non-human yet functional.
9. Feet are non-human, plantigrade and mechanically credible.
10. Human buttocks/gluteal cleft remain absent.
11. Human sexual dimorphism has not been reintroduced.
12. Torso-only diagnostic remains recognizably Saurin in construction.
13. Silhouette differs for anatomical reasons, not decoration.
14. Bare clay is convincing without scales.
15. The entire organism reads as one evolved/developed body rather than assembled reptile parts.

Do not chase polish.
