**Measurement normalization ruled out.** The W2 grid and the W1t S7-normalized grid give identical Marchfolk values, and all three t agree.

**Not a wrong direction.** The reading is clearly positive.

**Not a reference-state issue.** Normalized readings show Durrim far deeper: DU137 +8.6 % and DU122 +12.6 % against Marchfolk 173. That gap is allometry: Marchfolk itself deepens by +4.3 % when built down to 152 cm. **Composition does not rescue it.** The skeletal reading is composition-free. Skin AP depth swings −1.7 % to +4.2 % with composition, and the skin reading fails at reference composition.

**The smallest Durrim-specific contradiction is pelvic AP depth relative to the deep thorax.**
- Durrim's thorax is 13.5 % deeper than Marchfolk's (skeletal), but its pelvis only 0.8 % deeper.
- So pelvic AP / thoracic depth is 11 % below Marchfolk: the lower trunk steps down from a deep thorax to an ordinary-depth pelvis.
- The construction writes thorax depth (torso-depth target) but no Durrim pelvic depth.

**Diagnostic probes (W1t constructions; re-measured on the W2 grid; NOT applied).** Pelvis bone Z at ×1.04 / ×1.06 on the DU-NAT construction gives:

→ **D1.**
