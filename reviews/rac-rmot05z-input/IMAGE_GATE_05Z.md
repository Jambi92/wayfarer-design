# Pre-generation image gate (RM-OT-05Z §5) — form

Fill in one copy of this form for each candidate image. Record the image's filename, SHA-256 and dimensions.

**Any NO means REGENERATE IMAGE.** **UNCLEAR** also means REGENERATE IMAGE when the item is safety-critical for 3D generation: G1, G2, G5, G6, G7.

## Also check (05W negative checklist)

These rows come from `reviews/rac-rmot05w-source-brief/NEGATIVE_CHECKLIST.md`. Any "yes" rejects the image.

- **A1–A5:** presentation and contamination.
- **B1–B6:** stereotypes (bodybuilder, brute, heavy human, fat giant, Grask-like, monster).
- **C1–C8:** shape failures (box or barrel trunk, shallow torso, overhanging girdle, waist collapse, knob joints, oversized feet, tiny ankles or wrists, ogre legs).
- **D1–D5:** surface failures.
- **E1–E3:** the wrong identity channel.

## Ruling

**PASS / REGENERATE IMAGE**, with reasons.

A **PASS** authorizes only preparing the dry-run command and job metadata (05Z §6). Any `-Submit`, or any other paid generation, needs Tyler's **explicit approval immediately beforehand**. Nothing is resubmitted automatically.
