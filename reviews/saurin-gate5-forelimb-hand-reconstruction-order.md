## Gate 4 disposition

**Accept Gate 4 hindlimb/foot architecture as the structural foundation. Do not perform a Gate 4A isolated refinement pass.**

Lock against regression:
- accepted pelvis/sacrum/tail-root architecture;
- accepted hindlimb load path;
- upright obligate biped stance;
- plantigrade heel-bearing foot;
- structured rearfoot and midfoot;
- metatarsal/forefoot spread;
- five articulated digits as the provisional baseline;
- longer Saurin forefoot/toe contribution;
- current tail relationship and whole-body proportions.

Gate 4 surface anatomy is NOT final. Defer the following to whole-body integration:
- calf/lower-leg forms are still somewhat cord-like/over-clean;
- knee transitions need richer organic integration;
- digit spacing/segmentation is too orderly in places;
- heel volume is bulbous in some views;
- secondary asymmetry and soft/hard tissue transitions need refinement.

These are refinement issues, not grounds to reconstruct the Gate 4 system.

# Gate 5 — Forelimb / Wrist / Hand Functional Reconstruction

The forelimbs and hands are now the strongest remaining major anatomical system carrying the earlier humanoid/Rodin language.

Reconstruct them as one continuous functional system:

**shoulder/scapular interface → upper arm → elbow → forearm → wrist → metacarpal/palmar structure → five articulated digits → terminal claws**

Do not simply resurface a human arm and hand.

## Preserve / lock

Do not redesign:
- Gate 1 pelvis/sacrum/tail root;
- Gate 2A thorax;
- Gate 3A cervical architecture;
- Gate 4 hindlimbs and feet;
- canonical TS6.1 cranial identity;
- standing height and broad silhouette;
- current shoulder placement unless a small local transition adjustment is mechanically necessary.

The shoulder must remain compatible with the approved deep narrow-to-moderate thoracic shell and the Saurin scapular/clavicular concept.

## Shoulder and upper arm

The upper limb must appear to originate from the Saurin thoracic/shoulder system rather than from a human bodybuilding shoulder cap.

Translate functional requirements into non-human but believable anatomy:
- arm elevation/depression;
- forward/backward swing;
- rotation;
- stabilization against the thorax;
- force transfer into the forearm and hand.

Avoid isolated spherical deltoid forms and obvious human biceps/triceps copies.

Use directional masses, attachment planes, overlapping structures, and believable transitions into the chest/back.

Do not alter Gate 2A torso anatomy merely to make a human arm fit it.

## Elbow

Reconstruct the elbow around mechanical function rather than a human elbow silhouette with grooves added.

Show convincing:
- upper-arm termination;
- hinge/articulation region;
- posterior extension structure;
- medial/lateral stabilization;
- transition into the two major forearm structural paths.

It must remain capable of a believable humanoid range of manipulation without visually becoming a human elbow.

Do not add arbitrary spikes or monster ornamentation to make it “alien.”

## Forearm

The current forearms need a stronger Saurin-specific directional organization.

Build a readable relationship between:
- elbow;
- primary flexor/extensor masses;
- rotational structures;
- distal tendon/structural paths;
- wrist.

Prefer long, tensioned, interdigitating forms over rounded human muscle bellies.

The forearm should narrow convincingly toward the wrist without becoming a featureless tube.

## Wrist

The wrist must be an actual structural transition.

Do not let the forearm simply taper into a human hand.

Establish:
- distal forearm termination;
- carpal/wrist block;
- controlled narrowing;
- visible transition into metacarpal structure;
- enough mobility to support tool use, weapons, climbing, swimming motion, gestures, and ordinary manipulation without implying gameplay bonuses.

## Hand — major reconstruction

Treat the hand as reconstruction, not cosmetic refinement.

Canonical/provisional baseline:
- five digits;
- opposable thumb;
- functional grasping hand;
- terminal claws;
- no mammalian paw architecture.

Required chain:

**wrist → carpal base → metacarpal/palm framework → knuckle/articulation pattern → proximal/middle/distal digit segments → terminal claw integration**

The palm should have a Saurin structural identity rather than a human palm with elongated fingers.

Digits should:
- taper;
- show believable joint hierarchy;
- have differentiated lengths;
- avoid perfectly even spacing;
- preserve functional opposition of the thumb;
- integrate claws with terminal phalangeal structure.

Claws must not look glued onto fingertips.

Do not make:
- raptor talons;
- generic dragon claws;
- paw-like hands;
- oversized monster hands;
- human hands with pointed fingernails.

## Relationship to the feet

Hands and feet should look like they belong to the same species without being copies of one another.

Shared identity may include:
- distal articulation logic;
- terminal claw growth;
- joint rhythm;
- integument transition logic later.

But preserve functional specialization:
- feet = stance/load transfer/propulsion;
- hands = manipulation/grasping/tool use.

Do not mirror the foot anatomy directly onto the hand.

## Surface-detail firewall

Gate 5 is still anatomical construction.

Do NOT begin:
- regional scale fields;
- skin microtexture;
- pigmentation;
- material response;
- ornamental horn/display refinement;
- scars;
- decorative claw coloration;
- final eye treatment.

Basic claw geometry may be present only as required to prove terminal skeletal/digital integration.

## Evidence package

Return:
1. full-body front, profile, rear, front 3/4, rear 3/4;
2. close shoulder/upper-arm views;
3. close elbow views;
4. close forearm/wrist views;
5. isolated hand: dorsal, palmar, medial/lateral, front 3/4;

8. geometry-accounting overlay showing preserved vs rebuilt geometry.

## Acceptance target

Gate 5 succeeds when the arms/hands no longer read as inherited human/Rodin anatomy, yet remain clearly capable of sophisticated manipulation.

At full-body distance the forelimbs must support the same biological language now emerging in the pelvis, torso, neck, hindlimbs and feet.

Do not chase final polish inside Gate 5.

## What follows Gate 5

If Gate 5 architecture passes, **do not immediately begin scales or cosmetic finish.**

The next stage will be a dedicated **whole-body anatomical integration gate**. That pass will evaluate all accepted systems together and correct:
- remaining procedural/cord-like forms;
- abrupt system boundaries;
- excessive symmetry;
- inconsistent anatomical richness;
- local proportional tensions;
- knee/elbow transitions;
- heel/ankle and wrist transitions;
- neck/thorax integration;
- pelvis/thigh/tail integration;
- global silhouette and balance.

Only after the underlying creature passes that whole-body anatomical integration review do we move into Saurin surface identity: regional scale architecture, facial refinement, cranial display structures, claws as finished keratin structures, eyes, pigmentation, and material response.

## Anti-regression

Rodin B1/B2 remain references for anatomical richness, depth and useful spatial ideas—not biological authority.

Do not restore human-specific anatomy or replace approved Saurin anatomy with generic reptile/dinosaur/dragon conventions.

The goal remains a coherent organism whose anatomy makes the tail, thorax, neck, limbs, hands and feet look as though they evolved together.

**No UE5 implementation.**
