# Gorrund source — negative checklist (RM-OT-05W §3)

Use this twice:
1. on the 2D concept image, before any Rodin credit is spent;
2. on the resulting 3D source, at the whole-body gate.

**Any "yes" rejects or revises the source at source.** Do not start a repair campaign to save it (order §5).

Each item cites the canon it protects (GO = `specs/gorrund/GORRUND_V1.md` line, RA = `decisions/REFERENCE_ANATOMY_V1.md`). Where a prior Gorrund candidate failed this way, that candidate is named as evidence.

## F. Process (things that are not allowed at all)

- Using N1, N1R6, U8 or any rejected 05A–05P mesh as a shape target, donor, blend base, retopology base or concept reference other than "avoid this" (order §1, §4).
- Uniform scaling of another population or of a candidate to reach 229 cm (GO L31; RA R-2).
- Pasting, warping or SDF-building anatomy onto the source to make it pass. Corrections after the gate stay **local and justified** (order §2, §5).
