# Gorrund neutral source sculpt — brief (RM-OT-05W §3)

**What this is:** the brief for one new, purpose-built Gorrund **source body**. It works for an AI sculpt generator (image → Hyper3D Rodin) or for a human sculptor.

**What it is not:** a canon change or a measured target. Canon stays in `specs/gorrund/GORRUND_V1.md` (cited `GO L<line>`) and `decisions/REFERENCE_ANATOMY_V1.md` (cited `RA R-<n>` / `RA L<line>`). A source that matches this brief is only a candidate for the whole-body source gate (order §5). It is not an ARM.

**What to read with it:**
- `NEGATIVE_CHECKLIST.md`: what the source must not show;
- `NEGATIVE_REFERENCES_avoid_only.jpg`: labelled images of failed bodies, for avoidance only;
- `SUBMISSION_PACKAGE.md`: the ready-to-run route;
- `REVIEW_SPEC.md`: how the result is judged.

**The one-sentence target**

> One coherent mid-adult **leonine beastfolk** Gorrund, unclothed and unobstructed, standing upright and relaxed, whose body reads as **massive load-bearing skeletal architecture** — broad and deep thorax, thorax-carried shoulder girdle, deep integrated pelvis, substantial joints, large plantigrade five-toed feet — at **ordinary, central muscle and fat**, so the body would still read as Gorrund with the head and tail hidden.

Canon sources for this sentence: GO L9, L19–23, L87–89, L158; RA R-1, R-4, R-6, R-7.

## 1. Locked anatomy (canon; the source must show it)

| Region | Requirement | Canon |
|---|---|---|
| Species | Beastfolk: **leonine (lion-like) head** with muzzle, plus a **tail**. Both are species-identity channels; **they must not carry the body read**. | GO L9, L72, L89, L324; 05N order L60–61, L105, L138 |
| Identity principle | Massiveness is **skeletal scale** — breadth, depth, joint dimensions, axial scale, pelvis, long-bone dimensions — **independent of muscle and fat**. Not an enlarged human, bigger Skarn, thick Grask, bodybuilder, fat giant or hunched ogre. | GO L9–11, L19–23, L83, L158 |
| Thorax | Broad **and** substantially deep: a genuinely three-dimensional ribcage, never wide-and-shallow. Coherent and anatomically shaped: **never a barrel caricature, cylinder, box or uniformly enlarged human ribs**. Ribcage long enough to integrate the torso. | GO L39, L41–44, L180–185 |
| Rib depth vs belly | Deep ribs **without** a protruding abdomen; skeletal depth is separate from abdominal soft tissue. No "ogre belly". | GO L182, L187 |
| Shoulder girdle | Breadth **carried by the thorax**, not by overhang. Scapulae wrap a deep ribcage. Substantial clavicles and joints. **Skeletal shoulder breadth is separate from deltoid/trapezius bulk.** | GO L45, L196–199 |
| Neck | Moderate length, broad skeletal neck base integrated into girdle and upper thorax. **Not trapezius mass**; no missing neck or bull neck. | GO L46, L193, L199 |
| Spine / posture | Upright neutral bipedal alignment. **No hunch, forward head, permanent lean, exaggerated lumbar arch or bent knees.** | GO L48, L192, L103 |
| Pelvis | Depth-led and integrated with the axial body. Large hip joints sit under the load path, with no lateral outrigging. Broad, robust sacrum. Iliac breadth bounded by the lower trunk. **Not a uniformly enlarged human pelvis.** | GO L49–50, L190, L205–214 |
| Axial Load-Path Continuity | Girdle → thorax → lower trunk → pelvis → hip and proximal femur read as **one continuous load-bearing chain**. Waist definition is allowed; no abrupt collapse. Never a rectangle, barrel or "no waist" body. It must read **at low muscle**. | GO L737–749 |
| Legs | Large absolute femur and lower-leg dimensions. **Lower-limb share below Grask's, but never short-legged**: no short ogre thighs, no tiny legs. | GO L55–62, L218 |
| Knees / ankles | Substantial and load-bearing. **Never giant knobs, bulbous, spiked or armour-like.** Ankles substantial but may look narrower than calf and foot. | GO L63, L75, L218 |
| Arms | Large absolute size, **not long relative to stature**. "Long-armed" is not a Gorrund trait. Substantial elbows, structured forearms, wrists integrated with large hands. | GO L64–68, L224, L299–303 |
| Hands | Large, structurally substantial humanoid hands: four fingers plus an opposable thumb, fully dexterous, ordinary nails. **No paws, claws, mandatory fists or shovel hands.** | GO L69, L228–232 |
| Feet | **Large plantigrade humanoid feet with five non-prehensile toes and ordinary toenails.** Support comes from coordinated length, breadth and depth; **foot length is never exaggerated to signal size**. No paws, claws, ape feet, rectangular slab forefoot or comic heel. | GO L70–73, L230, L236–238, L268 |
| Composition | Reference composition is **population-centre muscle and fat with no regional offsets**. Gorrund do not require high muscularity. Muscle is never the reason they look massive. | RA R-4; GO L21–23, L83, L250 |
| Hair | No mane or scalp hair over measured landmarks: removed, or a zero-thickness cap. No body hair. Canon does not require a thick mane. | RA R-8; GO L389; 05N order L321 |
| Surface | One neutral matte material. Anatomy lives in geometry, not in texture, baked lighting or normal maps. | RA R-9, L125 (R-SKIN-1) |
| Stature | **Nominal reference 229 cm**, reached by proportion and never by uniform scaling. GOREF 230.9 cm is lineage bookkeeping, not a realization to copy. | GO L29, L31, L801; RA R-2 |
| Presentation | No clothing, jewellery, wraps, belt, loincloth, bracers, braids, equipment, scars, grime or posture-coded personality. A modesty proxy is allowed only if it covers no landmark. | RA R-7, R-12; order §3 |
| Sex configuration | One configuration for Wave 1. **No sex-specific biology is invented**; human patterns are never transferred. | RA §4 (L53); GO L83 |

## 2. OPEN morphology (the generator or sculptor may choose; we record it, it does not become canon)

The following are OPEN in canon, and the generator or sculptor may make reasonable choices:

- exact ribcage, shoulder and pelvic morphology (GO L44, L49, L159–160, L203, L214);
- torso-to-limb ratios, the femur-to-shank split and the upper-arm-to-forearm split (GO L218, L224);
- tail length, taper and tuft (GO L72);
- muzzle numerics (GO L324);
- ear morphology within the Gorrund ear family, i.e. broad, structured, rounded-to-angular upper contour, no elongated point (GO L372–376);
- skin tone (not required, GO L89);
- sex-related anatomy (OPEN, GO L83).

Any such choice is a **builder-chosen value** (RA R-14, §7). If the source is later accepted, accepting it is an authorship decision about those values, not a discovery.

## 3. Required presentation (builder choices declared; order §3)

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Figure | **One** full-body figure per image: no turnaround sheet, head studies, detail panels, text or extra figures. The 05M lesson: a sheet becomes a relief, or a multi-figure library with garments fused in. |
| Pose (R-6) | Upright anatomical standing; head level. Hips and knees extended but unlocked. Natural spine. **Both heels and soles flat down.** Feet parallel, about hip width, toes forward. Arms relaxed, hanging with small abduction (about 10–15°, a builder choice) so no limb touches the trunk; palms toward the thighs; fingers relaxed and separated. No crouch, hunch or aggressive pose. |
| Tail | At a relaxed reference carriage, **clear of the legs and not touching the ground**, so it can be cleanly separated for body-only review. |
| Clothing / hair | Fully unclothed for anatomical assessment. If a proxy is unavoidable: plain, close-fitting, minimal, covering no landmark (iliac crest / ASIS, gluteal fold, sacrum, inguinal line stay visible). No mane, braids or beard. Fur, if present, is short and **does not change the silhouette**. |
| Surface | Neutral unpainted-clay grey, matte. No tattoos, markings, strong pigment pattern or painted shadow. |
| Composition | Ordinary, healthy, central muscle and fat. No six-pack, vein or striation definition, bulging muscle masses, V-taper or fat belly. Also not artificially skinny limbs or featureless clay cylinders. |
| Camera / light | Plain mid-grey background, soft even studio light, no floor shadow. The whole figure in frame with margin; feet fully visible. |

**Views.** Needed for review and, if allowed, for multi-view generation:
- front;
- left profile;
- back;
- front three-quarter (about 30°), the best single-image depth cue for Rodin.

Review renders of any 3D result use orthographic front, side, back and 3/4 with a common ground line and grid (RA L74).

## 4. Matched-height contrasts the source must make obvious

The comparisons are at about 208–229 cm and similar composition, with head and tail hidden.

| Against | The other body | Gorrund must show instead | Canon |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Marchfolk** (human baseline, scaled to the same height for comparison only) | Ordinary human ribcage depth, pelvis and joint scale | Clearly deeper thorax relative to breadth and stature; thorax-carried girdle; depth-led pelvis; larger joints and long-bone scale. **No "big human".** | GO L166, L211, L751–757 |
| **Skarn** | Large powerful *human* architecture; muscle capacity is a Skarn trait | Non-human load-bearing system: greater axial depth and ALPC ratios. **Fails if muscle or absolute size carries the difference** ("extra-broad Skarn"). | GO L15, L110, L261, L293, L762 |
| **Grask** (E3 is the frozen control) | Rangy, limb-dominant, long forearm and shank, reach-oriented | Greater torso contribution, axial breadth and depth, joint presence and pelvis–torso integration; **lower limb and arm share without being short-limbed**. | GO L16, L57, L111, L170, L222, L260, L266 |

Silhouettes are compared normalized to the same displayed height.

## 5. Deliberately not in this brief

- **No new numeric targets.** Canon numbers stay as they are: 229 cm nominal, five digits, and the directional relations above. Source quality and whole-body organization are the first gate (order §3).
- **No dimensions or shape from N1/U8 or from the rejected 05A–05P bodies.** Those appear only on the labelled negative sheet.
