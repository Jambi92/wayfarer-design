## 1. Author ruling

v004 is **NOT accepted visually yet**.

The overall direction is substantially closer to the intended Grask than v001-v003 and should be preserved, but the body contains visible form-resolution problems that read as sculpted lumps rather than coherent anatomy.

Primary owner-observed defects:

1. upper thighs look lumpy / segmented;
2. mid-back has an unnatural lumpy/swollen region;
3. chest has a triangular / pointed bump that does not read as believable pectoral-ribcage anatomy;
4. upper arms / forearms contain isolated bulges that do not feel anatomically continuous.

Additional visual balance concerns already identified in the v004 owner review:
- abdomen/lower torso is too soft and rounded relative to the target;
- posterior/glute profile is too bulbous in profile/three-quarter;
- head is still slightly underscaled relative to the target;
- neck-to-shoulder integration could use slightly more structural mass;
- forearm mass distribution is too concentrated in spots even though overall arm thickness is useful;
- thighs need more coherent grounded mass, not merely more volume.

The next candidate is **v005**.

## 2. Mission

Perform a **controlled body-form cleanup and mass-redistribution pass** on v004.

Do not restart the Grask design.

Do not reopen the successful macro direction unnecessarily.

The goal is:

> **Preserve v004's grounded-troll silhouette and identity gains, while replacing lumpy, pointy, or isolated local mass with cleaner, biologically coherent primary and secondary forms that match Tyler's target image more naturally.**

This is primarily a **form-quality pass**, not a new identity exploration.

## 5. Mandatory correction A — upper thighs

### Current problem
The upper thighs show visible lumpiness / segmented bulges, especially in front and three-quarter views.

The mass does not read as one coherent load-bearing thigh.

### Required correction
Rebuild the thigh form into clean primary volumes:

- smooth hip-to-thigh transition;
- continuous anterior thigh mass;
- coherent medial/adductor mass;
- coherent lateral thigh plane;
- clear but restrained knee transition;
- no isolated blobs or abrupt bulges.

The thigh should feel:
- powerful;
- grounded;
- weight-bearing;
- troll-like;
- anatomically continuous.

### Do not
- simply blur/smooth everything;
- carve bodybuilder muscle separations;
- add additional bulges;
- narrow back toward v003;
- inflate the thigh globally without form logic.

### Visual gate
In front and three-quarter:
- the upper thigh must read as one coherent mass.

In profile:
- the thigh must transition cleanly into hip and knee without a bulbous local mound.

## 6. Mandatory correction B — mid-back

### Current problem
The mid-back has an unnatural swollen/lumpy patch.

It reads as localized geometry accumulation rather than coherent back anatomy.

### Required correction
Reorganize the back into cleaner large planes:

- trapezius / upper back;
- scapular plane;
- lat-to-ribcage transition;
- mid-back plane;
- lower-back taper.

The back should remain broad and powerful, but its form must flow continuously.

### Do not
- flatten the back into a featureless plane;
- add bodybuilder striations;
- create a hunch;
- create a central “hump”;
- overemphasize individual scapulae.

### Visual gate
Back and three-quarter views must show:
- no isolated mid-back mound;
- clean broad troll back mass;
- coherent left/right symmetry except intentional natural asymmetry.

## 7. Mandatory correction C — chest / pectoral / ribcage

### Current problem
The chest shows a triangular / pointed bump that does not read as natural anatomy.

The pectoral/ribcage relationship is too localized and pointy.

### Required correction
Rebuild the chest as:
- broader;
- flatter across the central chest;
- structurally integrated with the ribcage;
- naturally transitioning into deltoid and upper arm;
- firm without bodybuilder definition.

Clarify:
- sternum plane;
- pectoral mass;
- clavicular transition;
- lateral ribcage.

### Do not
- leave any triangular peak;
- create shelf-like pecs;
- make superhero/bodybuilder chest anatomy;
- over-flatten the entire upper torso.

### Visual gate
Front and three-quarter views:
- chest reads as one broad structural mass;
- no isolated triangular projection;
- ribcage/pec transition feels natural.

Profile:
- chest projection remains grounded and close to target-image massing.

## 8. Mandatory correction D — arms

### Current problem
The arms contain isolated bumps, particularly in upper-arm and forearm massing.

The shapes feel like discrete sculpt pushes instead of continuous muscle/bone volumes.

### Required correction
Rebuild the arm flow as continuous anatomical masses:

#### Upper arm
- coherent deltoid into upper-arm transition;
- smooth humeral cylinder;
- strong but restrained biceps/triceps volume;
- no isolated shoulder or mid-arm lump.

#### Elbow
- clear structural joint;
- not a knob.

#### Forearm
- preserve Grask forearm prominence;
- redistribute mass so it flows from elbow into wrist;
- maintain strong flexor/extensor mass without Popeye-style concentration;
- wrist remains sturdy but not swollen.

### Do not
- reduce the arms back to human-thin;
- create bodybuilder separation;
- retain any single “ball” of forearm mass;
- make the hands appear disconnected from the forearms.

### Visual gate
Front/profile/three-quarter:
- arm silhouette must read as heavy and continuous;
- no isolated bulges;
- forearm mass is substantial but smoothly distributed.

## 9. Secondary correction E — abdomen / lower torso

### Current concern
v004's abdomen/lower torso is too soft and rounded relative to Tyler's target.

### Required direction
Keep natural volume, but:
- reduce excess forward abdominal roundness;
- tighten ribcage-to-waist transition;
- maintain troll mass without “pot belly” unless intentionally required by the target;
- keep abdomen broad/solid rather than athletic-cut.

Do not:
- add six-pack definition;
- pinch the waist;
- create an alien narrow torso.

## 10. Secondary correction F — posterior / glute profile

### Current concern
Profile and three-quarter show a posterior/glute contour that is too bulbous relative to the target.

### Required direction
- reduce excessive rear projection;
- keep enough gluteal mass for the long lower body;
- create a firmer pelvis-to-thigh transition;
- avoid flat or human-fashion-model buttocks.

The result should feel grounded and functional.

### Neck

- maintain compact, load-bearing neck appearance;
- do not create wrestler neck or hunch.

Any head/neck adjustment must remain subordinate to the body-form cleanup.
