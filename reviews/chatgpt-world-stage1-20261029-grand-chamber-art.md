# Wayfarer Stage 1 — grand chamber art pass

2026-10-29



A separate grand-chamber development level now takes the first bounded art pass toward the approved cavern concept. Earlier levels remain preserved.



## Result

- Added 15 broad, embedded wall masses and 42 angular fallen blocks. The main cave shell and three major columns receive restrained surface relief.

- Refined scanned limestone, sediment and damp shading along the existing watercourse. Existing water geography and route grades remain unchanged.

- Reduced uniform illumination while retaining animated flame-light functions. Lower survey lanterns now use short, floor-fitted supports instead of very long hanging lines.

- Preserved the previously cleared first-opening chamber and existing character assets.

## Next art work

This is an intermediate environment pass, not finished cavern art. More varied ledge silhouettes, richer middle/lower-tier geology, grouped mineral growths and better-developed water/floor transitions remain necessary to reach the concept's visual richness. Close stair views also need further timber and inspection-light shading cleanup. Combat, final creatures, quest sequencing and shipping performance have separate acceptance work.
