Sagekin are "a fully human population with a distinct ancestral homeland. Their famous scholarly tradition is culture, never biology" (L3). Facial identity is distributional and multi-trait. The spec gives no numeric facial ranges.

---

## 1. Cranial vault / cranial proportions
- [ANAT] Sagekin differ mainly through "craniofacial proportions" and "forehead and skull proportions" (foundational §3 L21–28).
- [ANAT] "Sagekin will have their own distributions for skull proportions … These overlap strongly with Marchfolk, and no single feature is a definitive marker" (v1.0 §7 L105).
- [CTRL] Head and cranium: "Head width and depth, cranial height, forehead height and slope, temple width, face length" (v1.2 §2–7 L215).
- [ANAT] Tendency: "Moderate to slightly longer faces and a somewhat higher forehead, overlapping heavily with Marchfolk" (L215).
- [VAL] Sagekin are never uniformly "long-faced" (v1.5 §2 L431).

## 2. Forehead
- [ANAT] Tendency: "a somewhat higher forehead" (L215).
- [ANAT] "Forehead and skull proportions" is a differentiation axis (L28).
- [CTRL] "forehead height and slope" (L215).

## 3. Brow / supraorbital
- [CTRL] "brow-to-eye distance, brow prominence" falls in the eyes and orbital region (L216).
- No brow tendency is stated.

## 4. Orbit
- [CTRL] Region name "Eyes and orbital region" (L216). "Eye depth, spacing" (L216).
- [ANAT] "eye-region anatomy" is a differentiation axis (L23). "eye region" has its own distribution (L105, L209).
- No specific orbital tendency is stated.

## 5. External eye (aperture, lids, canthi, folds)
- [CTRL] "size, corner positions, angle, upper and lower lid structure" (L216). "Corner positions" is the only canthal reference.
- [ANAT] Tendency: "No mandatory eye shape" (L216).
- [VAL] All Sagekin are never "identical in … eyes" (L431).
- Folds: SILENT.

## 6. Ocular anatomy
- [ANAT] "natural hair-color and eye-color distributions" are a differentiation axis (L31).
- [ANAT] Eyes (working, not final): "Brown and dark brown common". Also possible: "Hazel, amber, green, gray and other human colors at lower frequencies" (v1.2 §9 L232).
- [OPEN] "Exact distributions wait for worldbuilding and visual validation" (L234).
- [CTRL] Eye color is among the inherited appearance traits with weighted population distributions (v1.4 §3 L340).
- [VAL] Facial animation validation includes "eye movement" (v1.5 §4 L439).
- Sclera, pupil, nictitating membrane, low-light vision and the magical distinction: SILENT.

## 7. Cheek / zygomatic
- [ANAT] "cheek and mid-face structure" is a differentiation axis (L22).
- [CTRL] "Cheekbone height and width, cheek projection, mid-face depth, fullness" (L218).
- [VAL] "The body-to-face soft-tissue link still applies" (L218). This refers to the Skarn proposed principle and is not restated here.

## 8. Midface
- [ANAT] "cheek and mid-face structure" (L22). The "mid-face" has its own distribution (L105, L209).
- [CTRL] "mid-face depth" (L218). The region is combined with cheeks.
- No specific midface tendency is stated.

## 9. Nasal anatomy
- [ANAT] "nose structure" is a differentiation axis (L24).
- [CTRL] "Bridge height, width and contour, length, projection, tip width, shape and rotation, nostril width and shape, alar flare" (L217).
- [CTRL] "Each adjustable independently where feasible, with no rigid racial nose types" (L217).
- [VAL] The clone failure test fails on "Nearly identical noses" (v1.4 §8 L370). The v1.5 test fails if "identical in nose" (L431).

## 10. Mouth / lips
- [CTRL] "Mouth width, upper and lower lip fullness, Cupid's bow, lip projection, corner position, philtrum length and depth" (L220).
- [VAL] "Keeps the nose, philtrum, lip, jaw and chin relationships" (L220).
- [ANAT] "mouth and lips" is a multi-trait contributor (L209).
- No population tendency is stated.

## 11. Jaw / mandible

- [CTRL] "Jaw width and angle, mandibular depth" (L219).
- [ANAT] "No mandatory configuration" (L219).

## 12. Chin

## 20. Facial hair biology (incl. eyebrows)
- [PRES] "Facial hair stays independent, from clean-shaven to substantial where biologically appropriate, and never determines ancestry" (v1.3 §5 L277).

- [VAL] The clone/stereotype test fails on "Mandatory facial hair" (L375).
- [ANAT] Hair biology (texture, density, natural color) is separate from style and "can follow population frequencies" (v1.3 §4 L273). Full human texture range (L46). Black and dark brown are common (L46, L231).
- Eyebrows: SILENT.

## 22. Locked validation tests touching the face
- [VAL] Validation principle: "Sagekin ancestry is recognizable across a large sample, not in every individual … no caricature for instant recognition" (v1.2 §12 L246).
- [VAL] Large-sample population test: 100 Marchfolk, 100 Sagekin and 100 Skarn with presentation neutralized. Sagekin should show "Overlapping but statistically shifted human traits" (v1.4 §7 L356–364).
- [VAL] Large-sample validation checks within-population diversity and between-population coherence. "Perfect classification … isn't required" (v1.5 §8 L457).
- [VAL] Clone and stereotype failure test (v1.4 §8 L368–379). Face-relevant failures:
  - nearly identical noses
  - one dominant facial template
  - nearly identical pigmentation
  - identical hair
  - mandatory facial hair
  - mandatory scholarly appearance
  - "Any single racial marker repeated excessively"
- [VAL] Cultural neutralization test: identity "stays statistically present through ancestry" (v1.4 §9 L383).
- [VAL] Cross-cultural presentation test: "Presentation never changes anatomy" (v1.4 §10 L387–394).
- [VAL] No artificial exaggeration: Sagekin are "never extremely tall, extremely thin or long-faced, never identical in nose, eyes, pigmentation or hair color" (v1.5 §2 L431).
- [VAL] Facial animation validation (proposed universal): neutral, speech, smile, anger, surprise, blink, eye movement and strong or extreme expressions. It "must keep individual identity and supported variation" (v1.5 §4 L439).
- [VAL] Fixtures (v1.5 §1 L414–427):
  - SG-10 Marchfolk-overlap edge case, "ambiguity accepted" (L195, L423)
  - SG-11 Elder
  - SG-12 Culturally neutral
  - SG-13 Sagekin ancestry with Marchfolk-style presentation
  - SG-14 Valid extreme-randomization result
  - SG-01–09 are body/height fixtures.

## 24. Pass 1 provisional creator-control list for the face
Classification (v1.2 L205): facial "regions, editing levels, control groupings, slider organization or other creator-facing control structure" are **APPROVED FIRST-PASS FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS / PROVISIONAL CONTROL ORGANIZATION**. "craniofacial anatomy, population tendencies, valid biological variation, anatomical relationships, ear biology, facial identity, validation, required customization capability and preset and randomization requirements stay approved". "Only their organization into a final facial-control system is provisional".

- [CTRL] Six region rows (v1.2 §2–7 L213–220):
  - Head and cranium: head width and depth, cranial height, forehead height and slope, temple width, face length.
  - Eyes and orbital region: eye depth, spacing, size, corner positions, angle, upper and lower lid structure, brow-to-eye distance, brow prominence.
  - Nose: bridge height, width and contour, length, projection, tip width, shape and rotation, nostril width and shape, alar flare. Each is "adjustable independently where feasible".
  - Cheeks and mid-face: cheekbone height and width, cheek projection, mid-face depth, fullness.
  - Jaw and chin: jaw width and angle, mandibular depth, chin width, height and projection.
  - Mouth and lips: mouth width, upper and lower lip fullness, Cupid's bow, lip projection, corner position, philtrum length and depth.
- [CTRL] Asymmetry, Restore Symmetry and Naturalize Face (proposed) carry over (L238).
- [CTRL] Facial hair is independent (L277). The hair style library is in v1.3 §4 (L273).
- [VAL] Multi-trait model: "No single trait determines ancestry" (v1.2 §1 L209).
- No ears row and no explicit master-slider prohibition.

## 25. Prohibited / forbidden controls and anti-patterns
- [VAL] "no rigid racial nose types" (L217). Not a "fixed 'racial face type'" or "rigid phenotype package" (v1.4 §5 L348).
- [VAL] "There is no exaggerated caricature just to make them look different" (L58). "no caricature for instant recognition" (L246).
- [VAL] "Recognizability never comes from pushing traits past the design" (L431).
- [VAL] Elven traits are never used for distinction (L147). Sagekin must "never [be] pushed toward elven anatomy to look more different" (L3).
- [VAL] Intelligence, education, weakness, frailty, "soft hands" and occupation are never encoded in anatomy (L17, L54, L66).
- [VAL] Hairstyle is never a biological racial marker (L46, L273).
- [VAL] Skin color alone is never a racial marker (L109).
- [VAL] Presentation presets "never overwrite race, anatomy, height, frame, muscle, body fat or face" (v1.3 §10 L297). Presentation presets never replace biological anatomy (v1.4 §1 L323).
- [VAL] Generation layers "are never collapsed into one race preset" (v1.5 §6 L449).
- [VAL] Manual customization always keeps the full biologically valid range (L340). Players can pick valid rare traits by hand (L224).

## 26. Positive identity statement for the face
- "Sagekin facial identity comes from overlapping distributions across many traits, not one canonical face. The contributing traits are cranium, forehead, eye region, cheeks, mid-face, nose, jaw, chin, mouth and lips, pigmentation and hair. No single trait determines ancestry" (v1.2 §1 L209).
- "A Sagekin stripped of hairstyle, clothing, jewelry, occupation and cultural presentation still has a plausible population-level ancestry, close enough to other humans that individual traits overlap naturally" (L58).
- "Sagekin ancestry is recognizable across a large sample, not in every individual" (L246). Neutralized identity "stays statistically present through ancestry" (L383).
- Head tendency: "Moderate to slightly longer faces and a somewhat higher forehead" (L215).
- Note: this is population-level identity. The face is explicitly not required to be individually identifiable (L113, L127, L364).

## 27. Cross-population facial boundary tests / comparators
- [VAL] Marchfolk: heavy overlap is intentional (L66, L105, L246). SG-10 overlap edge case (L195). v1.0 validation pairs with neutralized presentation, where "individual pairs don't have to be unmistakably different" (L117–127). Large-sample test (L356, L457).
- [VAL] Skarn: the large-sample test expects "a much stronger skeletal and mass distinction" (L362). The cross-cultural test includes Skarn ancestry with Sagekin presentation (L392).
- [VAL] Fenn/Aelari: v1.1 §2 says never reproduce their central-tendency anatomy (L147). v1.5 §9 is an active test (Pass 2 AC-7): "the most extreme valid Sagekin are compared against Fenn and Aelari. Single measurements may overlap, but complete Sagekin anatomy must never reproduce elven anatomy". The Fenn/Aelari-side tests are FN-17 and AE-21 (L461).

## 28. Measurement-deferred items (RM-*)
- None. No RM-* references in the spec.
- Detailed craniofacial ranges were deferred to "a later facial spec" (L105). v1.2 supplies tendencies, not numbers.

## 29. Presets / randomization / Simple-Advanced statements touching the face
- [CTRL] Two preset categories (proposed universal, v1.4 §1 L320–323):
  - Character preset: anatomy, face, age, composition, natural appearance, hair and presentation.
  - Presentation preset: never replaces biological anatomy.
- [PRES] Character presets: Academy Lecturer, Harbor Navigator, Urban Artisan, Merchant Traveler, Coastal Soldier, Heavyset Archivist, Young Wayfarer and Elder Astronomer. They are reproducible and fully editable (L327–336).
- [PRES] Presentation presets: Academy Formal, Harbor Practical, Merchant Urban, Traveler, Observatory Ceremonial and Working Artisan (L297).
- [CTRL] Population-aware randomization uses weighted distributions for facial anatomy, natural skin, hair color and texture, and eye color (L340).
- [CTRL] Frequency categories:
  - v1.2 §8 (L224): Common / Uncommon / Rare.
  - v1.4 §4 (L344): Very Common / Common / Uncommon / Rare.
- [CTRL] Selective randomization: Randomize All, Body, Face, Hair, Skin Details, Markings and Presentation, with locks. It "keeps the race unless the player explicitly changes it" (v1.4 §11 L398).
- [VAL] Deterministic generation (proposed): reproducible from configuration data and/or seeds (L453).
- Simple/Advanced: SILENT in the Sagekin spec. The flow is in PR L8–9.

## Project-rules context (decisions/PROJECT_RULES.md; not spec claims)
- PR L31–33: facial control organizations are provisional. The UFCA Review follows the 13 races.
- PR L18: population correlations normally use weighted distributions or validity envelopes rather than hard creator dependencies. This is consistent with Sagekin v1.4 §3–5.
- PR L19: populations may overlap in surface phenotype "without becoming anatomically interchangeable". This is relevant to the Marchfolk/Sagekin overlap and to the AC-4 ear pointer.
- PR L53: population, playable lineage, culture and social identity are distinct terms. This parallels Sagekin v1.3 §11 (ancestry, birthplace, culture, background).
- PR L54: unqualified "human" means Marchfolk HRP at matched normalized height.
- PR L16: the age triad. The Sagekin spec is silent.
- PR L65–71: R-SEX. The Sagekin spec defines no facial sex tendency, so "no shift" is valid per PR L69. This is a project-rule inference and not a Sagekin spec claim.

## Extraction notes
- **Superseded/clarified:** v1.0 §3 bullets (L80–84) are "kept as history", clarified by v1.1 §1 (Pass 2 AC-6, L89). This is body-only, with no facial impact.
- **Frequency vocabulary drift:** v1.2 §8 uses three tiers (L224). v1.4 §4 uses four, adding "Very Common" (L344). The later text plausibly governs, but the spec does not say so explicitly.
- **Mild tension:** the v1.2 tendency is "moderate to slightly longer faces" (L215), while v1.5 §2 says Sagekin are never uniformly "long-faced" (L431). This is consistent as a population tendency versus an anti-exaggeration cap, but the UFCA should note the boundary.
- **Region coverage:** the v1.2 table has six rows. It has no ears row (ears are covered by AC-4 at L149) and no "seven regions" claim. Asymmetry and age content is incorporated by reference ("earlier rules stay", L238) rather than restated.
- The cheek row's "body-to-face soft-tissue link" (L218) depends on the Skarn proposed-universal principle (Skarn v1.2 §7), which is itself pending validation.
- "Where biologically appropriate" for facial hair (L277) implies a bound that is not defined anywhere in the spec.
- Facial identity is explicitly statistical. Individual neutralized Sagekin may be indistinguishable from Marchfolk (L113, L127, SG-10). This differs from the Skarn per-individual equal-height test.
