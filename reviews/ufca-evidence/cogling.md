# UFCA-01 evidence — Cogling (specs/cogling/COGLING_V1.md, 3243 lines)

Status: Cogling v1.0 FIRST-PASS COMPLETE (L3, L3243). Part 3 (craniofacial, ears, facial customization) FIRST-PASS ACCEPTED (L1689); Part 4 (surface, hair, eyes, age presentation) FIRST-PASS ACCEPTED (L2138). Spec read in full (L1–L3243).

Facial anchor: **Fine-Scale Planar Integration** (§75, L1122–1134). Ear anchor: **Fine Folded Auricular Architecture** (§87, L1323–1331), a secondary tendency (L1393). Body anchor (primary body identity): Fine-Scale Elongated Articulation (§3, L31–43).

## 1. Cranial vault / cranial proportions
- [ANAT] FSPI: "an adult cranial envelope supports facial skeletal height at or slightly above the Marchfolk adult face-to-vault relationship" (§75, L1124).
- [ANAT] "**Adult face-to-vault proportion:** facial skeletal height relative to cranial-vault size sits broadly at or slightly above the Marchfolk adult relationship rather than moving toward the large-vault/small-face relationship of a toddler." (L1131)
- [ANAT] "Cogling heads remain adult-valid and relatively small in absolute dimensions. The cranial vault must not become the racial identity." (§77, L1163–1165)
- [ANAT] Natural allometry may make the head "a somewhat larger fraction of total height than in the Marchfolk Human Reference Population ... but cranial enlargement is not an authored racial feature." (L1167; also §18 L280, §51 L727–729)
- [ANAT] Variation: cranial length, breadth, height, posterior contour, forehead contour, temporal contour; "All combinations remain constrained by adult craniofacial integration." (L1169–1177)
- [ANAT] Head-to-body ratio "must remain within an adult-valid Cogling envelope and survive direct comparison with a human child" (§18, L282). Avoid "infant/toddler head share; bobble-head silhouette; oversized cranium as racial shorthand" (§51, L731–734).
- [ANAT] "Head size cannot be used as the main mechanism that makes Cogling look small." (§17, L274)
- [ANAT] Adult-valid head size "of roughly 11–13 cm" (§35 L522; §99 L1581; §207 L3170).
- [ANAT] "Cogling cranial-vault proportion, head size and other craniofacial anatomy imply **nothing about intelligence, cognition, curiosity, memory, inventiveness or learning ability**." (§161, L2480)
- [ANAT] Height change must not change "head size by identical percentage" (§62, L888).
- [ANAT] Helmets must preserve "approved cranial envelope ... actual head scale"; no oversized "gnome head" shell (§179, L2750–2758); helmets must not "enlarge or compress the head" (L2729).
- [CTRL] "cranial length/breadth/height" (§97, L1539).
- [OPEN] Head-to-body ratio / exact head allometry / exact cranial ratios (L284, L736, L1666, L3168–3169).

## 6. Ocular anatomy

## 13. External ear
- [ANAT] "**Fine Folded Auricular Architecture: a relatively compact adult ear with a clearly defined helix, antihelix and conchal bowl, fine cartilage thickness, and a subtly articulated upper-ear contour that remains rounded rather than pointed.**" (§87, L1327)
- [ANAT] "The ear should read as structurally detailed at small scale without becoming oversized. This is not an elven ear and does not terminate in a point." (L1329–1331)
- [ANAT] "Cogling ears remain proportionate adult humanoid ears. They are not biologically enormous." Variation: overall auricular height, width, projection from skull, vertical placement, rotation, lobe attachment, upper-ear contour (§88, L1335–1346). "The ear cannot be enlarged merely to improve silhouette readability." (L1348)
- [ANAT] "The defining ear signal comes from **fold clarity at fine scale**, not size." Fold variation: helix thickness, helix roll, antihelix prominence, superior/inferior crus relationship, conchal depth, tragus/antitragus prominence, lobular size, skull attachment (§89, L1352–1362). "The terminal upper contour remains rounded-to-softly angular and non-pointed." (L1364)
- [ANAT] Boundaries (§90, L1368–1391): vs Fenn/Aelari/Vael no pointed terminal elongation; vs Grask finer/more compact, no robust folded upper-ear body or terminal taper; vs Gorrund no broad deep bowl, substantial rim, broad skull attachment; vs Pipkin overlap expected, Cogling "may trend toward finer cartilage and crisper fold articulation", "neither ear system alone may classify the race"; vs Durrim finer cartilage, overlap valid; vs humans overlap valid.
- [ANAT] "**Fine Folded Auricular Architecture is a secondary population tendency, not a primary racial identifier.**" (L1393)
- [PRES] "Fine auricular fold detail is principally a **close-view/creator-camera trait**. It is not required to remain legible at ordinary gameplay or dialogue distance." (§99, L1594)
- [ANAT] Helmets must preserve ear placement (L2753); equipment fit accounts for "head and ear geometry" (L2710).
- [CTRL] "ear size/placement/projection; ear fold architecture" (L1557–1558).
- [OPEN] "exact ear size/fold distributions" (L1674); "exact ear distributions and ear mobility" (L3171).

## 18. Age-related facial change (age triad)
- [ANAT] "Chronological Age, Apparent Biological Age and Age Presentation remain separate concepts." (§94, L1509); "These cannot be collapsed into one slider or one visual stereotype." (§119, L1892–1897)
- [ANAT] Part 3 requires: "adults remain adult at young-adult presentation; age cannot be required to create racial identity; wrinkles and tissue aging cannot compensate for juvenile base proportions." (L1511–1514)
- [ANAT] Young adult must read mature; cannot depend on "enlarged eyes; reduced jaw; shortened midface; smooth doll-like skin; child fat distribution." Youthful adult may have smoother skin (§120, L1903–1912).
- [ANAT] Mature adult: fine lines, texture, subtle soft-tissue redistribution, graying/density changes; "No single age marker is mandatory." (§121, L1916–1923)
- [ANAT] Older adult: wrinkles and folds, texture, soft-tissue redistribution, graying/whitening, density change; "Old age must not turn every Cogling into a tiny wizard, inventor, elder or comic archetype." (§122, L1927–1937)

- [ANAT] Adult maturity must not rely on wrinkles, gray hair (§76, L1139–1141).

## 20. Facial hair biology (incl. eyebrows)
- [ANAT] "Where supported by individual biology, Cogling may grow facial hair." Facial hair "is not mandatory; is not required for adult read; does not define sex; does not imply occupation, wisdom or age; is not a racial identifier." (§113, L1800–1807)
- [ANAT] Valid styles eventually "broad beard, mustache, sideburn and mixed-growth patterns according to individual growth distribution." No "bearded tinkerer" default (L1809–1811).
- [OPEN] Sex-related/hormonal distributions in scalp, facial, body hair "remain OPEN and, if later approved, must use **soft correlations rather than hard creator dependencies**." (§114, L1819); "detailed facial/body-hair growth distributions" (L2121).

- [ANAT] Scalp hair architecture: strand thickness, density, pattern, growth direction, hairline, crown pattern, length potential, age changes; no hair texture/hairstyle required (§111, L1770–1780). Hair strand diameter "not assumed to scale linearly with total body height" (§131, L2033).

## 26. Positive identity statement for the face
- §75 anchor: "**Cogling craniofacial identity is organized around Fine-Scale Planar Integration: an adult cranial envelope supports facial skeletal height at or slightly above the Marchfolk adult face-to-vault relationship, while comparatively fine skeletal mass retains clearly angled junctions from orbit to zygoma, zygoma to maxilla, and mandibular body to ramus. The result is a mature face with defined planar transitions at very small absolute scale rather than enlarged individual features.**" (L1124)
- Sequence: "adult face-to-vault relationship → bounded orbital/brow region → angled orbit-to-zygoma transition → integrated mature midface → angled zygoma-to-maxilla transition → mature mandibular body/ramus junction" (L1128). Two carriers: adult face-to-vault proportion; defined planar junctions at fine mass (L1131–1132). "The identity comes from these coordinated relationships, not from one oversized feature." (L1134)
- §104 Part 3 identity: "**Cogling faces are defined by Fine-Scale Planar Integration: ... Their eyes, noses, ears and mouths remain broadly adult humanoid in scale, while Fine Folded Auricular Architecture gives the ear a compact, clearly articulated, rounded and non-elven fold system. Adult maturity must survive at the smallest valid stature without wrinkles, facial hair, expression or cultural cues, and camera/readability problems must be solved without enlarging biological features.**" (L1687)
- §206 final (face clause): "Their face is defined by Fine-Scale Planar Integration: mature face-to-vault contribution combines with fine skeletal mass and clearly angled orbital, zygomatic, maxillary and mandibular junctions.
