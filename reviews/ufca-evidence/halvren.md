## 1. Cranial vault / cranial proportions
- [ANAT] Craniofacial anatomy occupies "a developmentally coherent mixed space between human-family and elven-family architecture", covering cranium through ears, "with no single interpolation value for the whole face" (Part 3 §1-4, L164).
- [ANAT] "Cranial inheritance covers cranial height, length and breadth, cranial-to-face relationship and forehead relationships, shifted by specific ancestry, never a 'human skull to elf skull' slider." (§11-14, L181)
- [ANAT] Craniofacial is a candidate soft inheritance cluster: cranium, orbits, midface, jaw (Part 1 §13, L38).
- [ANAT] Skarn ancestry may shift toward "cranial and facial skeletal robustness, neck-head integration" (L177).
- [ANAT] No mandatory face length or width; broad, narrow, long and compact faces supported (L166).

## 2. Forehead
- [ANAT] Forehead relationships part of cranial inheritance (L181); forehead listed in mixed craniofacial space (L164).
- [ANAT] Aelari ancestry: "longer forehead-to-chin" (L175).
- No forehead-specific controls (SILENT beyond these).

## 3. Brow / supraorbital
- [ANAT] **Brow structure** ("skeletal anatomy around the orbit") is one of four separate eye-area systems, "never one 'eye' control" (L181).
- [ANAT] Skarn ancestry may shift toward brow structure presence; never "a required ... heavy brow" (L177).
- [CTRL] Advanced Mode possible group "brow and orbit" (L227).
- [CTRL] Asymmetry includes brow (L192).

## 4. Orbit
- [ANAT] **Orbital anatomy** ("skeletal socket relationships") separate system (L181).
- [ANAT] Vael ancestry: "somewhat broader cheeks and orbits than Aelari" (L176); "its established orbital and cheek relationships" (L181).
- [VAL] Strong-expression stress includes "strong Sagekin influence with elven orbits" (L221).
- [ANAT] Strong elven cranial and orbital relationships may co-occur with human-like nose etc. (L166).

## 5. External eye
- [ANAT] **External eye anatomy** ("visible lids and opening") separate system (L181).
- [ANAT] Fenn ancestry: "more open visible orbital and eye presentation than Aelari and Vael populations" (L174); may raise probability of a more open visible eye presentation (L181).
- [ANAT] Aelari ancestry: "longer and narrower visible eyes than Fenn" (L175); may raise probability of longer and narrower presentation (L181).
- [ANAT] "Elven ancestry doesn't automatically mean larger eyeballs or openings, upturned or almond eyes, or glowing eyes." (L181)
- [ANAT] "Personality is never encoded into eye anatomy: Halvren don't automatically look mysterious, sad, wise, seductive, alert, suspicious or gentle. Neutral anatomy stays neutral" (L181).
- [ANAT] Fenn contribution never "huge eyes" (L174).
- Canthi / folds: SILENT.

## 6. Ocular anatomy
- [ANAT] **Ocular anatomy** ("the eye itself") separate system (L181).
- [OPEN] Pupil morphology, retinal differences, low-light sensitivity, color perception, reflective structures, daylight response not finalized (Part 3 §33, L210).
- [OPEN] Vael low-light adaptation "stays a candidate inherited system, so Vael ancestry doesn't automatically give glowing eyes, slit pupils, eyeshine, giant eyes, night vision or daylight weakness" (L210).
- [ANAT] Biological iris separate from magical/supernatural effects; "Halvren ancestry doesn't produce glowing or magical eyes" (L210).
- [ANAT] Iris inheritance independent of orbital anatomy, external eye anatomy, pupil morphology, ocular physiology and magical effects (Part 4 §25, L266).
- [ANAT] Iris families: human family (dark brown, brown, hazel-like, amber, green, gray, blue, intermediates); elven family per ECR (adds blue-gray, green-gray, muted violet, other grounded variants); Vael ancestry (brown, amber, hazel-like, gray, blue-gray, green-gray, muted violet, very dark); "Vael ancestry doesn't require unusual iris color, and unusual color doesn't prove recent Vael ancestry" (L268-L272).
- [ANAT] "Iris colors are never averaged: a blue-eyed human and a muted-violet-eyed elf don't automatically produce blue-violet" (L274).
- [OPEN] "Pupil morphology is OPEN, never tied to iris color and never made exotic just for distinctiveness, with round pupils a valid conservative candidate." (L274)
- [ANAT] Magical effects never inherited iris traits and never overwrite biological eye data (L274).
- [OPEN] Class A source gaps: elven eye biology, Vael low-light mechanism (L492).
- Sclera, nictitating membrane: SILENT.

## 9. Nasal
- [ANAT] Inheritance: "Nasal root, bridge height and width, length, projection, tip, alar width, nostrils"; rule: "No universal 'half-elf nose' (small, narrow, straight, delicate or upturned), and source frequencies follow their own specs" (L188).

## 11. Jaw / mandible

Vael ancestry (more presence than Fenn and Aelari ancestry) and Skarn ancestry (a human-family mechanism) can both give stronger jaws, and they aren't treated as the same mechanism" (L189).
- [ANAT] Fenn: "somewhat reduced lower-face structural mass", "relatively light mandibular presence within the elven range"; never "a tiny jaw" (L174). Aelari: "somewhat narrower lower face", "relatively light mandibular presence" (L175). Vael: "somewhat more mandibular presence than Fenn and Aelari" (L176). Skarn: jaw presence, never "a required square jaw" (L177).
- [ANAT] "More human-like mandibular mass" valid mosaic element (L166); strong and light jaws supported (L166).
- [VAL] Anti-beauty: narrower jaws (L222); anti-generic: "slightly narrow jaw" (L223).
- [VAL] Resolution: Fenn and Aelari face wording keeps ECR reference relationships, no ambiguous unnamed comparatives (L457).

## 12. Chin

## 13. External ear
- [ANAT] "Halvren ears are not human ears with a pointiness slider." (L196) Whole-ear inheritance because human and elven foundations differ (L198).
- [ANAT] Human contribution: auricular root and base, helix, antihelix, concha, tragus and antitragus, lobe, human projection/curvature/vertical orientation ranges (L198).
- [ANAT] Elven contribution: shared non-human foundation (ECR), with "length, base width, tip length and sharpness, vertical angle, sweep, lateral projection, curvature, lobe and skull-root integration as separate variables" (L198).
- [ANAT] Example mixed ears (not packages): mostly human length with subtle elven taper; moderate elongation with human-like projection; stronger elven length with broader human-influenced base; short elven-derived ear still visibly non-human up close; more human-like lobe with otherwise mixed structure (L198).
- [VAL] "Ear parameters are coupled, so extremes combined (a very narrow human-like base with extreme elven length, projection and sweep) may be implausible and need relationship-aware constraints." (L198)
- [ANAT] Fenn ancestry: greater lateral projection than Aelari and Vael ancestry, rearward sweep; guard "Projection and sweep stay separate, and Fenn ancestry isn't just sideways ears" (L202).
- [ANAT] Aelari: more upward and backward orientation, relatively low lateral projection, moderate-to-long length, gradual taper; "Overlaps Vael on projection, with no strict Aelari-Vael ranking" (L203).
- [ANAT] Vael: broader base, lateral and backward orientation, moderately shorter taper than Aelari, stronger skull-root integration; "No bat-like, monstrous or mandatorily short ears" (L204).
- [ANAT] **"Ear length isn't a genealogy meter."** No ancestry percentage inferred from ears (L206).
- [ANAT] Natural left-right variation separate from damage (L206).
- [OPEN] Ear mobility OPEN; if established, inheritance sets musculature and range, not an animation toggle (L206).
- [ANAT] "No automatic enhanced hearing" (L206).
- [ANAT] External ear is a candidate soft inheritance cluster (L39).
- [CTRL] Advanced Mode group "ears" (L227).
- [VAL] Ear-only test (L219); HV-05, HV-06, HV-26 to HV-35 (L359-L360, L369).
- [VAL] Equipment: helmets, hoods, circlets, earrings, hair-ear-headgear interaction (L386).

## 21. Inherited / mixed development (thorough)

### 21a. Core inheritance principles (locked)
- [ANAT] Not humans with pointed ears, not generic elves with human proportions, not automatic 50/50 averages (Part 1 §1-4, L9).
- [ANAT] "There's no single mandatory Halvren body or face (locked)." Systems "can't combine arbitrarily, though, since developmental relationships keep the anatomy coherent." (L11)
- [ANAT] "Ancestry isn't arithmetic averaging (locked)." Never "human value plus elven value divided by two for each parameter"; inheritance may be polygenic, dominance-like, recessive-like, additive or nonlinear, with trait correlations, developmental constraints, population-specific inheritance and multigenerational recombination; genetic simulation depth unresolved (L13).
- [ANAT] Expression domains: "There's no single human-to-elf slider for the whole body"; domains include craniofacial anatomy, orbits and eyes, external ears, skin, hair, iris, lifecycle; domains "stay biologically coordinated" (§10, L27).

### 21b. Coupled inheritance / developmental coherence
- [VAL] "Developmental coherence (locked): inheritance is relationship-aware, and a character can't inherit unrelated extremes from different populations if the result is implausible." Coupled regions may need compatible ranges without equal ancestry strength — "development produces one coherent organism" (L31).
- [ANAT] Candidate soft inheritance clusters: axial skeleton; appendicular skeleton; distal anatomy; **craniofacial (cranium, orbits, midface, jaw)**; **external ear (foundation and morphology)** — "conceptual biological groupings, not creator sliders or code architecture yet" (L33-L41).
- [VAL] "Mosaic expression without patchwork": e.g. human-like torso, elven limbs, strongly elven ears, intermediate craniofacial — valid only with coherent transitions; fails if "body parts copied from separate race meshes and attached together" (L41).
- [ANAT] Face: "Expression is regional but coupled." Example: stronger elven cranial and orbital relationships + more human-like nose + intermediate midface + more human-like mandibular mass + strongly elven ears; "never a patchwork of disconnected source-race face parts" (Part 3, L166).
- [VAL] Ear parameters coupled; relationship-aware constraints (L198).
- [VAL] Chin "independently variable within compatible relationships" (L190).
- [ANAT] Body analogue: joints bridge long bones coherently; fails if gracile bone meets massive joint "because two ancestry values were inherited independently" (L133).

### 21c. Source-population contributions and no generic parent
- [ANAT] No generic elf parent (no averaged elven source erasing Fenn/Aelari/Vael differences) and no generic human parent (Marchfolk primary reference, Skarn and Sagekin valid) (L50).
- [ANAT] Human-family face: Marchfolk broad variation, Sagekin population tendencies, Skarn greater structural presence where Skarn spec supports it; never one "human face"; none means personality, intelligence or culture (L170).
- [ANAT] Elven face from ECR shared craniofacial family; no single feature defines elven ancestry (L170).
- [ANAT] Per-source facial table with "Never translated into" guards (L172-L177):
  - Fenn: compact relationships, more open orbital/eye presentation than Aelari and Vael, reduced lower-face mass, distinct cheek placement, light mandible within elven range — never childlike, cute, feminized, huge eyes, tiny jaw, mandatory beauty.
  - Aelari: facial verticality, longer forehead-to-chin, narrower lower face, vertical midface, light mandible, longer narrower eyes than Fenn — never aristocratic, perfect symmetry, tiny nose, mandatory beauty, aloof, feminized.
  - Vael: less elongation than Aelari, compact vertical distribution, stronger midface, broader cheeks and orbits than Aelari, more mandibular presence than Fenn and Aelari — never permanent scowl, villainous, gaunt, predatory, mandatory sharpness.
  - Skarn: probability shift toward brow, jaw, cranial and facial robustness, neck-head integration — never required square jaw, heavy brow, large nose, angry or masculinized; "A tendency is never a mandatory phenotype."
- [VAL] Resolution: Fenn and Aelari face (Part 3) keep ECR reference relationships; no ambiguous unnamed comparatives (L457). Human-family "robustness" normalized to "human-family skeletal architecture and structural distributions"; elven gracility compared against a named human population (L459).
- [ANAT] Every source contribution is phrased "may", "tendency" or "probability" (review L429).

### 21d. Genealogy vs phenotype
- [ANAT] Genealogical ancestry (actual family) and phenotypic expression (how it shows) aren't identical; "Genealogy is never inferred from one visible feature." (§11, L29)
- [ANAT] "A Halvren is never labeled '25% elf-looking' or '75% human-looking' from phenotype." Genealogy and visible expression stay distinct if shown (L58).
- [ANAT] "Ear length isn't a genealogy meter." (L206)
- [ANAT] "Ancestry can be visually latent (locked)." Phenotype isn't a genealogy report; inheritance must support non-midpoint multigenerational expression (L256).
- [VAL] Resolution **critical system distinction**: A. genealogical ancestry; B. ancestry-derived biological constraints (valid distributions genealogy produces); C. phenotypic expression. "Editing visible phenotype never silently rewrites genealogy, and a control that changes genealogy is never merely an appearance slider." (L463)
- [VAL] "Ancestry data survives appearance editing"; changing genealogy never arbitrarily destroys player appearance without explicit design behavior (L390).
- [VAL] Appearance data distinguishes genealogical inputs, phenotypic expression, final anatomical state, composition, presentation; storing final slider values alone may not suffice (L70); final anatomy stored as coherent anatomical state (L137).
- [VAL] When genealogy is set, randomization respects it but allows multiple phenotypes, "never one deterministic appearance per ancestry history" (L347).
- [VAL] Ancestry distribution test: histories change distributions without dictating appearance (L378).

- [VAL] Face: "A Halvren may strongly resemble any source population, which is biologically acceptable, but the system shouldn't give an unrestricted way to exactly recreate another population's complete craniofacial distribution, judged on the whole face, not one trait." (L227)

- [VAL] HV-01 "never a literal human preset under another label"; HV-02 never exactly reproducing Fenn, Aelari or Vael anatomy (L355-L356).
