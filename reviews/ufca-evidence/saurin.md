## 1. Cranial vault / cranial proportions
- [ANAT] Vault: "low-to-moderate cranial vault" (§36 L663); vs Marchfolk at matched head size: "lower relative cranial-vault height; greater front-to-back cranial length; stronger temporal/postorbital structural contribution" (§37 L720–723).
- [ANAT] "Saurin have a visibly substantial facial skeleton relative to the cranial vault"; distinction is "forward structural contribution and depth, not simply increasing facial height" (§38 L734–736). Variation compact-faced ↔ more rostrally expressed within one system (L738).
- [ANAT] "This does not mean a tiny braincase, primitive intelligence or animal cognition." Cranial shape has no relationship to intelligence, class or personality (§37 L728–730).
- [ANAT] Region hierarchy includes "cranial roof and temporal line" and "posterior skull and occipital transition" as decisive plane changes (§36a L684–685).
- [ANAT] Facial skeletal mass moderate-to-substantial relative to own cranial scale; "Cranial robusticity varies independently within race-valid relationships" (§60 L1020–1029).
- [ANAT] Neck-skull: skull base accommodates upright head; head balance accounts for rostral projection; no forward-head carriage required; occipital/cervical anatomy OPEN (§55 L958–966).
- [ANAT] Stature share: "head-height share trends near to modestly below the Marchfolk adult range" (§55 L970).
- [VAL] First-pass creator hard bounds: cranial length ±8 %; cranial width ±8 %; cranial depth ±7 % (§259 L4174).
- [VAL] Head-to-body proportion ±8 % ("head length 0.156–0.184 of standing height; the +8 % head-scale decision is the centre") (§258 L4162). Reference head length/H 0.170 (§263 L4232).
- [VAL] Frame does **not** change the skull (§258 L4166).
- [CTRL] Cranial group: overall head scale; vault height; cranial length; posterior cranial depth; temporal breadth (§73 L1174–1179).
- [ANAT] Every valid naked skull keeps Layered Rostral-Cranial Integration, brow → temporal/postorbital transition, embedded orbit, deep articulated jaw base (§259 L4172).
- [OPEN] Exact cranial measurements (§76 L1265).

## Extraction notes
1. **§44 vs §259:** §44 L822–824 lists orbital size, visible opening and eyeball as separate controls; §259 L4181 couples orbit/lids/aperture/eyeball. The §44 reconciliation note (L826) says visible-opening variation works inside that coupling. SAU-FACE-06 (L1243) still says "separated", which reads as orbit size vs opening *shape*, not an independent eyeball.
2. **§73 orbital spacing vs §259 lock:** §73 L1190 lists "orbital spacing" as a control and §74 L1224 lists it as a preset axis. §259 L4183 locks orbital placement/spacing to the accepted architecture, with numeric tolerance OPEN. So it is effectively not a creator control right now.
3. **§45/§76 nictitating membrane OPEN → resolved §98** (pointer §249 L4035). §58 horn firewall → superseded by §100 (L1000). §59 crest/eyebrow-horn firewall reconciled (L1006). The earlier "low-profile ridge only" rule is superseded (L1633, §150 L2443).
4. **Domain classification split:** low ridges/hornlets = FD-SURF; prominent structures have an FD-STRUCT core and an FD-SURF sheath (§100 L1643–1645). Structural ridges = skull (§36a; §155 L2527). §122 L1976 lists "keratinous ridges" under FD-SURF. A downstream schema has to keep the structural ridge (skull, cannot be toggled) separate from the keratin ridge (display, can be minimal).
5. **Rostral floor:** in prose it is an absolute non-overlap with Marchfolk/Grask/Gorrund (§39, §146). Numerically it is a provisional index of 0.255 (§259 L4177), and the cross-race check is OPEN because comparator canon has no numbers. Protective canon: the floor is absolute in kind and provisional only in number.
6. **Head scale:** §73 "overall head scale within race-valid bounds" is quantified in §258 as head-to-body ±8 % (head length 0.156–0.184 H; +8 % head-scale centre). §55 says head-height share is near to modestly below Marchfolk. These do not conflict but use different measures (length vs height share).
7. **Sex:** §62's historical "later biological decision" placeholder is closed by §263. There is no craniofacial sex shift, and the display family is sex-neutral.
8. **Baseline term:** per §157 L2562, "baseline" = canonical species anatomy. So "baseline vertical pupil" (§95) and "baseline nictitating membrane" (§98) are species anatomy, not defaults.
9. **Chin/cheek/forehead** have anatomy statements but no creator controls in §73. The nearest proxies are temporal breadth, orbital-platform breadth and mandibular angle (§36a L716).
10. **§36a status:** originally PROVISIONAL, now ACCEPTED (L671). Its creator-variation list (L716) adds controls that §73 lacks (structural-ridge prominence, transition strength, orbital rim, temporal definition, mandibular angle, posterior contour).
