## 1. Cranial vault / cranial proportions
- [ANAT] Cranial vault: "Suits an intelligent humanoid population: no reduced braincase or sloping forehead to look primitive, no oversized cranium to look alien (proportions need validation)." (Pt3 table, L352)
- [ANAT] Pt1: "Intelligence is never encoded through cranial size, posture, facial expression, movement or body shape." (L80)
- [ANAT] Facial elongation "distributed across cranial proportions, brow-to-orbit relationship, midface and maxillary vertical contribution, nose root, lower-face vertical contribution and mandible; never vertical scaling." (L350)
- [ANAT] Head integration: "The Grask head integrates with the moderate-to-long neck, shoulders, tall stature and rangy body, never a normal human head floating on a stretched body. No head-to-height ratio is locked, but a comically small, oversized or uniformly scaled human head is avoided, pending cross-race prototype validation." (L431)
- [ANAT] Positive identity includes "strong cranial-neck integration appropriate to their rangy body architecture." (L342)
- [ANAT] Adult read: minimum-height Grask never with "an oversized childlike head … or childlike facial architecture." (L19); no "childlike head-body relationships" (L714).
- [ANAT] Neck trends moderate-to-long relative to Durrim and Skarn; no swan-neck caricature or forward monster neck (L43); "Grask should not generally rely on extreme neck length for racial identity" (L172); neutral head carriage, no constant forward-head posture (L620).
- [VAL] Verticality evaluated via "facial vertical contribution relative to cranial and head dimensions" among other measures. (L475)
- Cranial breadth tendency: SILENT (no breadth-to-height tendency stated for the cranium).

## 13. External ear
- [ANAT] Pt3 statement: "> **Grask ears are elongated humanoid ears with a backward/upward taper that remain anatomically distinct from elven ears** (the original 'broader structural base' wording is superseded by the ear and craniofacial clarification below)." (L396)

- [ANAT] Positive architecture (clarification): "> **Grask ears possess a robust, visibly folded cartilage architecture through much of the auricle, with a comparatively substantial upper-ear body that retains structural volume before transitioning into a later terminal taper.**" (L445)
- [ANAT] "> **The Grask ear remains structurally substantial farther from the skull before narrowing into its terminal taper.**" Taper begins later along outward extent; never blunt triangles, thick spikes or horn-like ears; "robust cartilage architecture never means a uniformly thick ear." Reads "structured, folded auricle → sustained upper-ear body → later taper" not "base → continuous elegant taper" (conceptual, not a formula). (L449–451)
- [ANAT] "> **Grask ear attachment should preserve a clearly humanoid auricular root while supporting the ear's robust folded cartilage body and later taper.**" Attachment not defined as higher/lower/broader/stronger than elves. (L453–455)

- [ANAT] Orientation (backward/upward/lateral) variable and overlaps elves; "**orientation alone must never distinguish a Grask ear from an elven ear**." (L459; L398)
- [ANAT] Length moderately to strongly elongated; exact ranges OPEN. (L398, L459)
- [ANAT] Not "large Fenn ears on a troll head"; taper may occur "without needle points, knife-like ears or perfect triangles." (L398)
- [ANAT] Cuts, missing sections, scars acquired, never default anatomy or toughness signal. (L398)
- [ANAT] Ear pigmentation may vary with thickness (translucency, vascular influence) as observational surface phenomena, "never glowing or magical tips." (L510)
