## Acceptance decision

Gate 2A is **ACCEPTED AND LOCKED** as the first-pass Saurin torso anatomical organization.

Also retain the previously accepted Gate 1 pelvis/sacrum/tail-root architecture.

Do not continue polishing either region during Gate 3 except for microscopic seam blending strictly required at the neck-to-thorax boundary.

## Gate 3 purpose

Proceed with **head / neck / thorax integration**.

This is not a head redesign and not a whole-body resculpt.

The objective is to make the approved Saurin head, cervical anatomy, shoulder girdle, and Gate 2A thorax read as one continuous evolved organism.

Current weakness: the head/neck still reads too much like a separate component placed onto an otherwise successful body.

Gate 3 must establish convincing load and tissue continuity:

**cranial base → cervical system → shoulder/scapular region → deep thoracic shell**

while preserving the positive identity of the existing head and body.

## Governing principle

**Do not rebuild what Rodin already does well. Modify only what prevents it from being our Saurin.**

Rodin remains a quality/surface/anatomical-sophistication reference, not biological authority.

## Head identity — preserve

Preserve the accepted Saurin head direction and canonical **Layered Rostral-Cranial Integration**:
- low-to-moderate cranial vault;
- greater front-to-back cranial length than Marchfolk;
- broad integrated orbital/temporal platform;
- compact projecting rostral midface;
- lower/longer forehead slope;
- deep posterior mandibular base;
- no default human chin;
- no human external nose;
- no default human external lips;
- recessed auricular opening architecture rather than projecting pinnae.

Do not humanize the head.

Do not turn it into a generic dragon, crocodile, monitor lizard, dinosaur, or monster head.

This gate is principally integration, not facial-detail exploration.

## Neck objective

Build a **structural cervical transition**, not a human neck with enlarged sternocleidomastoid/trapezius forms.

The neck must convincingly:
- support the elongated rostral cranium;
- transmit head mass into the shoulder/thoracic system;
- maintain useful rotational/flexion anatomy;
- preserve an upright obligate-biped posture;
- transition naturally into the deep thoracic shell.

Avoid a simple cylindrical neck.

Avoid a huge rope-muscle neck.

Avoid a bird-like stalk.

Avoid a head visibly sitting on top of the torso.

## Dorsal cervical integration

Develop believable posterior cranial attachment and cervical load paths.

The rear skull should flow into a layered cervical structure that distributes into:
- upper scapular region;
- medial dorsal thorax;
- shoulder foundation.

Do not create a human trapezius cape.

Use directional planes and tissue paths appropriate to the Saurin head mass and posture.

The dorsal neck should visually explain how the head is stabilized during locomotion, turning, acceleration and tail-counterbalanced movement without implying a gameplay bonus.

## Lateral cervical integration

The side view is critical.

Create layered lateral neck planes connecting:
- posterior/temporal cranial base;
- mandibular region;
- cervical column;
- shoulder/scapular structures;
- upper thorax.

Maintain enough separation of forms to communicate articulation and motion.

Do not fuse the jaw, neck and shoulder into one featureless wedge.

## Ventral neck / throat

Build a convincing ventral cervical system from jaw base toward upper thorax.

It must not resemble a human throat pasted onto a reptilian head.

Use continuous but differentiated soft-tissue/structural planes appropriate to the deep jaw base and elongated cervical architecture.

Do not invent reproductive, display, inflatable, dewlap, venom, gill, or respiratory-specialization anatomy. Those are not authorized by this gate.

A modest natural throat contour is fine; a specialized throat organ is not.

## Shoulder / thorax junction

Gate 2A's torso map is locked.

Only the immediate neck/upper-shoulder boundary may be adjusted where necessary to integrate the cervical structures.

Preserve:
- thoracic depth;
- chest shield;
- pectoral fan organization;
- Gate 2A ventral/lateral load paths;
- existing shoulder locations and arm proportions.

The new cervical anatomy should **terminate into or interdigitate with** the accepted thoracic organization rather than overwrite it.
