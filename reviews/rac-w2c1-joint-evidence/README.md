# RAC W2C1 — joint-breadth normalization evidence

For `reviews/claude-rac-w2c1-grask-joint-normalization-gate.md`.

- `joint_sections.json` — elbow, wrist, knee and ankle breadth ÷ R-6 stature: exact plane section and the accepted slab (absolute and stature-scaled), per body (`tools/rac/w1/w2c1_drivers/joint_section.py`).
- `knee_normalization.json` — section-only knee trends per population, leave-one-out verdicts on the questioned knee corrections, accepted knee decisions re-checked on the resolved bodies (`w2c1_drivers/knee_eval.py`).
- `elbow_wrist_ankle_check.json` — slab ÷ section per body and the margins of accepted joint comparisons under both readings (`w2c1_drivers/ewa_check.py`).
- `tables.md` — generated by `tools/rac/w1/gen_w2c1_docs.py`. Drivers as run: `w2c1_drivers/AS_RUN.sh`.
