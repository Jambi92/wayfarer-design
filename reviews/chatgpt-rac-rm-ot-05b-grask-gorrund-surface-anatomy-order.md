## 1. Objective

Build and validate a **race-specific musculoskeletal / surface-anatomy layer** for Grask and Gorrund on top of the accepted 05A rebuilt skeletons.

This layer is biological anatomy, not body composition.

It may change:
- muscle-belly placement and length,
- tendon length and visible transition,
- fascial / aponeurotic surface flow,
- skeletal-to-soft-tissue transition,
- scapular / latissimus organization,
- shoulder integration,
- lumbar / sacral surface continuity,
- gluteal attachment and shelf form,
- abdominal wall relief as dictated by the race skeleton,
- joint-surface relief around condyles / malleoli / epicondyles,
- other homologous soft-tissue topology required to make the existing non-human skeleton read correctly.

It may **not** make race identity depend on:
- more or less muscle mass,
- more or less fat,
- obesity / emaciation,
- sex dimorphism,
- posture,
- clothing,
- texture,
- pigmentation,
- scars,
- hair,
- culture,
- class.

The existing composition system must remain free to vary independently after this layer is applied.

## 3. Grask surface-anatomy target

The 05A skeleton already provides the correct direction. The surface layer must stop translating that skeleton back into ordinary human soft-tissue topology.

Build a Grask-specific anatomical relief emphasizing:

1. **Long-tendon distal limbs**
   - relatively short / high calf bellies with a visibly longer distal tendon run,
   - relatively short / high forearm bellies with longer tendon run to the wrist / hand,
   - do not achieve this by making the entire body “skinny.”

2. **Scapular / back organization**
   - vertically extended scapular / latissimus relief,
   - a back surface that visually supports the 05A vertically extended scapular bed,
   - preserve the open sloped girdle.

3. **Shoulder integration**
   - remove the default rounded human deltoid-cap read where it fights the skeleton,
   - shoulder / upper-arm transition should look integrated into the Grask girdle rather than like a human shoulder pasted onto a long body.

4. **Pelvis-to-thigh transition**
   - retain the canon-valid pelvis proportions from 05A,
   - alter the soft-tissue transition enough that the posterior / lateral hip does not simply reproduce the MPFB human gluteal shelf,
   - do not narrow the pelvis beyond canon and do not use a juvenile or emaciated read.

5. **Distal architecture visibility**
   - preserve the large integrated hands and feet,
   - preserve stronger wrist / ankle / elbow / knee presence,
   - surface relief should reveal rather than bury these structural cues.

The desired read is **Grask anatomy**, not “marfanoid / lanky human.”

## 4. Gorrund surface-anatomy target

The 05A skeleton is a valid intermediate, but the human donor currently turns its axial structure into a “stocky / paunchy human” read. Correct that without breaking ALPC canon.

Build a Gorrund-specific anatomical relief emphasizing:

1. **Axial back / sacral continuity**
   - reduce the default human lumbar hollow where it contradicts the deep axial structure,
   - create a continuous load-bearing back mass into the sacrum / pelvis,
   - preserve ALPC continuity and do not create a hunch.

2. **Gluteal / pelvic attachment**
   - remove the default human gluteal shelf as the dominant posterior cue,
   - integrate the hip / gluteal surface into the deeper pelvis and columnar lower-body architecture,
   - do not make the result obese, lumpy, or caricatured.

3. **Abdominal wall**
   - the abdominal surface should follow the depth-led pelvis / lower trunk instead of reading as an ordinary human belly or “paunch,”
   - keep reference-composition neutrality; do not solve this with fat reduction.

4. **Structural joint relief**
   - knee, ankle, wrist and elbow relief should read as load-bearing architecture rather than simply increased circumference,
   - preserve the flat / broad distal-bone logic already established in 05A.

5. **Thorax / shoulder integration**
   - retain the broad neck-base / girdle support and deep thoracic chain,
   - avoid a generic bodybuilder, barrel-chest, or “big human” silhouette.

The desired read is **Gorrund anatomy**, not “heavy / stocky / paunchy human.”
