## Gate 7 objective

Build the first canonical **Regional Scale Architecture + keratin/display anatomy pass** over the frozen Gate 6 organism.

The result must read as one animal whose scales, folds, plates, claws, orbital structures, and display anatomy arise from local mechanical requirements. Avoid uniform "lizard skin" noise and avoid decorative armor plating.

### A. Regional scale architecture

Implement the four functional surface families already established in canon:

- **Protective structural fields:** dorsal/posterolateral cranium, upper posterior neck, dorsal trunk, selected lateral trunk, dorsal tail, portions of forearm/shin, dorsal hands/feet.
- **Transitional articulation fields:** neck transitions, shoulder/axillary transition, elbow/wrist, pelvis/inguinal transition, knee/ankle, tail root and other high-flexion boundaries.
- **Fine expressive fields:** orbital region, rostral transitions, jaw articulation and other facial areas requiring readable expression.
- **Ventral field:** broader, smoother, more transverse organization on ventral neck/trunk where appropriate, but **not** a continuous snake-like belly-scute plate system.

Scale direction, size and overlap must follow the underlying form and expected deformation. The surface must clarify anatomy rather than conceal it.

### B. Head identity

Preserve the frozen +8% skull and its Layered Rostral-Cranial Integration. Add surface treatment that strengthens:
- low-to-moderate vault,
- broad orbital/temporal platform,
- compact projecting rostrum,
- deep articulated jaw base,
- recessed auricular opening.

No human external nose, lips, projecting ears or mammalian facial conventions.

Eyes must support the established Saurin phenotype: vertical pupil and nictitating membrane as biological anatomy, with no magical/emissive treatment.

### C. Keratin/display structures — controlled exploration

The user has strongly favored horns/ridges/crests as the Saurin customization analogue to hair. Gate 7 should test this **without yet declaring every display form universal canon**.

Create a neutral naked-head baseline plus a controlled comparison set using the same frozen skull:
1. minimal cranial ridges,
2. low hornlets,
3. swept-back paired keratin structures,
4. crest-dominant form,
5. mixed/asymmetric display form.

These structures must grow from credible cranial attachment zones and must not reshape the skull underneath. They are phenotype/display anatomy, not equipment.

Do not make the neutral baseline look unfinished merely because it lacks large display structures.

### D. Hands, feet and claws

Preserve approved limb proportions and plantigrade stance. Surface treatment should improve the non-human read:
- fine flexible palmar/plantar scales,
- localized pad-like thickening at contact/load areas,
- structured digital joints,
- keratin claws integrated with terminal digits.

Do not use mammalian paw pads, smooth human palms/soles, or oversized fantasy talons.

### E. Tail surface continuity

The tail must remain biologically inevitable. Scale architecture must visibly continue pelvis → tail root → free tail without creating a costume seam or a separate "tail texture." Use regional transitions at the root that reinforce, rather than obscure, the Gate 6 sacral/caudal load architecture.

### F. Material target

For this gate use neutral diagnostic materials only. Surface response may range matte to satin/modest natural sheen. Do not use wet, metallic, plastic, gemstone or emissive skin.

Do **not** solve pigmentation/pattern diversity yet beyond whatever neutral contrast is necessary to inspect scale fields. Pigmentation/patterning should receive its own controlled pass after structural surface anatomy is accepted.
