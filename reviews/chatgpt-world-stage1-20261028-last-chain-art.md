# Stage 1 Last Chain chamber and approach art

A separate development level extends the approved descent-to-prison direction into the existing Last Chain chamber. A continuous rock approach leads to a dominant forged chain passing through open guide housings into floor and ceiling stone. Diagonal load members meet embedded bearing seats, with replacement plates and fasteners suggesting repeated maintenance. A timber inspection platform, low-rise stairs and static rope hoist support the excavation setting. The established footprint and floor elevations are retained.

The new rock volumes were joined to remove internal overlapping sheets. Thin backing beneath the original sloping approach floor seals narrow collision gaps while preserving the floor actors and transforms; separating the buried backing from the wall normals removed broad contact-shading smears. Five lantern hangers were refitted and their local brightness balanced. All 104 saved flame functions remain active. Fixed-camera gameplay images confirm visible illumination changes on both sampled walls and the floor, with a stable HUD.

The six new meshes total 113,588 triangles with one render section each and closed source components. All 3,600 local boundary rays hit geometry, and thirteen retained routes passed their accepted baseline comparison. Twelve continuous outward/return movement legs passed through the approach, chamber circulation and inspection stairs with 76 cm and 251 cm diagnostic characters, including camera turns. Following the final contact-shading refinement, four additional approach legs also passed. Reopening passed native checks, all 4,068,289 terrain samples remain identical, and the map check reported zero errors or warnings. A one-minute static gameplay sample averaged 119.36 FPS with 9.46 ms p99 frame time; AI, combat and shipping performance remain untested by that sample.

The compressed native checkpoint was verified, and the original project plus accepted earlier versions retain their hashes. The approved divine standing-height reference informs the scale; complete clearance still requires final model widths, adornments, reach and animations. Character assets, dialogue, the prisoner reveal and quest staging are unchanged. This is an initial chamber art pass: finer geological breakup, richer material separation and weathering remain next. Only prose is published here; assets, canonical bibles, screenshots and raw diagnostics remain private.
