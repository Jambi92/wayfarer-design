# Saurin — Gate 4 Limb-System Reconstruction Order

**From:** ChatGPT (Author)  
**To:** Claude (Auditor / diagnostic sculpt workflow)  
**Status:** Author instruction  
**Phase:** DESIGN / anatomical target development only — no UE5 implementation

## Gate 3A disposition

Accept **Gate 3A cervical organic integration as the structural foundation**. It is not final-detail anatomy.

Do not spend another isolated pass polishing the neck now. Preserve its successful architecture:
- skull seated into the cervical mass rather than perched on a tube;
- overlapping/interdigitating cervical tissue rather than parallel cable bands;
- dorsal nuchal mass flowing into the scapular/thoracic region;
- mandibular base flowing through a broad throat system into the chest;
- existing head height, placement, and canonical TS6.1 cranial identity.

Remaining surface lumpiness, throat cleanup, and secondary cervical definition are deferred until whole-body anatomical language is coherent.

## Next order: Gate 4 — hindlimb / foot load-path reconstruction

The legs and feet are now the strongest remaining Rodin/humanoid inheritance. Gate 4 must rebuild them as a **single Saurin locomotor system**, not decorate the existing human leg map.

### Preserve / lock

Lock and do not redesign:
- accepted Gate 1 pelvis/sacrum/tail-root architecture;
- B2 free tail and its established placement;
- accepted Gate 2A thorax;
- accepted Gate 3A head/neck integration;
- overall standing height and current broad body proportions unless a tiny local adjustment is mechanically required;
- upright obligate biped stance;
- **plantigrade baseline**;
- five-toe provisional anatomy;
- canonical longer forefoot/toe contribution than Marchfolk;
- broad, stable Saurin foot concept.

### Primary anatomical objective

Build a continuous load path:

**posterior pelvic/caudal platform → proximal femur/thigh → knee → lower leg → ankle/rearfoot → structured midfoot → metatarsal spread → long articulated digits → claws**

The hindlimb must look as though it evolved together with a heavy counterbalancing tail.

The tail is not an accessory behind a human pelvis. Femoral organization and proximal thigh masses must visibly make sense beside the sacral/caudal system already established at Gate 1.

### Thigh

Do not simply reproduce human quadriceps, hamstrings, adductors, or a bodybuilding thigh map.

Translate the required functions into Saurin-specific masses and tension paths:
- hip stabilization;
- femoral extension/flexion;
- rotation;
- adduction/abduction;
- caudofemoral/pelvic interaction;
- transfer of tail-generated and trunk-generated loads into the femur.

Prefer longer directional forms, overlapping planes, and integrated attachment zones over isolated rounded muscle bellies.

The proximal thigh must emerge convincingly from the accepted pelvis without recreating human buttocks, a gluteal cleft, or human inguinal anatomy.

### Knee

The current knee reads too much like a stylized human knee with added protrusions.

Reconstruct it around clear mechanical landmarks:
- femoral termination;
- patellar/extensor-equivalent region only if mechanically justified;
- collateral/lateral stabilization;
- tibial transition;
- posterior flexion volume.

It should remain believable for an upright plantigrade biped while possessing a visibly non-human organization.

Do **not** convert Saurin to digitigrade anatomy.

### Lower leg

Replace the current tubular/column-like quality.

The lower leg should have:
- clear front/back and medial/lateral organization;
- directional tension from knee to ankle;
- asymmetrical biological planes;
- identifiable load-bearing versus propulsion structures;
- a convincing taper into the ankle.

Avoid merely carving grooves into the existing Rodin cylinder.

### Foot — major redesign

Treat the foot as reconstruction, not refinement.

Required chain:

**rearfoot/heel → structured midfoot → metatarsal fan/spread → long articulated toes → terminal claws**

Requirements:
- plantigrade contact remains obvious;
- heel must actually participate in stance;
- midfoot must carry structure rather than becoming a flat slab;
- forefoot is longer and more expressive than a human foot;
- five digits remain provisional baseline;
- digits must have visible segment/joint logic;
- claws grow from terminal phalangeal structure, not appear glued onto toe tips;
- contact surfaces can later receive fine flexible scales/pad-like thickening, but do not solve surface detail in this gate.

Do not create:
- bird/raptor feet;
- dinosaur digitigrade feet;
- mammalian paws;
- enlarged human feet with claws;
- flat generated paddles.

### Stance and silhouette check

After reconstruction, evaluate front, profile, rear, front 3/4, and rear 3/4 at full-body scale.

The legs must support the approved **Counterbalanced Pelvic-Axial Architecture**. From profile, the center of support should look plausible beneath the trunk while the tail counterbalances posteriorly. From rear 3/4, the pelvis → femur → knee → ankle path should read continuously.

Do not force a permanent crouch.

## Critical anti-regression rules

The visual target and Rodin B1/B2 remain **quality, depth, silhouette, and anatomical-richness references**, not authority to restore human-specific anatomy.

Do not reintroduce:
- human pectoral plates;
- six-pack/rectus ladder;
- human navel/linea-alba treatment;
- human gluteal hemispheres or cleft;
- human external genital anatomy;
- generic human limb muscle maps merely relabeled as Saurin.

Likewise, do not overcorrect into generic dinosaur/dragon anatomy. The target is the specific Saurin biology already established in the canonical specification.

This remains diagnostic anatomical target development. **No UE5 implementation.**
