# RAC-09: Halvren Inherited Stature-Tail Authorship

**Author:** Claude
**Order:** `reviews/chatgpt-reference-anatomy-closure-phase1-order.md` §3 (RAC-09)
**Status:** PROPOSAL: the biological-authorship prerequisite for **RM-UB-05**. Evidence: `reviews/rac-evidence/D-halvren-saurin.md` Part 1.
**No centimetre limit and no frequency is set.** Those stay RM-UB-05 outputs.

## 1. Canon in force

- 152–213 cm is the **central population envelope**, "no longer an absolute hard biological envelope" (HV L481; UCCA §22).
- "Strong ancestry from shorter human-family distributions may allow some Halvren below 152 cm, and strong Skarn or Aelari ancestry may allow some above 213 cm" (HV L481).
- Limits are "never set by hard clipping, compressing all inherited heights into 152–213, uniform scaling or parental averaging" (HV L481).
- **Guardrail:** "tails don't make any source-race height automatically valid. The mixed developmental system still governs validity", so strong Skarn ancestry never authorizes "the full Skarn height and structure distribution, and likewise for Aelari or Marchfolk extremes" (HV L481).
- HV-49 / HV-50 are tail targets, each "not automatically" a minimum or maximum (HV L487–488).
- Sources: Marchfolk, Skarn, Sagekin (human family) and Fenn, Aelari, Vael (elven family) (HV L9, L22, L407). No other population is a Halvren source.

## 2. Which source ancestries can extend the envelope

Edge test against the source specs' canon ranges (HV restates them at L94–99):

| Source | Range (cm) | Below 152? | Above 213? | Can contribute to | Named in HV L481? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Marchfolk | 147–203 | yes (147) | no | **lower tail** | yes ("shorter human-family distributions"; "Marchfolk extremes") |
| Sagekin | 152–208 | **no** (min = 152) | no | neither, by range edge | only inside the plural "human-family distributions" |
| Skarn | 183–229 | no | yes (229) | **upper tail** | yes |
| Fenn | 157–211 | no | no | neither | no |
| Aelari | 168–221 | no | yes (221) | **upper tail** | yes |
| Vael | 157–203 | no | no | neither | no |

**Proposed rule H-1 (A, conditional on AD-R32 / H-5):** the lower tail is supported only by human-family ancestry, and among the sources only Marchfolk ancestry reaches below 152 cm. The upper tail is supported by Skarn ancestry (human family) and Aelari ancestry (elven family). Fenn, Vael and Sagekin ancestry do not by themselves extend either tail; they shift a Halvren within the central envelope toward their own tendencies (HV L101). This **narrows** canon's plural "shorter human-family distributions"; it follows from the source ranges **only if** Halvren tails stay within the source ranges (H-5). Without H-5, H-1 is a reading, not a derivation.

## 3. Are the tails asymmetric?

Canon never uses the word. The **direction** follows from canon numbers:

| Property | Lower tail | Upper tail |
|---|---|---|
| Contributing families | one (human) | two (human: Skarn; elven: Aelari) |
| Contributing sources | one (Marchfolk) | two (Skarn, Aelari) |
| Room to the most extreme source edge (derived: 152 − 147; 229 − 213) | ~5 cm | ~16 cm |
| Opposing ancestry | every Halvren carries elven ancestry, and no elven source goes below 157 cm (HV L436) | none systematic |
| Canon example | — | Skarn × Aelari "isn't automatically their midpoint" (HV L101) |

**Proposed rule H-2 (B):** the tails are **asymmetric**. The lower tail is narrower and has fewer contributing ancestries than the upper tail. Magnitudes come from RM-UB-05.

## 4. Source protection at tail statures

- Phenotypic boundary protection: "ancestry controls can't simply recreate another playable race" (HV L141); source protection applies to body and face (UCCA L293; UFCA L231).
- At tail statures the only comparators are **Skarn and Aelari** (upper) and **Marchfolk** (lower), so the source-passing risk **concentrates where the tails live** (derived).

**Proposed rule H-3 (A):** a tail Halvren must remain Halvren against the matched-stature source it approaches. **Proposed permanent tests:** HV-50 vs matched-stature Skarn and vs matched-stature Aelari; HV-49 vs matched-stature Marchfolk, each under N3/N4. Failure means exact source duplication (HV L145).

## 5. Developmental plausibility

Already locked and applying unchanged at tail statures:
- no implausible union of unrelated extremes (HV L31, L82);
- Halvren "don't normally reproduce the most population-specific skeletal extremes of a pure source population" (HV L105);
- joint bridging; no gracile bone through a massive joint (HV L133);
- edges reached by proportion, never by uniform scaling (HV L101; HV-11/12);
- height is never a parental or reference average (HV L101).

**Proposed rule H-4 (A):** a tail stature is valid only if the whole body reaches it through Halvren mixed development: segment shares, allometry and joints coherent (RM-UB-02/03 constraints for Halvren), never by taking one source's stature on another source's proportions.

## 6. Why source extrema do not automatically become Halvren extrema

1. Halvren have their own mixed-population envelope; they do not inherit "the union of every source population's valid extremes" (HV L82).
2. The guardrail: tails never make a source height automatically valid (HV L481).
3. Damping: pure-source skeletal extremes are not normally reproduced (HV L105).
4. Every Halvren carries **both** families; the second family's distribution pulls away from the first family's extreme (derived from the family structure, HV L9, and the source ranges).
5. HV-49 / HV-50 are explicitly "not automatically" a minimum or maximum (HV L487–488).

**Proposed bounding rule H-5 (author decision, not derived):** Halvren tails stay **strictly inside the union of source extremes (147–229 cm)** and do not automatically reach any named source's own extreme. This gives RM-UB-05 a bounding frame and invents no number inside it. It is a **biological choice**: canon supports it (L82, L481) but does not state it.

## 7. Qualitative frequency ordering

Supported by canon:
- the central envelope describes "the expected primary playable and population distribution" (HV L481), and tails are "some Halvren" (HV L481): **tails are minority outcomes**;
- validity ≠ frequency (HV L23; HV L101); rare valid traits stay manually creatable (HV L262); interim weights are never canon (UCCA L267).

**Proposed rule H-6 (B):** central > tails in frequency. **Not proposed:** any ordering between upper and lower tail. A derived argument exists (the lower tail has one contributing source and is opposed by the always-present elven ancestry), but canon does not state it; it is offered to the author as a rationale only.

## 8. Reachability without a lineage entry (author decision, E)

With no genealogy set, "the general Halvren mixed-population envelope applies" (UFCA L223). Canon does not say whether that general envelope includes the tails. Options:
- **Y-1:** tails reachable only through a genealogy that supports them (lineage-gated);
- **Y-2:** tails reachable without lineage by manual creation and Extreme randomization, with the same validity rules.

This is a creator-system decision (class E), not biology. It does not block RM-UB-05.

## 9. What RM-UB-05 must then measure

1. Per contributing ancestry (Marchfolk-heavy; Skarn-heavy; Aelari-heavy; Skarn × Aelari), the stature at which a coherent Halvren body stops passing its tests (H-3, H-4).
2. Allometry and joint coherence at those statures (RM-UB-02/03 for Halvren).
3. Frequencies: generator work under UCCA §20, interim and non-canon until accepted.

**Blocker status:** not a blocker for the central Halvren reference (general envelope at 178 cm, RAC-02 §4); blocks only HV-49/50 extreme meshes. RM-OT-04 (Saurin vs Halvren) needs no tail, because Saurin 168–208 lies inside the central envelope.
