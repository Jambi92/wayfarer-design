# 2. GORRUND — DIRECT STRUCTURAL SCULPT

## Objective

Bring the neutral Gorrund body reference into closer visual alignment with the approved **massive load-bearing non-human** anatomy without solving the problem through generic muscle bulk, obesity, or simple global width.

The key approved body concept is:

> **Axial Load-Path Continuity**

The body must visually communicate an integrated structural chain:

> **shoulder girdle → thorax → lower axial trunk → pelvis → proximal lower limbs**

The current generator reference is directionally useful but does not fully realize this architecture.

## Preserve

Preserve the approved reference stature and general identity:
- ~229 cm reference standing height;
- upright obligate biped;
- plantigrade stance;
- large absolute hands and feet;
- substantial joints and long bones;
- lower proportional limb contribution than Grask at equal height;
- arms large in absolute terms but not reach-specialized;
- no hunch, permanent stoop, lumbering caricature or monster posture.

## Sculpt priorities

### A. Shoulder girdle
Increase the sense that the shoulder girdle is structurally integrated into the thorax rather than sitting as ordinary humanoid shoulders on a large body.

Look for:
- stronger bony base beneath the shoulder mass;
- broad structural transition into the upper thorax;
- substantial clavicular/scapular region without simply creating bodybuilder deltoids;
- neck/shoulder/thorax continuity appropriate to a massive load-bearing frame.

### B. Thorax
The thorax should be a major carrier of Gorrund identity.

Strengthen:
- breadth;
- depth;
- ribcage volume;
- structural continuity into both shoulder girdle and lower trunk.

Do not create a barrel torso by merely inflating circumference uniformly. It should feel built around a large skeletal cage.

### C. Lower axial trunk
Avoid an abrupt narrow waist that visually disconnects chest from pelvis.

The lower trunk should transfer structural mass from thorax toward pelvis while still allowing valid body-composition variation.

This is **not** permission to erase waist anatomy or create a rectangular block. The goal is continuity of load path.

### D. Pelvis
Increase the sense of a large load-bearing pelvic foundation:
- substantial pelvic breadth/depth;
- credible connection to lower trunk;
- strong hip-base relationship;
- no tiny pelvis beneath an oversized chest.

### E. Proximal lower limbs
The upper legs/hip joints must look capable of receiving the body's axial mass.

Strengthen:
- femoral base;
- hip-joint transition;
- proximal thigh structural scale;
- relationship between pelvis and legs.

Do not achieve this by simply increasing thigh muscle volume.

### F. Joints / extremities
Maintain substantial:
- shoulders;
- elbows;
- wrists;
- hips;
- knees;
- ankles;
- hands;
- feet

relative to the body's scale.

Avoid swollen-looking joints. The target is skeletal structural presence.

## Gorrund anti-targets

Do NOT turn Gorrund into:
- a taller Skarn;
- a giant Durrim;
- a bodybuilder;
- an obese giant;
- a hunched ogre;
- a long-armed reach specialist;
- a generic scaled-up human.

At normalized height, Gorrund should differ from Grask because Gorrund concentrates visual authority through **axial load-bearing continuity**, while Grask expresses **rangy reach-oriented limb architecture**.

## Gorrund validation renders

After direct sculpting, provide:
1. true-scale front/profile/3Q against Marchfolk and Grask;
2. normalized-height front/profile/3Q against Grask;
3. neutral composition only;
4. same orthographic camera standard used in Iteration 3;
5. preferably a simple silhouette overlay or side-by-side with pre-sculpt Gorrund so structural changes can be isolated.

Do not add costume, armor, dramatic pose, or surface detail to conceal body relationships.

# 3. SAURIN — DEDICATED RACIAL SCULPT

## Objective

Transform the current useful Saurin body/tail placeholder into a coherent Saurin visual reference whose non-human identity follows the approved spec.

The current body and complete tail provide a useful base. The current human-derived head is **not approved** and must not become the racial reference.

The governing whole-body principle is:

> **Counterbalanced Pelvic-Axial Architecture**

with:

> deep mobile thorax → elongated lower axial trunk → strongly integrated pelvis/sacral base → substantial counterbalancing tail.

The tail is biologically mandatory and is a primary racial identifier.

## 3A. Tail-base integration — highest body priority

The tail must not look attached to an otherwise unrelated humanoid pelvis.

Sculpt a biologically convincing transition through:
- lower spine;
- sacral region;
- posterior pelvis;
- gluteal/hip region;
- caudal base.

The correct validation question is:

> **Does the tail look biologically inevitable on this body rather than attached to an otherwise unrelated humanoid?**

Preserve:
- complete tail;
- meaningful base mass;
- taper;
- articulation;
- sufficient posterior structural support.

The tail is not prehensile by default and is not automatically a weapon.

### Tail gameplay/silhouette authority

Tyler's rulings remain binding:
- tail cannot be toggled off or removed as presentation;
- armor/clothing may cover, drape over or sheath portions of it;
- coverage may never make the Saurin functionally appear tailless;
- the tail must **not make the player easier to hit**;
- do not derive a gameplay penalty from the visual tail volume.

Do not alter anatomy to solve gameplay collision. Collision/gameplay representation must later respect the no-punishment ruling.

## 3B. Head — Layered Rostral-Cranial Integration

Replace the human-derived placeholder with the approved head architecture:

> **low-to-moderate cranial vault → broad integrated orbital/temporal platform → projecting but compact rostral midface → deep articulated jaw base**

### Cranium
- lower relative vault height than Marchfolk;
- greater front-to-back cranial length;
- strong temporal/postorbital structural contribution;
- avoid a human vertical forehead/nasal-root read.

### Rostrum
The rostrum is a true skeletal projection, not a human face with a muzzle pasted on.

It must:
- project clearly beyond Marchfolk/Grask/Gorrund normalized adult facial projection ranges;
- have a broad integrated base;
- connect structurally into orbital/zygomatic regions;
- taper moderately;
- remain compact rather than becoming a long canine muzzle;
- avoid a dragon wedge or crocodilian snout.

Preserve the approved minimum rostral floor.

### Orbit / temporal platform
- broad integrated orbital-temporal structure;
- somewhat greater lateral eye spacing than Marchfolk tendency;
- moderate orbital depth;
- forward-facing eyes;
- no mandatory angry brow;
- no permanent predatory expression.

Remember: orbital size, visible eye opening and eyeball size are separate variables.

### Jaw
- deep posterior mandibular attachment;
- integrated into temporal/orbital platform;
- taper forward;
- no default human chin;
- no forced grin/snarl;
- mouth line follows the rostral jaw naturally.

Do not infer diet or bite-attack gameplay from head anatomy.

### Nasal region
- no human external nose;
- openings belong to the rostral anatomy;
- avoid automatic crocodile/snake/dragon shorthand;
- no aquatic specialization inferred from nostril placement.

### Auricular region
Use the approved **Recessed Auricular Opening Architecture**:
- posterolateral auditory opening;
- shallow soft-tissue/cartilaginous rim/recess;
- no projecting human pinna;
- no elf point;
- no human lobule.

## 3C. Surface — Regional Scale Architecture

Do not apply one uniform reptile texture.

The approved logic is:

> **protective structural fields → transitional articulation fields → fine expressive fields**

plus the integrated ventral field.

### Structural fields
Emphasize appropriate scale organization on:
- dorsal/posterolateral cranium;
- upper posterior neck;
- dorsal trunk;
- selected lateral trunk;
- dorsal tail;
- portions of forearm/shin;
- dorsal hands/feet.

### Articulation fields
Use finer/flexible organization at:
- neck flexion;
- axilla;
- elbows;
- wrists;
- lower trunk;
- hips;
- knees;
- ankles;
- tail transitions;
- finger/toe joints.

### Fine expressive facial fields
Preserve mobility around:
- eyelids;
- mouth;
- rostrum-cheek transition;
- jaw corners;
- auricular recess;
- throat.

### Ventral field
Create a broader/smoother/more transverse ventral organization through throat/anterior neck/chest/abdomen/tail underside, but **do not create continuous snake-like belly scutes or plate armor**.

### Material
Baseline biological material should read matte through satin/modest sheen.

Avoid:
- wet amphibian;
- metallic;
- stone;
- plastic;
- gemstone;
- emissive

as baseline biology.

## 3D. Eyes

Preserve the deliberate Saurin identity decisions:
- vertically elliptical pupils with dilation;
- translucent nictitating membrane.

These are identity phenotypes, not evidence of superior senses, aquatic bonuses or other gameplay traits.

Dialogue/gaze readability must remain possible.

## 3E. Cranial ridges

True horns are not baseline.

Optional approved structures are low-profile **integumentary keratinous ridges/scale crests** over the approved skull.

Do not turn them into:
- dragon horns;
- antlers;
- giant sails;
- cobra hoods;
- deployable frills.

## 3F. Hands / feet / claws

The final reference should begin expressing the approved non-human extremity treatment:
- five digits provisionally;
- opposable thumb provisionally;
- moderately elongated fingers;
- broad stable plantigrade feet;
- somewhat longer forefoot/toe contribution than Marchfolk;
- modest non-retractable claw-like nails;
- fine flexible palmar/plantar contact scales with localized pad-like thickening.

Do not make mammalian paw pads.
Do not make smooth human palms/soles.
Do not infer climbing or unarmed-damage bonuses from claws.

## 3G. Saurin anti-targets

Do NOT create:
- human + scales;
- human + pasted-on muzzle;
- human + tail;
- upright monitor lizard;
- miniature dragon;
- crocodile person;
- permanent crouched lizard stance;
- savage/primitive visual coding;
- automatic aquatic specialization;
- mandatory lean reptile physique.

Broad frame and body-composition variation remains valid.

## 3H. Saurin validation renders

Provide:

8. no cultural costume needed for biological validation.

The tail must not be cropped from identity-critical views.
