# 1. Disposition

## Gorrund — ACCEPT structural sculpt

The load-path sculpt succeeds at the intended structural problem. At normalized height, Gorrund now reads much more clearly against Grask as **axial / load-bearing / torso-led** versus **rangy / reach-oriented / limb-led**.

Accepted features include:
- stronger shoulder-girdle integration;
- larger skeletal thoracic cage;
- improved thorax → lower trunk continuity;
- stronger pelvic foundation;
- improved pelvis → proximal-femur transition;
- preservation of upright posture;
- no reliance on extra muscularity, obesity, hunching, or long-arm exaggeration.

Do not continue enlarging Gorrund merely for stronger silhouette separation. The remaining pectoral/topology artifact is an art-quality cleanup item and is not a reason to reopen structural design.

**Gorrund structural visual reference is accepted.**

## Saurin — PARTIAL ACCEPT

Accept the current direction for:
- complete mandatory tail;
- improved sacral/tail-base integration;
- body silhouette;
- Regional Scale Architecture as a reference system;
- vertical pupil concept;
- recessed auricular direction;
- general hand/foot scale treatment;
- upright plantigrade locomotor foundation.

However:

**THE CURRENT SAURIN HEAD IS NOT ACCEPTED.**

Tyler's assessment, which the Author agrees with:
> The head still looks like a human face that has been morphed forward and covered in reptilian surface treatment. It does not yet immediately read as a reptilian humanoid craniofacial system.

This is broader than Claude's noted human-derived lip limitation. Treat it as a craniofacial-architecture problem, not a mouth-only cleanup.

# 2. Saurin head — required reconstruction

Do **not** solve this with another small global face morph.

The next head must be reconstructed around the approved **Layered Rostral-Cranial Integration**:

> low-to-moderate cranial vault → broad integrated orbital/temporal platform → projecting but compact rostral midface → deep articulated jaw base

The design goal is not “more monstrous.” It is **less anatomically human**.

## A. Critical diagnostic

Create the next head so it passes this test:

> If scale texture, pigmentation, vertical pupils, tail, and body were hidden, would the bare head geometry still immediately read as a non-human reptilian humanoid rather than a human skull/face stretched into a muzzle?

If the answer is no, the craniofacial architecture is not finished.

Surface treatment must reinforce identity, not carry it.

## B. Orbital-temporal platform

The current eye region retains too much human facial-mask organization.

Reconstruct the bony/soft-tissue relationships surrounding the eyes so the orbit belongs to a broad reptilian cranial platform.

Required direction:
- stronger lateral/posterior orbital integration;
- stronger temporal/postorbital contribution;
- eyes embedded within the skull architecture rather than sitting in a human brow/cheek mask;
- remove human eyebrow/brow-pad logic where it survives in topology;
- reduce the recognizable human forehead → brow → eye socket → cheek sequence;
- preserve forward-facing gaze and dialogue readability.

Do NOT solve this with:
- huge eyes;
- extreme lateral eye placement;
- permanent angry brow;
- predatory scowl;
- monster caricature.

## C. Rostrum — rebuild from the root

The rostrum currently projects, but still reads partly as a human central face stretched forward.

Rebuild it so its **root begins farther posteriorly and laterally** and participates in the orbital/zygomatic/temporal structure.

The rostrum should:
- emerge from an integrated skull platform;
- have a broad structural root;
- project clearly beyond human facial architecture;
- taper moderately;
- remain compact;
- preserve the approved rostral floor;
- have upper and lower jaw structures that belong to the same cranial system.

Avoid:
- pasted-on muzzle;
- canine/dog muzzle;
- crocodilian snout;
- dragon wedge;
- human nose stretched forward.

## D. Eliminate residual human nasal/midface language

There should be no hidden human nose-mouth-cheek system visible underneath the scales.

Remove/reconstruct:
- human nasal bridge logic;
- human alar/nasolabial organization;
- human cheek-to-mouth folds;
- human philtrum/lip organization;
- any residual “nose over lips over chin” facial hierarchy.

Nasal openings must belong naturally to the rostral structure rather than occupy a modified human nose.

## E. Mouth and oral seam

Claude correctly noted that the current lips remain human-derived. This must be addressed more aggressively.

Target:
- little-to-no human vermilion-lip read;
- longer reptilian oral seam extending farther posteriorly along the jaw;
- mouth line integrated into the rostrum/jaw structure;
- neutral closure without grin/snarl;
- enough deformable tissue for eventual speech and expression without rebuilding human lips.

Do not make the mouth a rigid slit that prevents believable dialogue.

## F. Jaw and hinge

The posterior jaw needs to carry more visual authority.

Strengthen:
- posterior mandibular depth;
- jaw hinge region;
- relationship between mandible and temporal platform;
- structural transition from rostrum to posterior jaw.

The muzzle should not be the only non-human feature. The entire chewing/jaw apparatus must feel like it belongs to the skull.

No default human chin.

## G. Frontal silhouette

The **front view is currently the weakest view** and must become a primary validation target.

From front, the next head should show non-human relationships through:
- cranial width/height;
- orbital platform;
- rostral root and taper;
- jaw breadth/depth;
- mouth geometry;
- lack of human nasal/lip/chin organization.

The front silhouette must not require scales or pupils to explain that this is Saurin.

## H. Auricular integration

The recessed opening direction is correct.

Refine it so it feels integrated into the posterolateral skull rather than like a human ear that was collapsed.

Preserve:
- recessed opening;
- shallow rim/recess;
- no pinna;
- no lobule;
- no elf point.

## I. Eyes

The current vertical-pupil concept is accepted.

Preserve:
- forward-facing readable gaze;
- vertically elliptical pupil;
- future nictitating membrane.

Do not use eye exaggeration as a shortcut for reptilian identity.

# 3. Dedicated head mesh/topology authority

**Do not preserve human head topology merely because it is convenient, already rigged, or compatible with the current generator.**

If the approved Saurin anatomy cannot be reached convincingly through the human-derived MPFB head topology, create/use a dedicated non-human head reference mesh or substantially retopologize/rebuild the head.

Project authority remains:

> **Approved Design Specification > Open Decision Register > Prototype / tooling convenience**

Character anatomy determines later technical architecture. Technical convenience does not redefine Saurin biology.

A dedicated Saurin head is an acceptable and expected solution if needed.

# 4. Saurin hands and feet — REQUIRED distal-digit revision

Tyler has made an additional visual-anatomy decision:

> **Saurin fingers and toes should terminate in visibly pointed claw-bearing anatomy that is clearly different from human digits, but not aggressively oversized or weaponized.**

The current hands/feet still read too strongly as human fingers/toes with claw-like nails added.

Revise the distal anatomy itself.

## Hands

All fingers, including the thumb, should retain useful humanoid grasping capability, but:
- distal phalanges should taper more distinctly;
- fingertip silhouette should become non-human before the keratin claw begins;
- claw should continue naturally from the tapered digit;
- claws should be short-to-moderate, pointed and clearly visible at neutral pose;
- retain five digits and opposable thumb unless later design changes it;
- preserve dexterous object handling.

Target:
> **reptilian digit terminating in a claw**, not **human fingertip wearing a pointed fingernail**.

## Feet

Apply the same principle, somewhat more robustly:
- toes taper toward claw-bearing distal phalanges;
- claws visibly continue the digit axis;
- foot remains broad and stable;
- stance remains plantigrade;
- normal footwear/equipment accommodation remains possible;
- forefoot/toe contribution may remain longer than Marchfolk.

Do not turn the foot into:
- raptor/digitigrade anatomy;
- bird talons;
- giant monster claws;
- climbing hooks.

## Claw gameplay firewall

These claws are **biological visual anatomy**, not an automatic gameplay advantage.

Do not infer:
- unarmed damage bonus;
- armor penetration;
- climbing bonus;
- traction bonus;
- intimidation;
- dexterity bonus.

Likewise, their existence must not impose arbitrary equipment penalties.

# 5. Tail ruling remains unchanged

Preserve all prior Tyler rulings:
- tail is biologically mandatory;
- no creator tail-off toggle;
- complete tail remains a primary racial identifier;
- clothing/armor may lie over, drape across, wrap, sheath or partially cover the tail;
- clothing/armor must not erase the fact that a tail is present;
- the tail must not make the player easier to hit;
- visible tail volume does not automatically enlarge gameplay hit vulnerability;
- tail is not automatically a weapon or prehensile;
- biomechanical participation does not automatically create gameplay bonuses.

The current tail-base sculpt is accepted directionally. Do not undo it while rebuilding the head.
