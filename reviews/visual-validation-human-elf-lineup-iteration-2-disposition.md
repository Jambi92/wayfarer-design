# Visual Validation — Human/Elf Reference Bodies, Iteration 2 Disposition

**Date:** October 1, 2026
**Author/Reviewer:** ChatGPT
**Human authority:** Tyler
**Status:** VISUAL REFERENCE DISPOSITION / CLAUDE ACTION
**Compared:** feminine and masculine; true-scale and normalized-height; front, profile and 3/4
**Races:** Marchfolk, Skarn, Sagekin, Fenn, Aelari, Vael

## Authority and interpretation

These images are visual-validation artifacts. They do not replace or amend the canonical race specifications.

An **ACCEPT** below means the current neutral/reference body is sufficiently aligned with the approved first-pass anatomy to serve as a visual reference for future comparative work.

It does **not** mean:
- all members of that race have this body;
- the neutral body becomes a fixed preset;
- frame/composition/sex-related variation is narrowed;
- overlapping phenotypes with other races become invalid;
- the sculpt becomes higher authority than `specs/<race>/<RACE>_V1.md`.

The canonical specs remain authoritative.

## Overall Iteration 2 finding

Iteration 2 is a substantial improvement over Iteration 1.

The normalized-height diagnostic succeeds at separating stature cues from anatomical cues. The lineup now shows a useful hierarchy:

- Marchfolk/Sagekin read as related human populations;
- Skarn remains recognizably human while carrying much greater structural robustness;
- Fenn/Aelari/Vael read as an elven family;
- the three elven populations now have clearer internal differentiation.

The masculine/feminine references generally preserve racial architecture rather than allowing sex-related morphology to substitute for racial identity.

Do not pursue maximum silhouette separation. The target is anatomically coherent population identity with legitimate overlap, not six mutually exclusive caricatures.

# 2. Skarn — ACCEPT

## What is working

The normalized-height comparison confirms that Skarn identity survives removal of the 208 cm stature cue.

The model reads through:
- broader/heavier shoulder structure;
- stronger torso construction;
- substantial neck relationship;
- heavier joint transitions;
- generally greater skeletal structural presence.

This is the correct result: Skarn read as a large, powerfully constructed human population rather than merely an enlarged Marchfolk.

The distinction also does not appear to depend solely on current muscularity, which is essential.

## Instruction

**HOLD CURRENT REFERENCE.**

Do not add muscle simply to strengthen racial identity.

Future variants must still permit:
- lower-current-muscularity Skarn;
- higher-adiposity Skarn;
- Narrow/Balanced/Broad frame variation

while retaining the approved underlying structural tendencies.

## Instruction

# 4. Fenn — MINOR REFINEMENT, THEN ACCEPT

## What is working

Iteration 2 significantly improves Fenn.

The model now reads more clearly through:
- finer structural transitions;
- gracile skeletal quality;
- increased extremity emphasis;
- stronger separation from the human references.

The normalized masculine comparison is particularly useful because the Fenn identity remains visible after stature is removed.

## Remaining refinement

Apply **one small proportional refinement only**.

Increase distal/extremity emphasis slightly—approximately a visual 5–10% push relative to the current reference, not a literal global scale instruction.

Focus on relationships such as:
- forearm contribution;
- lower-leg contribution;
- hand/finger proportional presence where the model permits;
- fine wrist/ankle transitions.

The objective is to strengthen:

> **compact-centered elven body → increasingly emphasized distal extremities**

## Do NOT

Do not:
- simply make the entire Fenn thinner;
- reduce torso mass indiscriminately;
- make Fenn fragile;
- create oversized hands/feet;
- lengthen every limb segment equally;
- turn Fenn into a shorter Aelari.

This should be a subtle refinement, not an Iteration 3 redesign.

After that small change, treat the neutral Fenn body as **ACCEPTED** unless the adjustment creates a new problem.

The current masculine/feminine sets generally preserve racial architecture successfully.

# 8. Important interpretation: silhouette overlap is allowed

Do not respond to these approvals by trying to make every race recognizable from body silhouette alone at any distance.

Some overlap is intentional.

Especially:
- Marchfolk ↔ Sagekin;
- broad Fenn ↔ narrow Vael;
- individual elven variants across the family

may approach one another in isolated body silhouette.

Complete racial identity is carried by a combination of:
- skeletal/proportional anatomy;
- craniofacial architecture;
- ear anatomy where applicable;
- surface phenotype;
- movement;
- population distributions;
- other approved biological traits.

The goal is not “every naked body must be unmistakable at 100 meters.”

The goal is:

> **No race should depend on height, skin color, clothing, hairstyle or cultural styling to compensate for anatomy that fails to express its approved specialization.**

Iteration 2 now meets that standard for these references, subject only to the minor Fenn refinement.

### Normalized-height diagnostic
- equal displayed height for visual analysis only;
- clearly labeled NON-CANONICAL / DIAGNOSTIC;
- front;
- side;
- 3/4;
- same camera/projection assumptions.

Normalized sheets must never be mistaken for canonical stature.
