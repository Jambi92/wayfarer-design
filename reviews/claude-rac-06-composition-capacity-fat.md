## 1. Does capacity need authorship now? (Grask, Gorrund, Pipkin, Cogling)

**No.** Reasons:
1. A central reference body carries **population-centre Current Muscularity**, which by definition lies below the capacity ceiling (UCCA L161). No ARM needs the ceiling's value.
2. All four specs leave capacity **OPEN with no direction** (GR L72; GO L82, L228; PK L72, L182; CG L318, L826), and two ban a downward inference (PK L182 "never lowered because Pipkin are small"; CG L320 "Fine bones do not logically require low muscular-development capacity").
3. UCCA already forbids inventing a shift (UCCA L164) and supplies a working default: a VAL ceiling from the broad first-pass range.

**Locked directionality supported by canon (restated, not new):**

| Race | Supported directionality |
|---|---|
| SK | Higher capacity (SK L26, L80) |
| SG | Never reduced by scholarly reputation (SG L101) |
| HV | Skarn ancestry may shift the distribution or upper potential, never current build (HV L447) |
| PK | **No downward shift** from small stature (PK L72, L182) |
| CG | **No downward shift** from fine bones (CG L320) |
| GR, GO | None; "never inferred from silhouette / skeletal scale" (GR L72; GO L82) |
| SA | Variation axis; no population direction (SA L358–362) |
| MF, FN, AE, VA, DU | Term absent |

**Classification:** capacity distributions GR/GO/PK/CG **D** (later biology); not a reference-mesh blocker.

**Proposed rule (A):** for MF, FN, AE, VA, DU, where the term is absent, capacity is the universal VAL ceiling with **no population shift** (UCCA L163–164). This is a pointer, not biology.

## 3. Rules confirmed (carried, not new)

- capacity ≠ current muscularity ≠ gameplay strength (UCCA L154, L165);
- fat distribution ≠ amount (MF L282);
- no stereotype physiques: never Broad → muscular → high fat; no "round halfling", "bodybuilder troll", "ogre belly" (UCCA L169; PK L186);
- **current default (not settled biology):** no race has an authored sex shift in muscle or generic fat; only Saurin authors sex centres, and they are explicitly **not** fat or muscle (SA L4220). Under UCCA L174 shifts come only from race-canon distributions; Durrim's sex-related fat patterns stay OPEN (DU L135);
- **Saurin E/B are Biological Anatomy tissue, not fat**: they persist at low fat, stack with high fat under the thoracic d/w ≤ 1.00 guard, and are never folded into the fat control (SA L385, L4234, L4247; UCCA L175).
