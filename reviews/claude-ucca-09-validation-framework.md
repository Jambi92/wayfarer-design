# UCCA-09: Whole-Character Validation Framework

**Author:** Claude (auditor / architecture analyst)
**Order:** `reviews/chatgpt-ucca-phase1-order.md` §13
**Status:** PROPOSAL for author review.

**Carry-forward rule:** every existing permanent body and race test is **carried forward unchanged and remains authoritative**. Tiers organize the tests; they replace none. Numeric thresholds stay deferred.

**Machinery:**
- UFCA_V1 §16–17 is reused: PASS / CONSTRAIN / FAIL outcomes, the N0–N4 neutralization protocol, and the equal-height, matched-scale and population-sample comparison conditions.
- **Body extension of N-levels (proposed):**
  - **N2** includes neutral body hair, no clothing (or a neutral fitted proxy), and neutral body language.
  - **N3** includes uniform pigment and pattern (Saurin: uniform pigment, **tail present**; SA L614).
  - **N-Head** (body tests) neutralizes the head (GR L78; GO L86; COG-FACE-15).

### H. Stature / proportion validity

**Question:** do min, ref and max stature, combined with frame and composition extremes, stay valid **without uniform scaling**?

**Carried forward:** the UCCA-04 §7 list, plus:
- the Saurin stature accounting (SAU-FACE-22);
- the head-to-stature anti-juvenile validators;
- a **proposed "no-uniform-scale" test**: two statures of one character must not be related by a single scale factor across head, hands, feet and joints (GO L224; CG L888; PR L29).

### I. Frame / composition independence

**Question:** does each frame × composition combination hold, and does frame never move composition (or the reverse)?

**Carried forward:** MF frame test (L250); DU same-skeleton composition and same-composition frame (L146–147); SAU-BODY-06; SAU-CC-09; every race's N/B/B × low/high muscle/fat cast.

**Proposed universal:**
- the full **frame × sex-related anatomy × composition** factorial at reference stature per race. This is the Manny/Quinn supersession test (UCCA-05 §5);
- an **Athletic write test**: applying Athletic changes no Slot 3 value (MF L281).

### J. Surface / presentation separation

**Question:** do surface layers and presentation never alter anatomy, and does anatomy never require them?

**Carried forward:** SG cross-cultural (L391–398); FN-35, 36; AE-44; VA cross-cultural (L443, L523); GR/GO/PK/CG/SA presentation-stripped and surface-swap tests (SR-COMP-09; COG-CC-08; SAU-CC-04); SAU-SURF-21 (pattern ≠ paint); SAU-CC-20 (lighting).

**Proposed:** a **Body Language test**: every body-language preset leaves Anatomical Resting Alignment unchanged (PIP-MOVE-23 is the model).

### K. Whole-character integration with UFCA

**Questions:**
- One age driver: does face age = body age?
- One strength setting: are face and body randomized consistently?
- Do face-only and body-only selective randomization preserve the other side?
- Do head-to-stature and head-neck balance hold with face extremes?
- Do UFCA hair and display slots stay consistent with body hair and Saurin body display continuation?

**Carried forward:** COG-CC-04 / 05; SAU-CC-05; SAU-FACE-21 / 22; PIP-INT-04; HV-05 / 06.
