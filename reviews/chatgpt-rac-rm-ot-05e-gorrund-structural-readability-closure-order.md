# RAC RM-OT-05E — Gorrund structural-readability closure + conditional Stage-C order

## Authority / author rulings

This order follows author review of:

- `reviews/claude-rac-rm-ot-05d-true-skeletal-envelope-race-anatomy-gate.md`
- evidence in `reviews/rac-rmot05d-evidence/`

The 05D implementation corrections are accepted as the working technical basis:

- true skeletal floor instead of treating MPFB `-LEAN` donor soft tissue as literal bone;
- verified outward normal convention;
- skeleton-anchored anatomical measurement stations;
- composition-volume restoration.

RM-OT-05 remains OPEN. Nothing is promoted yet. D4 / Wave 3 / UE5 implementation remain blocked.

### Author ruling 1 — Grask E3

**ACCEPT Grask E3 as the developmental race-anatomy direction for Stage C.**

This acceptance is specifically for the anatomical organization demonstrated in 05D:

- forward / high upper-chest and scapular organization;
- distinct shoulder-to-trunk integration;
- keel / thoracic organization;
- high calf belly + long tendon-cord organization;
- non-human limb-section and back-profile read;
- the other 05D E3 features required to preserve its robust species readability.

However, the following are **not** accepted Grask traits and must not be introduced literally:

- digitigrade feet or digitigrade joint geometry;
- altered knee / ankle joint order;
- crouched or forced posture;
- a pathological or caricatured hunch;
- a pose trick that creates the race read.

The accepted skeleton and R-6 posture remain unchanged.

If later full-view validation shows that the soft-tissue organization creates an excessively literal "digitigrade" or "hunched" visual read, tune only the responsible tissue organization enough to remove that false implication **without collapsing Grask back into ordinary human-family readability**.

Do not reopen Grask candidate searching in 05E unless a real validator failure forces a narrowly scoped correction.

### Author ruling 2 — GR-P2b measurement semantics

**ACCEPT the 05D skeleton-protected interpretation.**

GR-P2b and related protected race relationships are to be evaluated on the accepted skeleton / anatomical envelope at skeleton-anchored stations.

The organized skin surface remains a **diagnostic surface reading**, not the canonical definition of the protected skeletal relationship.

Do not restore human donor soft tissue merely to make the organized-surface ratio reproduce an old legacy skin measurement.

### Author ruling 3 — Gorrund

Gorrund remains **CONSTRAINED / NOT ACCEPTED**.

Do not promote E1 or E2.

The next step is one final **Gorrund-only local structural bracket** aimed at eliminating the "extreme bulk human" alternative explanation.

If this bracket cannot produce a robust species read while preserving canon, stop and recommend escalation to an external hand-sculpted Gorrund base mesh.

---

## 1. Core objective

Produce a Gorrund body whose species identity survives height normalization and low composition because of **anatomical organization**, not because it is merely:

- bigger;
- wider;
- fatter;
- more muscular;
- heavier;
- more barrel-chested;
- or an extreme human body type.

The required read is:

> **A distinct upright, plantigrade humanoid race whose body architecture is recognizably Gorrund before face, ears, skin, hair, clothing, texture, or stature are available.**

This order is deliberately narrow. Do not reopen general race design.

---

## 2. Locked canon and technical constraints

Keep all prior locked constraints, including:

- upright humanoid plan;
- two arms / two legs;
- five digits;
- human-compatible hands and plantigrade feet;
- normal joint sequence and joint centres;
- accepted long-bone axes and bone-scale relationships;
- accepted Gorrund stature route and range;
- accepted Gorrund deep-pelvis / load-path relationships;
- frame vs composition separation;
- no uniform whole-body scaling;
- no forced stance or posture trick;
- no identity by fatness or muscle amount;
- no canon rewrite to fit the generator.

The 05D true skeletal floor remains the hard anatomical floor.

The 05D skeleton-station measurement method remains authoritative.

Do not regress to the old `-LEAN` soft-tissue guard.

---

## 3. Gorrund developmental baseline

Use **Gorrund E1** as the primary 05E structural baseline because it already carries the posterior-column idea without depending as strongly on extra mass.

Keep **Gorrund E2** as a provenance / comparison control only.

Do not simply add more of E2's dorsal mass.

05E must search for **organization cues that remain legible when bulk is reduced**.

Candidate count must stay tight:

- baseline E1;
- no more than **three** new local candidates;
- retain only candidates that pass minimal technical guards before rendering the full gate.

---

## 4. Required anatomy investigation

The local bracket must concentrate on **load-path and joint-transition architecture**.

The intent is not to make Gorrund "monster-like." It is to make the same accepted skeleton carry a race-specific soft-tissue / sectional organization.

### 4.1 Knee / shank / ankle

Investigate canon-valid organization around the same accepted bones that can create a Gorrund load-bearing read, such as:

- broader or more architectural periarticular tissue pads where allowed by the true floor;
- a deliberate transition from thick proximal column to narrower distal support;
- race-specific calf / tendon distribution that is not just "more muscle";
- ankle transition that reads as load-bearing and columnar while remaining plantigrade;
- section-shape changes around the shank rather than simple circumference increase.

Do not alter joint centres, tibia/fibula axes, foot plantigrade geometry, or toe count.

### 4.2 Elbow / forearm / wrist

Explore analogous upper-limb cues:

- a structurally abrupt elbow-to-forearm transition;
- race-specific forearm section shape;
- load-column continuity through wrist;
- tissue organization that makes the arm read as Gorrund at low composition.

Hands remain five-digit and human-compatible.

Do not solve the race read by enlarging hands.

### 4.3 Posterior axial load path

Strengthen the **upper back -> thoracolumbar region -> sacrum -> posterior hip -> hamstring** continuity as a structural path, not as a fat pad or bodybuilder back.

The read should survive low composition.

Preferred cues are:

- section geometry;
- continuity / interruption patterns;
- posterior plane organization;
- sacral transition;
- controlled thoracolumbar depth distribution;
- relation of axial mass to limb columns.

Avoid a single exaggerated hump, buttock, or spinal ridge.

### 4.4 Pelvis -> thigh transition

Retain the accepted deep-pelvis canon, but make the skin/tissue organization less ordinary-human.

The transition may be more load-column-like or abrupt where the true floor permits.

Do not recreate a human gluteal apex by habit, and do not simply inflate the buttock.

### 4.5 Trunk section

The Gorrund trunk may remain deep and slab-like, but **depth alone is not sufficient**.

Look for section-shape organization that supports the race read at low composition:

- anterior/posterior distribution;
- flank / lumbar organization;
- thoracic-to-pelvic continuity;
- less ordinary-human waist / abdominal contour.

Do not increase total composition amount to obtain the effect.

## 5. Composition firewall

Every retained 05E candidate must be shown at minimum in:

- reference composition;
- low composition;
- Narrow frame;
- Broad frame.

The defining Gorrund cues must persist in low composition.

A candidate fails if the species read disappears when muscle/fat amount is reduced.

A candidate also fails if its "distinct" read can reasonably be explained primarily as obesity, muscular hypertrophy, or simple body width.

Keep surface-volume accounting explicit and preserve the route's composition amount as in 05D.

## 7. Replicated blind-read gate

The 05D context effect must be explicitly tested, not avoided.

Every blind run must include **Grask E3** so Gorrund is judged in the presence of the stronger non-human humanoid reference that caused the 05D run-2 collapse.

### Protocol

Use:

- body only;
- head / neck removed;
- height-normalized;
- neutral grey and black silhouette;
- front + profile as the minimum paired views;
- shuffled coded identities;
- fresh uninformed scorers.

Classification remains:

A. ordinary human-family body variation  
B. unusual / extreme human-family body variation  
C. distinct humanoid race/species anatomy

Require each scorer to state:

- classification;
- confidence;
- features driving the choice;
- whether the read is mainly due to bulk / fatness / muscularity;
- whether the body forms a stable group;
- whether Grask and Gorrund can be distinguished without labels.

### Replication requirement

Run at least **two independent replicated blind runs**, each with **3 fresh uninformed scorers**.

A Gorrund candidate may clear the focused gate only if **all** of the following are true:

1. Reference composition receives **C from at least 2 / 3 scorers in each replicated run**.
2. Low composition receives **C from at least 2 / 3 scorers in each replicated run**.
3. It forms a stable Gorrund cluster separate from Marchfolk and Skarn.
4. It remains distinguishable from Grask E3.
5. The majority explanation is **anatomical organization**, not bulk, fatness, muscularity, stature, or a single caricatured feature.
6. At least one cited cue is **axial / load-path organization** and at least one is **limb / joint-transition organization**.
7. No systematic human-control collapse is required for the cluster to exist.

A pass in one context and failure in another is still a failure.

If no candidate meets this gate, **STOP** and return a clear recommendation for an external hand-sculpted Gorrund base mesh. Do not keep iterating indefinitely.

## 8. Minimal technical guards

Before blind testing, each retained body must pass:

- exact stature target;
- ground contact;
- no uniform scaling;
- accepted joint centres unchanged;
- accepted bone scales / long-bone axes unchanged;
- true skeletal floor respected;
- no posture trick;
- plantigrade foot preserved;
- hand / foot topology and digit count preserved;
- gross self-intersection no worse than the accepted route by the established tolerance;
- arm / trunk clearance;
- composition ordering preserved;
- frame ordering preserved;
- protected Gorrund skeletal relations unchanged under the accepted 05D measurement semantics;
- surface-volume amount restored / reported as in 05D.

Also report whether any new cue depends on a region whose floor is still only an unresolved skeleton proxy rather than a real bone mesh.

- frame extremes;

- protected anatomical relations at skeleton-anchored stations;
- body-clearance / intersection guards;

- any earlier UFCA / MPFB / ALPC guards that remain authoritative.

Resolve only real implementation regressions.

Do not reintroduce donor-human soft tissue merely to satisfy superseded own-skin measurement behavior.

### 9.3 Full-roster read

Rerun a full-roster matched-scale body lineup after the Gorrund winner and Grask E3 are carried through validation.

The purpose is to ensure:

- Grask and Gorrund remain individually readable;
- neither collapses into Skarn / Marchfolk at matched height;
- neither depends on scale alone;
- Saurin remains coherent with the same roster;
- the overall 13-race body family has believable variation without accidental duplication.

### 9.4 Author acceptance package

If the full suite passes, prepare an author package containing:

- final proposed Grask E3 validation sheets;

- reference + low composition + Narrow + Broad;

**Do not self-promote. Stop for author review.**

## 10. Failure path

If the final 05E Gorrund bracket fails the replicated gate:

1. keep Grask E3 as the accepted developmental direction, still awaiting full Stage-C validation;
2. leave Gorrund unpromoted;
3. keep RM-OT-05 OPEN;
4. state that the procedural generator has been exhausted for Gorrund body readability under current canon;
5. recommend an **external hand-sculpted Gorrund base mesh** constrained to the accepted skeleton, stature route, plantigrade feet, five digits and protected proportions;
6. specify exactly which anatomical regions the sculpt must change relative to the current generated body;
7. do not rewrite canon;
8. do not start D4 or UE5 implementation.
