## Overall judgment

Targeted Sculpt 2 is a meaningful improvement and establishes the correct technical foundation: the Saurin now use a dedicated non-human head rather than a morphed human head. **Do not return to the human-derived head and do not restart from Targeted Sculpt 1.**

However, Targeted Sculpt 2 is **not accepted as final visual anatomy**. The current skull has moved away from "human face morphed reptilian," but now reads too much like a simplified/stylized gecko or lizard mascot. The goal is neither extreme.

### Governing visual target

> **The Saurin should look believable standing next to a photorealistic human—not like a human wearing reptilian prosthetics, and not like a cartoon lizard enlarged into human proportions.**

The Saurin must remain unmistakably reptilian from bare geometry alone, but the skull must possess convincing adult anatomical construction, layered planes, functional jaw architecture, and coherent transitions into the neck.

## HOLD / DO NOT REOPEN

Preserve these Targeted Sculpt 2 decisions:
- dedicated Saurin head topology; no human head topology;
- Layered Rostral-Cranial Integration as the governing architecture;
- no human external nose, nasal bridge, alar forms, philtrum, vermilion lips, chin, or projecting pinnae;
- compact projecting rostrum rather than a human face;
- oral seam extending substantially farther posteriorly than a human mouth;
- recessed auricular opening;
- forward-readable gaze;
- vertical pupils;
- Regional Scale Architecture;
- accepted Saurin body architecture;
- accepted sacral/tail-base integration;
- complete mandatory tail;
- five fingers with opposable thumb;
- five toes and plantigrade stance unless an existing canonical spec says otherwise;
- claws carry no automatic gameplay advantage.

Do not alter Gorrund or any other race.

# A. Head refinement — highest priority

## A1. Orbits and eyes

The current eyes are too exposed and appear perched on the dorsolateral corners of the skull. This contributes strongly to the gecko/mascot read.

Refine the **skeletal housing around the eyes**, not merely the eyeballs:
- seat the eyeballs more convincingly inside developed orbital anatomy;
- strengthen supraorbital and postorbital structural support;
- create clearer transition from cranial vault/temporal platform into the orbital region;
- retain readable forward gaze and approved modest lateral spacing;
- retain vertical pupils;
- avoid giant or juvenile-looking eyes;
- avoid a permanent angry brow/scowl;
- avoid simply shrinking the eyes while leaving crude orbital mounds;
- the front and 3/4 views must read as a coherent reptilian skull surrounding the eye, not eyeballs attached to a blockout.

The orbit should contribute to **Layered Rostral-Cranial Integration**: cranial vault → temporal/postorbital platform → orbit → rostral root should be a continuous anatomical system.

## A2. Rostrum

Keep a genuine projecting rostrum, but revise its current bulbous/blunt presentation.

Required:
- reduce the rounded/bulbous snout-tip appearance;
- establish more convincing planar transitions from orbital platform into rostral root;
- allow subtle dorsal, lateral, and ventrolateral rostral planes;
- preserve a broad integrated root and moderate taper;
- nasal openings remain openings in the rostral structure, not a modified human nose;
- preserve the canonical minimum rostral projection floor.

Avoid:
- human nose + muzzle;
- dog/canine muzzle;
- crocodile-long snout;
- dragon wedge;
- turtle/beak morphology;
- perfectly rectangular box snout;
- soft bulbous gecko muzzle.

The rostrum should look structurally inevitable from the skull behind it.

## A3. Jaw and oral seam

The posterior extent of the Sculpt 2 mouth is correct in principle. Preserve it.

The current seam, however, is too straight and diagrammatic. Refine it into functional anatomy:
- introduce subtle anatomically plausible curvature and changes in thickness along the oral margin;
- strengthen the posterior mandibular body/ramus/hinge read beneath the temporal platform;
- make the lower jaw clearly function as a substantial articulated structure rather than the bottom half of a shape cut by a ruler line;
- preserve absence of a human chin;
- preserve absence of human lips;
- closed neutral mouth must remain possible;
- do not force a grin, snarl, frown, or predatory expression;
- retain future dialogue/speech deformation feasibility.

Do not add exaggerated exposed teeth merely to signal "reptile." Inner mouth/dentition can remain unresolved for this visual pass if necessary.

## A4. Cranial roof and posterior skull

The present flat-topped/blockout quality is too simplified.

Refine:
- cranial vault should remain low-to-moderate relative to humans, but not appear like a flat cap;
- establish organic dorsal curvature and/or subtle planes appropriate to the approved long front-to-back skull;
- strengthen temporal/postorbital volume without creating a mammalian temple;
- ensure occipital/posterior skull volume transitions coherently into the neck;
- maintain adult proportions and avoid inflated braincase/juvenile cues.

## A5. Neck, throat and skull transition

The current near-cylindrical thick neck weakens the result.

Refine the integration among:
- posterior skull;
- jaw musculature/hinge region;
- throat;
- cervical column;
- upper torso.

The neck should support the projecting head biomechanically without reading as a simple tube. Preserve upright posture. Do not introduce a permanent hunch or forward-head monster posture.

The final silhouette should communicate that the head and neck evolved as one organism.

# B. Hands and claws — retain with minor refinement

Targeted Sculpt 2's tapered distal phalanges and claws are a successful direction and should be retained.

The defining principle remains:

> The digit itself tapers toward a pointed distal phalanx, and the claw continues the digit's anatomical axis rather than appearing as a human nail attached to a human fingertip.

For Sculpt 3:
- retain pointed/tapered fingers and thumb;
- retain short-to-moderate natural claws;
- preserve grasp capability and opposable thumb;
- consider reducing visible hand-claw length approximately **10–20% from Sculpt 2** if necessary to avoid combat-talon appearance;
- retain modest downward curvature;
- no oversized raptor talons;
- no gameplay inference.

Do not regress to rounded human fingertips.

# C. Feet — additional anatomical refinement

The toes/claws are improved, but the side-view foot still reads too much like a human foot with reptilian distal additions.

Refine while remaining plantigrade:
- preserve broad stable foot;
- preserve longer forefoot contribution than Marchfolk;
- make toe segmentation and distal taper more visibly structural;
- integrate the toe bases/forefoot into a Saurin-specific foot architecture rather than a human foot terminating in claws;
- maintain credible plantar contact surface and weight-bearing;
- claws continue toe axes;
- avoid digitigrade anatomy;
- avoid bird/raptor feet;
- avoid giant talons;
- avoid webbing unless separately approved later.

The bare foot should be visibly non-human even if claws and scales are visually deemphasized.

# D. Photorealistic anatomy test

This pass must be judged as if these creatures will stand next to realistic human characters in a modern high-fidelity UE5 game.

Do **not** solve racial identity by exaggeration alone.

A successful Saurin head should simultaneously satisfy:
1. unmistakably non-human/reptilian in bare geometry;
2. adult rather than juvenile/cute;
3. biologically coherent rather than assembled from themed features;
4. sophisticated enough for close dialogue shots;
5. capable of neutral expression without looking emotionless or permanently hostile;
6. not dependent on scales, pupils, coloration, tail, horns, or dramatic lighting to communicate race;
7. compatible with the approved rostral, orbital, mandibular and auricular specification;
8. credible beside photorealistic Marchfolk rather than stylistically belonging to a different game.
