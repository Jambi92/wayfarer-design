# Visual Validation Notes — Human and Elf Reference Lineup, Iteration 1

**Date:** October 1, 2026
**Reviewer:** ChatGPT (Author), at Tyler's request
**Status:** REVISION REQUEST FOR VISUAL REFERENCE MODELS
**Scope:** Marchfolk, Skarn, Sagekin, Fenn, Aelari, Vael — masculine and feminine reference bodies
**Authority:** These notes do NOT amend the canonical race specifications. The approved specs remain authoritative. The models are visual-validation artifacts and must move toward the specs, not redefine them.

## Overall finding

The lineup is useful and the height relationships read clearly. However, **height differentiation is currently stronger than anatomical differentiation**.

If labels were removed and the figures normalized to the same standing height, several bodies—especially among the three elven populations—would still read too strongly as variations of a common humanoid template.

The next sculpt pass should focus on **skeletal/proportional relationships rather than surface detail or added muscularity**.

## Marchfolk

**Direction: HOLD as baseline.**

The Marchfolk model is functioning well as the broad-spectrum human reference against which the other populations can be compared.

Do not exaggerate it merely to create contrast. Its value is as the neutral human anatomical reference.

## Skarn

**Direction: MOSTLY HOLD; modestly reinforce structural robustness if needed.**

The current model successfully reads as larger and more substantially constructed than Marchfolk rather than merely taller.

For the next pass:
- preserve the larger overall scale;
- preserve broad shoulder/torso relationships;
- preserve heavier limb/joint presence;
- if distinction needs strengthening, do it through **skeletal/joint robustness and torso construction**, not simply additional muscle mass;
- do not make muscularity mandatory.

The approved Skarn identity remains large-scale powerful human anatomy, not a uniformly bodybuilder physique.

## Sagekin

**Direction: MOSTLY HOLD.**

The current subtle distinction from Marchfolk is appropriate because Sagekin remain fully human.

Preserve:
- somewhat more linear overall construction;
- the tendency toward longer-limbed proportions;
- subtle narrower-ribcage tendency.

Do not exaggerate the Sagekin into a fantasy-gracile body type. Broad, muscular and heavier Sagekin must remain valid within the approved range.

## Fenn

**Direction: STRENGTHEN EXTREMITY-EMPHASIZED GRACILITY.**

The current Fenn is moving in the correct direction but does not yet express the approved skeletal specialization strongly enough when compared at normalized height.

Next sculpt pass should strengthen:
- gracile skeletal transitions;
- comparatively fine joint relationships;
- limb/extremity emphasis;
- long hand/finger relationships where visible;
- the relationship between a compact-centered torso and more emphasized extremities.

Do **not** solve this by simply making the entire body thinner or frailer.

Desired read:
> a distinct elven skeletal architecture whose gracility and proportional emphasis become especially apparent through the limbs and extremities.

## Aelari

**Direction: MOSTLY HOLD; VERIFY DISTRIBUTED ELONGATION.**

Aelari currently reads most successfully of the three elven models.

Preserve the tall, vertically distributed silhouette.

In the next pass, verify that the 190 cm stature is produced through coordinated elongation across:
- cranial/neck relationship;
- torso;
- waist transition;
- limbs;

rather than being achieved primarily through longer legs.

The longer neck/torso/waist contribution is important to distinguishing Aelari from Fenn.

Do not make Aelari simply a taller Fenn.

## Vael

**Direction: PRIORITY REVISION.**

Vael currently does not express the approved population identity strongly enough in the frontal lineup and reads too close to a moderate-height gracile humanoid/elf.

Strengthen:
- thoracic depth;
- central-body structural presence;
- compact/deeper-bodied continuity;
- wrist and ankle transition presence;
- moderate joint/base presence;
- integration between torso, pelvis and limbs.

Do **not** turn Vael into:
- a broad dwarf-like body;
- a muscular Skarn-like body;
- a short Aelari;
- a dark-skinned version of another elf.

Desired read:
> the deepest and most structurally continuous of the three elven body architectures, while remaining unmistakably elven.

A side view is especially important because much of Vael's approved distinction is **depth**, which cannot be adequately judged from a frontal silhouette alone.

## Masculine/feminine consistency

The current lineup is encouraging in that masculine and feminine models generally retain their underlying racial relationships.

Continue enforcing:
- sex-related anatomy must not replace racial anatomy;
- masculine must not automatically equal the broad/robust version of a race;
- feminine must not automatically equal the narrow/gracile version;
- overlapping adult body phenotypes remain valid;
- each race's structural identity must survive across sex-related variation.

## Required next visual-validation render

Before these six are treated as reliable visual references, produce a second iteration with:

1. **Front view**
2. **True side/profile view**
3. **Three-quarter view**
4. Identical camera/lens assumptions across all races
5. Identical ground plane and scale treatment
6. Standing-height labels
7. Neutral posture
8. Comparable neutral/reference frame and composition

Avoid perspective/camera differences that artificially change proportions.

The side view is required to judge:
- thoracic depth;
- pelvic depth;
- cranial projection;
- spinal/axial relationships;
- Vael's deeper central architecture;
- whether Aelari elongation is genuinely distributed.

## Normalized-height diagnostic

In addition to the true-scale lineup, produce a **normalized-height comparison** in which all six reference models are temporarily displayed at the same image height for visual analysis only.

This does NOT alter canonical stature.

Purpose:
- remove height as an identity cue;
- test whether skeletal/proportional identity remains readable;
- identify models that currently rely too heavily on stature.

Priority comparisons:
- Marchfolk vs Sagekin;
- Marchfolk vs Skarn;
- Fenn vs Aelari;
- Fenn vs Vael;
- Aelari vs Vael.

## Acceptance principle

The goal is not maximum visual difference.

The goal is:

> **Each model should express its approved anatomical specialization clearly enough that height, skin color, clothing, hairstyle and cultural styling are not doing the work that skeletal anatomy is supposed to do.**

Marchfolk/Sagekin may remain comparatively subtle because both are human populations. The three elven populations require stronger skeletal-family differentiation from humans and clearer internal differentiation from one another.

No canonical spec changes are authorized by this note. If a future sculpt reveals that an approved specification itself is inadequate, that issue should be raised separately for Tyler's decision.
