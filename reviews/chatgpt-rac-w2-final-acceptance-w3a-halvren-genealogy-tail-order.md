Do not invent a magnitude merely to force asymmetry.

A tail Halvren must remain Halvren against the source population whose stature it approaches.

Permanent tests:

- **HV-49 vs matched-stature Marchfolk**;
- **HV-50 vs matched-stature Skarn**;
- **HV-50 vs matched-stature Aelari where Aelari has a valid adult at that height**;

all under the existing Halvren N3/N4 source-protection rules.

- the nearest valid Aelari endpoint may be used only as an **endpoint source-family diagnostic**;
- label it clearly as endpoint evidence, never as matched height;
- do not extrapolate Aelari anatomy beyond its valid range.

## H-4 — mixed development

Tail stature is valid only if the **whole Halvren body develops coherently at that stature**.

No:

- source height pasted onto another source's proportions;
- uniform scaling;
- parental averaging;
- one-dimensional height extension;
- copied Marchfolk / Skarn / Aelari body with Halvren ears.

## H-6 — minority outcome

Tail statures are minority outcomes relative to the 152–213 cm central envelope.

No ordering between the **upper-tail frequency** and **lower-tail frequency** is pre-authored.
