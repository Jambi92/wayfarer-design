# 3. REBUILD PRINCIPLE

The previous probe search demonstrated that re-proportioning and re-thickening the MPFB human surface cannot satisfy the canon.

Therefore:

- it is explicitly authorized to go **beyond the current MPFB human surface anatomy** for Grask and Gorrund;
- the rebuild may sculpt / reconstruct race-specific body architecture in the girdles, pelvis, axial chain, joint architecture and limb transitions;
- the accepted W2 numeric relationships and canon act as constraints, not as a requirement to preserve the old vertex realization;
- do not use head, face, ears, pigmentation, texture, hair, pose, clothing or muscle exaggeration to carry the race identity.

The decisive proof is body-only architecture.

Comparable precedent: the Saurin W2I reference rebuild, but do not copy Saurin anatomy.

---

# 4. GRASK — REQUIRED ARCHITECTURAL READ

The final Grask body must read as a **distinct rangy non-human upright humanoid**, not a stretched human.

Preserve the existing Grask canon, including:

- 198–239 cm accepted stature envelope;
- reach-dominant / limb-dominant silhouette;
- lower torso contribution relative to limbs;
- strong lower-leg and forearm contribution;
- GR-G2 vertically extended, close-seated scapular organization;
- GR-P2b / P3 long-lever hip / pelvis organization;
- large integrated hands and feet in absolute terms;
- coherent long-lever mechanics;
- upright tool-user plausibility;
- no hunch, digitigrade conversion, ape arms, giant-human shortcut or stick-thin caricature.

## 4.1 Architecture that must become surface-legible

At minimum, explore race-specific geometry for:

- scapular bed / shoulder origin and thorax-to-humerus transition;
- clavicular / upper-thorax relationship if already supported by canon;
- pelvis / acetabular organization and the visible hip-to-femur transition;
- femur / lower-leg relationship as one coherent long-lever system;
- forearm / elbow transition;
- hand and foot integration / absolute presence;
- waist / flank transition so the body does not simply read as a human scaled lengthwise.

Do not add unsupported anatomical novelties.

---

# 5. GORRUND — REQUIRED ARCHITECTURAL READ

The final Gorrund body must read as a **distinct axial / load-bearing non-human upright humanoid**, not a broad or heavy human.

Preserve the existing Gorrund canon, including:

- 208–251 cm current accepted world-validation envelope and its qualifiers;
- deep / broad load-bearing thorax;
- Axial Load-Path Continuity (ALPC);
- strong thorax → lower trunk → pelvis continuity;
- substantial proximal limb architecture;
- visibly substantial joints;
- high absolute skeletal structural scale;
- integrated larger hands / feet where canon supports them;
- upright tool-user plausibility;
- no ogre, barrel, obesity, permanent-bodybuilder, hunch or "bigger Skarn" shortcut.

## 5.1 Architecture that must become surface-legible

At minimum, explore race-specific geometry for:

- thoracic cage form and depth / breadth organization;
- shoulder-to-thorax integration;
- lower-trunk load path into pelvis;
- pelvis / proximal femur organization;
- knee / ankle / elbow / wrist structural shape, not just circumference;
- long-bone cross-section and transitions;
- hand / foot structural integration;
- sagittal profile so the axial chain reads as load-bearing without becoming barrel-shaped.

Do not add unsupported anatomical novelties.
