# 2. AD-W3A-1 — AUTHOR RULING: ACCEPT SOURCE-CONDITIONED LEG-SEGMENT DEVELOPMENT

**RULING: YES, WITH A BOUNDED DEVELOPMENTAL COUPLING RULE.**

A source-supported upper-tail Halvren may inherit the stature-relevant leg-segment development of the source that actually supports that exceptional stature.

This is biologically required by the existing Halvren mixed-development principle:

> If a source contributes enough developmental architecture to support an exceptional stature, the Halvren body may also express the source-linked segment-development relationship needed to make that stature coherent.

This is **not** permission to copy the source race's leg wholesale.

This is **not** permission to convert Halvren into Skarn or Aelari morphology.

This is **not** an ancestry slider.

This is **not** permission to canonize the W3A probe's arbitrary half-strength value.

## 2.1 Required implementation in the diagnostic model

Replace the W3A "probe only" treatment with a proper hidden Halvren developmental dependency.

The dependency must:

- operate only when the relevant source genuinely supports the upper-tail stature under H-1;
- remain subordinate to Halvren complete-body identity;
- preserve source protection;
- preserve frame independence;
- preserve composition independence;
- vary continuously;
- introduce no visible anatomical pop;
- use only as much source-linked leg development as is required for coherent Halvren development;
- never automatically drive a Halvren to the source race's own exact segment proportions;
- never become a player-facing ancestry percentage.

Do **not** hard-code "0.5 strength" simply because the W3A probe used it.

Resolve the smallest coherent continuous coupling rule supported by the accepted anatomy.

## 2.2 Skarn-supported upper tail

Skarn support may carry the accepted Skarn stature-relevant lower-limb development, including the short-femur / corresponding lower-leg relationship, into a Halvren upper-tail body where needed.

Target question:

> Can a Skarn-supported Halvren remain a coherent Halvren through the demonstrated upper tail while the coupling is resolved continuously rather than as a one-off probe?

The W3A evidence demonstrates a coherent candidate at **228.8 cm**.

Treat **228.8 cm as demonstrated reachable evidence**, not as a universal Halvren maximum and not as automatic reach for every Skarn-supported Halvren.

Preserve H-5:

> valid Halvren tail stature remains strictly below 229 cm.

Do not convert 229 cm into a valid endpoint.

## 2.3 Aelari-supported upper tail

Aelari support may carry the accepted Aelari thigh / calf developmental relation where needed for a coherent Aelari-supported Halvren.

However:

- Aelari source support remains capped by the valid Aelari adult range;
- Aelari alone does not support Halvren stature above **221 cm**;
- do not extrapolate Aelari biology above 221 cm;
- the W3A 220.8 cm probe is valid evidence that the coupling can solve the lower-leg failure near the Aelari ceiling.

For an Aelari-supported Halvren, the upper source-supported reach may approach the source ceiling, but the closure must determine the demonstrated valid Halvren endpoint inside that cap under the new coupling rule.

## 2.4 Combined Skarn + Aelari conditions

Above the Aelari adult range:

- **Skarn must be the stature-supporting source**;
- Aelari may still contribute valid mixed anatomy;
- Aelari must be treated as endpoint / source-family evidence only under D1;
- do not claim Aelari supplies stature support beyond 221 cm.

Combined-source bodies must still read as Halvren complete anatomy.
