Build a production-ready Grask whose **base body shape alone** reads immediately as:

> **a tall, grounded, troll-like humanoid with orc-adjacent ruggedness, substantial skeletal mass, long functional limbs, and a primitive powerful silhouette — not human, not alien, not bodybuilder, and not a generic fantasy orc.**

The result must work with:
- neutral gray material;
- bald head;
- no skin color;
- no texture detail;
- no scars;
- no armor;
- no culture cues;
- minimal modesty covering only.

The shape itself must carry the identity.

## 4. Critical difference from v003

v003 is still too vertically narrow and too elegant in its body flow.

The target image shows a creature that is:
- tall but **not stick-like**;
- rangy but **not narrow-bodied**;
- powerful without bodybuilding exaggeration;
- broad enough through the clavicle/ribcage to feel grounded;
- thick enough through neck, upper torso, forearms, thighs and calves to support the stature;
- long-limbed without spidery proportions;
- large-handed and large-footed without alien elongation;
- upright and balanced, not hunched.

v004 must make this macro correction first.

## 5. Macro silhouette — highest priority

### 5.1 Torso width and mass

The target is **not** a narrow/deep alien trunk.

Build:
- a moderately broad clavicular/upper-ribcage frame;
- a solid ribcage with clear lateral mass;
- a torso that narrows only moderately toward the waist;
- visible abdominal volume and structural weight without obesity;
- enough back/lat/scapular mass to make the body feel three-dimensional and powerful.

Avoid:
- pinched waist;
- thin vertical cylinder torso;
- superhero V-taper;
- barrel-chested bodybuilder;
- broad Skarn proportions.

The torso should read as a **lean but heavy-framed troll**, not a lean human.

### 5.2 Shoulder / neck relationship

The target image carries much of its identity through the neck-to-shoulder transition.

Required:
- thicker, shorter-looking neck integration;
- strong trapezius transition;
- shoulders that feel structurally wide and load-bearing;
- deltoid/upper-arm attachment that looks powerful but natural.

Do not:
- make the neck long or pencil-thin;
- create wrestler/bodybuilder traps;
- create a hunch.

### 5.3 Pelvis / hips

Keep the pelvis narrower than the upper torso, but not tiny.

Required:
- grounded pelvic block;
- believable hip socket placement;
- enough gluteal/upper-thigh mass to support long legs;
- no fashion-model hip taper.

## 6. Arms and hands

The target has long arms, but they are **substantial**, especially through upper arm and forearm.

### Arms
- retain Grask reach;
- increase perceived structural thickness where needed;
- preserve long forearm contribution;
- avoid skinny tube forearms;
- avoid bodybuilder muscle separation.

### Hands
The target hands are large, heavy and functional.

Build:
- broader palm mass;
- thick, functional fingers;
- pronounced but natural knuckle structure;
- fingers that are long enough for Grask identity but not spidery;
- thumb base that feels load-bearing and powerful.

Do not:
- use delicate human hands;
- use alien fingers;
- add claws;
- change digit count.

The hand should read as **troll hand**, not “large human hand” and not “alien hand.”

## 7. Legs and feet

The target lower body is tall but strongly planted.

### Thighs
- substantial upper-thigh mass;
- avoid narrow femur-cylinder appearance;
- preserve long-leg architecture.

### Knees
- clear structural joint;
- not oversized knobs.

### Calves / shins
The target has visibly substantial calves.

Required:
- fuller calf mass;
- grounded ankle transition;
- lower leg that looks capable of carrying a very tall body.

Avoid:
- runner-thin calves;
- exaggerated sculpted calf peaks.

### Feet
Target:
- large, broad, grounded plantigrade feet;
- strong heel and forefoot;
- functional toes;
- no extreme length;
- no alien taper;
- no paw structure.

Feet must visually anchor the body.

## 8. Head / face

The target image gives a much better Grask face direction than v002/v003.

### Overall head
Required:
- compact-to-moderate vertical head proportion;
- broad cranial/facial block;
- heavy bone structure;
- head that feels integrated with the strong neck.

Do not return to:
- v001 human face;
- v002 elongated alien mask;
- v003 partially elongated human/troll hybrid.

### Brow / orbit
- strong brow structure;
- deep-set eyes;
- heavy but not permanently angry;
- orbit structure should read robust and primitive.

### Nose
- broader, stronger nasal structure;
- not giant;
- not long and narrow;
- avoid alien vertical nose stretch.

### Midface / cheeks
- broad, solid cheek structure;
- less narrow facial taper;
- enough lateral mass to support the jaw.

### Jaw / chin
- substantial jaw;
- broad, squared/rounded troll jaw block;
- strong chin;
- no giant underbite;
- no exaggerated orc tusk jaw.

### Mouth
- broad, firm mouth;
- neutral expression;
- no caricature.

## 9. Ears

The target image uses compact lateral ears with a subtle pointed/folded character.

Use:
- moderate lateral projection;
- robust cartilage;
- slightly non-human contour;
- compact size relative to head.

Avoid:
- elf-like long points;
- v002/v003 large alien ears;
- ordinary human ears.

The ear should support the troll identity without dominating it.

## 10. Muscle / soft-tissue rule

The target image has visible strength, but v004 must not depend on sculpted muscle definition for species identity.

- natural soft transitions.

Avoid:

- bodybuilder pectorals;
- superhero deltoid separation;
- extreme vascular/muscle detail.

The final base should still work across Grask body-composition variation.
