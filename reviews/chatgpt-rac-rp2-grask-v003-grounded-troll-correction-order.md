## 4. Highest-priority correction — head / face

The v002 face overshot because too many elongation cues stacked together:
- brow-to-menton 16.3 → 21.3 cm;
- crown-to-menton 27.2 → 32.2 cm;
- narrowed midface;
- elongated nose;
- set-back forehead;
- deeper chin/ramus.

### Required v003 direction

Retain:
- stronger brow/orbit structure than v001;
- more integrated brow-to-nose transition than v001;
- more substantial zygomatic / mandibular structure than v001;
- clearly non-human Grask craniofacial organization.

Reduce:
- total vertical facial elongation substantially from v002;
- the long “alien mask” impression;
- excessive nasal/midface length;
- the oversized head contribution relative to stature.

### Target feel

The head should read:
- **troll-like**
- grounded
- broad enough in key structural planes to feel robust
- vertically organized, but not vertically stretched

Do not create:
- a flat human face;
- a muzzle;
- a giant orc jaw;
- an underbite;
- a huge nose;
- tiny eyes;
- permanent scowl.

### Quantitative guidance

Use v002 as the maximum, not the goal.

As a starting range:
- retain only roughly **40–60% of the v002 facial vertical increase over v001**;
- restore part of the v001 midface width;
- reduce total head-height increase materially;
- keep enough jaw/zygoma depth to remain non-human.

Do not treat these percentages as hard final numbers if the visual read says otherwise.

## 5. Ears — robust troll ear, not elongated alien/elf ear

v002 ear length 6.5 → 8.7 cm plus 1.6× upper-ear elongation and extra stand-off pushed the read too far.

### Keep
- folded, clearly non-human auricle;
- late taper;
- visible front/3-quarter species read;
- non-elven internal fold language.

### Reduce
- overall upper-ear elongation;
- excessive lateral stand-off;
- long pointed silhouette.

### Target
A compact-to-moderate, robust, folded Grask ear with a late taper.

It should look:
- non-human;
- non-elven;
- non-alien;
- anatomically substantial.

Prefer **fold complexity and robust cartilage mass** over simple ear length as the identity carrier.

## 6. Torso / girdle — restore grounded structural mass

v002 narrowed the torso 5.5% while deepening it 5%, on top of very long limbs.

That combination contributes strongly to the alien read.

### v003 must

Retain:
- rangy long-limbed silhouette;
- long forearm and lower-leg emphasis;
- non-human girdle architecture;
- improved joint structure.

Restore:
- some thoracic breadth from v001;
- a more grounded ribcage-to-waist transition;
- enough upper-body structural presence to support 218 cm stature convincingly.

Do not:
- widen into Skarn;
- create bodybuilder V-taper;
- create barrel chest;
- shorten limbs;
- add generic orc bulk.

### Visual requirement

From the front:
- limbs should still dominate;
- but torso must no longer resemble an extremely narrow alien trunk.

## 7. Neck

Retain enough neck mass to keep the head integrated.

Avoid:
- pencil neck;
- giant wrestler neck;
- alien long-neck read;
- forward monster neck.

## 8. Hands — retain species structure, reduce alien exaggeration

v002 hands:
- 29.4 → 32.5 cm;
- +5% finger elongation;
- +14% finger thickness;
- +7% palm breadth;
- knobby joints.

The enlarged knobby concept is useful, but the total exaggeration contributes to alien anatomy.

### v003 direction

Retain:
- larger absolute hand scale than v001;
- stronger metacarpal / knuckle structure;
- robust functional digits;
- non-human silhouette.

Reduce:
- overall hand length from v002;
- finger elongation;
- excessive spatulate/knobby exaggeration.

Do not:
- add claws;
- change digit count;
- make fingers horror-long;
- make hands paw-like.

Target:
**large troll-like humanoid hands**, not alien hands.

## 9. Feet — retain robust identity, reduce overgrowth

- toes +16%;
- broader forefoot;
- heel extension.

This is too much combined with the narrow torso and long limbs.

### v003 direction

Retain:
- more robust foot identity than v001;
- clear heel / instep / forefoot structure;
- plantigrade support;
- non-human-but-humanoid proportions.

Reduce:
- total foot length and breadth from v002;
- toe elongation;
- forefoot exaggeration.

Do not:
- make paws;
- make digitigrade feet;
- add claws;
- make oversized fantasy troll feet.

Target:
**large, grounded plantigrade troll feet**, not alien extremities.

- excessive joint swelling;
- skeletal “grey alien” stylization.
