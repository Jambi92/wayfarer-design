## 1. What the Saurin floor actually needs

The Saurin rostral floor must lie **outside the maximum valid adult projection** of Marchfolk, Grask and Gorrund (SA L756, L2399; r3 L112: "the Saurin floor must exceed the **maximum valid** FPI of each comparison population"). RMQ rule 2: "Central values alone never establish a boundary" (RMQ L98).

So the authorship each of RM-CF-02/03/04 needs is **the upper boundary of valid adult projection, plus a named diagnostic face that embodies it**, not a central value.
