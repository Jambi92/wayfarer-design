## Purpose

Gate 8 establishes how a living Saurin's accepted anatomy and Regional Scale Architecture carry natural color, biological pattern and material response.

This is **not** another anatomy or scale-geometry pass.

Freeze Gate 6 and Gate 7 completely.

## 1. Frozen authority

Do not change:
- skeleton, proportions, posture, pelvis, limbs, hands, feet or tail;
- +8% accepted skull, rostrum, jaw, orbital-temporal platform or tympanic recess;
- tail path, root geometry or caudofemoral/load-path anatomy;
- Regional Scale Architecture assignments and Gate 7 transition solution;
- scale geometry/relief merely to make pigmentation easier;
- eye geometry, vertical pupil or nictitating membrane;
- accepted Cranial Keratin Display attachment families.

Any apparent anatomical problem discovered during color work must be reported, not silently sculpted away.

## 2. Natural material baseline

Establish a physically believable Saurin integument response before dramatic coloration.

The natural scale surface should live primarily within:
- matte;
- dry satin;
- modest localized sheen where biologically credible.

Reject as normal baseline:
- uniformly glossy/wet;
- plastic;
- metallic;
- gemstone;
- emissive;
- rubber;
- painted-toy appearance.

Material response must reinforce the Gate 7 scale hierarchy rather than flatten it. Structural, articulation, expressive, ventral and contact regions may respond differently, but they must remain one coherent organism.

## 3. Pigmentation architecture

Build pigmentation as biological phenotype, not as a flat RGB skin picker.

Develop a layered system capable of producing believable variation through:
- dominant body hue/family;
- secondary regional hue shifts;
- dorsal/ventral value relationships;
- local saturation variation;
- facial emphasis where biologically plausible;
- distal variation on hands, feet and tail;
- keratin coloration;
- iris coloration;
- natural asymmetry.

Color boundaries should normally follow biological development and anatomy rather than arbitrary paint-mask borders.

Do not assign color families to sex, class, culture, personality, morality, intelligence, biome, profession or gameplay statistics.

## 4. Biological pattern families

Develop a restrained but broad first-pass library of inherited pattern logic.

Test at minimum:
- near-uniform / very low-pattern;
- dorsal mottling;
- lateral mottling/blotching;
- banding;
- broken banding;
- longitudinal/axial striping;
- speckling/freckling;
- regional contrast fields;
- mixed pattern;
- naturally asymmetric expression.

Patterns must deform and flow with anatomy. They cannot look like decals laid across unrelated body regions.

Tail patterns must continue biologically from the sacral/root region into the free tail rather than beginning at a seam.

Facial patterns must respect rostral, orbital, jaw and cranial planes and must not obscure the non-human structural read.

## 5. Pattern hierarchy and scale hierarchy

Pigmentation must cooperate with Regional Scale Architecture.

Test whether:
- large structural fields can carry larger pattern masses without becoming camouflage noise;
- fine expressive facial fields can carry finer variation without becoming visually dirty;
- ventral fields retain coherent identity without defaulting to a cartoon pale belly;
- articulation regions do not form accidental rings at every joint;
- contact surfaces remain functional and readable;
- tail pattern flow strengthens rather than breaks pelvic-tail continuity.

Do not make every scale individually multicolored merely to prove detail.

## 6. Range without subraces

Produce broad phenotype diversity without creating accidental fixed biological subraces.

The same Saurin population must plausibly support:
- subdued earth-range individuals;
- cool-range individuals;
- warmer-range individuals;
- darker and lighter overall values;
- higher and lower contrast;
- strongly patterned and minimally patterned individuals.

Do not label these as geographic, cultural or ancestry groups yet.

No phenotype family should imply temperament or class.

## 7. Cranial Keratin Display material

Apply biologically credible keratin response to the accepted display families:
- neutral/minimal;
- low hornlets;
- swept-back paired;
- mixed/asymmetric;
- restrained crest.

Keratin may differ from scales in roughness, translucency, value, growth banding and wear logic where credible.

Do not make display structures automatically:
- brightly colored;
- sexually dimorphic;
- culturally decorated;
- magical;
- weapon-like.

Test subtle phenotype relationships between display keratin and surrounding pigmentation, but preserve independent variation where biologically credible.

## 8. Eyes and nictitating membrane

Retain Gate 7 eye anatomy.

Develop natural iris/pupil/material presentation with:
- vertical pupil remaining clearly legible;
- iris color variation;
- plausible corneal response;
- nictitating membrane remaining translucent and biologically readable.

No emissive/glowing baseline eyes.

Do not infer enhanced night vision, magical sight or other gameplay effects from appearance.

## 9. Age, environment and applied appearance firewall

Gate 8 is primarily **inherited natural phenotype**.

Do not mix into the biological pattern system:
- body paint;
- tattoos;
- dyes;
- ritual markings;
- dirt/mud;
- blood;
- scars;
- burns;
- occupational wear;
- deliberate keratin coloring;
- magical corruption/effects.

Those belong to Applied/Acquired or later environmental/presentation work.

If minor natural wear is needed solely to judge material realism, keep it diagnostic and clearly separate.

Do not perform the full age pass here.
