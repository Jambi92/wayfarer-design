## 2. OPTION B — ACCEPT AS CURRENT DIAGNOSTIC CALIBRATION, NOT PERMANENT BIOLOGY

Accept **Option B** as the current RM-UF-05 diagnostic calibration for the evidence regime in which it was derived.

For the current W3-era diagnostic case only:

- do not treat them as race-membership tests;

- do not use them to reject legitimate central concentration;

**Option C is rejected for canon use** because it forces a spread floor and incorrectly rejects valid centrally concentrated states.

# PART B — RM-OT-05 WORLD-SCALE / STATURE REVIEW

Proceed to RM-OT-05 only after Part A writeback is complete and internally consistent.

## 6. PURPOSE

RM-OT-05 is the roster-wide stature / world-scale review.

The queue describes:

> **Stature distributions and world-scale extremes (76–251 cm), all populations.**

The purpose of this block is to establish a single coherent roster-scale record from existing accepted canon and reference anatomy.

It must answer:

- what are the current author-accepted stature hard bounds for each playable population;
- what central/reference stature points are accepted;
- which populations overlap at matched stature;
- whether every accepted extreme is still consistent with its race-specific proportion, allometry and body-architecture rules;
- whether the entire roster remains visually and biologically coherent on a common world scale;
- which questions are true anatomy bounds versus still-open population-frequency questions.

This is not yet engine implementation.

---

## 7. FREQUENCY FIREWALL

**Do not invent stature distributions.**

For every race, classify stature information into the strongest source class actually supported by canon:

- HARD MIN / HARD MAX;
- CONDITIONAL / INHERITED OUTER REACH;
- CENTRAL / REFERENCE POINT;
- SOFT TENDENCY;
- FREQUENCY AUTHORED;
- FREQUENCY OPEN;
- NOT DEMONSTRATED.

If a race has hard bounds but no authored frequency curve, report the interval and leave its frequency **OPEN**.

Do not assume normal, Gaussian, uniform, triangular, beta or any other stature distribution.

Do not infer "average" merely from the midpoint of a hard interval.

Halvren ancestry-sensitive reach must remain governed by its inheritance rules and must not be flattened into a generic fixed 50/50 or source-midpoint model.
