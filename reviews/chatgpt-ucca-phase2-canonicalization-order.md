### AD-C7 — ACCEPT CLAUDE'S CONSERVATIVE RULE
Body hair is bound only where canon supports it. For MF, SK, SG, FN, AE, VA and HV, where biology is silent, keep body-hair controls hidden pending biological authorship. DU, GR and GO remain BIO OPEN. Do not infer mammalian body hair merely because a population has scalp/facial hair.

### AD-C8 — MODIFY / AUTHOR DECISION
Natural subtle body asymmetry is a **universal representable capability** for all playable populations, analogous to ordinary individual biological variation. It is not a racial identity trait, deformity, injury, culture, or Presentation.

Rules:
- neutral default = zero/near-zero asymmetry;
- ordinary subtle offsets only unless a race spec explicitly authorizes more;
- must preserve joints, gait-support geometry, paired-limb functional coherence and population identity;
- may not simulate acquired injury, missing anatomy, pathological deformation, or major length discrepancy;
- acquired asymmetry remains Acquired History;
- do not invent race-specific asymmetry distributions or numeric ranges in Phase 2.

Saurin's existing canon remains authoritative where more specific.

### AD-C9 — AUTHOR RESOLUTION
Split Environmental state into persistent and transient subtypes.
- **Environmental-persistent:** tanning/sun response, weathering, dryness and ordinary reversible/accumulated callusing.
- **Environmental-transient:** dirt, dust, soot, mud, wetness and comparable temporary contamination/state.
- **Acquired:** scars, burns, healed injuries, permanent/localized damage, and callus-like tissue change only when it has become a persistent individual-history alteration rather than ordinary environmental response.

This reconciles MF and GO wording by state/degree rather than declaring one population's biology exceptional. Do not force environmental or acquired state by race, class, culture or occupation.
