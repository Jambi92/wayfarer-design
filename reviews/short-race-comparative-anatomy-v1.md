# Short-Race Comparative Anatomy Review v1.0

**Scope:** Durrim, Pipkin, Cogling  
**Phase:** DESIGN ONLY  
**Purpose:** cross-race validation after all three short-race first passes. This review does not replace the canonical race specs and does not authorize UE5 implementation.

## 1. Review question

Can Durrim, Pipkin and Cogling remain clearly different biological populations when height, composition, surface phenotype, hair, clothing, culture, occupation and personality cues overlap?

The answer must come from positive anatomy, not from dwarf/halfling/gnome stereotypes.

## 2. Three positive body anchors

| Race | Positive body anchor | Core anatomical sequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Durrim** | **Compact Structural Concentration** | short adult stature → high skeletal structural presence → broad/deep vertically compact torso → substantial joints → reduced limb contribution → relatively substantial hands/feet |
| **Pipkin** | **Low-Set Compact Trunk Architecture** | light compact adult skeleton → moderate thorax → compact lower trunk → mature structurally broad pelvis → proportionally sustained limbs |
| **Cogling** | **Fine-Scale Elongated Articulation** | narrow stable central core → near-human total limb contribution → fine shafts/joints → distal redistribution within limbs → emphasized hands/fingers |

These are different anatomical mechanisms, not three settings on a single short-body slider.

## 3. Stature relationships

Current provisional adult envelopes:
- **Cogling:** ~76–107 cm; reference ~91 cm.
- **Pipkin:** ~91–122 cm; reference ~107 cm.
- **Durrim:** ~122–152 cm; reference ~137 cm.

Important boundaries:
- Cogling and Pipkin intentionally overlap through roughly **91–107 cm**.
- Pipkin maximum and Durrim minimum meet around **122 cm**, creating a boundary case even without a broad overlap.
- Cogling and Durrim do not currently overlap in stature.

Height is therefore useful context but cannot be the primary biological discriminator.

## 4. Structural-mass axis

At normalized displayed height:

**Cogling → Pipkin → Durrim** broadly progresses from finer to greater skeletal structural presence.

This is not a universal linear morph:
- Cogling identity also requires within-limb distal redistribution.
- Pipkin identity requires Low-Set Compact Trunk organization.
- Durrim identity requires compact structural concentration and substantial joints/axial structure.

Muscularity and adiposity cannot substitute for skeletal structural presence.

## 5. Central-body comparison

### Durrim
Broad/deep, vertically compact thoracic architecture with strong torso-pelvis continuity and high structural presence.

### Pipkin
Moderate thorax transitioning through a compact lower trunk into a mature pelvis that carries strong silhouette importance.

### Cogling
Comparatively narrow stable central core with adult axial/trunk contribution broadly near the Marchfolk adult proportional range; the pelvis is mature but not the primary silhouette anchor.

**Boundary rule:** a Broad Cogling cannot become Durrim by increasing width; a Narrow Durrim cannot become Cogling by reducing muscle; a Pipkin cannot be reduced to the midpoint between them.

## 6. Limb comparison

### Durrim
Lower limb contribution to total height, substantial long-bone structure and joints. Hands/feet are substantial relative to stature.

### Pipkin
Proportionally sustained limbs support the low-set trunk without becoming limb-dominant. Valid slight lower-leg emphasis may occur but is not the racial anchor.

### Cogling
Broadly near-human total limb contribution, with length redistributed **within** the limbs: somewhat less proximal share and more distal forearm/lower-leg/hand/finger contribution.

**Boundary rule:** distal segmentation alone cannot distinguish Pipkin and Cogling. Cogling requires the complete narrow-core + fine-shaft/joint + distal-redistribution system.

## 7. Hand and foot comparison

**Durrim:** structurally substantial hands and feet, broad weight-bearing relationships.

**Pipkin:** adult hands/feet coherent with light compact proportionality; neither giant-halfling feet nor tiny child extremities are required.

**Cogling:** hands are a supporting identifier through fine construction and proportionally emphasized fingers; feet remain moderate adult plantigrade structures and are not a climbing/balance adaptation.

No race gains crafting, dexterity, stability or climbing skill from extremity anatomy alone.

## 8. Positive facial anchors

| Race | Positive facial anchor |
| --- | --- |
| **Durrim** | Compact adult craniofacial foundation with greater cranial breadth/depth relative to facial vertical height and strong integrated midface/mandible/head-neck structural presence |
| **Pipkin** | **Integrated Mature Facial Architecture** — mature adult facial relationships integrated across cranial base, temple/zygoma, central midface and adult lower-face framework |
| **Cogling** | **Fine-Scale Planar Integration** — adult face-to-vault contribution broadly at/slightly above Marchfolk adult relationship plus clearly angled orbit→zygoma, zygoma→maxilla and mandibular body→ramus junctions despite fine skeletal mass |

All three must remain adult without beard, wrinkles, hairstyle, makeup or presentation.

## 9. Ear comparison

**Durrim:** broadly humanoid, non-elven ears; exact population distribution remains race-specific.

**Pipkin:** adult short-race ear variation must not become oversized/cute shorthand.

**Cogling:** secondary **Fine Folded Auricular Architecture** tendency: relatively compact adult ear, fine cartilage, clear folds, rounded/non-pointed termination. It is secondary and may overlap Pipkin/Durrim/human ears.

Ear shape cannot carry the three-race distinction by itself.

## 10. Surface phenotype

Surface phenotype may overlap broadly across the three races.

Skin pigmentation, hair color/texture, iris color, freckles, facial hair, scars, tattoos and age presentation cannot be assigned as mandatory racial separators.

Neutral gray-material testing remains required.

## 11. Composition stress

For every race, test:
- Narrow/Balanced/Broad frames;
- low/high muscularity;
- low/high adiposity;
- combinations that visually push toward the neighboring short race.

Failure conditions include:
- Broad Cogling reading as Durrim.
- High-fat Cogling or Durrim reading as Pipkin solely through softness.
- Narrow low-muscle Durrim losing structural concentration.
- Broad/high-muscle Pipkin becoming Durrim.
- Narrow/low-fat Pipkin becoming Cogling.
- High-muscle Cogling losing fine skeletal identity.

## 12. Equal-height and boundary tests

### SR-COMP-01 — Cogling/Pipkin at ~91 cm
Match frame neighborhood, muscularity, adiposity, age, surface and presentation. Cogling must retain narrow-core/fine-distal organization; Pipkin must retain Low-Set Compact Trunk identity.

### SR-COMP-02 — Cogling/Pipkin at ~107 cm
Repeat at the opposite end of their overlap. Height cannot decide the result.

### SR-COMP-03 — Pipkin/Durrim around ~122 cm
Use maximum-height Pipkin and minimum-height Durrim with matched composition/presentation. Pipkin remains light compact adult proportionality; Durrim retains structural concentration, torso depth/breadth, joint presence and lower limb contribution.

### SR-COMP-04 — normalized displayed height, all three
Normalize displayed stature for diagnostic comparison. This is not a valid in-world body state; it isolates proportional anatomy.

### SR-COMP-05 — silhouette only
Neutral silhouettes with hair, ears, clothing and surface removed.

### SR-COMP-06 — torso obscured
Check whether limbs/joints/hands/feet still provide supporting distinctions without becoming sole identifiers.

### SR-COMP-07 — extremities obscured
Check whether central-body architecture alone retains the three positive spaces.

### SR-COMP-08 — face only
Normalize framing and remove hair/surface/presentation. Each positive facial anchor remains distinguishable.

### SR-COMP-09 — surface swap
Apply overlapping plausible surface phenotypes across the three. Race identity must survive.

### SR-COMP-10 — composition inversion
Broad/high-muscle Cogling vs Narrow/low-muscle Durrim; heavy Pipkin vs lean Durrim; lean Pipkin vs higher-fat Cogling.

### SR-COMP-11 — adult/child firewall
Minimum-height Cogling and Pipkin plus age-appropriate human children at similar absolute scales where possible. Adult skeletal and facial maturity must remain clear.

### SR-COMP-12 — movement neutralization
Neutral locomotion without racial personality stereotypes. Anatomy may change gait geometry, but movement style cannot rescue an anatomically weak distinction.

## 13. Creator-control implications

The three races must not share a single short-race master morph.

A unified conceptual schema may share semantic dimensions such as:
- height;
- frame;
- muscularity;
- adiposity;
- regional proportions;
- facial domains.

But each race requires its own:
- validity envelopes;
- relationship constraints;
- distributions;
- anatomical response curves.

A creator control that is valid for all three may still produce different anatomical effects by race.

## 14. Randomization implications

Biological Randomization must not use stereotypes to separate the races.

It must not force:
- Durrim toward muscle/beards;
- Pipkin toward roundness/large feet/cuteness;
- Cogling toward thinness/freckles/goggles/old age.

At overlap/boundary heights, randomization must preserve the full race-specific anatomical relationship system.

## 15. World-scale implications

The short-race range now spans roughly **76–152 cm**, approximately a twofold difference in standing height from minimum Cogling to maximum Durrim.

World validation must therefore consider:
- reach;
- eye height;
- stairs;
- ladders;
- seating;
- beds;
- counters;
- doors/handles;
- cover;
- camera/dialogue;
- equipment fit;
- canonical object dimensions.

The solution cannot be a single "short race" interaction offset.

## 16. Equipment implications

Equipment fitting must preserve each race's different anatomy:
- Durrim structural breadth/depth and substantial joints/extremities;
- Pipkin low-set trunk/pelvic relationships;
- Cogling fine distal segmentation and long-finger relationships.

Canonical equipment dimensions do not automatically scale with holder height.

## 17. Gameplay firewall

This comparative review approves no automatic gameplay advantage or penalty based on the three anatomical identities.

It does not decide:
- Durrim breath/swimming legacy traits;
- Pipkin stealth legacy trait;
- Cogling gameplay traits;
- size-based collision/reach/combat effects;
- racial stats;
- race/class restrictions.

Those remain for the later race-biology-gameplay review.

## 18. Comparative identity summary

> **Durrim concentrate structure. Pipkin organize a light adult body around a low-set compact trunk and mature pelvic foundation. Cogling redistribute fine-scale articulation distally within a narrow stable adult body.**

The three races are therefore not a large/medium/small sequence, not stout/average/thin archetypes, and not dwarf/halfling/gnome stereotypes.
