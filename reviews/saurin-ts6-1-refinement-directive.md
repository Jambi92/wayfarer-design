# Saurin TS6.1 Refinement Directive — Preserve the Breakthrough, Finish the Organism

**Author:** ChatGPT  
**For:** Claude  
**Status:** Author direction / diagnostic sculpt instruction  
**Scope:** Saurin visual diagnostic refinement only. **Do not begin UE5 implementation.**  
**Project owner direction:** Tyler wants the Saurin to read unmistakably as a reptilian humanoid while remaining an original Wayfarer race rather than a copied real reptile, generic lizard-person, crocodilian, dinosaur, or dragon.

## 1. Overall assessment of TS6

TS6 is the first Saurin head iteration that successfully breaks the persistent **"human head morphed into a reptile"** problem.

The most important evidence is the **bare TS5 vs TS6 comparison**. TS5 bare still reads as a rounded mammalian/humanoid cranium carrying a projected rostrum. TS6 bare finally has a non-human construction before scales, pupils, horns, crests, or other display structures are added.

Therefore:

> **TS6 cranial architecture is the new foundation. Do not restart the Saurin head.**

This is a refinement pass, not another redesign.

The successful principle from TS6 should remain:

> **Broad organic surfaces + decisive anatomical transitions.**

Do not regress toward TS5 softness. Do not solve remaining issues by adding more scales, horns, spikes, or surface noise.

## 2. What TS6 successfully established and must be protected

Preserve all of the following:

- Non-human cranial construction visible on the bare head.
- Stronger supraorbital/orbital structural organization.
- Distinct rostral planes rather than a human central face stretched forward.
- More decisive orbit-to-rostrum transitions.
- Better lateral cranial/temporal organization.
- Clearer cheek/rostral structural breaks.
- A jaw that belongs to the rostral skull rather than a human jaw underneath a muzzle.
- Posterior cranial mass that no longer reads as a simple human skull.
- Forward-facing readable eyes with the approved vertical-pupil identity.
- Recessed auricular-opening architecture.
- Regional Scale Architecture as a surface system layered over anatomy rather than substituting for anatomy.
- The principle that cranial display structures are individual phenotype/customization, **not the source of racial identity**.

The feminine minimal-display sheet is especially important: the Saurin remains recognizable without dramatic horns. Keep that property.

## 3. Primary TS6.1 objective

Produce a **TS6.1 refinement**, not TS7.

The goal is to take the successful TS6 skeletal concept and make it feel like a coherent living organism rather than a diagnostic hard-surface blockout.

The test is:

> If all scales, pigmentation, pupils, horns, crests, and decorative keratin are removed, does the head still read as a plausible non-human reptilian Saurin skull covered by living tissue?

The answer should remain yes after refinement.

## 4. Reduce excessive faceting WITHOUT returning to human softness

The TS6 bare head succeeds structurally, but several transitions are now slightly too geometric/faceted. In the front view especially, the central brow/rostral region can resemble an angular mask.

Do **not** remove the underlying planes.

Instead distinguish three levels:

1. **Primary skeletal planes** — must remain clearly legible.
2. **Secondary anatomical transitions** — should bridge those planes through plausible bone/tendon/muscle/soft tissue.
3. **Surface scale relief** — belongs above both and must not create the underlying form.

Refine the supraorbital, central frontal, rostral-root, lateral orbital, zygomatic-equivalent, temporal, and mandibular transitions so they feel grown rather than machined.

Avoid:
- polygonal mask appearance;
- razor creases everywhere;
- identical ridge depth across the head;
- smoothing everything back into a sphere;
- using scale texture to hide weak transitions.

We want **sharp anatomical decisions, not sharp-edged geometry everywhere**.

## 5. Mouth and jaw — dedicated refinement required

This is the largest remaining facial issue.

The current TS6 mouth is much better than the early rounded/human versions, but the oral seam still becomes too thin, straight, and graphically uniform in profile.

The mouth must look like a functional reptilian oral apparatus capable of:
- closing under tension;
- opening around a real jaw hinge;
- speech/dialogue articulation;
- subtle expression;
- eating and breathing as a living organism;

without acquiring human lips.

### Required mouth/jaw work

Refine:
- upper maxillary margin;
- lower mandibular margin;
- posterior mouth corner;
- jaw hinge region;
- soft-tissue compression around closure;
- transition from rostrum into posterior cheek/jaw;
- submandibular/throat attachment;
- anterior closure at the rostral tip.

The upper and lower margins should not simply be two halves of one perfectly straight incision.

Allow subtle structural variation in the mouth line created by the underlying maxilla/mandible and tissue thickness.

The posterior corner must communicate where the oral opening terminates and how the jaw rotates.

Do **not** add:
- mammalian vermilion lips;
- human smile corners;
- permanent grin;
- snarl;
- crocodilian tooth exposure;
- exaggerated overbite/underbite;
- automatic visible teeth;
- a flexible cartoon mouth.

Maintain the approved firewall: jaw/dentition morphology does not automatically imply bite combat, diet, aggression, personality, or gameplay bonuses.

## 6. Front view remains a critical acceptance test

Do not judge TS6.1 primarily from profile.

The front view is where previous iterations repeatedly collapsed back into human/mammalian facial organization.

TS6.1 front view must retain:
- non-human orbital framing;
- non-human rostral root;
- clear lateral facial architecture;
- a rostrum that emerges from the skull as an integrated structure;
- absence of human nose/philtrum/lip/chin organization;
- adult, intelligent, expressive readability.

Do not make the face reptilian merely by narrowing the eyes or making an "angry" brow.

Neutral expression is mandatory.

## 7. Neck, throat, and skull integration

TS6 has improved the skull enough that the relatively smooth cylindrical neck now looks under-designed by comparison.

Do not solve this with spikes.

Develop a plausible transition among:
- posterior cranium;
- jaw hinge;
- submandibular region;
- throat;
- cervical column/musculature;
- lateral neck;
- shoulder/thoracic inlet.

The neck should communicate that it supports a forward-projecting rostral head and belongs to the same animal.

Explore restrained structural planes/ridges where biologically justified, especially around:
- posterior jaw attachment;
- occipital/posterior skull transition;
- lateral cervical attachment;
- throat-to-sternum transition.

Preserve upright neutral head carriage. Do not introduce a permanent forward hunch or dinosaur posture.

## 8. Cranial keratin display system — keep it, but keep it secondary

The display experiment is successful enough to continue.

Current useful phenotype families include:
- minimal/subtle ridge expression;
- swept-back paired keratin;
- crest-dominant configurations;
- mixed/asymmetric configurations.

These should function as the Saurin's race-specific personal visual-expression system in the broad design space occupied by hairstyles for hair-bearing races.

However:

> **A Saurin with minimal display must still be unmistakably Saurin.**

Display structures cannot repair a weak skull.

Preserve distinction between:
- **skull anatomy / FD-STRUCT**
- **integumentary keratin display / FD-SURF / Natural appearance**

Do not silently convert the entire system into true bony horns.

Permit natural variation in:
- presence/absence where already allowed;
- number;
- placement within biologically valid zones;
- length;
- thickness;
- taper;
- sweep;
- curvature;
- crest height/extent;
- symmetry/asymmetry;
- wear/chipping where acquired-history rules permit.

Keep extremes relationship-aware and avoid dragon-antler silhouettes unless later deliberately approved.

## 9. Full-body propagation of the TS6 design language

The next diagnostic should stop treating the head as an isolated sculpture.

Carry the principle **"broad organic surfaces + decisive anatomical transitions"** into the body, but at lower intensity than the skull.

Candidate locations:
- shoulder/scapular transition;
- clavicular/thoracic inlet equivalent;
- elbow;
- wrist;
- pelvis/hip transition;
- knee;
- ankle;
- tail base;
- selected dorsal/lateral tail transitions.

Do not cover the body in arbitrary ridges.

The body should remain compatible with the approved **Counterbalanced Pelvic-Axial Architecture**:
deep mobile thorax → elongated lower axial trunk → strongly integrated pelvis/sacral base → substantial counterbalancing tail.

The tail must look biomechanically inevitable on the body, never attached to an otherwise humanoid pelvis.

## 10. Hands and feet

Retain Tyler's approved direction that Saurin digits terminate in clearly non-human pointed claws.

The claws should:
- continue the digit axis;
- emerge from tapered distal phalanges;
- be clearly more pointed than human nails;
- remain restrained enough for believable manipulation and footwear design;
- remain non-retractable unless separately decided.

Hands must still support believable grasping and tool/weapon use.

Feet remain provisionally plantigrade and must maintain stable contact surfaces.

Do not let claws automatically imply:
- climbing bonuses;
- unarmed damage bonuses;
- predatory behavior;
- aggression.

## 11. Tail customization — integrate into the complete-character pass

Continue the previously explored tail phenotype system.

The mandatory tail remains a primary racial anatomical feature and **never receives an on/off toggle**.

Preserve coupled relationships:

> **tail base size × length × mass × taper × pelvic support**

Useful phenotype axes may include:
- overall length within approved envelope;
- proximal base mass;
- gradual vs stronger taper;
- relative lateral breadth;
- dorsoventral depth where biomechanically valid;
- resting curvature;
- restrained dorsal keratin expression;
- inherited biological banding/pattern;
- scale-field variation consistent with Regional Scale Architecture.

Do not treat these as fixed subraces.

Do not make a tail variant automatically imply:
- swimming ability;
- balance bonus;
- attack reach/damage;
- climbing;
- habitat;
- caste;
- sex;
- personality.

The previously explored shorter/heavier, longer/gradual, laterally broader, restrained-dorsal-keratin, slender/banded, and balanced examples can remain useful phenotype demonstrations if they survive coupled-validity review.

## 12. Masculine and feminine / sex-related presentation

Produce both masculine and feminine diagnostic bodies, but remember the universal rule:

Saurin sex-related anatomy is non-human and is not yet fully defined.

Do not solve sex differentiation by copying human dimorphism.

Do not silently force:
- frame;
- muscularity;
- rostrum size;
- horn/display size;
- pigmentation;
- pattern;
- tail size;
- personality.

Overlapping adult phenotypes must remain valid.

The diagnostic bodies can use current provisional presentation differences only to help us inspect anatomy, not establish mandatory biological dimorphism.

## 15. Explicit non-goals

TS6.1 is **not** permission to:
- restart the head;
- copy one real reptile species;
- turn Saurin into crocodile people;
- turn Saurin into monitor-lizard people;
- turn Saurin into dragons;
- add spikes everywhere;
- make horns mandatory;
- encode aggression;
- encode aquatic mastery;
- infer diet;
- infer intelligence/personality from anatomy;
- implement UE5 assets/systems.

Real reptile skull/head references should be used as **anatomical research**, especially to understand how reptilian skulls organize planes, openings, jaw hinges, rostra, orbital supports, and muscle attachment. Synthesize principles; do not trace a species.

## 16. Acceptance criteria

TS6.1 succeeds if all are true:

1. Bare head remains unmistakably non-human and reptilian in construction.
2. It is recognizably the same successful TS6 design, not a replacement concept.
3. Front view does not collapse into a human face.
4. Structural planes remain clear but no longer look mechanically faceted.
5. Rostrum is integrated into the cranium.
6. Mouth reads as a living oral apparatus rather than a straight cut line.
7. Jaw hinge and posterior oral termination are believable.
8. No mammalian lips/chin/nose return.
9. Neutral expression remains readable.
10. Neck and skull belong to the same organism.
11. Minimal-display Saurin remains racially identifiable.
12. Display variants create individuality without changing species identity.
13. Full body carries restrained structural continuity from skull through pelvis/tail.
14. Tail remains anatomically inevitable and mandatory.
15. Clawed hands/feet read non-human while remaining functionally plausible.
16. Masculine/feminine examples do not establish accidental hard sexual dimorphism.
17. No phenotype implies unapproved gameplay.
18. No culture/class/personality is encoded biologically.
19. No UE5 implementation begins.

## 17. Design target

The desired endpoint is not simply "more reptile."

It is:

> **A coherent intelligent reptilian humanoid whose anatomy looks as though it evolved/developed as one organism: a structurally reptilian skull with an integrated compact rostrum, decisive but organic cranial planes, a functional non-mammalian jaw, a supportive cervical transition, a counterbalanced pelvic-axial body, mandatory customizable tail, Regional Scale Architecture, restrained clawed digits, and optional keratin display structures capable of carrying individual visual identity without carrying the species identity by themselves.**

Please treat TS6 as the successful anatomical breakthrough and refine forward from it.
