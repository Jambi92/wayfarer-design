# Stage 1: lower-works art direction and surface flame response — v030

A separate development level begins the approved cavern-to-prison concept direction in the existing lower-works room. Continuous natural rock surrounds aged counterweights, forged guide frames, pulleys, ropes and visible metal repairs. A timber inspection deck, low-rise stairs and a static hoist support the excavation setting. The established footprint and floor are retained. This is an initial contained art pass; the wider prison treatment and final environment art remain future work.

The player reported that animated flames had little visible effect on nearby walls. Private light functions and matched flame materials now share stronger irregular brightness variation across 104 fixtures, using twelve phase/rate presets. The change is saved in native materials and runs with engine time. A fixed-camera gameplay capture measured approximately 36–39% wall luminance range relative to the mean and 42% on the sampled floor, excluding flame and lantern pixels. The HUD stayed constant. These rendered-image measurements retain normal exposure and do not claim physical light-unit calibration. The implementation uses Unreal's documented [animated light functions](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/using-light-functions-in-unreal-engine).

The six new meshes total 62,304 triangles with one render section each and closed source components. Twelve continuous movement legs passed with 76 cm and 251 cm diagnostic characters through the room, machinery loop and inspection stairs, with camera turns and endpoint inspection. All 576 local boundary rays hit geometry. Reopening passed native checks; all 4,068,289 terrain samples remain identical, and the map check reported zero errors or warnings. A one-minute static editor sample averaged approximately 119.5 FPS with 9.33 ms p99 frame time. AI, combat and shipping performance are not covered by that sample. The compressed checkpoint was verified, and the original project plus accepted earlier versions retain their hashes.

The newly approved divine standing-height reference informs subsequent prison planning. Complete clearance still depends on final model widths, adornments, reach and animations. Character assets, dialogue, the prison reveal and Last Chain geometry were not changed. This pass advances the approved visual direction without claiming that the entire cavern or prison now matches the concept art.

Only prose is published here. Assets, canonical bibles, screenshots and raw diagnostics remain local.
