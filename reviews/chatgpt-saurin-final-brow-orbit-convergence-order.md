**rostral/brow plane → supraorbital protective edge → temporal/postorbital plane change → jugal/quadrate flow**

Use changes in **projection, taper, plane angle, and blending**, not decorative grooves or added structures.

- Keep the anterior/medial brow structurally legible.
- Allow its projection to diminish progressively as it travels laterally/posteriorly.
- The rear brow should dissolve into the postorbital/temporal architecture rather than terminate as a shelf.
- Preserve a crisp enough plane break to protect the non-human cranial read.
- Do **not** round the area back into the previously rejected orbital bump.

This is a micro-convergence pass, not permission to invent a new brow.
