# RAC RM-OT-05D — True skeletal-envelope separation + Grask / Gorrund race-anatomy order

## Authority / ruling

This order follows the author review of:

- `reviews/claude-rac-rm-ot-05c-grask-gorrund-base-surface-candidate-gate.md`
- evidence in `reviews/rac-rmot05c-evidence/`

RM-OT-05C correctly failed its own acceptance gate. **Do not promote any 05C candidate.** RM-OT-05 remains OPEN and D4 remains blocked.

Author ruling for the next stage:

1. **Grask C is the developmental baseline only.**
2. **Gorrund B is the developmental baseline only.**
3. Neither is accepted biology yet.
4. **Do not revise Grask or Gorrund canon to make the generator easier.**
5. **Do not move to an external hand-sculpt yet.**
6. The next correction is architectural: stop treating the MPFB minimum-body / `-LEAN` soft-tissue surface as though it were literal bone where that prevents canon-compliant species anatomy.

This is **RM-OT-05D: true skeletal-envelope separation and race-specific tissue reconstruction.**

---

## 1. Core objective

Build a race-anatomy stage in which the protected floor is a **true bony / skeletal envelope**, not the human-derived MPFB minimum-body soft-tissue silhouette.

The purpose is to allow Grask and Gorrund to express materially different humanoid anatomy while keeping all locked skeletal facts intact.

The required visual target is not “more stylized human.”

The target is:

> **A distinct humanoid race/species whose body is still canonically upright, plantigrade, five-digit and normally jointed, but whose anatomy does not read as an ordinary human body variant.**

“Lanky human,” “stocky human,” “bodybuilder,” “unusual human,” “thin human,” or “heavy human” are all failures.

---

## 2. Canon that MUST remain locked

Do **not** solve readability by changing any of the following:

- upright humanoid plan;
- two arms / two legs;
- five digits;
- plantigrade feet;
- normal joint sequence / arrangement;
- posture tricks or forced stance;
- accepted stature ranges;
- accepted joint centres / bone lengths / bone-scale relationships;
- accepted Grask W2C skeletal relationships;
- accepted Gorrund W2D skeletal relationships;
- Grask GR-P2b / GR-P6 as skeletal / anatomical relationships;
- Gorrund deep-pelvis / load-path canon;
- sex-neutral reference-body policy;
- frame vs composition separation;
- no uniform whole-body scaling.

No canon rewrite is authorized by this order.

---

## 3. Correct the false “bone = minimum human body” assumption

The 05C report identified the key implementation blocker: portions of the MPFB minimum-body `-LEAN` surface carry human buttock, chest, shoulder-cap and related soft-tissue forms, but the current guard protects that surface as though it were bone.

That must not remain the limiting envelope in 05D.

### 3.1 Build / derive a true protected skeletal envelope

Create a reproducible **bony envelope / skeletal proxy** from the accepted skeleton data and joint architecture.

It must protect what is actually skeletal:

- joint centres and extents;
- long-bone axes and accepted lengths;
- bony joint breadths / condylar regions;
- pelvis bony extents required by accepted W2 relations;
- shoulder-girdle / ribcage bony extents where they are genuinely skeletal;
- hands / feet skeletal floors;
- any other explicitly accepted bony station used by the validators.

It must **not automatically preserve**:

- a human gluteal apex;
- human abdominal wall curvature;
- human waist indentation;
- human pectoral contour;
- human deltoid cap;
- human calf / biceps belly pattern;
- human lumbar hollow;
- any other soft-tissue form merely because it exists on the MPFB minimum body.

If a region cannot be separated reliably into “required bony floor” vs “human donor soft tissue,” stop and document the exact region and dependency instead of silently freezing the donor surface.

### 3.2 Normal direction

05D must use a verified outward normal convention.

Add an explicit automated normal-orientation test. Do not alter 05B reproducibility merely to clean up old evidence; 05D should use the corrected convention.

---

## 4. Validator-coordinate correction

The 05C report also found that organized surfaces can cause the current own-skin station search to re-anchor to a different height and create false regressions.

For 05D:

- establish **skeleton-anchored anatomical station levels** for organized race surfaces;
- use the same skeletal/anatomical station coordinates when comparing the old and new surfaces;
- report both the fixed anatomical reading and any legacy own-skin reading where useful;
- do not fail a candidate because the surface changed the search location while the protected skeletal relation stayed unchanged.

This is a measurement correction, **not** permission to weaken a canon guard.

---

## 5. Starting anatomy directions

Do not reopen the six-way 05C search from scratch.

Use:

- **Grask C** as the 05D developmental baseline;
- **Gorrund B** as the 05D developmental baseline.

Derive a small local bracket around each only if necessary to find the strongest canon-valid result. Keep candidate count tight.

### 5.1 Grask anatomical intent

Preserve the 05C strengths that survived low composition:

- long-tendon / high-belly limb organization;
- distal tendon cords;
- integrated shoulder-girdle direction;
- scapular / latissimus wing organization;
- reduced ordinary-human pectoral / gluteal read.

With the true skeletal envelope, specifically investigate whether Grask can now gain stronger race identity through:

- shoulder-to-neck integration that is no longer forced to retain a human deltoid cap;
- a pelvis-to-thigh transition that is not constrained by donor soft tissue;
- a sacral / gluteal organization that is not simply a thin-human buttock;
- a more convincing limb-section architecture around the same accepted bones;
- a torso section and back profile that remain lean but cease reading as “skinny human.”

Do not turn Grask into a creature with altered joints, digitigrade legs, extra anatomy or caricature.

### 5.2 Gorrund anatomical intent

Preserve the 05C strengths:

- deep slab-like axial trunk;
- minimal waist;
- continuous posterior column;
- columnar limb musculature;
- load-bearing mass organization.

With the true skeletal envelope, specifically restore / explore the cue that was lost when the donor buttock was protected:

- upper back -> sacrum -> posterior hip should form a convincing continuous load path;
- gluteal anatomy must not default to a human buttock apex if the actual skeleton does not require it;
- thigh-to-shin and pelvis-to-thigh transitions may become more structurally abrupt / columnar where canon allows;
- keep the result adult, functional and non-caricatured.

Do not solve this merely by making Gorrund fatter, wider or more muscular.

## 6. Composition and frame firewall

Every candidate that reaches the gate must be demonstrated at minimum in:

- reference composition;
- low composition;
- Narrow frame;
- Broad frame.

The race read must survive low composition.

Frame and composition may modulate the race body, but must not create or erase the race’s anatomical identity.

Do not encode “Grask = always thin” or “Gorrund = always bulky” as the identity test. Their species anatomy must remain visible across valid body composition.

## 8. Revised blind-read protocol

The previous wording “non-human” biased scorers toward body-plan changes that canon intentionally forbids.

Change the question.

Scorers must classify each unknown body as one of:

A. **ordinary human-family body variation**  
B. **unusual / extreme human-family body variation**  
C. **distinct humanoid race/species anatomy**

They must provide the feature(s) driving the classification.

### Acceptance target

A race candidate may proceed only if, for both reference and low-composition states:

- at least **2 of 3 uninformed scorers** independently classify it as **C**;
- the candidate forms a stable cluster separate from Marchfolk / Skarn controls;
- the separation is attributed to anatomical organization, not simply stature, fatness, muscularity or one exaggerated feature;
- no systematic human-control collapse is needed to create the cluster.

Also ask the scorers whether they can distinguish **Grask vs Gorrund** without labels after height normalization.

If either race fails this focused readability gate, **STOP**. Do not run the exhaustive validator suite and do not promote anything.

- composition ordering preserved;
- frame ordering preserved;
- fixed anatomical-station checks for the locked W2 skeletal relations.

Report any surface-volume change separately. 05D may reorganize the base surface, but it must not silently redefine composition amount.
