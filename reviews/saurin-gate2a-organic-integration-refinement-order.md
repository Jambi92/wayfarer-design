## Decision

Gate 2 is accepted **directionally**, but is **not yet locked**.

The new anatomical organization is substantially correct and must be preserved:
- human pectoral plates and under-pec shelf removed;
- rectus/six-pack ladder removed;
- linea alba groove and navel removed;
- thoracic depth and overall torso silhouette preserved;
- keeled chest shield established;
- pectoral fan established;
- costal arches established;
- continuous ventral shield established;
- long oblique load paths established;
- thorax → lower axial trunk → pelvic/caudal architecture remains legible.

Do **not** redesign this map. This pass is about making that map read as **living anatomy rather than mathematically constructed surface relief**.

## Primary problem

Gate 2 improved Saurin identity but lost too much of Rodin B1's high-frequency anatomical tension and organic variation.

The greatest issue is the ventral trunk. The current long vertical forms are too straight, evenly spaced, clean, and mechanically bounded. They risk reading as:
- armor channels;
- sculpted ribs;
- manufactured paneling;
- procedural grooves.

The pectoral fan has a similar, smaller issue: its fascicles are too regular.

The solution is **not** to restore human anatomy. The solution is to add organic tension, variation, and integration inside the approved Saurin organization.

## Required refinement

### 1. Preserve the anatomical map

Keep:
- keel;
- chest shield;
- pectoral fan;
- costal arches;
- ventral shield;
- long oblique chains.

Do not restore:
- two human pec plates;
- under-pec shelf;
- six-pack blocks;
- transverse rectus segmentation;
- linea alba groove;
- navel.

### 2. Organicize the ventral shield

The ventral shield must remain one continuous anatomical system, but it should stop reading like a smooth manufactured plate with straight parallel channels.

Introduce restrained variation in:
- width along its length;
- surface crown;
- edge tension;
- left/right contour;
- transition into the lower trunk;
- local relief intensity.

Any longitudinal structures should subtly drift, converge, diverge, soften, or terminate organically rather than maintaining ruler-straight paths.

Do **not** create obvious repeated segments.

### 3. Integrate shield and obliques

The current shield margins and oblique system should feel mechanically continuous.

Create subtle interdigitation/tension transitions where the ventral shield hands load into the long oblique paths. Avoid a clean “central panel + side straps” appearance.

The eye should read one living muscular/fascial system organized around a deep axial trunk.

### 4. Refine the pectoral fan

Preserve the fan concept and its keel-to-humeral load path, but reduce procedural regularity.

Vary fascicle:
- width;
- prominence;
- spacing;
- curvature;
- termination/blending.

The fan must still read as a coherent functional system, not random surface noise.

Do not convert it back into human pectoralis major anatomy.

### 5. Restore restrained anatomical tension

Use untouched Rodin B1 as the **quality reference for anatomical richness**, not as the biological map.

Restore some of B1's:
- subtle tension breaks;
- shallow depressions;
- compression/extension changes;
- soft-to-firm transitions;
- nonuniform surface rhythm.

These details must follow the new Saurin load paths.

This is the same interpretation rule established earlier: Rodin is a reference for anatomical sophistication, continuity, silhouette, tension, and believable surface behavior. It is **not authority for human-specific anatomy**.

### 6. Preserve thoracic mass

Do not flatten the chest or make the torso narrower/tubular while refining the surface.

The deep thoracic shell and existing profile depth are important successes of Gate 2.

### 7. Maintain asymmetry discipline

Subtle biological irregularity is desirable. Do not introduce conspicuous pose asymmetry or deformation that changes the neutral anatomical target.

Think natural anatomical variation, not visibly different left/right construction.

## Hard locks

## Anti-regression requirements

The refinement fails if it:
1. restores recognizable human pecs;
2. restores a six-pack or rectus ladder;
3. creates a human linea alba/navel;
4. turns the ventral shield into armor-like plating;
5. creates repetitive decorative grooves;
6. erases anatomical definition into a generic smooth tube;
7. changes the accepted pelvis/tail-root architecture;
8. changes overall body proportions or stance.
