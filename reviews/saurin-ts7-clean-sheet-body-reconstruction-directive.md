**Do not solve structural problems with surface treatment.**

The primary organism chain is:

**reptilian cranial base  
→ distinct cervical architecture  
→ structured thoracic shell

**thorax → shoulder girdle → upper limbs → clawed hands**

and

**pelvis → hip/femoral support → lower limbs → plantigrade clawed feet**

The body must appear designed around this system.

The tail is not an accessory attached after the pelvis is built.

# 6. Cervical architecture — rebuild from zero

Do not use a human neck cylinder.

Design the neck as the mechanical bridge between the approved Saurin cranium and thoracic structure.

Explore:
- stronger occipital-to-cervical continuity;
- differentiated dorsal, lateral and ventral cervical planes;
- a credible jaw-hinge/throat transition;
- cervical mass appropriate to supporting the projecting rostral head;
- broadening/redistribution approaching the thoracic inlet;
- visible directional structure without permanent tension.

Required read:

**skull → cervical system → thorax**

not:

**reptile head → human neck → human shoulders**

Neutral posture remains upright and intelligent. Do not force a dinosaur horizontal neck or monster hunch.

---

# 7. Thorax — design a Saurin thoracic shell

Do not copy the human ribcage surface silhouette.

The Saurin thorax should retain mobility and breathing volume but possess its own large-form organization.

Explore a controlled combination of:
- deeper thoracic dimension;
- clearer dorsal/lateral/ventral planes;
- more structured rib-field transitions;
- less spherical/soft chest rounding;
- a distinct ventral midline/sternal field;
- shoulder-root integration suited to the Saurin girdle;
- lower thoracic transition into the elongated axial trunk.

This is NOT external armor.

Think living ribcage and musculature with reptilian structural organization.

---

# 8. Shoulder girdle — do not inherit human clavicular geometry

Part 1 already established:
- deep narrow-to-moderate thoracic shell;
- shoulder/scapular relationship organized around that shell;
- clavicle present provisionally but not the dominant transverse carrier.

Now express that positively.

The shoulder must not simply be a human deltoid placed on a human ribcage.

Explore:
- scapular placement/orientation appropriate to the thoracic shell;
- less human horizontal clavicle-dominant read;
- clearer shoulder-root planes;
- smooth functional arm elevation despite different resting architecture.

Saurin must still be capable of humanoid gameplay interactions and tool use.

---

# 9. Axial trunk — remove the human waist template

The lower axial trunk is one of Saurin's defining anatomical regions.

Do not build:
human ribs → pinched waist → human pelvis.

Instead establish:

**thoracic shell → elongated lower axial trunk → integrated pelvic/sacral complex**

The lower trunk may narrow or change cross-section, but not because a conventional human hourglass waist is being preserved.

Give it its own dorsal, lateral and ventral structural transitions.

The torso should feel longer and more continuous with the pelvis/tail system.

---

# 10. Pelvis/sacrum — clean-sheet reconstruction required

This is one of the most important TS7 tasks.

Do NOT preserve the human pelvis surface construction.

The Saurin pelvis must support:
1. upright bipedal loading;
2. femoral articulation;
3. elongated axial trunk;
4. a substantial mandatory tail;
5. caudal musculature;
6. stable locomotion and turning.

Approved tendency:
- longer front-to-back pelvic relationship than Marchfolk tendency;
- stronger posterior structural contribution;
- hips/femora organized relative to posterior mass and caudal continuation.

Design the pelvis from these requirements.

---

# 11. Absolutely no human buttock construction

Do not sculpt two human gluteal hemispheres and remove the crack.

That is still human gluteal anatomy.

Create a new posterior pelvic muscular system appropriate to the Saurin's bipedal locomotion and tail-bearing sacral architecture.

It must plausibly perform hip extension/stabilization while visually integrating into:
- posterior pelvis;
- sacrum;
- proximal tail;
- upper femoral structures.

There should be **no conventional human butt crack / gluteal cleft**.

There should also not be a smooth featureless posterior.

This needs positive anatomy, not subtraction.

---

# 12. Tail root — tail begins inside the architecture

The tail must feel like the continuation of the axial skeleton and associated musculature.

Do not:
- glue a cone to the pelvis;
- begin the tail from a tiny socket;
- place it between human buttocks;
- hide the base;
- make the base arbitrarily thin.

The proximal tail should emerge through a broad, structurally supported sacral/caudal transition.

Tail base, tail length, tail mass and pelvic support remain coupled.

Tail customization remains approved in principle, but TS7 first establishes the neutral biological foundation.

---

# 13. Neutral tail path

Correct the frontal silhouette permanently.

Neutral resting logic:

**sacral origin → posterior projection → controlled lateral/caudal sweep as appropriate**

The proximal tail must not hang downward between the thighs.

Validation:
> In a neutral straight-front view, the tail must not be readily mistaken for external genital anatomy.

Do not merely rotate the same attached cone. Solve the origin and resting mechanics anatomically.

---

# 14. Limbs — stop using rounded human tubes

Arms and legs can remain humanoid enough for interaction and locomotion without having human surface anatomy.

Explore controlled Saurin limb identity through:
- clearer long-bone planes;
- sharper but biological joint transitions;
- differentiated dorsal/ventral or anterior/posterior muscular fields;
- restrained tendinous definition;
- less continuous rounded blending;
- reptilian-compatible scale-field boundaries later.

Do NOT make the limbs skeletal, insectoid, digitigrade, monstrous or excessively thin.

---

# 15. Hands

Retain the useful hand direction:
- five digits provisionally;
- opposable thumb provisionally;
- moderately elongated fingers;
- tapered distal phalanges;
- pointed restrained claws continuing the digit axis.

Hands must remain credible for:
- weapons;
- tools;
- crafting;
- climbing interactions where ordinary humanoids could climb;
- object manipulation;
- expressive gestures.

Claws do not automatically grant combat/climbing bonuses.

---

# 16. Feet

Retain:
- plantigrade baseline provisionally;
- broad stable contact;
- longer forefoot/toe contribution than Marchfolk;
- five toes provisionally;
- tapered distal digits;
- restrained claws;
- specialized palmar/plantar-style contact-scale architecture later.

Do not simply attach claws to human feet.

Develop the forefoot, toe spacing, distal taper and ankle/foot transition as Saurin anatomy while preserving stable plantigrade locomotion and equipment feasibility.

# 19. Structural angularity — important but controlled

Tyler correctly identified that the old body was excessively rounded.

Use the approved head lesson throughout the body:

**large plane → anatomical transition/ridge → neighboring plane**

rather than:

**rounded blob → rounded tube → rounded blob**

However:
- no external exoskeleton;
- no blade-like ribs;
- no spikes everywhere;
- no arbitrary fantasy armor anatomy;
- no loss of joint flexibility.

Use reptile observation as anatomical inspiration, not literal copying of one Earth species.

Potential inspiration categories:
- monitor lizards for axial/limb integration;
- iguanids for scale/ridge transitions;
- crocodilians for planar cranial/dermal logic and tail-root mass;
- large terrestrial lizards for limb and girdle relationships.

Saurin are their own fictional species.
