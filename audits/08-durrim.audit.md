# Audit: Durrim Character Design v1.0 (first pass complete)

Auditor: Claude. These are notes to check, not changes. Nothing here overrides the spec in `races/08-durrim.md`; Tyler decides what, if anything, changes.

## Open notes

- **Height compared with the brief and the game.** The original brief's roster put Durrim at 0.65× Marchfolk (about 112 cm). The new 137 cm reference is about 0.79×, and the range starts at 122 cm, so the spec supersedes the brief's height as the newer authority (§36 retires the old multiplier as a mass signal). The game currently draws Durrim at 0.82× (about 142 cm), which is within the new range and close to the reference, so unlike Fenn and Vael the prototype runs the right way.
- **Overlap with Sagekin (§5).** Sagekin start at 152 cm, so the Durrim-Sagekin overlap is only at the 152 cm edge. The Marchfolk overlap (147–152 cm) is the real one. The Halvren central envelope also starts at 152 cm.
- **Current game compared with Part 1 (not a change).** The prototype description reads "Stout mountain folk who live in halls carved from the living rock. Tough as stone, steady of hand, and loyal to the bone," which encodes mountain living, toughness and loyalty (§39). The trait text "Deep lungs from the mines (longer breath), but they swim like stones" (breath 1.25×, swim 0.85×) gives a mining and lung-size justification that §42 says not to assume yet. The attributes lean Strength 90 and Stamina 90, which §41 says anatomy doesn't automatically justify. All stay as prototype values under the authority rule, the open racial-attributes decision and the §42 legacy-trait review.
- **Consistent with earlier guardrails.** Part 1 matches the Vael v1.1 §27 warning (Durrim differ fundamentally in stature, bone proportions, limb contribution, center of mass, joints, hands and feet and torso) and the Elf Comparative Review's rule that Broad elves are never Durrim anatomy.

* **Resolved: Part 2 §54 now runs at about 152 cm as a Cross-Population Equal-Height Boundary Test (consistency-resolution patch). Original finding: a 150 cm Sagekin was outside the approved Sagekin range.** The equal-height comparison places Durrim, Marchfolk and Sagekin at about 150 cm, but the Sagekin spec's range is 152–208 cm, so a 150 cm Sagekin isn't valid Sagekin anatomy. Durrim top out at 152 cm and Marchfolk start at 147 cm, so 150 cm works for Durrim and Marchfolk. The simplest fixes are running the matrix at about 152 cm (the one height all three share, and Part 1 §44 already says 150–152 cm), or keeping 150 cm for Durrim and Marchfolk and adding Sagekin at 152 cm. Now corrected by the consistency-resolution patch.
* **Wording (minor).** §23's "greater palm contribution to total hand length than some taller humanoid populations" doesn't name the populations under the terminology rule. Part 1 §25 calls Durrim feet "moderate relative length" while Part 2 §31 says "relatively large foot dimensions compared with total stature." These fit together (large overall mainly through breadth and depth, per §33), but the reading isn't stated. Recorded as written.

- **Part 3 check: no contradiction with Parts 1–2 or universal rules.** The head-to-height contribution matches Part 1 §9, the beard and facial-hair separation matches Part 1 §38 and §37, and the non-elven ear rule matches the Elf Comparative Review's elven ear architecture. The comparisons name Marchfolk as the reference (cranial breadth §5, facial verticality §7). The Part 2 §54 Sagekin-at-150 cm item is still open.

* **Clarification check: no contradiction with Part 3, Parts 1–2 or universal rules.** The head-neck integration and depth tendencies extend Part 1 §9–10 and Part 3 §15 and §21. The soft face-body correlation matches Part 3 §26, and the lighting and neutral-reference rules match the universal lighting invariance. Two items to flag, not contradictions:
* **Layer letters collide (resolved: renamed to Facial Diagnostic Domains FD-STRUCT to FD-OBS).** The diagnostic facial layers (A structural anatomy, B soft tissue, C skin, D hair, E presentation, F observational) reuse letters A–D from the Character Architecture Layers (A biological anatomy, B skeletal frame, C physical composition, D personal presentation) with different meanings, so "Layer B" or "Layer D" is ambiguous without a qualifier. Clarification 2 §19 says they don't replace the architecture layers, but the Marchfolk consistency resolution asked that "layer" always be qualified. Naming them "diagnostic facial layer A–F" (or using different labels) would avoid confusion.
* **Existing facial-editor wording in approved specs (resolved: approved first-pass functional requirements with provisional control organization).** The universal facial architecture is now deferred until all 13 races are done, but approved Marchfolk v1.2 (three levels of facial editing and seven facial regions: head and skull, brow and eyes, nose, cheeks, jaw and chin, mouth and lips, ears) and Skarn ("the editor uses the same seven regions as Marchfolk") already describe player-facing facial controls. These may need to be read as provisional under the deferral rather than as the settled hierarchy.

- **Part 4 check: no contradiction** with Durrim Parts 1–3, the clarification patches, universal rules or cross-race rules. It uses the FD domain names correctly, keeps lighting invariance and the three age concepts, keeps age surface changes separate from structural depth (clarifications 2–3), and "naturally low-pigment variants if later justified" matches Part 3 §39's rule that natural silver or white hair stays distinct from aging. One mapping note, not a conflict: Part 4's biological, environmental and presentation split lines up with the universal Skin Appearance Layers (Natural, Environmental, Applied or Acquired), though Part 4 doesn't name them. The game's current Durrim breath trait text ("Deep lungs from the mines") still reflects the mining framing that §11 and §41 separate from biology, and stays as prototype.
