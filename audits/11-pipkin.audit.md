# Audit: Pipkin Character Design v1.0 (in progress)

Auditor: Claude. These are notes to check, not changes. Nothing here overrides the spec in `races/11-pipkin.md`; Tyler decides what, if anything, changes.

## Open notes

- **Part 1 audit: no contradiction needing immediate correction.** Pipkin stay clear of child anatomy, slender-Durrim, miniature Fenn and Grask, and Cogling pre-design; body fat, big feet, bare feet, hairy feet and culture aren't identity; "light" never means weak; short legs never mean slow and short stature never means a stealth bonus; equipment and world never scale; and the Durrim traits quoted in the triangle and boundary test match the Durrim tab and the Gorrund final clarification (compact structural concentration, vertically compact torso, shorter limb contribution).
- **Positive distinction from humans is thin (the main thing to settle in Part 2).** Every Pipkin comparative trait is defined against Durrim: lighter skeleton, less compact torso, smaller joints, lighter long bones, greater limb contribution, more moderate hands and feet. Against Marchfolk, the approved traits (preserved limb contribution, moderate torso, light-to-moderate construction, moderate hands) are close to ordinary human proportions, and §88 leaves the human relationship to "later refinement." The only traits that clearly differ from a scaled human are somewhat greater head contribution and modestly larger feet relative to stature, and those are exactly the traits Part 1 must keep from reading as childlike or caricature. That leaves the scaled-human test (PIP-BODY-16) and the human-child test (PIP-BODY-15) without a positive body anchor. This is the same kind of gap Gorrund had with Durrim; a Pipkin-specific body relationship versus Marchfolk (for example in torso shape, pelvis, limb segment balance or hand and foot structure) would give both tests something to check beyond "not a child, not a Durrim."
- **Head and child-read tension.** §10 allows a larger head contribution than taller humans, while §9 forbids an oversized cranium. Children also have a larger head relative to height, so this is the trait most likely to push toward a child read; Part 3 will need the adult craniofacial system to carry maturity, and the head-to-body ratio stays OPEN as intended.
- **Durrim boundary is a single point.** Pipkin max and Durrim min are both about 122 cm, so the equal-height test sits at one stature (like Gorrund–Sagekin at 208 cm).
- **Original brief vs Part 1 (not a change).** The brief's race table described Pipkin as "large head, strong legs, substantial feet, dexterous hands, low center of gravity." Part 1 keeps dexterous hands and substantial-but-not-caricature feet, softens "large head" to a somewhat greater head contribution that must not look oversized, and makes the low center of mass an emergent consequence with no bonus. The approved spec wins; the brief's wording is worth updating in the final terminology review, like Gorrund's "long arms."
- **Current game compared with Part 1 (not a change).** The prototype Pipkin class hasn't been re-read in this session; its values stay non-authoritative, with implementation-level verification deferred as with Gorrund.

* **Part 2 audit: no contradiction; resolves the Part 1 human-distinction, single-point boundary and "large head" notes.** Low-Set Compact Trunk Architecture gives the scaled-human (PIP-BODY-16) and human-child (PIP-BODY-15) tests positive anchors, and the choice supports the adult read: in human children the pelvis is relatively narrow and the thorax and abdomen relatively large, so a mature, relatively broad pelvis under a moderate thorax points away from a child silhouette. Pelvic breadth isn't the sole identifier (PIP-BODY-18, STRESS-07), feet and head are secondary, light construction isn't weakness, muscle and fat don't carry identity, Cogling isn't defined, correlations stay soft, and the Durrim contrast matches the Durrim tab and the Gorrund final clarification.
* **Sex-coding risk in the pelvis relationship (ambiguity worth a patch).** In human anatomy, a pelvis that is broad relative to the thorax and shoulders is one of the main sex-dimorphic signals (female-typical). §9 says the Pipkin pelvis isn't "feminized anatomy," but the core specialization is defined against "Marchfolk reference anatomy" without saying which sex, and Pipkin sex-related anatomy and dimorphism are OPEN. Two practical risks: male Pipkin might read female-coded to players who carry human expectations, and any future Pipkin pelvic dimorphism would stack on top of the racial trait. Stating that the pelvis-to-thorax tendency is measured within sex (male Pipkin against male Marchfolk, female against female), and that it's expressed through adult pelvic structure rather than a hip-to-shoulder ratio alone, would keep the racial trait separate from sex dimorphism. Logged OPEN.
* **"Low-set" is defined by relative importance (terminology, minor).** §4 defines low-set as "relative structural importance of the lower trunk and pelvic region," which is qualitative. The tests (thorax-pelvis relationship, limb-trunk relationship) carry it well enough for first pass; Part 5 or prototyping may want a concrete measure (for example pelvic breadth and depth relative to thoracic breadth and depth, or where the trunk's structural mass sits vertically).
