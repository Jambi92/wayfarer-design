## 3. Minor notes (non-blocking)

### 3a. FD domain wording

The AGREED definitions (Durrim consistency patch) and §101A differ in two small ways:

| Domain | AGREED definition | §101A wording |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FD-SURF | Skin surface, including "scars where appropriate" | — |
| FD-PRES | Cosmetics, tattoos, grooming, jewelry | Puts scars under FD-PRES |
| FD-OBS | Lighting, camera, expression, pose | "Camera, lighting, animation and rendering" |

Aligning §101A to the AGREED definitions when convenient would keep Cogling consistent with Pipkin and Durrim.

### 3b. Vault size never implies cognition

The new face-to-vault tendency means a relatively smaller vault compared with the face. Ordinary allometry pushes very small adults the other way (a relatively larger head and vault), so this is a deliberate racial relationship. That's fine.

It may be worth one line saying cranial-vault proportion carries no intelligence or cognitive meaning. That would match the existing culture firewall (§27, "intelligence is not inferred") and close off a stereotype reading in either direction.

### 3c. The ear term still says "compact"

Fine Folded Auricular Architecture is described as "a relatively compact adult ear." That's harmless now that the ear is explicitly secondary. Leave it for the Short-Race Review.
