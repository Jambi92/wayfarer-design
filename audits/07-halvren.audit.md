Auditor: Claude. These are notes to check, not changes. Nothing here overrides the spec in `races/07-halvren.md`; Tyler decides what, if anything, changes.

## Open notes

- **The current game compared with Part 1 (not a change).** The prototype draws Halvren with the shared Manny and Quinn mannequin at 1.0 scale, and the appearance record stores only frame, height, two colors and finish, with no genealogy, expression or ancestry data (§33). Its description reads "Born between two peoples and fully at home in neither. Agile and adaptable - natural wanderers," which assumes first-generation parentage and a fixed social identity (§4, §7) and uses stereotypes §31 rules out. Its attributes lean Agility 90 and Dexterity 85, which could read as encoding "graceful." All of these are prototype values under the authority rule and the open decisions on race descriptions and racial attributes, left unchanged.
- **Height scope for Part 2.** The source populations span roughly 157 cm (Fenn and Vael minimum) to over 220 cm (Aelari maximum, with Skarn's reference at 208 cm), and Marchfolk start at 147 cm. That's why §19 avoids a midpoint. It also means the world-compatibility rule (validate the approved target anatomy) may have to cover a very wide Halvren envelope.

* **Part 2 source check: no conflicts with the approved source specs.** All six comparison height ranges match their race tabs, including Skarn 183/208/229 cm and Sagekin 152/178/208 cm. The Skarn traits (broader clavicles, deeper ribcage, more substantial neck base and pelvis, larger hands and feet, more natural muscle volume) match the Skarn spec. The Sagekin traits match its tendencies (slightly longer limbs relative to torso, more linear silhouette, possibly narrower ribcage, not narrow or thin). The Fenn, Aelari and Vael traits match the Elf Comparative Review and its final clarification.
* **Skarn "muscle-volume potential" compared with composition independence (resolved: capacity versus current muscularity).** §9 lets Skarn ancestry contribute "greater natural muscle-volume potential" (which matches the Skarn spec's "more natural muscle volume"), while §29 and Part 1 §21 say composition stays fully separate from ancestry and "Skarn ancestry = muscular" is a stereotype. These can both hold if potential means a shifted valid range or distribution for muscle, not a default muscle amount, but that reading isn't stated. It matters for randomization weighting and for any composition range that depends on ancestry.
* **Wording under the terminology rule (minor).** A few §9–11 tendencies don't name their comparison target, for example Skarn "greater skeletal robustness," "deeper ribcage," "larger hands/feet" (presumably than other human-family ancestry or Marchfolk), and Fenn "greater skeletal gracility" (presumably than the other elven ancestries, per the final clarification). "Larger hands/feet" also doesn't say absolute or proportional. Recorded as written.
* **Game scale for reference (not a change).** The prototype's Halvren scale of 1.0 is about 173 cm against the new 178 cm reference (about 1.03× Marchfolk). That's close and in the right direction, unlike Fenn and Vael.

- **Part 3 source check: no conflicts.** Fenn, Aelari and Vael facial and ear tendencies match the Elf Comparative Review with its Part 2 terminology clarification and final consistency clarification (Fenn most lateral ear projection, Aelari lower than Fenn with no strict Aelari-Vael ranking, Fenn mandible light "within the elven range" with no strict Fenn-Aelari ranking). The Skarn facial tendencies match the Skarn spec (a slightly larger, more robust skull, somewhat stronger brow, more jaw mass, more substantial neck-to-jaw transition, all "tendencies, not requirements"), and §10's guard against required square jaws, heavy brows and large noses mirrors the Skarn spec's own. The Sagekin face (moderate to slightly longer face and somewhat higher forehead, overlapping heavily with Marchfolk) is covered by §5's "established population-level tendencies."
- **Skarn facial list is a subset (not a conflict).** The Skarn spec also lists a more substantial midface and cheeks and a somewhat larger nose. §10 names brow, jaw, cranial robustness and neck-head integration as examples ("such as"), so the other Skarn tendencies presumably still apply through §5.

* **Part 4 source check: no conflicts.** The human skin, undertone, hair and iris lists match Marchfolk pre-inheritance Part 2. The Fenn list matches the corrected Fenn range (Elf Review Part 3 clarification), the Aelari list matches the Aelari spec, the Vael skin families match Vael v1.3, and the Vael iris list matches Vael v1.3 §16 (Vael lists muted violet "where validated"). The elven iris list matches Elf Review Part 3 §17, and the silver-versus-aging rule matches the universal principle in Elf Review Part 3 §14. Held for the end-of-Halvren gap review, per your instruction.

- **Patch check: no new contradiction.** The normalized Fenn gracility, Fenn thoracic depth, Aelari height and Aelari neck terms match the Elf Comparative Review and its final consistency clarification. The Skarn terms match the Skarn spec. The capacity versus current-muscularity split reconciles Part 2 §9 with Part 1 §21, Part 2 §29 and HV-09. One note for accuracy, not a conflict: the Sagekin spec already lists "slightly less ribcage depth" (alongside a slightly greater leg share of height, slightly longer forearms, hands and fingers, a slightly shorter torso relative to stature and a somewhat more linear silhouette) among its tendencies relative to Marchfolk, so shallower Sagekin thoracic depth counts as "separately established" under the patch's "narrower ribcage" clause, as a tendency.
