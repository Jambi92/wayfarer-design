# Audit: Elf Comparative Review v1.0 (complete)

Auditor: Claude. These are notes to check, not changes. Nothing here overrides the spec in `reviews/elf-comparative-review.md`; Tyler decides what, if anything, changes.

## Open notes

- **The heights match the race tabs.** Fenn 157/181/211, Aelari 168/190/221 and Vael 157/178/203 cm all match the approved specs.
- **Waist wording (resolved).** Part 1 §7 gives Fenn a "longer waist transition relative to compact torso center." The Aelari spec (v1.0 and v1.1) says Aelari have a longer torso and waist than Fenn, and §8 repeats "longer waist transition" for Aelari. These read as consistent if the Fenn phrase is relative to their own compact torso and the Aelari phrase is relative to Fenn, but the two uses of "longer" have different reference points. Settled by the waist-transition clarification added to Part 1.

* **Unnamed comparison targets in Part 2 (resolved).** A few Part 2 phrases don't name what they compare against. §4 Fenn "relatively open visible orbital presentation" and §8 Fenn "more open" appear to mean more open than Aelari and Vael, but this isn't stated. §14 Fenn "lighter average lower-face mass" and Aelari "lighter average mandibular mass" probably mean lighter than equivalent humans (per §4) and possibly lighter than Vael. §23 Fenn "greater outward/backward projection" probably means greater than Aelari and Vael. Settled by the Part 2 comparative-terminology clarification above.

- **Fenn skin summary compared with the Fenn spec (Part 3 §5), resolved by the Part 3 clarification.** Part 3 summarizes Fenn skin as "broad earth/warm-natural pigmentation families." The approved Fenn spec's valid range is broader and more specific: fair and light, warm beige, golden and tan, olive, copper, bronze, light to deep brown and rich darker brown, with broad warm and neutral undertones and some cooler ones. "Earth/warm-natural" could be read as narrowing it by dropping the fair and light end and the cooler undertones. The Fenn tab stays authoritative for Fenn in the meantime. Relatedly, the Fenn and Aelari valid skin ranges overlap heavily (both include fair and light, olive, bronze and deeper brown), which fits §25's overlap rule, but means their population difference will come mostly from frequency and undertone, not valid range.
- **Gameplay attributes compared with §21 (not a change).** The game gives Aelari higher Wis and Int and Fenn sneak and swim multipliers. Part 3 keeps "wisdom" non-biological and leaves the Fenn traits for later balance review, so these remain prototype gameplay values, already covered by the open racial-attributes decision.

* **Fenn gracility wording (Part 4 §19 compared with Part 1 §4), resolved by the final consistency clarification.** Part 1 gives Fenn "greater average gracility" (ranked above Aelari and Vael), while the final matrix gives Fenn and Aelari both "strong average gracility." That could read as Fenn and Aelari now being level. Recorded as written.
* **Two unnamed comparison targets in Part 4 (terminology rule), resolved by the final consistency clarification.** §7 Aelari "longer average neck" presumably means longer relative to torso than Fenn and Vael (per Part 1 §12), and §26 Aelari "closer-to-skull profile" presumably means closer than Fenn and Vael.
* **Aelari maximum height in the current game (§16), now recorded as a known gap in the final consistency clarification.** The prototype applies Aelari scale 1.02 with a ±7.5% height slider, well short of a 221 cm target. That's the known uniform-scaling gap, left unchanged, but world-compatibility testing will need the approved maximum, not the prototype's.
