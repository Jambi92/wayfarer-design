# Saurin Character Design v1.0

**Status:** IN PROGRESS — Part 1 proposed for audit  
**Phase:** DESIGN ONLY — no UE5 implementation authorized

Saurin are the thirteenth and final playable race first-pass design.

# Part 1 — Biological foundation, stature and core skeletal architecture

## 1. Core rule

> **Saurin are a distinct reptilian humanoid population, not humans with scales, not upright monitor lizards, not miniature dragons, and not a biological embodiment of savagery, primitiveness or aquatic mastery.**

Their racial identity must survive removal of:
- scales/color pattern;
- tail silhouette;
- claws;
- clothing;
- cultural objects;
- animation personality;
- environmental context.

The skeleton and body relationships must carry a positive non-human identity before surface phenotype is added.

## 2. Design objective

Part 1 establishes:
- adult stature;
- overall skeletal organization;
- torso/pelvis relationship;
- limb architecture;
- hands and feet;
- tail as anatomy;
- posture;
- frame/composition compatibility;
- biological boundaries against existing races.

It does **not** yet decide:
- detailed craniofacial anatomy;
- scale morphology/coloration;
- eyes;
- detailed tail appearance;
- movement;
- swimming;
- breath-holding;
- racial gameplay bonuses;
- culture;
- class restrictions;
- UE5 implementation.

## 3. Positive body specialization — Counterbalanced Pelvic-Axial Architecture

Saurin body identity is provisionally anchored by **Counterbalanced Pelvic-Axial Architecture**.

The defining relationship is:

> **deep mobile thorax → elongated lower axial trunk → strongly integrated pelvis/sacral base → substantial counterbalancing tail**

This creates a body whose centerline continues functionally through the pelvis into the tail rather than ending visually at a human-like sacrum.

The tail is not an accessory attached to an otherwise human skeleton. It is part of the approved axial architecture.

## 4. Adult stature

Provisional standing-height envelope, measured to the top of the head in neutral upright stance and **excluding tail length**:

| Minimum | Reference | Maximum |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ~168 cm / 5'6" | ~188 cm / 6'2" | ~208 cm / 6'10" |

This is a first-pass biological envelope, subject to:
- cross-race validation;
- tail/world-space validation;
- animation validation;
- equipment validation.

Saurin are not defined by exceptional height. Individuals may overlap Marchfolk, Sagekin, Fenn, Aelari, Vael and Halvren stature ranges.

## 5. Scale-independent identity

At Marchfolk-matched height and neutral gray material, a Saurin must remain clearly non-human through:
- axial/pelvic architecture;
- tail integration;
- thoracic organization;
- limb-joint relationships;
- hand/foot anatomy.

Height cannot be the primary discriminator.

## 6. Axial trunk

The Saurin trunk is not a human torso with a tail socket.

First-pass direction:
- thorax trends moderately deep rather than extremely broad;
- lower axial trunk has greater longitudinal contribution than a typical Marchfolk relationship;
- ribcage-to-pelvis transition remains visibly integrated rather than strongly pinched at a human waist;
- lumbar/sacral organization must support the continuation into the tail;
- the abdomen remains flexible and adult, not barrel-rigid.

Exact vertebral count and rib arrangement remain OPEN.

## 7. Thorax

The thorax should support a distinctly reptilian-humanoid body without copying Gorrund axial mass or Vael thoracic depth.

Provisional tendency:
- moderate breadth;
- meaningful anteroposterior depth;
- long lower-rib/abdominal transition;
- less clavicle-dominant upper-body silhouette than humans.

This does not imply reduced respiratory capacity, increased breath-holding, or aquatic adaptation. Those questions remain separate.

## 8. Shoulder girdle

Saurin retain functional humanoid arms with a shoulder girdle suited to broad reach and overhead use.

Shoulder breadth varies by frame.

The shoulder silhouette should not require:
- exaggerated human clavicles;
- permanently hunched scapulae;
- wing-like shoulder blades;
- bodybuilder width.

Exact clavicular/scapular anatomy remains OPEN pending detailed comparative validation.

## 9. Pelvis and sacral base

The pelvis is a primary racial carrier.

First-pass direction:
- adult, structurally stable pelvic ring;
- comparatively strong posterior integration with the sacral/tail base;
- enough breadth/depth to support bipedal locomotion and tail loading;
- no juvenile narrow pelvis;
- no Gorrund-like massive load-bearing pelvis;
- no Pipkin low-set compact-trunk specialization.

The posterior pelvis should read as designed around a real continuation of the vertebral axis.

## 10. Tail

All baseline Saurin possess a biological tail.

The tail:
- emerges from integrated sacral/caudal anatomy;
- is muscular and articulated;
- tapers gradually;
- contributes meaningful mass;
- is not prehensile by default;
- is not a weapon by automatic racial rule;
- is not a decorative cosmetic toggle;
- is not automatically aquatic-specialized.

Provisional neutral tail length: approximately **55–80% of standing height**, measured anatomically from the caudal base to tip along the relaxed centerline.

Exact distribution and segment count remain OPEN.

Tail length must not be implemented later as a simple visual scale detached from mass, base dimensions and articulation.

## 11. Tail variation

Valid biological variation may include:
- total length;
- base breadth/depth;
- taper rate;
- segment proportions;
- muscular development;
- resting curvature within anatomical limits.

Relationships must be constrained.

A long tail with an implausibly tiny base or a very massive tail with inadequate pelvic/sacral support is invalid even if each individual control lies within its nominal range.

## 12. Tail and individuality

Tail anatomy must support meaningful individual variation without becoming:
- crocodile tail = swimmer;
- thick tail = strong;
- thin tail = agile;
- long tail = fast;
- short tail = civilized.

Those are unsupported stereotype/gameplay mappings.

## 13. Center of mass

The tail changes real body mass distribution.

Saurin center of mass must eventually be derived from:
- trunk proportions;
- pelvic architecture;
- limb proportions;
- tail mass/pose;
- muscularity;
- adiposity.

Part 1 does not grant automatic balance, knockback resistance, agility, turning or swimming benefits.

## 14. Lower limbs

Saurin are **obligate upright bipeds for ordinary locomotion**.

First-pass lower-limb direction:
- substantial but not Gorrund-massive long bones;
- moderate-to-long leg contribution;
- mature knees;
- distinct ankle/foot architecture;
- no permanently crouched “lizard stance.”

The legs must support an upright neutral stance without hiding the tail architecture through a forward hunch.

## 15. Plantigrade baseline

Saurin are provisionally **plantigrade**.

This avoids making digitigrade posture the sole source of reptilian identity and supports believable humanoid interaction architecture.

Their feet may still be distinctly non-human through:
- metatarsal relationships;
- toe proportions;
- breadth;
- pad/sole organization;
- nail/claw structures later.

Plantigrade does not mean human-foot anatomy.

## 16. Feet

First-pass foot architecture:
- broad enough for stable bipedal support;
- longer forefoot/toe contribution than Marchfolk tendency;
- strong heel-to-midfoot transition;
- five toes provisionally retained unless later anatomy review provides a compelling alternative;
- non-prehensile;
- no automatic webbing.

Exact toe proportions, claws/nails and surface anatomy are later-part questions.

## 17. Upper limbs

Arms remain humanoid in functional reach but need not duplicate human segment ratios.

Provisional direction:
- moderate total arm contribution;
- forearms may carry somewhat greater proportional contribution than Marchfolk without approaching Cogling's defining distal redistribution or Grask reach specialization;
- wrists structurally clear;
- hands distinctly non-human in proportion.

Exact humerus/forearm ratios remain OPEN.

## 18. Hands

Saurin hands are provisionally:
- five-digit;
- one opposable thumb;
- adult functional grasp;
- moderately elongated fingers;
- structurally defined knuckles/joints;
- non-prehensile beyond ordinary humanoid grasp.

Claw/nail anatomy is deferred to surface/extremity detail.

Hand morphology grants no automatic crafting, climbing, weapon, spellcasting or swimming advantage.

## 19. Joint scale

Joint dimensions should remain visibly adult and suited to the approved skeleton.

Saurin are not:
- fine-jointed like the Cogling tendency;
- structurally concentrated like Durrim;
- massively jointed like Gorrund.

Frame variation can alter joint breadth/depth within race-valid envelopes.

## 20. Skeletal mass

Saurin skeletal structural presence is provisionally moderate-to-substantial.

This means:
- more structural presence than a gracile Fenn tendency;
- less defining mass than Durrim or Gorrund;
- broad individual variation;
- no automatic muscularity.

Bone robusticity and muscular development remain separate dimensions.

## 21. Frame presets

Saurin support race-specific:
- Narrow;
- Balanced;
- Broad

skeletal frame presets.

They are editable starting points, not castes.

A Narrow Saurin remains Saurin through axial/pelvic/tail architecture.

A Broad Saurin cannot become Gorrund simply through breadth.

## 22. Composition

Saurin support broad independent variation in:
- Current Muscularity;
- Muscular Development Capacity;
- body-fat amount;
- body-fat distribution.

A Saurin may validly be:
- lightly muscled;
- heavily muscled;
- lean;
- high-adiposity;
- narrow;
- broad;
- or combinations thereof.

“Reptilian” does not mean lean, muscular or low-fat.

## 23. Soft-tissue mass

The body must support believable soft tissue over non-human skeletal architecture.

Adipose tissue is not prohibited by scales or reptilian ancestry.

No universal “dry lizard” silhouette is allowed.

Exact regional fat-distribution tendencies remain OPEN.

## 24. Sex-related anatomy

Selecting sex-related biological anatomy does not determine:
- height;
- frame;
- muscularity;
- body fat;
- tail length;
- tail thickness;
- face;
- coloration;
- hair-equivalent structures if any;
- culture;
- class;
- personality.

Population-level correlations may later exist as soft distributions, not hard creator dependencies.

Exact dimorphism remains OPEN.

## 25. Posture

Neutral Saurin posture is upright and adult.

The tail may require subtle whole-body compensation, but Saurin are not biologically required to:
- hunch;
- crouch;
- thrust the head forward;
- splay the legs;
- drag the tail;
- hold the arms away from the body.

**Anatomical Resting Alignment ≠ Cultural/Personal Body Language.**

## 26. Tail resting alignment

A neutral tail should project posteriorly from the integrated caudal base and settle into a gravity-consistent curve appropriate to its mass and length.

It should not require:
- constant ground dragging;
- permanent upward scorpion curl;
- rigid horizontal extension;
- decorative serpentine posing.

Individual pose and animation remain later questions.

## 27. Biological randomization

Biological Randomization must preserve coupled relationships among:
- stature;
- trunk proportions;
- pelvis;
- sacral base;
- tail base;
- tail length/mass;
- limb proportions;
- frame;
- composition.

Independent valid sliders may still create an invalid combined body.

Relationship-aware constraints are mandatory.

## 28. Presentation firewall

Part 1 biology cannot encode:
- tribalism;
- primitiveness;
- aggression;
- stoicism;
- coldness;
- cunning;
- predation;
- swamp identity;
- desert identity;
- seafaring;
- priesthood;
- warrior identity.

Those belong to culture, background, personality or occupation.

## 29. Dragon firewall

Saurin are not miniature dragons.

Part 1 does not approve:
- wings;
- horns;
- dragon ancestry;
- breath weapons;
- magical elemental organs;
- draconic facial architecture;
- automatic elemental resistance.

Any future fantastical trait requires its own explicit design decision.

## 30. Aquatic firewall

The legacy brief associates Saurin with **5× breath duration** and **fastest swimming**.

Part 1 records these as **legacy gameplay traits under review**, not anatomical conclusions.

This body design does not reverse-engineer:
- webbed hands/feet;
- flattened swimming tail;
- aquatic nostrils;
- enlarged lungs;
- hydrodynamic body shape;
- swimming-specific limb proportions

merely to justify those legacy bonuses.

If those traits are retained later, their biological/gameplay basis must be deliberately reviewed.

## 31. Comparative boundaries

### Marchfolk
Saurin are not humans with scales/tails. Axial-pelvic-tail integration and non-human extremity relationships must survive surface removal.

### Fenn
Saurin do not use elven gracility, long extremities or ear anatomy as identity.

### Vael
Meaningful thoracic depth may overlap, but Saurin identity is pelvic-axial/caudal rather than Vael compact deep-bodied elven continuity.

### Durrim
Saurin do not use compact structural concentration, shortened limb contribution or high joint mass as identity.

### Grask
Saurin are not rangy reach specialists. Moderate-to-long legs and modest forearm emphasis cannot become Grask global limb dominance.

### Gorrund
Saurin are not massive load-bearing humanoids. Broad Saurin retain different axial organization and substantially lower skeletal mass.

### Cogling
Any modest forearm emphasis is not Cogling Fine-Scale Elongated Articulation: Saurin do not combine fine shafts/joints with defining distal redistribution.

## 34. Part 1 identity statement

> **Saurin are a distinct reptilian humanoid population whose body is organized around Counterbalanced Pelvic-Axial Architecture: a deep mobile thorax continues through an elongated lower axial trunk into a strongly integrated pelvis and sacral base that supports a substantial articulated tail. Their upright plantigrade bipedal skeleton uses moderate-to-substantial structural mass, mature non-human extremity relationships and broad frame/composition variation without requiring a crouched lizard posture, dragon traits, aquatic specialization, muscularity or behavioral stereotypes. The tail is a real part of their mass and axial anatomy, but the race must remain recognizable even when the tail and surface phenotype are diagnostically hidden.**

Part 1 is **PROPOSED FOR AUDIT**.
