# Gorrund Character Design v1.0 (first pass complete)

This is the Gorrund specification, a distinct large humanoid population. It's design only, with no UE5 changes, and it began only after Grask v1.0 was recorded first-pass complete. v1.0 arrived in five parts, all complete: Part 1 (biological foundation, stature and massive load-bearing skeletal architecture), Part 2 (detailed body proportions, hands, feet and composition architecture, with its boundary and arm clarification), Part 3 (craniofacial anatomy, ears, hair and facial-hair biology, with its craniofacial and ear identity clarification), Part 4 (pigmentation, skin, hair color, eyes, aging and environmental appearance, with its pigmentation envelope clarification) and Part 5 (movement, animation, equipment, world interaction and final first-pass review, with its final body-anchor and prototype-scope clarification). **Gorrund Character Design v1.0 is FIRST-PASS COMPLETE**; implementation-level prototype verification is deferred until project files can be inspected, and no UE5 implementation is authorized.

# Part 1 Biological foundation, stature and massive load-bearing skeletal architecture

## 1–5. Core identity, anti-definition and the large-race triangle

> **Gorrund are a distinct large humanoid population defined by massive load-bearing skeletal architecture, high absolute structural scale, broad/deep body organization, substantial joints and strong axial-to-limb integration. Their identity comes from skeletal construction, not from mandatory muscularity, obesity, ugliness or exaggerated monster anatomy.**

Gorrund aren't enlarged humans, bigger Skarn, thick Grask, muscular trolls, fat giants, mandatory bodybuilders or obese characters, hunched ogres or primitive monsters, and they aren't slow, stupid or violent because they're large.

| Population | Comparative identity (not gameplay classes) |
| --- | --- |
| Skarn | **Large-scale powerful human architecture**: human biology at unusually large, powerful structural scale |
| Grask | **Rangy reach-oriented non-human architecture**: tall, limb-dominant, long-reach organization |
| Gorrund | **Massive load-bearing non-human architecture**: high absolute structural scale with broad and deep skeletal construction and strong load-bearing relationships |

"Massive" means biological structural scale first (skeletal breadth and depth, joint dimensions, axial scale, pelvic structure, limb-bone structural dimensions), never automatically muscular, fat or heavy-looking from clothing.

> **Gorrund skeletal massiveness exists independently from Current Muscularity and Body-Fat Amount.**

Low-muscle, high-muscle, lean, higher-fat, low-muscle and higher-fat, and high-muscle and high-fat Gorrund are all valid.

## 6–10. Stature and adult read

| Minimum | Reference | Maximum |
| --- | --- | --- |
| about 208 cm (6'10") | about 229 cm (7'6") | about 251 cm (8'3") |

This is a **FIRST-PASS BIOLOGICAL RANGE — SUBJECT TO CROSS-RACE AND WORLD VALIDATION**, genuinely large while distinct from Grask's rangy height identity. A minimum-height Gorrund overlaps several populations, a reference Gorrund overlaps upper Skarn and upper Grask, and a maximum Gorrund exceeds all previously approved anatomy, so **height alone must never establish Gorrund identity.** If about 251 cm survives validation, Gorrund become the tallest current playable population, superseding Grask's 239 cm only as the **current world-validation upper stature**, never a permanent maximum. Height is never uniform scaling; extra stature distributes through spine, torso, pelvis, femur, lower leg, neck and head-body relationships (detail in Part 2). Minimum-height Gorrund read clearly adult and maximum-height Gorrund stay coherent, without childlike head-body relationships, giant-human scaling or exaggerated fantasy-giant proportions.

> **Gorrund skeletal identity emphasizes load-bearing structural scale rather than Grask-style leverage/reach or Skarn-style powerful human anatomy.** It must be visible under neutral composition.

| Thoracic depth | A major dimension: **Gorrund trend toward substantial thoracic skeletal depth relative to stature and breadth, producing a genuinely three-dimensional load-bearing torso rather than a wide but shallow body** |

## 26–43. Limbs, joints, hands, feet and bone scale

Lower limbs visibly support the structure: large absolute femoral and lower-leg dimensions, substantial knees and ankles and large supportive feet, never tiny legs to emphasize the torso. Against Grask at equal height, Gorrund generally have lower proportional limb contribution and greater axial contribution, which isn't short limbs:

> **Grask use limb elongation more strongly as a stature/reach signal; Gorrund use overall structural scale and axial architecture more strongly.**

| Region | First-pass direction |
| --- | --- |
| Femur | Large absolute length and structural dimensions, credibly integrated with pelvis and knee; no short cartoon-ogre thighs |
| Lower leg | Proportionally coherent, never copying Grask lower-leg emphasis (femur-to-lower-leg split in Part 2) |
| Knees and ankles | Major load-bearing regions with substantial absolute dimensions, never giant knobs, armor-like bone or monster joints, and ankles never artificially narrowed to make the body look larger |
| Upper limbs | Large in absolute dimensions without Grask's proportionally reach-dominant architecture (segment relationships in Part 2) |
| Reach | **Large absolute reach is not the same as Grask-style increased reach relative to stature** |
| Upper arm | Large absolute length, high structural scale, strong shoulder and elbow integration, no disproportionately long upper arms |
| Forearm | Substantial and functional, never copying Grask forearm emphasis |
| Elbows | Substantial for the long-bone scale, never tiny human elbows on giant limbs, spikes or knobs |
| Hands | Large in absolute terms, structurally substantial and fully capable of fine manipulation, never mandatory fists, paws, claws or shovel hands (Part 2) |
| Feet | Large in absolute terms, credible and plantigrade (Part 2) |
| Plantigrade | Locked: **Gorrund are upright plantigrade humanoid bipeds for first-pass design**, with no digitigrade legs, hooves or ape-like feet without separate justification |
| Digit count | No unusual count invented; expectation is **five digits per hand and foot unless a compelling biological reason emerges**, not locked until the Part 2 audit |

> **Substantial joint structural presence appropriate to large load-bearing bones, without exaggerating the joints into monstrous anatomy.**

This is one of the strongest body signals across shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees and ankles. Long bones have greater absolute cross-sectional structural scale than smaller humanoids, with no numbers yet; prototyping validates plausibility, silhouette, movement, equipment and joint transitions. "Thick-boned" isn't technical language; use skeletal breadth, skeletal depth, bone cross-sectional structural scale and joint dimensions.

## 44–58. Frame and composition

Narrow, Balanced and Broad are editable starting configurations, not subtypes. **Narrow** Gorrund keep axial depth, load-bearing scale, substantial joints and Gorrund integration, never becoming Grask, Skarn or a giant human. **Broad** Gorrund stay plausible, avoiding maximum width everywhere, a rectangular torso and automatic muscle or obesity. **Balanced** is a creator starting point, never canonical, most correct or average in every dimension. Height and frame stay distinct across shorter, reference and taller with narrow or broad frames, within relationship-aware validity.

Gorrund don't require high current muscularity, and a low-muscle Gorrund stays structurally massive. Whether their muscular-development capacity differs from humans or other races is **OPEN**, never inferred from skeletal scale. High-muscle Gorrund stay Gorrund, but muscle is never the only reason they look massive, and neutralizing muscle preserves identity. Body fat varies broadly; higher fat layers over the anatomy (never "large belly = ogre identity"), and lean Gorrund never become Grask, Skarn or giant humans. High muscle with high fat is supported but isn't the canonical body, and low muscle with higher fat is supported with thorax, shoulders, pelvis, joints and limb structure still readable. Regional composition varies across neck, shoulders, arms, chest, back, abdomen, glutes, thighs and lower legs, with no creator-control hierarchy finalized. Sex-related anatomy and dimorphism are **OPEN**, never assuming human-identical dimorphism, huge males and small females, or one sex broad or muscular and the other narrow or soft; all share one Gorrund foundation.

## 59–70. Head, skin, hair, culture, intelligence, strength, legacy traits and movement

> **Gorrund body identity must remain recognizable even if the head is visually neutralized.**

It never relies on tusks, a giant jaw, tiny eyes, a huge nose or a monster face (detail in Part 3). No skin color is required (not green, gray, brown, pale or fantasy-ogre coloration), and Gorrund aren't assumed bald, hairy, bearded or sparse-haired. Biology never encodes Gorrund as primitive, violent, brutish, tribal, nomadic, cannibalistic, uneducated, unintelligent, mercenaries or laborers.

> **Gorrund body size, cranial appearance, posture and movement must not be used to imply intelligence.** No "dumb ogre" coding.

No gameplay strength is assigned from size; skeletal structure and muscle may inform physical systems later, but racial stats and gameplay consequences are **OPEN**. "Gorrund are slow swimmers" and "Gorrund are easy to spot while sneaking" stay **LEGACY GAMEPLAY TRAIT — SUBJECT TO LATER REVIEW**, with no biology invented: no high body density, poor buoyancy, lung differences, limb inefficiency, fear of water or cultural water avoidance, and no automatic inference that large size means worse stealth (future design may weigh visual size, cover, noise, skill, environment and balance). The locomotion baseline is upright humanoid bipedal, with no slow movement, lumbering gait, wide waddle or heavy stomp established.

> **Load-bearing skeletal architecture describes anatomy, not movement speed or animation tempo.** Animation is never slowed just to communicate mass.

## 71–79. Reach, equipment, world, camera and collision

Anatomical, interaction, combat and camera or targeting reach stay separate, and Gorrund's large absolute body grants no automatic gameplay range.

> **Canonical equipment dimensions do not automatically scale with the holder.** A Gorrund holding a Marchfolk-sized sword holds the same sword, which may look proportionally smaller, and that isn't an error.

Armor and clothing fit actual anatomy (torso depth, shoulder breadth, pelvis, joint positions, hands, feet, frame, composition), never uniformly enlarged human gear. Doors, ceilings, stairs, furniture, beds, ladders, tables, counters, tunnels and interaction spaces are validated against Gorrund anatomy, which is never shrunk to fit the prototype world. At about **251 cm (8'3")**, maximum Gorrund create real doorway, ceiling, furniture, camera, dialogue, equipment and collision requirements, which are legitimate constraints. World compatibility is never solved by permanent crouching, hunching or bent knees; spaces that can't accommodate them become explicit world or gameplay design issues. Seating is affected by height, torso depth, pelvic breadth, femur dimensions and overall body space, with no universal chair pose. Camera offsets are never multiplied by character scale, and collision is **OPEN**, never one uniformly scaled capsule.

# Part 2 Detailed body proportions, hands, feet and composition architecture

## 1–5. Core principle, signature and height construction

> **Gorrund anatomy must read as a coordinated massive load-bearing skeletal system, not as a human body enlarged uniformly or as independent width/depth sliders applied to a generic humanoid.**

The body signature comes from the combination of high absolute skeletal scale, broad and deep axial construction, substantial shoulder and pelvic architecture, large load-bearing joints, large long-bone dimensions, limbs that support the body without Grask-style reach dominance, and large integrated hands and feet; no single trait defines the population. "Axial" means structural emphasis through spine, ribcage, shoulder girdle, pelvic integration and torso-to-limb transitions, never a torso that visually overwhelms the limbs.

> **Gorrund generally possess a greater proportional torso contribution to total standing height than Grask at matched height, while retaining fully adult, functional limb lengths.**

At matched height, Grask express more stature through limb length, arm span, forearm and lower-leg contribution, and Gorrund through axial scale, torso structure, pelvic and shoulder integration and overall skeletal scale, without either being the mathematical inverse of the other.

## 6–22. Torso, shoulders, pelvis, spine and neck

Thoracic breadth, depth and vertical length, abdominal vertical contribution, waist skeletal relationships, and pelvic breadth and depth are independent, never one Torso Mass concept.

| Region | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Thoracic breadth | A strong population tendency toward substantial breadth, but **the lower valid end of Gorrund thoracic breadth must still preserve Gorrund identity through depth, joints, axial integration and overall skeletal scale** |
| Thoracic depth | One of the strongest positive signals: meaningful front-to-back ribcage depth relative to stature, never a cylindrical barrel chest |
| Depth vs belly | **Thoracic skeletal depth** (ribcage) stays separate from **abdominal soft-tissue projection** (muscle, visceral contour, fat); a lean Gorrund can have deep ribs without a protruding abdomen |
| Cross-section | Anatomically shaped, never circular, rectangular or boxy (morphology for later modeling) |
| Ribcage length | Substantial enough to integrate the large torso, never just a widened and deepened short human ribcage |
| Upper and lower ribcage | Upper and lower breadth, taper and flare vary, with no universal V or barrel |
| Waist | Varies within valid anatomy; narrower isn't human or bodybuilder, broader isn't obese or unfit |
| Abdomen | External shape comes from skeleton, muscle, fat amount and distribution and posture; no "ogre belly" |
| Shoulders | Skeletal breadth, clavicular contribution, scapular relationship, joint placement and thorax-to-arm transition are separate, never external circumference as the measure |
| Shoulder-to-pelvis | Continuous variation from more shoulder-dominant through balanced to more pelvis-present, not subtypes |
| Pelvis | **Gorrund pelvises possess substantial load-bearing skeletal dimensions and strong integration with both the deep axial torso and large lower limbs**, with meaningful breadth, depth and hip-joint scale; exact morphology **OPEN**. Not every pelvis is extremely broad, and a narrower valid pelvis still supports large hip joints and femora, upright gait and axial integration |
| Pelvis vs hips | Skeletal pelvic breadth, gluteal muscle and regional fat stay separate; external hip circumference is never a proxy |
| Spine | Supports the torso without permanent forward lean, exaggerated lumbar arch or hunch; neutral posture stays upright |
| Neck | Part 1's moderate vertical contribution and high integration kept, with length, skeletal breadth and depth and attachment varying; a low-muscle Gorrund still has substantial skeletal neck integration, never dependent on trapezius |

**Shoulder-girdle and thoracic architecture (RAC W1d, author-accepted October 5, 2026; `reviews/chatgpt-rac-w1d-author-decisions-continuation-order.md` §3):** relational architecture only; the girdle is the first link of Axial Load-Path Continuity (L736–742).
- **Girdle carried by the thorax (GO-G1; AD-G5):** girdle breadth is carried by thoracic breadth and depth, not by girdle overhang — shoulder-joint breadth ÷ thoracic breadth is not greater than the Marchfolk (MF) relationship; large absolute shoulder breadth comes from the large thorax (L41, L45, L738, L742).
- **Girdle wraps a deep thorax (GO-G2; AD-G5):** the scapulae sit on a deep, posterolaterally broad ribcage; the arm origin sits within the thoracic depth envelope, and the girdle's anteroposterior placement follows thoracic depth (L42, L45, L187).
- **Clavicle and girdle joints (GO-G3; AD-G5):** clavicle with substantial cross-section for its length; sternoclavicular, acromioclavicular and glenohumeral joints share Gorrund joint presence; shoulder-joint scale, upper-arm structure and thorax stay compatible (L74, L76, L253).
- **Neck–girdle integration (GO-G4; AD-G5):** a broad skeletal neck base integrated into the girdle and upper thorax, readable at low muscle and never produced by trapezius (L46, L192, L747).
- **Shoulder-to-pelvis variation (GO-G5; AD-G5):** the girdle–pelvis breadth relation varies continuously (shoulder-dominant ↔ pelvis-present) within ALPC-2 and ALPC-4; extremes that break continuity are invalid (L119, L188).
- **Frame (GO-G6; AD-G5):** Narrow reduces girdle, thoracic and pelvic breadth coherently; depth, joint scale and continuity stay (L241, L243, L746; UCCA L131).
- **Cross-race (GO-G7; AD-G6, AD-G7):** girdle (skeletal shoulder) breadth ÷ stature > Grask at matched height (derived from L40 and the accepted thoracic ordering, R2 L54); thoracic depth ÷ thoracic breadth > Skarn and > Grask (derived from L42 and P2O L24). Gorrund vs Skarn girdle breadth stays **undetermined**; that difference is architectural (GO-G8; R2 L56; AD-4).
- **Firewall (AD-G15):** girdle size and Axial Load-Path Continuity neither imply nor require higher Current Muscularity or Muscular Development Capacity, and neither muscle amount nor capacity may satisfy, rescue or fail any girdle or ALPC check (L21, L45, L82, L747; UCCA L154, L156). Girdle checks use skeletal acromial/glenohumeral landmarks, never bideltoid breadth (AD-G10). **Exact morphology and numeric envelopes remain OPEN** (L159).

**Pelvic architecture (RAC W1d, author-accepted October 5, 2026; `reviews/chatgpt-rac-w1d-author-decisions-continuation-order.md` §2):** relational architecture only, read at matched normalized stature against MF and Skarn (SK); the pelvic half of ALPC (L744–745). The pelvis grows in depth and hip-joint scale more than in breadth.
- **Basis (PV-D1):** a human-homologous underlying bone plan whose depth : breadth and hip-joint : breadth relationships depart from MF and SK as below, plus robust construction; never a uniformly enlarged human pelvis (L49). No decorative pelvic marker is authored.
- **Sacral link (GO-P1; PV-D19):** sacrum and lumbosacral junction broad and robust relative to the lumbar column, so the lower axial trunk transfers into the pelvis without a structural "neck"; human-homologous orientation; no exaggerated lumbar arch. Frame-level statement (L191, L736, L738).
- **Hip-joint scale (GO-P2a; PV-D14):** hip-joint and proximal-femur scale large in absolute terms; hip-joint scale ÷ iliac-crest breadth ≥ MF and ≥ SK; held at Narrow frame (L76, L189, L745, L746).
- **Hip-joint placement (GO-P2b; PV-D14):** hip joints sit under the axial load path (bitrochanteric ÷ iliac-crest breadth ≈ MF, no lateral outrigging); leg share < Grask, never short-legged (L217, L744, L745).
- **Iliac structure (GO-P3; PV-D14, PV-D17):** iliac-crest breadth bounded by lower-trunk breadth (the pelvis does not project laterally as an independent hip structure); robust construction; vertical contribution supports the large axial vertical contribution (≥ Grask relative to stature). No pelvic vertical direction vs Skarn is authored (AD-4) (L169, L738, L744, L773).
- **AP depth — depth-led pelvis (GO-P4; PV-D14):** AP depth ÷ iliac-crest breadth > MF and > SK, continuous with the deep thorax; held at Narrow frame (L42, L189, L243, L743, L744, L746).
- **Transverse breadth (GO-P5; PV-D14):** *(PV-D1/PV-D22 general acceptance)* "may trend substantial"; no single breadth identity; Narrow valid; breadth is not the ALPC carrier (L50, L189, L241).
- **Lower trunk (GO-P6; PV-D14, PV-D15):** thorax → lower trunk → pelvis with no abrupt step in breadth or depth; waist definition allowed; shoulder-to-pelvis balance free (L188, L743, L744).
- **Validation and scope (PV-D16, PV-D17, PV-D20, PV-D22):** canon is validated on skeletal/bony-landmark geometry; skin-surface pelvic readings are composition-inclusive diagnostics only (L190, L747–748). Construction is checked visually on orthographic renders for W1. No sex-related pelvic shift is authored (RA L53); the obstetric firewall is absolute (RA L122). **Exact morphology and numeric envelopes remain OPEN** (L49, L189).

## 23–38. Legs, arms and joints

Legs stay long enough for a tall upright humanoid, but **Gorrund lower limbs generally contribute less proportionally to standing height than Grask lower limbs at equal height**, never meaning short-legged. Femora have large absolute length and cross-sectional scale with strong hip and knee integration, stay compatible with sitting and locomotion, and never form a universal short-femur ogre silhouette. Lower legs have large absolute length, substantial scale and strong ankle integration without reproducing Grask's lower-leg emphasis. The femur-to-lower-leg relationship varies (slight femur emphasis, balanced, slight lower-leg emphasis) without extreme mismatches for variety, with ratios **OPEN**. Knees have high absolute scale suited to femur, lower leg and stature, never shrunk to emphasize limb mass or made bulbous, spiked or armor-like. Ankles have substantial absolute scale but may still look narrower than calf and foot; substantial never means uniform limb diameter.

Arms have large absolute dimensions integrated with the axial-heavy body:

> **Grask should generally exhibit greater arm length and arm-span contribution relative to total stature than Gorrund.**

Arm span stays appropriate to the anatomy, neither extremely short nor Grask-extended (distribution **OPEN**), and emerges from shoulder breadth and upper-arm, forearm and hand length, never one slider. Upper arms have large absolute length and structure with strong shoulder and elbow transitions, visible even at low muscle. Forearms are substantial and functional but, unlike Grask, never one of the strongest identifiers, with moderate upper-arm-to-forearm variation and no extremely short or Grask-like forearms (ratios **OPEN**). Elbows are substantial in absolute terms and coordinated with arm scale, never isolated knobs, and wrists integrate large hands with substantial forearms, never tiny or uniformly columnar.

## 39–49. Hands

> **Gorrund hands are large, structurally substantial humanoid hands designed for both load-bearing manipulation and fine dexterity.** They aren't monster paws.

> **Five digits per hand and five digits per foot**: four fingers and an opposable thumb, and five toes. This is locked for first-pass anatomy.

Hand length, palm length, breadth and depth, finger length and breadth, segment relationships, thumb dimensions and wrist breadth and depth are separate, never one Hand Size. Tendency: **Gorrund palms possess substantial breadth and depth relative to their overall hand length, producing a structurally powerful hand without eliminating dexterity** (not a hard requirement). Fingers stay fully articulated, capable of fine manipulation and integrated with the palm, never universally short, thick or stubby, with relative length, breadth, taper and knuckle presence varying and long-fingered Gorrund valid. The thumb supports opposition, precision and power grip, and dexterity is never reduced for an ogre stereotype. Nails are ordinary protective structures, never mandatory claws, talons or black monster nails. Hands are validated open, fisted, in precision pinch, handling coins, writing or tool grip, cups, one- and two-handed weapon grips and ladders, and a maximum-height broad-frame Gorrund must still plausibly handle small canonical objects. Across neutral hands: Skarn a large human hand; Durrim a relatively substantial hand for short stature; Grask a large hand integrated with long forearm and reach architecture; Gorrund a very large absolute hand with load-bearing breadth and depth; never relying on claws or nails.

## 50–58. Feet

> **Gorrund possess large plantigrade humanoid feet whose structural scale supports their stature and load-bearing body architecture without becoming oversized monster feet.**

Length, breadth, depth, heel, midfoot, forefoot breadth, toe length and breadth and the ankle-foot transition are independent. Feet are large in absolute terms, but foot length is never exaggerated to signal size; support comes from coordinated length, breadth, depth and ankle integration. Heels suit stature, gait and load transfer without comic oversizing, the forefoot may be broad but never a rectangular slab, and toes stay humanoid and non-prehensile, never clawed, extremely long or ape-like, with ordinary toenails. Feet are compared across Marchfolk, Skarn, Durrim, Grask and Gorrund in relation to the whole body, never designed in isolation. Foot, pelvis and leg architecture set the anatomical base of support, never an automatic stability bonus, reduced agility or knockback resistance (gameplay decisions).

## 59–68. Frame

Narrow, Balanced and Broad alter skeletal breadth relationships only, never directly setting height, muscle, fat, hand or foot size, face or personality. Population-level correlations with other dimensions may exist, but **a population-level anatomical correlation does not automatically create a hard creator-control dependency**; relationship-aware validity and weighted distributions replace rigid locks. **Narrow** Gorrund may reduce relative thoracic, shoulder and pelvic breadth within valid limits but keep meaningful thoracic depth, substantial joints, large long-bone scale and strong axial integration.

> **Narrow skeletal breadth does not automatically mean low skeletal depth.**

## 69–84. Composition

High muscle wraps the skeleton without erasing joint relationships, creating a generic bodybuilder, turning the Gorrund into Skarn or becoming the racial identifier. ## 1–18. Transverse Structural Continuity and the Durrim distinction

**Transverse Structural Continuity** is project anatomical terminology, not a creator-facing slider name. ## 1–11. Axial Load-Path Continuity

Massive load-bearing architecture, broad and deep axial construction, substantial shoulders and pelvis, large joints and long bones can overlap Durrim conceptually, and absolute size isn't a sufficient positive distinction, so a Gorrund-specific body relationship is added:

> **Gorrund possess pronounced axial load-path continuity: the shoulder girdle, thoracic structure, lower axial trunk, pelvis and proximal lower limbs form a strongly integrated vertical load-bearing chain, with skeletal transitions that preserve substantial structural continuity from the upper torso through the pelvis into the legs.**

**Axial Load-Path Continuity** is project anatomical terminology, not a creator-facing slider. The body reads as one load-bearing system, **shoulder girdle → thorax → lower axial trunk → pelvis → hip and proximal femur**, visually and biomechanically communicating continuous support rather than individually enlarged regions attached together. It never means uniform torso width, no waist, a rectangular or barrel body, a giant pelvis or shoulders, a thick abdomen, high fat or muscle, column-shaped legs or one "massiveness" slider.

| Transition | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Shoulder to torso | The substantial shoulder girdle integrates with the thorax, never huge shoulders on a comparatively ordinary torso as the racial recipe |
| Thorax to lower trunk | The broad, deep thorax transitions without abrupt skeletal collapse, while visible waist definition, narrower frames, low fat and athletic composition stay valid; the relationship is skeletal load paths, not circumference |
| Lower trunk to pelvis | The pelvis reads as integrated with the axial body, not an independently widened hip structure; morphology stays **OPEN** but its relationship to the torso is now positively constrained |
| Pelvis to leg | Hip-joint scale, proximal femur, pelvis and upper-leg dimensions suit receiving load from the pelvis and axial skeleton, never mandatory enormous thighs |
| Narrow frame | Keeps the continuity: reduced breadth never removes thoracic depth, joint scale, pelvic and proximal-limb integration or structural continuity (a critical identity test) |
| Low muscle | The continuity is especially readable through skeleton; it fails if it appears only once muscle is added |
| Fat | Low fat reveals rather than creates the structure; high fat may soften the silhouette without eliminating the skeletal relationships |

**ALPC operational definition (RAC W1d, author-accepted October 5, 2026; `reviews/chatgpt-rac-w1d-author-decisions-continuation-order.md` §3, AD-G8/AD-G9):** ALPC is a validator over resolved skeletal values, never a creator control (L738; UCCA L92). It is demonstrated when the **normalized skeletal breadth and depth profile** of the axial chain — girdle → thorax → lower axial trunk → pelvis → hip/proximal femur — has a valid humanoid shape, carries proportionally more of the thoracic dimension down into the lower trunk, pelvis and proximal femur than the Marchfolk (MF) reference profile does, does not take its upper end from girdle overhang, and keeps this under Narrow frame and low composition. Every criterion is a ratio to the body's own thorax, compared **only in direction** with the MF profile shape; height, frame breadth and composition cannot satisfy it by themselves.
- **Stations** (external skeletal landmarks; RA L122): S1 girdle (acromial/glenohumeral level); S2 thorax maximum (TB, TD); S3 lower thorax (inferior costal margin); S4 lower axial trunk (narrowest skeletal level between S3 and S5); S5 pelvis upper (iliac crest; pelvic AP at crest); S6 pelvis–hip (hip-joint centres; inter-acetabular and bitrochanteric breadth; pelvic AP at hip level); S7 proximal femur (subtrochanteric shaft; femoral head/neck scale).
- **ALPC-0 valid shape (validity screen; MF also passes):** breadth non-increasing S2→S4 and non-decreasing S4→S6; at most one local minimum (at S4) between S2 and S6; no local minimum at S5; no step at S1→S2; at reference S4 < S2 (L182, L738, L743).
- **ALPC-1 no abrupt skeletal collapse:** S3 ÷ S2 and S4 ÷ S2, breadth and depth, greater than MF's (L183, L736, L743).
- **ALPC-1b ribcage length (AD-G9, adopted):** ribcage vertical length ÷ S2 breadth not less than MF's — the ribcage is not widened without lengthening (L183, L773).
- **ALPC-1c costal–iliac gap (AD-G9): report-only**, never a pass condition (it could press toward "no waist", L738).
- **ALPC-2 pelvis integrated, two-sided:** (a) no collapse — S5 ÷ S2 and S6 ÷ S2 (breadth) and pelvic AP ÷ TD not less than MF's; (b) no independent widening — S5 ÷ S4 and S6 ÷ S4 (breadth) not greater than MF's (L49, L189, L738, L744).
- **ALPC-3 pelvis → proximal femur:** femoral head/neck scale ÷ S6 pelvic breadth and subtrochanteric shaft breadth ÷ femur length not less than MF's; thigh soft tissue excluded (L76, L189, L253, L745).
- **ALPC-4 girdle → thorax (= GO-G1):** S1 shoulder-joint breadth ÷ S2 TB not greater than MF's (L742).
- **ALPC-5 frame invariance:** ALPC-0…4 pass on GOR-BODY-04 (Narrow) and GOR-BODY-12; depth criteria pass on GOR-BODY-14 at least against MF (L179, L243, L746; UCCA L131).
- **ALPC-6 composition invariance:** ALPC-0…4 pass on the skeleton/skeletal proxy, and the skin profile of GOR-BODY-16 (low muscle, low fat) shows the same directions; a body that passes only at high muscle fails; soft-tissue measures are never used (L45, L190, L264, L747, L748; UCCA L156).
- **ALPC-7 separation from Skarn (architectural; AD-1 carrier):** on equal-height Broad Skarn, the Gorrund ALPC-1 and ALPC-2(a) ratios are greater than Skarn's beyond the accepted method marginal threshold. This is an architecture comparison, not torso or limb share, and does not touch AD-4 (L292, L700, L779; P2O L24).
- **ALPC-8 separation from Durrim:** the existing matched-display silhouette test (L775) applies on the same stations.
- **Firewall (AD-G15):** ALPC neither implies nor requires higher Current Muscularity or Muscular Development Capacity (L21, L82, L747). Measurement method and pass bookkeeping: RMQ RM-LR-06. **Exact morphology and numeric envelopes remain OPEN.**

## 12–17. Durrim body distinction

Approved Durrim anatomy is unchanged: compact body architecture, high skeletal presence relative to stature, a broad, deep, vertically compact torso, strong torso-pelvis continuity, coordinated shorter limb contribution and lower center-of-mass anatomy.

|  | Durrim | Gorrund |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary structural specialization | **Compact structural concentration**: substantial skeletal presence compressed into short adult stature, a vertically compact torso and coordinated reduced limb contribution | **Axial load-path continuity** across shoulder girdle, thorax, lower trunk, pelvis and proximal lower limbs within a massive tall-body architecture |
| Limb contribution | Coordinated shorter limb contribution relative to the compact body | Not shorter: limbs stay fully substantial contributors to a tall adult body, while less reach-dominant than Grask |
| Torso verticality | Vertically compact torso | Not compact: substantial ribcage vertical length and large axial vertical contribution suited to a tall body, so a Gorrund is never a vertically scaled Durrim |

The distinction is never merely a small versus a large structural body. **Matched-display silhouette test:** Durrim and Gorrund silhouettes normalized to similar displayed height, with muscle, fat, clothing, face, hair and presentation neutralized, show Durrim compact concentration, a vertically compact torso, reduced limb contribution and short-stature proportions versus Gorrund axial load-path continuity, greater vertical axial development, fully substantial tall-body limb contribution and distinct shoulder-thorax-pelvis-proximal-leg integration; identical silhouettes after normalization fail. **Equalized torso-breadth test:** where useful, Durrim and Gorrund with similar normalized torso breadth stay distinct through torso vertical relationships, limb contribution, pelvic relationships and shoulder-thorax and pelvis-leg integration, never breadth alone.

## 18–20. Identity update and face-body relationship

> **Their massive body architecture is further distinguished by axial load-path continuity: coordinated structural integration from the shoulder girdle through the thorax and lower axial trunk into the pelvis and proximal lower limbs. This separates Gorrund from Durrim compact structural concentration as well as from Grask limb-dominant reach specialization and Skarn large-scale human anatomy.** (Supplements the Part 5 §128–135 identity statement; read the two together.)

**Axial Load-Path Continuity** (body) and **Transverse Structural Continuity** (face) describe different anatomical systems and never collapse into one universal Gorrund slider; they may share a design language without being mechanically coupled, and more body continuity never requires more facial continuity, each varying independently within relationship-aware Gorrund limits.
