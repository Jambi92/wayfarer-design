# Pipkin Character Design v1.0

**Status:** IN PROGRESS  
**Approved/authored:** Parts 1–2  
**Implementation:** DESIGN ONLY

## Part 1 — Biological Foundation, Stature & Core Skeletal Architecture

### Core rule
> Pipkin are not humans scaled down to halfling height, child-proportioned adults, slender Durrim, or small Cogling.

### Short-race direction
- Durrim: compact structural concentration; high skeletal presence; broad/deep vertically compact torso; substantial joints; coordinated shorter limb contribution.
- Pipkin: light compact adult proportionality; short adult stature; lower skeletal structural presence than Durrim; comparatively lighter torso; proportionally sustained limbs; smaller adult joint architecture.
- Cogling remains undesigned. Do not predefine Cogling to differentiate Pipkin.

### Provisional stature
- Minimum: ~91 cm / 3'0"
- Reference: ~107 cm / 3'6"
- Maximum: ~122 cm / 4'0"
- First-pass biological range, subject to cross-race and world validation.
- ~122 cm Pipkin/Durrim contact is a **Cross-Population Equal-Height Boundary Test**, not a broad overlap.

### Adult-read requirements
Adult Pipkin must never rely on juvenile human anatomy. Do not use oversized cranium, huge eyes, tiny jaw, juvenile shoulder/pelvic relationships, childlike hands/feet, or juvenile fat distribution as racial shorthand. Somewhat greater head contribution relative to total height may occur, but it is secondary and cannot establish maturity.

### Skeletal direction
Pipkin combine short adult stature, comparatively light skeletal construction, moderate torso compactness, preserved functional limb contribution, smaller adult joints, and coherent adult hands/feet. "Light" describes skeletal breadth/depth, joint dimensions, and long-bone structural dimensions relative to Durrim; it never means weak, frail, thin, underweight, or delicate.

### Limbs and extremities
- Limb contribution remains comparatively preserved versus Durrim; this does not mean unusually long limbs.
- Arms/legs are short absolutely but adult and functionally proportioned.
- Hands are adult, fully dexterous, moderate in size for stature.
- Feet are adult, plantigrade, and may be somewhat substantial relative to stature, but never exaggerated caricature feet.
- Five digits per hand and foot are locked; ordinary protective nails.
- Barefootedness and hairy feet are not biological requirements.

### Frame and composition
Narrow/Balanced/Broad skeletal frames are supported. Frame is independent from height, current muscularity, body-fat amount/distribution, face, hair, personality, and class. Full adult muscularity and body-fat variation are valid. Muscular-development capacity and sex-related anatomy/dimorphism remain OPEN.

### Gameplay/culture
Culture, personality, intelligence, food, farming, hospitality, thievery, sneakiness, rural life, and similar tropes are not biology. The legacy “harder to notice while sneaking” trait remains a gameplay trait subject to later review; small size does not automatically approve stealth benefits. Movement speed, reach consequences, collision, equipment practicality, cameras, mounts, and size gameplay remain OPEN. Canonical equipment and world objects do not automatically scale to Pipkin.

## Part 2 — Detailed Body Proportions, Human Differentiation, Hands, Feet & Composition Architecture

### Positive Pipkin body specialization
> **Pipkin possess a low-set compact trunk architecture in which a mature, structurally broad pelvis contributes strongly to the central body silhouette while the thorax remains comparatively moderate in breadth and depth and the limbs retain substantial proportional contribution to total stature.**

Internal project shorthand: **Low-Set Compact Trunk Architecture**. This is anatomical design terminology, not a creator-facing slider.

### Meaning
The core relationship is:
**moderate thorax -> compact lower trunk -> mature structurally broad pelvis -> proportionally sustained limbs**

"Low-set" refers to the relative structural importance of the lower trunk/pelvic region, not sagging posture, low shoulders, crouching, heavy belly, or short legs. "Compact trunk" does not mean Durrim-like vertical compression, barrel torso, no waist, round body, or high body fat.

### Marchfolk differentiation
At normalized displayed height:
- Marchfolk retain approved human adult torso/pelvis/limb relationships.
- Pipkin show more compact central trunk organization, greater pelvic structural contribution relative to thoracic scale, sustained limbs despite short stature, and a distinct thorax-to-pelvis relationship.

Pipkin therefore must not reduce to ordinary human proportions miniaturized to ~1 meter.

### Thorax and pelvis
- Thorax is fully adult, functionally substantial, moderate in breadth/depth.
- Pelvis carries greater structural importance relative to thorax than Marchfolk reference anatomy as a population tendency.
- Pelvis has mature three-dimensional adult anatomy; exact morphology remains OPEN.
- Pelvic breadth is skeletal and must not be confused with sex, gluteal muscle, fat distribution, or external hip circumference.
- Thorax-to-pelvis transition remains coherent; no small-chest/huge-hip caricature.
- Lower valid pelvic breadth must still read Pipkin, so hip width alone cannot carry identity.

### Durrim differentiation
Durrim: vertically compact broad/deep thorax, strong torso-pelvis continuity, high skeletal structural presence, reduced limb contribution.

Pipkin: moderate thoracic scale, low-set compact trunk, greater pelvic contribution relative to thorax, lighter skeletal construction, greater proportional limb contribution.

Durrim pelvis participates in compact structural concentration; Pipkin pelvis participates in low-set compact trunk architecture with sustained limb contribution.

### Limbs
Pipkin legs contribute more to standing height than Durrim at matched height, but Pipkin are not required to exceed ordinary human adult leg proportions. Femoral and lower-leg contribution remain meaningful and adult; exact segment balance remains OPEN. Arms remain adult and proportionate; no Grask-like forearm or reach specialization. Arm-span distribution remains OPEN.

### Hands
> Pipkin possess fully adult dexterous hands with moderate structural scale relative to stature, neither Durrim-substantial nor childishly small.

Hand length, palm dimensions, finger dimensions, thumb, and wrist are independent relationship-aware dimensions. Adult palm/finger/joint/wrist integration must distinguish Pipkin hands from juvenile hands rather than size alone.

### Feet
> Pipkin possess plantigrade adult humanoid feet with modestly elevated structural contribution relative to total stature, supporting balance and locomotion without becoming exaggerated fantasy-halfling feet.

Foot contribution may trend somewhat larger relative to stature than Marchfolk adult reference anatomy, but this is secondary and variable. Identity must survive lower-valid foot size. Length, forefoot breadth, midfoot, heel, depth, and ankle are distinct dimensions. Larger feet cannot attach through implausibly tiny ankles.

### Composition
Skeletal Frame, Current Muscularity, Body-Fat Amount, and Body-Fat Distribution remain separate. Muscle wraps the approved skeleton and cannot turn Pipkin into Durrim. Higher fat cannot create Pipkin identity. Low-muscle/low-fat Pipkin must remain mature adults. Regional muscularity and fat distribution remain variable. Muscular-development capacity remains OPEN.

### Human-adult diagnostic
Normalize Marchfolk and Pipkin displayed height and control frame/composition/presentation. Hide head/hands/feet where useful. Pipkin must retain low-set compact trunk organization, mature pelvic contribution, distinct thorax/pelvis relationship, and coordinated limb/trunk relationships. If the bodies become identical, FAIL.

### Human-child diagnostic
At similar actual height, compare adult Pipkin to a human child without relying primarily on the head. Check mature thorax, shoulder girdle, pelvis, limb segmentation, joint maturity, hands, and feet. Detailed adult craniofacial maturity must be reinforced in Part 3.

### Head terminology clarification
Earlier shorthand “large head” is superseded. Approved interpretation:
> Pipkin may possess somewhat greater head contribution relative to total stature within an adult proportional system, but an oversized head is NOT a defining racial trait.

### Durrim boundary
At ~122 cm:
- Durrim: compact structural concentration, vertically compact broad/deep torso, higher skeletal presence, larger joints, reduced limb contribution, substantial hands/feet.
- Pipkin: low-set compact trunk, moderate thorax, greater pelvic contribution relative to thorax, lighter skeleton, smaller joints, greater proportional limb contribution, moderate hands, modestly substantial feet.

Broad Pipkin vs Narrow Durrim at this boundary is mandatory.

### Combined validity
Relationship-aware constraints must cover thorax/pelvis, pelvis/proximal leg, torso vertical contribution/limbs, femur/lower leg, shoulder/thorax, hand/wrist, foot/ankle, and frame/joints. Do not create a Human->Pipkin master slider or max-everything preset. Population tendencies remain soft unless anatomy requires a dependency.
