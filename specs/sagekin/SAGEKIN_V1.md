# Sagekin Character Customization v1.5 (first-pass complete)

This document opens with the working concept written before the Sagekin customization spec began. It is design only, with no UE5 changes. Sagekin are a fully human population with a distinct ancestral homeland. Their famous scholarly tradition is culture, never biology. The foundational direction below comes first, then v1.0 (racial anatomy, height and population variation), v1.1 (body proportions and anatomical relationships) v1.2 (facial anatomy, population variation and individual identity) v1.3 (skin, hair, cultural presentation and homeland visual language) v1.4 (presets, population-aware randomization and racial validation) and v1.5 (final validation and technical handoff). The next race was Fenn; Sagekin stay fully human, never pushed toward elven anatomy to look more different.

## 1. Geographic origin

Sagekin come from an ancestral homeland across the sea from the game's main region. The working idea is a warm southern or southeastern maritime region with an old urban civilization known for universities, archives, observatories, medicine, mathematics, navigation, philosophy, magical study and long-distance trade. Its geography, nation names, climate, architecture and history are left to worldbuilding.

## 2. Biology versus culture

This is a strict line.

| Biological (ancestry) | Cultural (tradition) |
| --- | --- |
| Population history and physical tendencies | The scholarly tradition, institutions, dress and grooming |

Intelligence, education, weakness, frailty, "soft hands" and scholarly occupation are never encoded in anatomy. A Sagekin can be a scholar, farmer, sailor, soldier, laborer, merchant, athlete, crafter or anything else. Other races may join Sagekin institutions where the worldbuilding allows.

## 3. Physical differentiation

Sagekin are fully human, with recognizable population tendencies distinct from Marchfolk. These show mainly through:

- craniofacial proportions
- cheek and mid-face structure
- eye-region anatomy
- nose structure
- jaw and chin relationships
- forehead and skull proportions
- population pigmentation ranges
- hair texture and density distributions
- natural hair-color and eye-color distributions
- subtle average body-proportion differences

No single trait is mandatory, and traits overlap with other human populations.

## 4. Body direction

Sagekin stay within broadly human proportions, with no extreme size identity like Skarn. One possible tendency to explore is somewhat longer-limbed proportions, pending visual validation, and never meaning thin. Sagekin support Narrow, Balanced and Broad frames; lean, muscular, athletic and high-body-fat physiques; wide height variation; and full age variation.

## 5. Pigmentation direction

A broad natural range suits a warm maritime homeland. The possible population center runs through warm beige, olive, bronze, brown and deeper brown complexions, with wide undertone variation. These are tendencies, not rigid ancestry rules. Exact ranges come in the detailed spec.

## 6. Hair

Sagekin have the full human range: straight, wavy, curly, and tightly curled or coiled. Frequencies may differ from Marchfolk, and dark brown and black may be common without being required. Hairstyle is never a biological racial marker.

## 7. Cultural presentation (developed separately)

Possible visual language includes sophisticated textiles, academic or institutional garments, practical travel clothing, jewelry, university insignia, family symbols, geometric or astronomical motifs, writing implements, scientific and magical instruments, and distinctive grooming. None of it is needed to look biologically Sagekin.

## 8. Existing description

Phrases like "brilliant minds, soft hands, impatient with fools" are treated as in-world stereotypes or sayings, not biological facts. Intelligence, personality, occupation and weakness are never anatomy.

## 9. Design goal

A Sagekin stripped of hairstyle, clothing, jewelry, occupation and cultural presentation still has a plausible population-level ancestry, close enough to other humans that individual traits overlap naturally. There is no exaggerated caricature just to make them look different.

# v1.0 Racial anatomy, height and population variation

This is design only, built on the foundational direction above and the universal framework.

## 1. Biological foundation

Sagekin are a geographically distinct human population, never defined by scholarship, intelligence, weakness, frailty, occupation or personality. Their differences from Marchfolk are subtler than Skarn's, and heavy overlap is intentional.

## 2. Height (provisional)

| Minimum | Reference | Maximum |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 152 cm (5'0") | 178 cm (5'10") | 208 cm (about 6'10") |

Height overlaps heavily with Marchfolk and is never the main racial marker. These values await visual and technical validation.

## 3. Proportional tendencies (population, not mandatory)

Compared with Marchfolk, Sagekin trend toward:

- slightly taller average stature
- slightly longer limbs relative to torso
- a slightly more linear average silhouette
- a possibly somewhat narrower average ribcage
- normal human hands and feet
- broad individual variation

None of this is exaggerated into elf-like anatomy.

> **Clarified by v1.1 §1 (Pass 2 AC-6):** hands and feet remain human anatomy while the population may trend toward longer forearms, hands and fingers; the ribcage tendency is reduced depth, not an undefined global narrowing. The bullets above are kept as history.

## 4. Skeletal frames

Narrow, Balanced and Broad all apply within Sagekin ranges. Broad Sagekin stay possible, and Sagekin ancestry never means narrow or thin.

## 5. Limb proportions

Subtle population differences are explored in arm length, forearm proportion, leg length, thigh-to-lower-leg relationship, torso length, and hand and foot size. The working direction is a slightly longer-limbed average, while shorter-limbed and longer-torso Sagekin stay supported.

## 6. Physical composition

Sagekin have the full human range of muscle, fat distribution, regional development, conditioning and age-related composition. Their scholarly reputation never reduces Muscular Development Capacity.

## 7. Craniofacial foundation

Sagekin will have their own distributions for skull proportions, forehead, eye region, cheeks and mid-face, nose, and jaw and chin. These overlap strongly with Marchfolk, and no single feature is a definitive marker. Detailed ranges come in a later facial spec.

## 8. Pigmentation

The warm maritime direction stays, with broad pigmentation and undertone variation. The population center may differ from Marchfolk but overlaps with it. Skin color alone is never a racial marker.

## 9. Cultural independence

Without scholarly clothing, jewelry, insignia, hairstyle, cultural markings or accessories, a Sagekin is still biologically valid. Because both are human populations, some individuals may look ambiguous without cultural or family context, and that is intentional.

## 10. Validation (later)

| Marchfolk | Sagekin |
| --- | --- |
| 178 cm | 178 cm |
| Narrow | Narrow |
| Balanced | Balanced |
| Broad | Broad |
| Muscular | Muscular |
| High body fat | High body fat |
| Neutralized presentation | Neutralized presentation |

Unlike Skarn, individual pairs don't have to be unmistakably different. The goal is believable population-level distinction, not exaggerated separation.

# v1.1 Body proportions and anatomical relationships

This is design only, preserving v1.0, the foundational direction and all universal rules.

## 1. Silhouette direction (population tendencies only)

Sagekin stay fully human. Compared with the Marchfolk reference, they trend toward:

- a slightly greater leg share of height
- slightly longer forearms, hands and fingers
- a slightly shorter torso relative to stature
- slightly less ribcage depth
- a somewhat more linear silhouette

Sagekin are never defined by thinness.

## 2. Elven boundary (cross-race rule)

Sagekin customization stays within human proportional ranges and never reproduces Fenn or Aelari central-tendency anatomy, which will get more strongly different skeletons later. Pointed ears, exaggerated limbs, very light bones and other elven traits are never used to set Sagekin apart.

**Ears (Pass 2 AC-4):** Sagekin follow the Marchfolk human-family auricular anatomical foundation (Marchfolk Part 2 §3–4) unless this spec explicitly modifies a tendency. This does not make Sagekin head anatomy Marchfolk-equivalent.

## 3–5. Region controls

| Region | Controls to explore | Sagekin tendency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Torso and ribcage | Torso length, ribcage width and depth, waist length and transition, shoulder width, pelvis width | Slightly shorter, shallower torso relative to height. Broad Sagekin keep genuinely broad torsos |
| Arms, hands and fingers | Arm length, upper-arm and forearm proportion, hand length and breadth, finger proportion | Somewhat longer fingers and slightly narrower hands, subtle and human |
| Legs and feet | Leg length, thigh and lower-leg proportion, thigh and calf thickness, foot length and breadth | Somewhat greater leg share of height. Feet stay structurally believable |

## 6. Physical composition

The full human range of muscle, fat, regional development and conditioning stays. These combinations are explicitly validated: Broad and highly muscular, Broad and high body fat, Narrow and muscular, and Short and Broad. Sagekin identity never depends on being tall, narrow, lean or weak.

## 7. Regional muscularity

The proposed universal regional-development framework from Skarn applies. The same muscle settings can give different silhouettes on different racial skeletons.

## 8. Derived mass

The proposed derived-mass principle still holds: height, skeleton, muscle, fat and proportions produce estimated mass. There's no primary weight slider, and exact estimated weight isn't shown until the system can calculate it credibly.

## 9. Anatomical relationships

Controls keep anatomy coherent, with no simple mesh stretching:

- Arm length keeps the shoulder, elbow and wrist relationships.
- Forearm changes keep the elbow and wrist transitions.
- Hand and finger changes keep the palm and joints.
- Leg length keeps the hip, knee and ankle relationships.
- Torso changes keep the spine and pelvis relationship.
- Chest depth changes ribcage volume, not surface inflation.

## 10. Permanent validation characters

| ID | Configuration |
| --- | --- |
| SG-01 | Reference: 178 cm, Balanced |
| SG-02 | Minimum height, 152 cm |
| SG-03 | Maximum height, 208 cm |
| SG-04 | Long-limbed, near the proportional boundary |
| SG-05 | Short and Broad |
| SG-06 | Broad and highly muscular |
| SG-07 | Broad and high body fat |
| SG-08 | Narrow and muscular |
| SG-09 | Long torso |
| SG-10 | Marchfolk-overlap edge case: a valid Sagekin that looks very close to Marchfolk (ambiguity accepted) |

## 11. Cross-race validation

Compare equivalent configurations across Marchfolk, Sagekin and Skarn, and later against Fenn and Aelari. Sagekin stay human in skeletal organization and proportional limits.

# v1.2 Facial anatomy, population variation and individual identity

This is design only, preserving v1.0–v1.1, the foundational direction and all universal rules.

**Classification (race-specific facial-control organization status):** where this spec defines facial regions, editing levels, control groupings, slider organization or other creator-facing control structure, that material is **APPROVED FIRST-PASS FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS / PROVISIONAL CONTROL ORGANIZATION.** Sagekin craniofacial anatomy, population tendencies, valid biological variation, anatomical relationships, ear biology, facial identity, validation, required customization capability and preset and randomization requirements stay approved as established. Only their organization into a final facial-control system is provisional, and the section is kept as input to the Universal Facial Customization Architecture Review after all 13 first-pass races are complete.

**UFCA status (UFCA Phase 2, October 5, 2026):** the universal facial creator organization is now canonical in `decisions/UFCA_V1.md`. The Sagekin facial control organization in this spec stays as approved requirements and is routed to its UFCA slots (`reviews/claude-ufca-08-phase1-architecture-audit.md` Appendix A); Sagekin anatomy, tendencies, validators, tests and OPEN items are unchanged. Ears use the human-auricle family (Pass 2 AC-4). Under UFCA AC-U1, "eye size" in this spec means bony orbit size (direct control) plus visible eye aperture (direct control); eyeball size is derived from the orbit and is never an independent slider.

**UFCA final closure (author decision, October 5, 2026; `reviews/chatgpt-ufca-final-closure-order.md`):** Eyebrow-hair biology is bound in UFCA slot 11 (ordinary variation in density/fullness, distribution/coverage, strand/coarseness character where ordinary hair biology supports it, and natural colour relationship to the individual's hair/pigmentation). Grooming, trimming, shaping, cosmetics, dye, styling and deliberate removal stay Personal Presentation. Eyebrows encode no culture, personality, class, attractiveness or sex stereotype, and no race-specific eyebrow morphology is implied; no numeric ranges are set.

## 1. Multi-trait population model

Sagekin facial identity comes from overlapping distributions across many traits, not one canonical face. The contributing traits are cranium, forehead, eye region, cheeks, mid-face, nose, jaw, chin, mouth and lips, pigmentation and hair. No single trait determines ancestry.

## 2–7. Region controls

| Region | Controls to explore | Sagekin tendency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Head and cranium | Head width and depth, cranial height, forehead height and slope, temple width, face length | Moderate to slightly longer faces and a somewhat higher forehead, overlapping heavily with Marchfolk |
| Eyes and orbital region | Eye depth, spacing, size, corner positions, angle, upper and lower lid structure, brow-to-eye distance, brow prominence | No mandatory eye shape |
| Nose | Bridge height, width and contour, length, projection, tip width, shape and rotation, nostril width and shape, alar flare | Each adjustable independently where feasible, with no rigid racial nose types |
| Cheeks and mid-face | Cheekbone height and width, cheek projection, mid-face depth, fullness | The body-to-face soft-tissue link still applies |
| Jaw and chin | Jaw width and angle, mandibular depth, chin width, height and projection | No mandatory configuration |
| Mouth and lips | Mouth width, upper and lower lip fullness, Cupid's bow, lip projection, corner position, philtrum length and depth | Keeps the nose, philtrum, lip, jaw and chin relationships |

## 8. Population frequency (proposed universal)

Biological validity and population frequency are separate. A trait can be valid for a race without being equally common. Frequencies are Common, Uncommon and Rare, and may shape NPC generation, race-aware randomization and curated presets. Players can always pick a valid uncommon or rare trait by hand.

## 9. Pigmentation direction (working, not final)

| Feature | Likely population center | Also possible |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skin | Warm beige, olive, bronze, brown and deeper brown, with broad undertones | Other natural variation |
| Hair | Black and dark brown especially common | Broader natural variation where valid |
| Eyes | Brown and dark brown common | Hazel, amber, green, gray and other human colors at lower frequencies |

Exact distributions wait for worldbuilding and visual validation.

## 10. Age and asymmetry

The earlier rules stay: age-aware facial anatomy, identity kept across aging, subtle Advanced asymmetry, Restore Symmetry and the proposed Naturalize Face. There's no generic elderly Sagekin face that replaces the individual.

## 11. Mixed ancestry (future)

Mixed ancestry isn't designed or built now. Architecture choices must not make it impossible later, especially between closely related human populations (Marchfolk, Skarn, Sagekin) and for the existing Halvren concept.

## 12. Validation principle

Sagekin ancestry is recognizable across a large sample, not in every individual. Overlap with Marchfolk is intentional, and there's no caricature for instant recognition.

# v1.3 Skin, hair, cultural presentation and homeland visual language

This is design only, preserving v1.0–v1.2 and all universal rules.

## 1. Homeland cultural pillars (provisional)

The scholarly tradition grows out of a whole civilization, never just universities and libraries.

| Pillar | Covers |
| --- | --- |
| Sea | Navigation, maritime trade, shipbuilding, fishing, travel, exploration |
| Written Word | Archives, literature, law, recordkeeping, education, scholarship |
| Heavens | Astronomy, calendars, navigation, mathematics, possibly magical study |
| Craft | Medicine, engineering, architecture, instrument-making, textiles, fine craftsmanship |

## 2. Skin

The universal four layers stay: natural, environmental, applied and acquired. A proposed universal rule is that natural pigmentation and environmental tanning or sun exposure are separate systems.

## 3. Regional environmental appearance

Where feasible, the face, hands, forearms, feet and general exposed skin can each show their own history. That can suggest a sailor, craftsperson, traveler, laborer, academic or sheltered city life without assigning an occupation, and it's never an automatic occupational preset.

## 4. Hair

Biological hair (texture, density, natural color) is separate from hairstyle. The biology can follow population frequencies, while the style is cultural and personal. The future style library includes short practical cuts, long, straight, wavy, curly and coiled styles, braids, tied and wrapped styles, shaved elements, formal arrangements and ornaments. No style is required for Sagekin ancestry.

## 5. Facial hair

Facial hair stays independent, from clean-shaven to substantial where biologically appropriate, and never determines ancestry.

## 6. Cultural visual language

Motifs include circles and celestial cycles, arcs and horizons, stars and navigation, grids and measurement, interlocking geometry, and waves. They may appear in architecture, manuscripts, textiles, jewelry, institutional seals, coins, equipment, markings and decorative arts. The culture isn't reduced to book imagery.

## 7. Clothing direction

Lightweight layered textiles suit the warm maritime homeland, with strong variation by region, wealth, occupation, institution, religion, travel, military service and personal taste. Robes aren't universal, and Sagekin abroad may dress locally.

## 8. Jewelry and personal objects

Fine personal objects include rings, earrings, necklaces, bracelets, hair ornaments, brooches, signets, inscribed pendants, institutional tokens, navigation tools, writing implements, measuring instruments, and lenses or instrument cases. These reflect culture, background and identity, not biology.

## 9. Tattoos and markings

There's no universal Sagekin tattooing. Particular cultures, religions, institutions, professions, families or individuals may use celestial, navigational, geometric, family, memorial, religious or institutional marks, or purely personal decoration. Unmarked Sagekin are completely normal.

## 10. Presentation presets (provisional)

The presets are Academy Formal, Harbor Practical, Merchant Urban, Traveler, Observatory Ceremonial and Working Artisan. They never overwrite race, anatomy, height, frame, muscle, body fat or face.

## 11. Character identity layers (proposed universal)

| Layer | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Ancestry | Biological and population heritage |
| Birthplace | Where the individual was born |
| Culture | The community and traditions that shaped them |
| Background | Prior life, occupation, training or experience |

None is derived from another. For example, a character can have Sagekin ancestry, be born in Marchfolk lands, live mainly in Marchfolk culture, and have a craft or military background. The gameplay implementation is unresolved.

## 12. Cultural independence validation

All of these remain biologically valid Sagekin: traditional homeland, maritime worker, academic, soldier, artisan, merchant, traveler, raised abroad, and fully non-Sagekin presentation.

# v1.4 Presets, population-aware randomization and racial validation

This is design only, preserving v1.0–v1.3 and all universal rules.

## 1. Two preset categories (proposed universal)

| Category | Covers | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Character preset | A complete, editable starting character: anatomy, face, age, composition, natural appearance, hair, presentation | Built from standard customization data |
| Presentation preset | Grooming, hairstyle, markings, accessories, clothing and other cultural or personal appearance | Never replaces biological anatomy |

## 2. Sagekin character presets (provisional)

1. Academy Lecturer.
2. Harbor Navigator.
3. Urban Artisan.
4. Merchant Traveler.
5. Coastal Soldier.
6. Heavyset Archivist.
7. Young Wayfarer.
8. Elder Astronomer.

These are starting concepts only. They never assign class, stats, personality, permanent occupation or abilities, and all are reproducible and fully editable.

## 3. Population-aware randomization

Race-aware randomization grows into population-aware randomization. Inherited appearance can use weighted population distributions: height, body proportions, facial anatomy, natural skin, hair color and texture, eye color and similar traits. Manual customization always keeps the full biologically valid range.

## 4. Validity versus frequency

**Biological validity** is whether a trait is supported by the population. **Population frequency** is how often it appears in randomization or NPC generation. The designer-facing categories are Very Common, Common, Uncommon and Rare. Exact percentages aren't defined, and the implementation may use continuous weighted distributions rather than category rolls.

## 5. Soft trait correlations (proposed universal)

One generated trait may gently shift the odds of another. This is never a rigid phenotype package or fixed "racial face type". The goals are population coherence, individual diversity, no implausible fully independent randomization and no repeated stereotyped faces. The implementation is unresolved.

## 6. Regional subpopulations (future worldbuilding)

The homeland may later hold subpopulations with subtly different distributions, such as coastal, interior, island, or regional and city groups. None is defined now, and one broad Sagekin population model is used.

## 7. Large-sample population test

Generate 100 Marchfolk, 100 Sagekin and 100 Skarn, first with presentation neutralized. The desired result is:

| Race | Expected result |
| --- | --- |
| Marchfolk | Human Reference Population; broad human variation |
| Sagekin | Overlapping but statistically shifted human traits |
| Skarn | A much stronger skeletal and mass distinction |

Not every individual needs to be identifiable by ancestry, and some Marchfolk and Sagekin ambiguity is intentional.

## 8. Clone and stereotype failure test

Generation fails if large samples show:

- [ ] Nearly identical noses.
- [ ] One dominant facial template.
- [ ] Universally tall, thin Sagekin.
- [ ] Nearly identical pigmentation.
- [ ] Identical hair.
- [ ] Mandatory facial hair.
- [ ] Mandatory scholarly appearance.
- [ ] Any single racial marker repeated excessively.

Identity must come from combinations and distributions.

## 9. Cultural neutralization test

The test uses neutral clothing and grooming where practical, with no cultural jewelry, insignia, identifying markings or occupation-specific accessories. Sagekin identity stays statistically present through ancestry.

## 10. Cross-cultural presentation test

| Ancestry | Presentation |
| --- | --- |
| Sagekin | Sagekin |
| Sagekin | Marchfolk |
| Marchfolk | Sagekin |
| Skarn | Sagekin |

Presentation never changes anatomy. A Marchfolk in Sagekin dress stays biologically Marchfolk, and a Sagekin raised in Marchfolk culture stays biologically Sagekin.

## 11. Selective randomization

The options are Randomize All, Body, Face, Hair, Skin Details, Markings and Presentation, with parameter and group locks where feasible. Randomization keeps the race unless the player explicitly changes it.

## 12. Validation goal

The system can generate many Sagekin who look individually distinct, stay anatomically human, show believable population relationships, avoid cloning and scholarly stereotypes, cover broad body, age and face variation, overlap naturally with Marchfolk, and remain statistically distinguishable as a population.

## 2. No artificial exaggeration

Recognizability never comes from pushing traits past the design. All Sagekin are never extremely tall, extremely thin or long-faced, never identical in nose, eyes, pigmentation or hair color, and never elven in limb proportion. Individual ambiguity with Marchfolk is fine, and population coherence is the goal.

## 3. Animation validation

The tests cover idle, walk, run, acceleration and deceleration, turning, jumping and landing, crouching, stairs and slopes, foot IK, hand placement, weapon grips and environment interactions. There's no universal "Sagekin movement style". Movement differences come from anatomy, composition, equipment, animation state and later gameplay systems.

## 4. Facial animation validation (proposed universal)

Customized faces are checked in neutral, speech, smile, anger, surprise, blink, eye movement and strong or extreme expressions. Facial animation must keep individual identity and supported variation.

## 5. Equipment and cross-cultural clothing

The tests cover Sagekin cultural clothing, Marchfolk clothing where wearable, shared clothing, armor, gloves, boots, helmets, weapons and attachment points. Clothing never changes ancestry: Marchfolk in Sagekin dress stay Marchfolk, and the reverse holds too.

## 6. Population generation layering (conceptual)

Ancestry → inherited, population-aware appearance → individual anatomical variation → birthplace and culture → background → personal presentation.

These are never collapsed into one race preset. The generator may use weighted distributions, anatomical constraints, soft trait correlations where justified, race-specific ranges and individual random variation. The implementation is unresolved.

## 7. Deterministic generation (proposed universal)

Generated characters should be exactly reproducible, from stored configuration data, deterministic seeds or both. This supports NPC persistence, save and load, testing, bug reproduction, preset creation and multiplayer consistency. The implementation isn't chosen yet.

## 8. Large-sample validation

The test uses neutralized samples of 100 Marchfolk, 100 Sagekin and 100 Skarn. It checks for **within-population diversity** (no clones or templates) and **between-population coherence** (distributions reflect the intended ancestry). Perfect classification of every individual isn't required.

## 9. Fenn and Aelari boundary (active test)

Activated by Pass 2 AC-7: Fenn and Aelari are defined and carry their own Sagekin-boundary tests (FN-17, AE-21), so the most extreme valid Sagekin are compared against Fenn and Aelari. Single measurements may overlap, but complete Sagekin anatomy must never reproduce elven anatomy.

## 10. Universal architecture lessons

| Race | What it established |
| --- | --- |
| Marchfolk | Human Reference Population; deep human customization |
| Skarn | Large-scale skeletal and mass variation, and world-scale compatibility |
| Sagekin | Population distributions, ancestry and culture separation, subtle population variation, population-aware generation |

One universal system supports all three, without forcing identical anatomy or separate creators.
