# Part 1 — Racial foundation, scale and positive body anatomy

## 1. Core design statement

> **Cogling are not miniature humans, smaller Pipkin, slender Durrim, child-proportioned adults, or biological embodiments of tinkering.**

Cogling are a distinct very-small humanoid population whose first-pass anatomy must remain recognizable after culture, clothing, tools, hairstyle and occupation are removed.

The earlier brief provides starting direction—very small stature, compact torso, narrow limbs, relatively long fingers and fine-scale physical behavior—but every inherited phrase is subject to anatomical clarification rather than stereotype preservation.

## 2. Short-race design space

The three short playable populations occupy different positive anatomical spaces:

- **Durrim:** compact structural concentration — short adult stature, high skeletal structural presence, broad/deep vertically compact torso, substantial joints and reduced limb contribution.
- **Pipkin:** light compact adult proportionality — Low-Set Compact Trunk Architecture, mature pelvis, moderate thorax, comparatively light skeleton and proportionally sustained limbs.
- **Cogling:** **independently defined below**; they are not assigned the leftover midpoint between Durrim and Pipkin.

Part 1 may compare against approved races to prevent convergence, but Cogling anatomy must be stated positively first.

The formal Short-Race Comparative Anatomy Review occurs only after Cogling's own first pass is complete.

## 3. Proposed positive specialization

Cogling are built around **Fine-Scale Elongated Articulation**.

> **Cogling possess a very-small adult humanoid architecture organized around a narrow stable central core and near-human total limb contribution, with comparatively fine skeletal shafts and a distinctive redistribution within the limbs toward the forearms, lower legs, hands and fingers. Their identity comes from where limb length and articulation are distributed, not from becoming globally limb-dominant or reach-specialized.**

Project shorthand: **Fine-Scale Elongated Articulation**.

This is anatomical design terminology, not a creator-facing master slider.

The core body sequence is:

**narrow stable central core → near-human total limb contribution → fine limb shafts → within-limb distal redistribution → proportionally emphasized hands/fingers**

The specialization must remain visible without tools, goggles, hats, machinery or occupation cues.

## 4. Very-small adult stature

Cogling are intended to be the smallest playable humanoid population in the current roster.

The old brief's ~0.45× human-height reference is **preliminary and non-authoritative**.

For first-pass testing, use a provisional adult stature envelope of approximately:

- **Minimum:** ~76 cm / 2'6"
- **Reference:** ~91 cm / 3'0"
- **Maximum:** ~107 cm / 3'6"

These values are provisional pending:
- Cogling body validation;
- the Short-Race Comparative Anatomy Review;
- full-roster world-scale/accessibility validation;
- camera/collision/interaction review.

The current provisional ranges create an **intentional overlap zone of approximately 91–107 cm with Pipkin**. This is not a single-height boundary.

The ~91 cm Cogling reference meets the current provisional Pipkin minimum, while the ~107 cm Cogling maximum meets the current Pipkin reference. Cogling and Pipkin must therefore remain anatomically distinct across a meaningful shared stature zone rather than relying on height as the discriminator.

No whole-body scale multiplier is authoritative.

## 5. Adult-read requirement

Every valid playable adult Cogling must read as an adult from anatomy alone.

Cogling adulthood must never depend on:
- facial hair;
- wrinkles;
- clothing;
- occupation;
- voice;
- tools;
- posture;
- personality;
- or cultural presentation.

Adult Cogling must not be created by combining:
- an oversized cranium;
- enlarged eyes;
- tiny nose or jaw;
- shortened midface;
- juvenile shoulder/pelvic relationships;
- infant-like hands/feet;
- toddler-like limb taper;
- or childlike body-fat distribution.

Very small stature is not juvenile anatomy.

## 6. Central body architecture

Cogling possess a **narrow stable central core**.

At normalized displayed height, total torso contribution remains broadly near the Marchfolk adult range rather than becoming strongly limb-dominant. The Cogling-specific signal comes from a comparatively narrow adult thoracic/pelvic core combined with redistribution **within** otherwise broadly near-human total limb contribution.

The thorax is fully adult, moderate in depth and comparatively narrow-to-moderate transversely. The pelvis is mature and integrated without becoming the dominant silhouette anchor. The central body therefore reads as an adult structural core rather than a shortened support between exaggerated limbs.

This differs from:
- Durrim structural concentration, with greater breadth/depth, joint scale and vertical compactness;
- Pipkin Low-Set Compact Trunk Architecture, where the mature pelvis contributes strongly relative to the moderate thorax;
- Fenn anatomy, where greater overall limb share contributes to the silhouette;
- Grask anatomy, where limb dominance and reach specialization are major structural signals.

Exact thorax-to-pelvis and torso-to-limb ratios remain OPEN pending validation.

## 7. Skeletal construction

Cogling trend toward **fine skeletal construction**.

Fine means:
- comparatively narrow long-bone shafts;
- smaller absolute joint structures;
- lower skeletal breadth/depth than Durrim;
- lower structural mass than a height-normalized Marchfolk reference;
- and clean segment transitions.

Fine does **not** mean:
- brittle;
- weak;
- undernourished;
- sickly;
- hollow-boned;
- low-density bone;
- or incapable of substantial muscular development.

Bone material properties are not inferred from visual gracility.

## 8. Distal segment emphasis

Cogling positive identity includes proportional emphasis toward distal limb segments **within a broadly near-human total arm and leg contribution to stature**.

At population level, Cogling may trend toward:
- relatively greater forearm contribution within total arm length;
- relatively greater lower-leg contribution within total leg length;
- hands that occupy a larger proportional share of arm length than in normalized Marchfolk;
- fingers that are proportionally long relative to palm length;
- feet whose length supports the distal-emphasis system without becoming oversized.

The corresponding proximal segments accommodate that redistribution so the race does not become globally limb-dominant.

This is **not** permission for extreme spider-like limbs, giant hands, giant feet or implausibly thin bones.

Exact segment ratios remain OPEN for later measurement/prototyping.

## 9. Arms and reach

Cogling arms remain adult humanoid arms.

Total arm contribution remains broadly near the Marchfolk adult range at normalized height. Cogling are not a reach-specialized population in the Grask sense.

The primary distinction is **segment distribution and distal articulation**, not maximum reach.

At equal height with a Pipkin, a Cogling may present a different arm/torso relationship, but no fixed reach superiority is approved until comparative validation.

## 10. Hands

Cogling hands are a major supporting anatomical identifier.

They should be:
- fully adult;
- proportionally noticeable without becoming huge;
- structurally fine rather than Durrim-like;
- capable of broad natural hand-shape variation;
- and integrated with relatively long fingers.

Five digits per hand are the first-pass humanoid baseline unless later evidence establishes otherwise.

Long fingers must not become:
- claws;
- talons;
- skeletal caricatures;
- universally thin fingers;
- or a biological symbol of engineering.

Finger length is anatomy. What a Cogling learns to do with those fingers is culture, training and occupation.

## 11. Fine motor control firewall

The old brief describes "fine motor control" and "precise hand movements."

Part 1 does **not** convert visible hand anatomy into an automatic gameplay dexterity bonus.

Approved anatomy may support plausible fine-scale hand motion, but:
- manual skill is not automatically higher;
- crafting skill is not biological;
- engineering aptitude is not biological;
- lockpicking is not biological;
- tool proficiency is not biological;
- and intelligence is not inferred from hand structure.

Whether Cogling receive any gameplay-level dexterity, interaction or crafting effects remains OPEN.

## 12. Legs

Cogling legs remain fully adult and plantigrade.

The lower leg may make a somewhat greater contribution **within total leg length**, while total leg contribution to stature remains broadly near the Marchfolk adult range, consistent with Fine-Scale Elongated Articulation.

This does not make Cogling:
- digitigrade;
- stilt-legged;
- unusually tall for their torso;
- miniature Fenn;
- spring-loaded jumpers;
- or inherently fast.

Femur-to-lower-leg balance requires explicit validation.

## 13. Feet

Cogling feet are adult plantigrade humanoid feet.

They should support:
- stable adult stance;
- the approved lower-leg relationship;
- normal footwear;
- and broad individual variation.

They must not become:
- Pipkin stereotype inversion through tiny feet;
- giant halfling feet;
- child feet;
- prehensile feet;
- or automatic climbing adaptations.

Exact foot length, width, arch and toe relationships remain OPEN.

## 14. Joints

Cogling joints are relatively small in absolute dimensions and visually articulated, but not fragile.

"Articulated" means that anatomical transitions at wrist, elbow, ankle and knee remain legible within the very-small body rather than disappearing into uniform scaled-down forms.

This does not require knobby joints or exposed-bone caricature.

Joint breadth/depth ranges remain OPEN.

## 15. Shoulder architecture

Cogling shoulders are adult and structurally coherent with the thorax and arms.

They trend away from Durrim breadth and mass but must not become juvenile sloped shoulders by default.

Clavicular breadth, scapular relationship and shoulder-to-thorax integration require later validation.

No single shoulder-to-hip ratio defines Cogling identity.

## 16. Pelvis

Cogling possess a fully mature adult pelvis.

The pelvis must provide adult locomotor and sex-related anatomy without becoming:
- childlike/narrow through immaturity;
- Pipkin's primary lower-trunk identity mechanism;
- Durrim-like structural mass;
- or an external hip-width stereotype.

Pelvic breadth, depth, height and sex-related morphology remain OPEN for detailed Part 2 work.

## 17. Neck and head support

The neck must support an adult head without creating either:
- a toy-like oversized-head silhouette;
- or a disproportionately thick Durrim/Gorrund-like neck.

Neck length and circumference vary with frame, sex-related anatomy, muscularity and individual genetics.

Head size cannot be used as the main mechanism that makes Cogling look small.

## 18. Head-to-body relationship

The old gnome archetype often relies on a large head. Cogling do not.

A Cogling head may occupy a somewhat greater fraction of total stature than a tall adult population simply because of allometric scaling, but **racial identity cannot depend on deliberate head enlargement**.

Head-to-body ratio must remain within an adult-valid Cogling envelope and survive direct comparison with a human child.

Exact ratios remain OPEN.

## 19. Frame variation

Cogling support meaningful skeletal-frame variation.

Narrow, Balanced and Broad may be used as provisional creator-facing starting concepts if they remain valid for Cogling anatomy, but they are not human frame copies.

A Broad Cogling remains fine-scale relative to Durrim structural concentration. A Narrow Cogling remains healthy and adult rather than fragile or childlike.

Frame changes must preserve Fine-Scale Elongated Articulation.

## 20. Composition independence

Current muscularity and body-fat amount/distribution remain separate from skeletal frame.

Valid Cogling include:
- low-muscle and highly muscular adults;
- low-fat and high-fat adults;
- regionally developed physiques;
- varied soft-tissue distribution.

High muscularity must not erase the underlying fine skeletal system.

High body fat must not erase adult joint/limb organization or turn Cogling into Pipkin/Durrim caricatures.

Low muscle/fat must not become frailty.

## 21. Muscular Development Capacity

Cogling require a distinction between:
- **Muscular Development Capacity** — biological potential/envelope;
- **Current Muscularity** — the individual's present developed muscle mass.

The population may have its own capacity distribution, but exact limits are OPEN.

Fine bones do not logically require low muscular-development capacity.

## 22. Body-fat distribution

Body-fat amount and distribution are distinct controls.

Cogling do not have a required:
- round belly;
- thin body;
- soft body;
- childlike fat pattern;
- or "tiny old inventor" silhouette.

Sex-related and individual distribution patterns remain subject to the universal anatomy model and later detailed review.

## 23. Sex-related anatomy

Cogling follow the universal four-layer character-creation amendment.

Sex-related physical anatomy may affect pelvis, thorax, soft tissue and other biological structures where appropriate, but does not automatically determine:
- height;
- frame;
- muscularity;
- fat amount;
- facial identity;
- hairstyle;
- clothing;
- class;
- culture;
- occupation;
- or personality.

Magnitude and morphology of Cogling sex-related dimorphism remain OPEN.

## 24. Digits and nails

First-pass Cogling use:
- five fingers per hand;
- five toes per foot;
- ordinary humanoid nails.

No claws, talons, specialized gripping pads or tool-use structures are implied.

## 25. Surface phenotype boundary

Part 1 does not define Cogling pigmentation, hair, eyes, freckles or other surface phenotype.

Those traits cannot be invented here to rescue anatomical distinctiveness.

Cogling must remain recognizable in neutral gray material with hair, clothing and tools removed.

Surface phenotype will receive its own later section.

## 26. Facial anatomy boundary

The brief's phrase "expressive face" is not yet a facial specification.

Expression capacity is not a substitute for positive craniofacial anatomy, and Cogling are not defined by:
- huge eyes;
- oversized nose;
- permanently raised brows;
- smiling default;
- wrinkles;
- or comic facial proportions.

Part 2/3 will establish positive facial identity after the body foundation is audited.

## 27. Culture firewall

Engineering and tinkering are explicitly cultural/occupational possibilities, not biological identity.

Cogling biology does not require:
- inventiveness;
- intelligence;
- curiosity;
- eccentricity;
- mechanical aptitude;
- crafting skill;
- goggles;
- tools;
- workshops;
- machines;
- hats;
- academic behavior;
- or comic speech/personality.

A Cogling farmer, soldier, sailor, courtier, hunter, scholar or laborer remains equally biologically valid.

## 28. Movement firewall

The old brief says "quick steps, frequent turns, efficient climbing, precise hand movements and small physical adjustments."

These are **legacy movement descriptions subject to later review**, not automatically approved gameplay traits or biological consequences.

Part 1 approves only anatomy. It does not yet grant:
- faster acceleration;
- superior turning;
- superior balance;
- climbing bonuses;
- dexterity bonuses;
- stealth bonuses;
- or faster interaction speed.

Movement must later be derived from approved anatomy and separated from gameplay statistics, as with Pipkin.

## 29. Equipment and object-scale boundary

Very small stature does not authorize automatic scaling of canonical weapons, tools or world objects.

Equipment fit and equipment feasibility remain separate.

Cogling may eventually require extensive race-aware equipment, interaction and world-scale solutions because of their stature. Those requirements do not justify changing the anatomy.

## 30. World-scale consequence

If the provisional minimum of ~76 cm survives validation, Cogling will materially extend the current lower end of playable stature.

Doors, stairs, ladders, furniture, counters, crafting stations, interaction points, cameras, collision, combat presentation and traversal must therefore be tested against Cogling target anatomy.

The world adapts to approved playable anatomy; Cogling are not made taller merely to fit Marchfolk-authored geometry.

## 31. Prototype authority

Any existing prototype Cogling scale, stats, collision, animation, class restriction, race description or equipment behavior is **PROTOTYPE / NON-AUTHORITATIVE** unless independently approved.

The known prototype presentation is approximately **0.72× Marchfolk scale (~125 cm)**. This exceeds the current provisional Cogling maximum (~107 cm), overlaps/exceeds the Durrim minimum (~122 cm), and even places prototype Cogling above the prototype Pipkin (~0.70×/~121 cm), reversing the intended current Cogling/Pipkin stature relationship. These prototype values have no design authority.

No implementation audit is performed in Part 1.

## 33. Combined-proportion validity

Cogling controls cannot be validated independently.

A valid height, narrow frame, long fingers, emphasized lower leg and small joints can still combine into an invalid childlike or caricatured body.

The future creator must enforce relationship-aware validity among:
- stature;
- head contribution;
- torso contribution;
- pelvis;
- shoulder structure;
- arm and leg segment distribution;
- hand/palm/finger proportions;
- foot proportions;
- joint dimensions;
- frame;
- muscularity;
- body-fat distribution;
- and sex-related anatomy.

No "Cogling Proportion" master slider is approved.

## 34. Part 1 identity statement

> **Cogling are a very-small adult humanoid population defined by Fine-Scale Elongated Articulation: a compact stable central body transitions into comparatively narrow limb shafts and clearly articulated distal joints, with proportional emphasis toward forearms, lower legs, hands and especially fingers. Their small scale never requires juvenile anatomy, oversized heads or eyes, fragile bodies, miniature equipment, or biological tinkering stereotypes. Their anatomy supports broad frame and composition variation while culture, occupation, movement performance and gameplay traits remain separate.**
