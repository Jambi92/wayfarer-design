# Wayfarer live mannequin combat rehearsal

October 31, 2026. Combat review now has an articulated sword-and-shield opponent. The new isolated rehearsal uses two stock mannequins and the real Vanguard combat component, allowing attacks, directional blocks, timed parries, counters and guard breaks to be judged as exchanges between fighters.

The partner approaches, adjusts distance, turns into its telegraph and alternates attacks with defensive reactions. A successful defense can lead into a counter. Separate practice behaviors allow active sparring, attack practice, held guard, timed-parry practice and a passive opponent. The partner can be paused or reset. This is local rehearsal behavior; navigation and campaign enemy AI still need their own development.

Both fighters use the connected standing and moving sword choreography. New copied clips add shield-to-counter entries, hit reactions, a parry redirect, the attacker's parried recoil and heavy-hit or broken-guard recovery. A light hit during a committed swing adds a small visual flinch without cancelling the attack. That flinch preserves the character's native crouch offset. Hard interruptions follow the actual combat stagger. Guard-to-counter transitions preserve the raised shield entry, and held guard can resume after committed recovery.

Verification covers the new pawn's planted chains, movement handoff, jump and crouch; sixteen two-sided hit, block, parry, heavy-block, exhausted-guard, rear-hit, miss and counter cases; and a normal-speed exchange against the active partner. Damage, stamina, interruption and animation results are checked together. Retained attack-chain, locomotion, ranged, swimming and world-obstruction checks run alongside copied-clip and live sword/shield clearance audits. Native side views are used to review the contact poses.

The work remains in separate copied rehearsal levels with an opt-in pawn. Earlier pawn defaults, approved environments, character assets, original clips, configuration, saves and the original movement donor are checked for preservation. Native assets, screenshots, source changes and detailed diagnostics stay private, with a verified compressed revision on the E drive.

This is the first paired-combat refinement pass. Contact timing, directional reactions and footwork still need visual polish. The current contact model uses capsule reach, an attack arc and world obstruction; final blade traces, finished character retargeting, multiplayer and the complete class abilities remain unfinished. Player aim and travel continue to use physical player input.
