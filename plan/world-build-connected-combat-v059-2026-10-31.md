# Wayfarer connected full-body combat study

October 31, 2026. The preceding sword-and-shield prototype widened the sword arcs and corrected the shield arm, but standing attacks still used an upper-body overlay. The new isolated combat rehearsal adds planted whole-body cuts: a small lifted stance adjustment, foot and heel pivots, knee compression and pelvis-led rotation. The heavy attack uses a deeper weight transfer.

Buffered follow-ups enter from the preceding action's finish. Two cuts, a thrust and a heavy cut have separate standing and moving versions; eight legal buffered relationships have matched entry copies for both modes. The recovery poses keep the weapon in a useful low guard rather than returning every cut to the generic ready pose. The shield arm retains its stable relationship to the torso.

Starting movement, jumping or crouching during a standing attack explicitly releases the planted-body montage and starts the moving overlay at the current swing phase. Damage is not replayed, attack timing is retained and movement remains controlled by the existing locomotion system. A moving attack stays with locomotion when the player stops, avoiding a sudden plant halfway through the cut. The new animation copies are loaded before combat.

Validation covers five new actual-input body-motion scenarios, the nine retained attack-chain/commitment scenarios, shield guard/catch transitions, movement and ranged controls, and seven world-clearance cases. Compressed animation readback checks limb lengths, planted toe positions, linked entry positions and conservative shield clearance. A separate live audit measures evaluated sword/shield transforms through combat and guard blends. Native review covers the planted cut, recovery, return-cut and thrust joins, heavy transfer and moving handoff.

The work uses a separate pawn, copied rehearsal route and lab. Prior pawn defaults, approved environments, models, rigs, original animation assets, configuration, saves and the original project are checked for preservation. The compressed E-drive revision is verified by archive readback. Public updates remain prose only.

This is the first connected stock-mannequin choreography pass. It does not establish final character retargeting, physical blade traces, multiplayer, named class abilities or finished animation quality. Aim continues to follow physical player facing; the change adds no automatic travel or steering toward selected targets.
