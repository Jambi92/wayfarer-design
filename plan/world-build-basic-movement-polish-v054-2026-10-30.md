# Wayfarer basic movement polish and combat-reference review

October 30, 2026. Movement Polish v054 is a separate opt-in pawn and copied rehearsal route/lab. It refines basic locomotion while the class-specific fighting animations are developed. Existing maximum travel speeds, jump impulse, turn rate and the preceding melee-clearance rules are retained. The original project, earlier development levels, approved assets and character rigs are preserved.

The baseline exposed minimum-rate overstriding at slow walking speed, a stopping animation that could remain upright after crouching, and tread-water animation during vertical-only swimming. The new pawn matches slow walking playback to movement speed, hands stopping/turning poses over promptly when stance changes, and evaluates vertical swimming as active swimming. Crouch/stand camera transitions ease independently of immediate capsule collision changes. A modest acceleration/braking adjustment makes starts responsive and stops less abrupt without long drift.

Final-build validation passes 44 directional/stance/clearance checks, the 69-check original-control regression and 12 recorded movement-measurement checks. Coverage includes all eight run and crouch directions, diagonal speed, turn-in-place playback, low-ceiling stand prevention, walking/running/sprinting, stamina, jumping/landing, camera changes, guarding, evaluated melee/ranged clips, ammunition, swimming, diving, surfacing and physical ramp exit. Camera transition measurements use a transient slow-motion lab run to obtain controlled frame intervals; normal control regressions use normal simulation speed. The copied connected route also passes its pawn/animation/production-roster smoke check and map check has zero errors and warnings.

The completed Class Moves and Combat Bible and its 40 schematic motion studies were reviewed for the next animation stage. The briefs usefully distinguish source mechanics, proposed choreography and undeveloped effects. Two discrepancies against later source revisions were recorded privately before integration. The reference files are preserved unchanged; schematic GIFs remain pose and continuity studies rather than importable production animation. Vanguard and Lightkeeper have detailed briefs; the other twelve classes retain their stated development boundaries.

This milestone does not complete weapon-specific branching combos, class abilities, final weapon contact, all-race retargeting, multiplayer or the whole tutorial. It provides a reversible basic-movement foundation. Source/configuration/save preservation and previous-checkpoint hashes are checked before creating a compressed E-drive backup, which is verified by archive readback. Public updates remain prose only; native files, screenshots, canonical references and diagnostics remain private.
