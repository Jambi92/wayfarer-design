# Major-build concept gate and tutorial capacity

User direction, 2026-10-29: every major portion of the build must have concept art before implementation, providing a clear basis for what the team is aiming for.

## Required sequence

For each major build portion, establish canonical purpose, prepare section-specific concept art and a scaled layout/cutaway, and obtain user approval before building. The layout must show player scale, traversal, vertical connections and important sightlines. Test a reversible blockout for gameplay and pacing before detailed art, and compare native-engine views against the approved concept at milestones. A broad visual-direction image does not settle every future area's spatial design.

## Current tutorial assessment

The grand-cavern study is approximately 110 by 80 metres. That is substantial room volume, not proof of a complete tutorial. The latest retained grand-chamber route definitions measure approximately 147.4m for the reveal route, 114.3m for the stair route and 21.4m for the habitat route. They are route definitions, not an additive measure of unique cave extent. Test totals across repeated routes and body-size cases are not unique content length.

Full tutorial spatial capacity and pacing have not been accepted. Existing geometry reserves main-progression and escape functions, but traversal acceptance does not demonstrate that optional incidents, combat teaching, NPC activity, investigation and return trips form a coherent playable tutorial.

The accepted Prologue contains five optional preparation incidents:
- Bad Support: a secondary-gallery inspection with reinforcement or safe closure and later escape consequences.
- Missing Worker: a search and recovery branch with an actual escorted return.
- Stolen Equipment: investigation, component records and custody across the preparation window.
- Medical Supplies: camp stock and approved supply alternatives, including caravan/escort opportunities.
- Old Drainage: service-passage clearing, restored flow and a verified later escape connection.

These incidents are not all exclusively underground. The full layout must respect the source's camp window and returns.

## Next planning deliverable

Before another major cavern build, prepare one complete scaled Prologue master layout with linked concept views: surface/camp, main descent and teaching/encounter spaces, optional incident locations and branch loops, lower works, protected first-opening prison staging, triage/junction, archive and all three escape alternatives. Identify shared routes, connections, actor/support positions and outstanding decisions.

Use a quest-to-space coverage table and a playable pacing blockout to decide where expansion is needed. Distinct branches, useful loops and vertically connected spaces should be designed before choosing arbitrary extra distance or chamber counts. This record approves no new geography, quest event, tutorial duration or final map dimension.

The existing development level is preserved. This planning update makes no native level or character changes.
