## Recommended structure

Keep the grand natural cavern near its established 110 × 80 m study target. Propose distinct discovery/combat, worker-search, support, drainage and habitat branches, followed by lower component works, deep repair/load investigation and an untouched first-opening prison. Reuse of the grand study as an upper discovery hub is a proposed role change from its earlier emergency-route study, requiring review.

Preserve the authored sequence: first descent and two-way-door observation; actual return to camp; preparation window; lower investigation and revised assessment; first opening and commitment; darkness/triage/voice; one of three usable escape connections; regroup; archive; familiar-work consequences; falling-behind response; final upper-exit view; daylight assembly and saved resolution.

Bad Support belongs in a secondary gallery, Missing Worker in an escorted recovery branch, Stolen Equipment in the supply/component custody investigation, Medical Supplies at camp with an ordinary work/escort alternative, and Old Drainage in a physically cleared and verified service connection. Optional incidents never become hidden prerequisites. Support closure remains a valid barricaded safety decision; a repair request is not completed reinforcement. Emergency continuation remains available when optional preparation is skipped.

All three escape alternatives rejoin underground before the archive. The prison remains free of expedition tools, timber scaffolds, platforms and installed work lights that could not already be inside at first opening. Character work is untouched. Habitat art is provisional and does not approve a new quest, final creature design or bestiary entry.
